Stavkirke

other · Norway · Nordic

A medieval Norwegian timber church built on a post-and-plank frame, with steep shingled roofs, carved portals, and dragon-head finials. Around thirty stave churches survive of the more than a thousand once built, among them Borgund, Urnes, and Heddal. In folklore the churches mark where the old faith met the new, and legends tell of trolls turned to stone at the sound of their bells.
