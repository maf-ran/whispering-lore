Adinkra Cloth

garment · Ghana · West Africa

Adinkra is the stamped cloth of the Akan, dyed in dark tones and printed with the symbolic motifs that bear the same name, each one carrying a proverb and a moral lesson: the sankofa bird that looks back to learn, gye nyame, 'except God,' and dozens more that together form a written language in cloth. The dye is made from the bark of the badie tree, boiled down to a deep black, and the blocks are carved from calabash, pressed into the fabric to stamp the patterns row by row.
