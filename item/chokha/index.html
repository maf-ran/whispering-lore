Chokha (Georgian Men's Coat)

garment · Georgia · Caucasus

The chokha is the traditional men's coat of the Caucasus, a long woollen garment with a fitted waist, a flared skirt and, most distinctively, rows of small cartridge cases - the gazyr - sewn diagonally across the chest. The gazyr began as real ammunition holders, with silver, wood or bone caps, and survived as ornament long after firearms made them obsolete, so the decorated chest became the emblem of Caucasian dress.
