Ade (Yoruba Beaded Crown)

crown · Nigeria · West Africa

The ade is the beaded crown of the Yoruba oba, the king, a cone- or basket-shaped headdress whose cascading fringe of glass beads veils the wearer's face, leaving only the mouth visible as he speaks. The patterns worked in the beads are a language of their own: birds, the Ogboni eye, the royal face, and motifs recalling Oduduwa, the founding ancestor from whom the kingship descends, whose divine gaze the veil is said to shield his subjects from.
