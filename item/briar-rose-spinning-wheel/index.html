Briar Rose's Spinning Wheel

tool · Germany · Germanic

The spinning wheel in the Grimms' fairy tale 'Briar Rose' (Dornröschen, KHM 50) is the instrument of the sleeping curse: the princess pricks her finger on its spindle, and the whole castle falls asleep for a hundred years. The king had ordered every spindle in the land burned to cheat the witch's prophecy, but an old woman in a forgotten tower still spins, and curiosity leads the princess to the fatal prick.
