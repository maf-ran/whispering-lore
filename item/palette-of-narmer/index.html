The Palette of Narmer

ritual object · Egypt · Egyptian

A ceremonial siltstone palette from Hierakonpolis carved around 3100 BCE, showing King Narmer on one face smiting a captured enemy with a mace and on the other reviewing decapitated captives, long read as an early record of the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt. The falcon-god Horus perches above a bound foe on the same face, holding a rope, and the king wears the white crown of Upper Egypt on one side and the red crown of Lower Egypt on the other.
