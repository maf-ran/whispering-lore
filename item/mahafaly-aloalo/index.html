Mahafaly Aloalo Funerary Post

ritual object · Madagascar · East Africa

The aloalo are the carved funerary posts of the Mahafaly people of southwestern Madagascar, tall wooden stelae that stand on the stone tombs of chiefs and wealthy families. The name comes from alo, 'intermediary' or 'messenger', and the posts are believed to help the dead cross into the community of the ancestors.
