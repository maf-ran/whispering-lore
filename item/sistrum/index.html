The Sistrum

musical instrument · Egypt · Egyptian

A sacred rattle of looped bronze or copper rods strung across a frame, shaken in the cults of Hathor and Isis to please the goddess and to repel evil with its hissing, sibilant sound, called sesheshet in Egyptian after the noise it makes. Priestesses, queens, and kings bore it in temple ritual, and queens are depicted holding it in both hands; great ceremonial versions, often of gilt bronze, stood as temple furniture.
