The Cup of Jamshid

household object · Iran · Persian

The cup of the legendary shah Jamshid, in which, according to Persian tradition, the whole world could be seen as in a mirror, so that nothing on earth or in the seven climes was hidden from the king, who used it in divination. Despite a common modern assumption, the cup does not appear in Ferdowsi's Shahnameh; it belongs to later literature, where it is also given to the Kayanid king Kay Khosrow, and it became a byword for far-seeing wisdom in the verses of Hafez and Attar.
