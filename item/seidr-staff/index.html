The Völva's Staff

ritual object · Iceland · Nordic

The staff of the seeress or völva, who in saga accounts carried a knobbed staff as the badge of her office. Eiríks saga rauða describes the seeress Þorbjörg with a staff topped with a brass knob as she worked her seidr. Iron rods found in Viking-age female graves may be such staffs, though the identification is debated by archaeologists. The staff was more than a tool: it was the visible sign of the woman who could chant the seidr, see the future, and speak with the dead.
