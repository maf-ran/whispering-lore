Sibylline Books

other · Italy · Mediterranean

The nine books of prophetic verses the Cumaean Sibyl offered to the Roman king Tarquin, of which she burned six when he refused her price and sold the last three for the full amount of the original nine; Livy and Dionysius of Halicarnassus record the story.
