Mangalsutra

jewelry · India · South Asia

The mangalsutra is the necklace that marks a Hindu woman as married, tied around her neck by the groom during the wedding ceremony and then worn for life, though widows set it aside. The name means 'auspicious thread', and the simple gold and black-bead ornament of the north differs from the heavier gold pendant called thali worn in the south, where the tying ceremony is called mangalyam and is the most solemn moment of the wedding.
