Ngoma Drum (Swahili Coast)

musical instrument · Tanzania · East Africa

The ngoma is the wooden drum of the Swahili coast of Tanzania and Kenya, built as a barrel or goblet with a hide head and played in the ensembles that accompany weddings, dances, and life-cycle rites. In Swahili the word ngoma covers drum, dance, and festivity together, and the drumming groups of the coast keep a repertory of named rhythms passed down through families.
