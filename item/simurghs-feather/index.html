The Feather of the Simurgh

other · Iran · Persian

The feather given by the great bird Simurgh to the abandoned prince Zal and later to his son Rostam, to be burned whenever they needed the bird's aid, at whose smoke Simurgh came flying from Mount Alborz. In the Shahnameh the feather carries the infant Zal to safety when he is cast out for his white hair, and in Rostam's direst need, when the hero lies bound and helpless in a pit, the burning feather summons Simurgh, who teaches him how to escape.
