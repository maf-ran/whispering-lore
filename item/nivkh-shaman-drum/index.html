Nivkh Shaman's Drum

ritual object · Russia (Sakhalin/Khabarovsk) · Asia

Oval frame drum of the Nivkh people of Sakhalin and the Amur region, with a wooden handle, used by the shaman to accompany spirit journeys and healing rituals. Its sound marks the shaman's passage between the worlds, as recorded by ethnographers including Emiko Ohnuki-Tierney in her study of Nivkh shamanism. The drum is made with a bent wooden rim and a hide head, tightened while wet and dried over the frame, with a carved handle or cross-bar on the underside.
