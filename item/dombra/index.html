Dombra (Kazakh Two-String Lute)

musical instrument · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Pear-shaped, long-necked two-stringed plucked lute of the Kazakh steppe, the country's signature instrument. Solo instrumental pieces (kuy) performed on dombra are central to Kazakh identity and the tradition of Dombra Kuy is inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List. Legendary kuishi like Qurmangazy composed famous kuys on it, and epic singers accompanied their tales with the instrument.
