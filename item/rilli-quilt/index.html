Rilli Quilt

household object · Pakistan · South Asia

The rilli quilt is the patchwork bedding of Sindh, sewn by women from scraps and recycled cotton cloth cut into triangles, diamonds, squares and zigzag bands and joined in bright, ordered compositions. The craft is passed from mother to daughter, and the same word covers quilts made by applique, patchwork or embroidery, often combining several techniques in one cover.
