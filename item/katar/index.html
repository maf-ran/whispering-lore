Katar

weapon · India · South Asia

The katar is the Indian punch dagger, a straight, usually double-edged blade set between two parallel side bars that form an H-shaped grip, the whole weapon being pushed forward with the fist like a punch. Because the palm push concentrates the whole force of the arm behind a single point, the katar could pierce mail and helmets in close combat, and it became the characteristic weapon of Rajput and Maratha warriors from about the 16th century onward.
