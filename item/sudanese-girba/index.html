Sudanese Girba Water-Skin

container · Sudan · North Africa

The girba is the traditional water-skin of Sudan, made from the whole hide of a goat turned inside out so that the sealed legs hang as a bag while the neck serves as the opening for filling and pouring. The soft leather container is carried over the shoulder or slung on a donkey, and its porous surface slowly lets a thin film of water evaporate, which cools the contents even under the fierce heat of the northern Sudan.
