Katsina Owl Doll

religious object · United States · North America

The owl katsina doll, or mongwau tihu in Hopi, is a carved and painted cottonwood figure of the owl spirit of the Hopi katsinam, given to children so they may learn the owl's identity and power. The owl is a night hunter whose eyes and voice mark it as a being of wisdom and, in some traditions, a messenger connected to the spirit world. Like all katsina tihu the doll is carved from the soft root of the cottonwood, a wood the Hopi link to water and moisture, and painted with the colours and markings of the particular spirit.
