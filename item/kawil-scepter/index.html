K'awiil Scepter

ritual object · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

The K'awiil scepter, often called the manikin scepter, is a short staff topped by a small figure of the Maya lightning god K'awiil, a deity shown with a mirror set in his forehead, an elongated snout and a serpent leg. Classic Maya rulers carried it as proof of divine kingship, and royal portraits at Copan, Tikal, Piedras Negras and Palenque show it held at accession and in dance rituals, its snake leg trailing to the ground.
