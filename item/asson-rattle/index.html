Asson

ritual object · Haiti · Caribbean

The asson is the sacred rattle of Haitian Vodou, a gourd fitted with a handle and wrapped in a beaded net threaded with snake vertebrae, held only by the houngan or the mambo, the priest and priestess who lead the congregation. Its sound opens the ceremony: as the rattle is shaken before the peristyle, the lwa, the spirits, are called into the space, and its rhythm marks the moment they descend to possess the faithful.
