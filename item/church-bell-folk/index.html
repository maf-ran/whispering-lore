The Church Bell Against Storms

musical instrument · Sweden · Swedish

Swedish church bells were believed to protect the parish: they were rung to break up thunderclouds, drive away storms and pestilence, and even to help stop fires. The casting of a new bell was accompanied by rites, and the bells were thought to carry a blessing that evil powers could not withstand. Ringing against bad weather ('ringning mot oväder') is recorded in many Swedish parishes.
