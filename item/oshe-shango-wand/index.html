Oshe Shango Dance Wand

ritual object · Nigeria · West Africa

The oshe is the carved wooden dance wand of the Yoruba Shango cult, its upper end shaped into a double-bladed axe, the emblem of Shango, the god of thunder and lightning. Dancers of the cult swing paired oshe during worship, and the axe is said to carry the deity's power: the flash of the blade is his lightning, the heft of the wood his strength, and the double edge his two faces of destruction and renewal.
