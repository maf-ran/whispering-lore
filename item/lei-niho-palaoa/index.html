Lei Niho Palaoa (Whale-Tooth Pendant)

jewelry · Hawaii · Polynesian

Pendant of the Hawaiian chiefs made from a carved sperm whale tooth suspended on a braid of human hair, worn by men and women of high rank. The hair cord was itself a gift of honour, braided from hair offered by relatives and friends of the wearer, and the whole ornament was kapu — forbidden to anyone beneath the chiefly class.
