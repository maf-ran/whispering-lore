The Tunisian Fibula

jewelry · Tunisia · North Africa

The large silver pin or brooch, often triangular with a central coral or enamel stud and a long pointed pin, worn by Amazigh women across Tunisia, Algeria, and Morocco to fasten the shawl, and passed down as a bridal heirloom and protective talisman. Known as a tazabt in Tamazight, it is among the most characteristic ornaments of Maghrebi dress, its shape echoing the Roman-era brooches that secured the cloaks of the same region.
