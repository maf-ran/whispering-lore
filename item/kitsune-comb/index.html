Kitsune's Crescent Comb

jewelry · Japan · Japanese

The crescent-shaped comb of Japanese legend is the small hair ornament that a shapeshifting kitsune is said to drop when its disguise is betrayed, and finding it is supposed to bring a strange fortune or a lasting bond with the fox. The comb appears again and again in tales of fox wives, in which a man marries a beautiful woman who turns out to be a fox in human form; at the crucial moment the tail is seen, or the comb is left behind when the wife vanishes, and the discovery ends the marriage.
