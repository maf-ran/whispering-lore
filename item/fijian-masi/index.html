Fijian Masi (Barkcloth)

other · Fiji · Pacific

Barkcloth of Fiji, beaten by women from the inner bark of the paper mulberry into broad sheets, which are felted together on a wooden anvil and painted with geometric designs in black, brown and red. Masi is made in working parties for gifts, dress and ceremony: it wraps the body at weddings and funerals, lines the walls of the chiefly house, and the finest decorated pieces are given on occasions of state.
