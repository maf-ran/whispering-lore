The Golden Cradle

household object · Sweden · Swedish

A cradle of gold in a Swedish folk tale, in which a special child is laid and around which the tale's wonder unfolds. In 'The Golden Cradle', the precious cradle marks the child's destiny and the family's fortune, and its making or finding drives the story. The cradle, the most intimate of household objects, is here lifted out of the everyday into the realm of the marvellous, a sign that the child in it is no ordinary child.
