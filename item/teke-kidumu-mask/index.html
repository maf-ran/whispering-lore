Teke Kidumu Mask

ritual object · Congo · Central Africa

The kidumu is a round, flat face mask of the Teke people of the Republic of the Congo, cut from wood and painted with bold geometric patterns in red, white, and black. Worn by the men of the kidumu society at initiations, funerals, and the dances that mark the transfer of authority from one generation to the next, the mask is held before the face horizontally or vertically depending on the region.
