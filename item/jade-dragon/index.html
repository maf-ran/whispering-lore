Jade Dragon

other · China · East Asia

The jade dragon of the Han dynasty is a small, coiled dragon pendant carved from nephrite jade, found in the tombs of the Chinese aristocracy of the 2nd century BC to the 2nd century AD, where it was laid among the burial goods as a symbol of the celestial forces that governed the dead and of the soul's journey through the heavens.
