Sweetgrass Basket

container · United States · North America

The sweetgrass basket is the coiled basket of the Gullah people of the South Carolina Lowcountry, woven from sweetgrass with palmetto fronds and longleaf pine needles, a craft carried from West Africa to the American rice coast. The tight coil stitch descends directly from West African prototypes, and for centuries the baskets were practical tools, above all the broad fanners used to winnow rice on the coastal plantations.
