Pysanka (Ukrainian Decorated Egg)

ritual object · Ukraine · Slavic

Ukrainian Easter egg decorated by the wax-resist method: a stylus called pysachok draws lines of hot beeswax on the shell, which is then dyed in successive colours, ending with the wax melted away to reveal the pattern. Pysanky bear solar, plant and animal motifs linked to spring rebirth, and eggs were kept in the house as protective charms. The practice is ancient, and ornamented eggs from the region date back to pre-Christian times; the egg is sometimes said to carry the hope of the resurrection and the turning of the year.
