Dala Horse

other · Sweden · Swedish

The Dala horse (Dalahäst), the carved wooden horse of Dalarna, painted in red, white, blue, and green with a floral saddle. The craft grew from toys carved during long winter evenings in the 17th century into a famous local industry around Mora, where the horses are still hand-carved and painted. The Dala horse has become the best-known symbol of Swedish craft.
