Samovar (Russian Tea Urn)

household object · Russia · Slavic

Metal urn with a central fire-tube used to boil water for tea, a fixture of the Russian home from the early 18th century. The great workshops at Tula and in Moscow produced brass, nickel and silver samovars; around the samovar the family gathered for the evening tea, and it became a byword for hospitality in Russian daily life and literature. A charcoal fire burns in the inner tube, keeping the surrounding water at a rolling boil while the small teapot steeps on top, and the strong tea is poured from the spigot and diluted to taste in each cup.
