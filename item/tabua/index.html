Tabua (Whale Tooth)

ritual object · Fiji · Pacific

Polished sperm whale tooth of Fiji, its base pierced and threaded with a cord of braided sennit, presented with a formal speech as the highest mark of respect and goodwill. The tabua is offered to seal covenants between chiefs, to atone for wrongdoing, to open negotiations or requests that are then rarely refused, and it is kept by the recipient as the living record of the promise.
