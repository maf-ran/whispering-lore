Raven Banner

other · Norway · Norse

A battle standard woven in the shape of a raven, carried by Viking commanders to signal the presence of a great leader. The Orkneyinga saga tells of Earl Sigurd's banner Landeyðan, said to predict victory by flapping its wings, and the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle records a raven banner among Danish Vikings in 878. The raven was Odin's bird, making the banner a war-charm as much as a flag.
