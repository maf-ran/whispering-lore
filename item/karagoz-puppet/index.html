Karagoz

other · Turkey · Mediterranean

Karagöz, the shadow puppet of Ottoman Turkish theatre, gives its name to the traditional play performed behind a lit cotton screen with flat, articulated figures of dyed translucent camel hide worked on sticks, which cast coloured shadows as the single puppeteer, the hayali, moves them. The hero Karagöz, a bald, rough-spoken everyman, bickers endlessly with his polite, wordy friend Hacivat, and their exchanges of puns and misunderstandings drive the comedy, framed by music, song, and a stock cast drawn from the Ottoman city.
