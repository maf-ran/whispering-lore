Kultrún

musical instrument · Chile · South America

The kultrún is the sacred drum of the Mapuche of Chile and Argentina, a shallow wooden bowl covered with a hide head and beaten with a single stick or by hand, played almost exclusively by the machi, the ritual specialist and healer. Its head is painted with a quartered design, a cross and the four directions, which orients the ceremony and maps the structure of the Mapuche universe, and around it the ngillatun ceremony of prayer and thanks is built.
