Lukkustafir

rune stave · Iceland · Nordic

Lukkustafir, the luck staves of the Huld manuscript, a set of ten separate signs each drawn for good fortune in a different undertaking. The grimoire gives them as a family of practical charms for games, trade, travel, and daily life, so that the luck seeker could choose the sign that matched the task. They remain among the most reproduced of the Icelandic staves, second only to Vegvísir and Ægishjálmr in modern popularity.
