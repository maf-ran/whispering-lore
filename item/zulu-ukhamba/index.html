Zulu Ukhamba Beer Pot

cooking vessel · South Africa · Southern Africa

The ukhamba is the clay beer pot of the Zulu, a round, low-necked vessel fired from local clay and used to brew and serve utshwala, the sorghum beer at the centre of Zulu hospitality. Beer is not everyday drink but the medium of ceremony: it is brewed by women, offered to the ancestors at the start of every gathering, and shared at marriages, funerals, and the community work-parties that bind homesteads together.
