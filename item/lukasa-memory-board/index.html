Lukasa Memory Board

other · Democratic Republic of the Congo · Central Africa

The lukasa is a wooden memory board of the Luba peoples of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, its surface covered with beads, shells, and metal pins arranged in clusters that encode historical and ritual knowledge. A member of the Mbudye association, the court's body of historians, reads the board by touch, tracing the beads as a mnemonic that calls forth genealogies, royal protocol, migration legends, and the esoteric teachings of the kingdom.
