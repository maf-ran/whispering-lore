Xalam Lute

musical instrument · Senegal · West Africa

The xalam is a plucked five-string lute of the Wolof of Senegal, with a calabash or carved wooden bowl body, a skin head, and strings grouped into two melody and three rhythm strings. Played by griots (gewel), it produces the interlocking lines that back praise-songs, genealogies, and narrative epics, and its twanging rhythms support both festive dance music and the serious recitation of lineage history.
