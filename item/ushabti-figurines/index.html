Ushabti Figurines

ritual object · Egypt · Egyptian

Small mummiform figurines placed in Egyptian tombs to perform field labour for the dead in the afterlife, inscribed with spell 6 of the Book of the Dead, in which the figure answers 'here I am' when summoned to work in the fields of the god of the dead. A single burial could hold several hundred, from a modest clay set to the 413 ushabtis buried with Tutankhamun in faience, wood, and stone, each furnished with the tiny hoe and basket of the peasant.
