Singing Bowl

ritual object · Nepal · Asia

The singing bowl is a metal bowl of the Himalayan Buddhist world that produces a long, sustained tone when its rim is rubbed with a wooden striker or struck, and it is used in offerings, meditation and the opening and closing of ceremonies. The bowls are cast in bronze and brass bell-metal alloys, often in the workshops of the Kathmandu valley, and many carry embossed decoration or an incised invocation such as the six-syllable mantra Om Mani Padme Hum.
