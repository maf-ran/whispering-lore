Cook Islands Adze (Toki)

tool · Cook Islands · Polynesian

Stone-bladed adze of the Cook Islands, ground from fine-grained basalt and lashed to a wooden handle with sennit, used to fell and shape the timber for canoes, houses, war clubs and carvings. The toki was the essential tool of the master craftsman, and the quarries that supplied the prized basalt — above all the great quarry on Mangaia — were controlled by chiefly families and treated as resources of mana.
