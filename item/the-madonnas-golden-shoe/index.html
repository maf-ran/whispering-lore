The Madonna's Golden Shoe

jewelry · Sweden · Swedish

The golden shoe of the Virgin Mary's statue, at the centre of the Swedish tale 'The Fiddler Who Got the Madonna's Golden Shoe'. In the tale a fiddler who plays for the church is rewarded when the Madonna herself bestows her golden shoe on him, a gift from the living statue that proves his music found favour in heaven's eyes. The tale is one of Sweden's many stories of a church statue that comes alive, a motif with deep roots in medieval legend where painted Marys nod, weep, or reach out to the faithful.
