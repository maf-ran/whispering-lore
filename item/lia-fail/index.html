Lia Fáil

ritual object · Ireland · Celtic

Lia Fáil, the Stone of Destiny, stands on the hill of Tara and is one of the four treasures of the Tuatha Dé Danann in Irish tradition. In the Lebor Gabála Érenn it is counted among the treasures carried into Ireland, and later tales say the stone cried out beneath the rightful king when he stood or stepped on it, so that the whole assembly heard its voice. The best-known account is in Baile in Scáil, where the stone roars under the high king Conn of the Hundred Battles and reveals the line of kings who will follow him.
