Kraków Szopka

religious object · Poland · Slavic

The Kraków szopka is a Nativity crib built as a glittering model of the city itself, cramming Wawel Cathedral, St Mary's Basilica, castle towers, spires and arcades into a many-storeyed facade of cardboard, tin foil and painted paper around the central stable.
