The Green Knight's Axe

weapon · England · Celtic

The enormous green-steel axe carried by the Green Knight into King Arthur's hall at Camelot on New Year's Eve in the fourteenth-century alliterative poem Sir Gawain and the Green Knight. The weapon, described as an unwieldy blade of enormous size, is the instrument of the beheading game: Gawain strikes off the Knight's head with it, and the Knight calmly picks up the severed head, which reminds Gawain to meet him at the Green Chapel in a year and a day.
