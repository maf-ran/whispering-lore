Yaranga (Chukchi Portable Dwelling)

household object · Russia (Chukotka) · Arctic

Large hemispherical tent dwelling of the coastal Chukchi, built on a wooden frame of poles and whale ribs and covered with walrus skins, with reindeer hides laid inside against the cold. A family camp could stretch up to twenty metres; inside, fur-lined sleeping chambers formed separate rooms. The yaranga was central to Chukchi coastal life.
