Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi

weapon · Japan · Japanese

The legendary grass-cutting sword drawn from the tail of the eight-headed serpent Yamata no Orochi by the storm god Susanoo, who then offered it to his sister Amaterasu; it is the sword of the Three Sacred Treasures of the Japanese imperial regalia, with the mirror and the jewel. Its name, 'grass-cutter', recalls the later story of the hero Yamato Takeru, who escaped a burning field by using the blade to mow the grass and turn the fire back on his enemies.
