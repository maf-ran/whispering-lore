Xhosa Beadwork

jewelry · South Africa · Southern Africa

Xhosa beadwork of South Africa is made with imported glass beads sewn onto leather, cotton, or cloth supports to create necklaces, aprons, headdresses, and panels of dress worn at the great passages of life. At initiation, the beads speak: the colours and patterns worn by a young man in the circumcision school, and by the women who celebrate his return, carry coded messages of love, status, and clan that are meant to be read by the initiated.
