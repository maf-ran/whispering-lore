The Rök Runestone

other · Sweden · Östergötland

The Rök stone (Rökstenen), the famous ninth-century runestone of Östergötland, raised beside Lake Vättern in memory of a dead son. Its inscription of more than 700 runes is the longest known runic text in the world, a riddling composition in verse that mentions Theodoric the Great, named heroes, and the runes themselves, and has been read as both a memorial and a locked challenge to be unravelled.
