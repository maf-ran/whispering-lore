The Sword of Goliath

weapon · Israel · Middle East

The sword of the Philistine champion Goliath, taken as spoil after the shepherd David slew him and later kept wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod at the sanctuary of Nob, where the fugitive David received it, saying there was none like it. The weapon is described in the First Book of Samuel, and David's possession of it anchors his kingship narrative in the biblical account, the champion's own blade becoming the king's trusted arm.
