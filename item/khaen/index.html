Khaen

musical instrument · Laos · Southeast Asia

The khaen is the free-reed mouth organ of Laos, built from rows of bamboo tubes bound around a wooden wind-chest, whose drone-and-melody sound is the soul of the Lao folk song called lam. The player breathes in and out through a single mouth-hole while covering the finger-holes of the tubes, producing the interlocking melody and drone that lam singers improvise against, and the instrument accompanies courtship, festivals, and ritual across Laos and northeastern Thailand.
