Witch Broom

tool · Sweden · Swedish

The broom (kvast) that, in Swedish folk belief, carried witches to Blåkulla, the witches' sabbath mountain, especially on Maundy Thursday before Easter. The 'påskkärring', the Easter witch, was thought to ride a broom, or a cow, goat, or pitchfork, to Blåkulla. People blocked chimneys and hid brooms behind stoves to stop the flights, and the belief shaped Sweden's Easter-witch custom.
