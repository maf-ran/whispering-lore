Valaška

tool · Slovakia · Slavic

The valaška is the small axe of the Carpathian shepherds, a light iron head set on a long wooden haft that reaches the waist, serving at once as walking staff, digging tool and weapon. Slovak bača shepherds and hikers of the High Tatras carry the valaška through the mountains, and its handle is often ornamented with engraved patterns or metal mountings that mark the maker and the owner.
