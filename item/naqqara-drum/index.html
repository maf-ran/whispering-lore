Naqqara Drum

musical instrument · Pakistan · South Asia

The naqqara is a pair of kettledrums, usually of copper with goatskin heads, beaten with two sticks and carried before rulers or sounded from gate towers to announce arrivals, processions and festival days. The instrument came into South Asia with the Indo-Islamic courts, where paired kettledrums were part of royal ceremonial music, and it remains in use at shrines and at processions across Pakistan and the wider Muslim world.
