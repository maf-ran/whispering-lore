Palau Storyboard

other · Palau · Pacific

Carved wooden panel of Palau recording the myths, histories and village legends of the islands in low-relief figures. The form descends from the painted facade boards of the bai, the great men's meeting house, whose gables carried scenes of the ancestors and of village life, and from the 1930s carvers began to translate those painted narratives into portable low-relief wood.
