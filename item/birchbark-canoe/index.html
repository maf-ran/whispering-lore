Birchbark Canoe

ship · Canada · North America

The birchbark canoe, wiigwaasi-jiimaan in Ojibwe, is the light, silent and remarkably strong craft built of sheets of birchbark sewn over a cedar frame with spruce root and sealed with spruce gum, the great carrier of the Anishinaabe and other northern peoples. Light enough for one person to carry across a portage, a full canoe could still move families, furs and goods across hundreds of miles of lakes and rivers, which is why French traders and the voyageurs adopted it wholesale.
