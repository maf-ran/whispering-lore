False Face Mask

religious object · Canada · North America

The False Face masks of the Haudenosaunee are distorted human faces carved from living basswood, painted red and black, and used by the False Face Society in curing ceremonies that drive sickness from the sick. The twisted features recall the forest beings of tradition, above all the Great Face who is said to have broken his own face in a contest with the sun and gained power over disease, appearing in the story of the Flying Head.
