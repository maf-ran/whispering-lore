Trobriand Yam House (Bwaima)

other · Papua New Guinea · Pacific

Ornate storehouse of the Trobriand Islands of Papua New Guinea, raised on posts with a steeply pitched roof and a decorative triangular gable, first described in the anthropological studies of Bronisław Malinowski. The facade is built of wooden slabs tied with coconut-husk fasteners, and yams — the central wealth and prestige crop — are displayed in tight rows through the slits so that the house announces its owner's abundance.
