The Church Keys

other · Sweden · Swedish

The church key used in the Swedish divination called nyckelprövning, the key test. A key was bound between the pages of a Bible, which was then hung or turned while names were read, and it was believed the key would point out or shake at the name of a thief. The key-and-Bible test is documented in Swedish folk tradition for finding stolen goods, and it was trusted because the church key and the holy book brought God's judgement into the search. The test belonged to the wider family of divination by the Bible, psalm, or sieve that country people used when the law could not find the guilty. Beca
