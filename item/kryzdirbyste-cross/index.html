Kryždirbystė Cross

religious object · Lithuania · Baltic

Carved oak wayside crosses of the Lithuanian cross-crafting tradition, one to five metres tall, with small roofs, carved figures and flower and geometric ornament, standing at village entrances, crossroads, cemeteries and shrines. Once consecrated by a priest, a cross is sacred and draws offerings — food, rosaries, scarves and aprons — seeking fertility, healing or protection, and the craft's masters, the kryždirbiai, shape stogastulpiai, koplytstulpiai and full crosses in styles that vary from region to region.
