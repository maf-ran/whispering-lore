The Golden Keys

other · Sweden · Swedish

Golden keys that appear in a Swedish folk tale, unlocking a reward or a forbidden treasure for the hero who obtains them. The keys belong to the tale's wonder-logic: ordinary locks fall open before them, and possession of the keys brings the hero to fortune. The motif is told as part of the tale 'The Golden Keys'.
