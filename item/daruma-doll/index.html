Daruma Doll

other · Japan · Japanese

The daruma is a rounded paper-mache doll modelled on Bodhidharma, the semi-legendary founder of Zen Buddhism, whose hollow weighted body always rocks back upright when tipped. The likeness plays on a famous story in which Bodhidharma sat facing a wall in meditation for nine years, until his legs withered and were lost; the doll keeps his determined, round form and his fierce brow.
