Ægishjálmr

rune stave · Iceland · Nordic

Ægishjálmr, the 'helm of awe' or 'helm of terror', is a protective sign of Norse and Icelandic tradition, drawn as a wheel of radiating prongs or staves. In the poem Fáfnismál the dragon Fáfnir is said to have worn the helm of awe, and the hero Sigurðr takes it as part of the dragon's hoard, suggesting a literal helmet in the oldest layer of the story. From the early modern period the name came to be attached to an eight-armed magical stave of the Icelandic galdrabækur, drawn on the forehead between the brows or on the brow of a person to strike fear into enemies and give victory and protectio
