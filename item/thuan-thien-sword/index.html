Thuan Thien Sword

weapon · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Thuan Thien, the Sword of Heaven's Will, is the magic blade given to the farmer-king Le Loi during his revolt against the Ming occupation of Dai Viet in the fifteenth century, when the golden turtle god Kim Qui surfaced from the waters to present it. With the sword Le Loi's army defeated the Ming and he founded the Later Le dynasty, ruling as the national hero of Vietnamese independence. After the victory, when the king was boating on the lake in Hanoi, the great turtle rose again and seized the sword, returning it to its divine owner, and the lake was renamed Hoan Kiem, the Lake of the Return
