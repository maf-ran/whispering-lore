Nkul Slit Gong

musical instrument · Cameroon · Central Africa

The nkul is a wooden slit gong of the Beti and related peoples of southern Cameroon, made from a hollowed log with a long longitudinal slit that is beaten with sticks to produce two distinct tones. In forest villages where sound carries poorly, the nkul served as a talking instrument: different rhythms announced deaths, births, hunting results, danger, and the summons to ritual, and a skilled beater could be understood over several kilometres of valley.
