Claddagh Ring

jewelry · Ireland · Celtic

The Claddagh ring, a gold or silver finger-ring shaped as two hands clasping a crowned heart, has been made in Galway since the seventeenth century and is named after the old fishing village of Claddagh beside the city. The design descends from the medieval 'fede' ring, whose clasped hands stood for faith and betrothal, with the heart and crown added locally.
