Gris-Gris Bag

ritual object · United States · North America

The gris-gris bag is a small flannel pouch, often red, filled with herbs, roots, coins, stones or personal items and made by Louisiana root doctors and hoodoo practitioners to carry a 'work' for love, luck, money, protection or success. The name gris-gris is used in New Orleans, while the same charm is known as a mojo bag or hand elsewhere, and the tradition blends West African spiritual practice with colonial folk belief.
