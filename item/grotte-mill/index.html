Grotte Mill

tool · Sweden · Nordic

The great hand-mill owned by King Fróði, which ground out gold, peace, and abundance. In the Eddic poem Grottasöngr the giantesses Fenja and Menja were forced to turn it until they raised an army; carried aboard a ship, the mill then ground salt until it sank, and so the sea became salt. The same wonder-mill lies at the heart of the Swedish folktale 'Why the Sea Is Salt'.
