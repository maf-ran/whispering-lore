Selkie's Seal-Skin

garment · Scotland · Celtic

The seal-skin of the selkie is the central object of the seal-people tales of Scotland, Ireland and the northern isles, a pelt that lets its wearer take human form on land. A selkie who wishes to return to the sea must put the skin back on, so a human who steals and hides the skin can hold a selkie bride or husband captive, and the marriage lasts only until the skin is found and returned.
