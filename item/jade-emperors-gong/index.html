The Jade Emperor's Gong

musical instrument · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

The Jade Emperor's gong is the bronze instrument of a Vietnamese folk tale in which a frog leads a deputation of animals to heaven to complain that rain never falls. Because the frog croaks so loudly, the Jade Emperor hears it above the clouds and summons the delegation; the frog is granted the power to control rain, and to this day the croaking of frogs is said to warn of coming rain.
