Solomon Islands Shell Money

other · Solomon Islands · Pacific

Traditional currency of the Solomon Islands, made from small discs ground from shell — the red money of Malaita being prized above all — drilled and strung on fibre into long strings and rolled coils. Shell money is still exchanged in bride-price, compensation, feasts and chiefly obligations, its value fixed by the species of shell, the fineness of the grinding and the length of the string.
