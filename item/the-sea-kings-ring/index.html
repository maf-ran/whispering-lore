The Sea King's Ring

jewelry · Sweden · Swedish

A golden ring given by the sea king in the Swedish ballad 'Agneta and the Sea King', a version of the old ballad about the maiden taken by the sea folk. The ring is the gift that binds the maiden to her underwater master, the token of a marriage that keeps her below the waves while her family mourns her above; in the ballad's ending the ring and its owner part in sorrow when the world above finally reclaims her.
