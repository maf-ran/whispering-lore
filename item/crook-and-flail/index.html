The Crook and Flail

ritual object · Egypt · Egyptian

The crossed crook (heka) and flail (nekhakha) carried by Osiris as emblems of royal authority and of the shepherd's care for his flock, laid crossed over royal mummies in their coffins. Temple reliefs show the living pharaoh handing the pair to the god of the dead and receiving it back, and gilt and blue-faience examples were buried with kings such as Tutankhamun, whose tomb yielded several full sets.
