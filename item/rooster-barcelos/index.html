Rooster of Barcelos

other · Portugal · Mediterranean

The Rooster of Barcelos comes from the Portuguese legend of a Galician pilgrim who, condemned to hang for a theft he did not commit, stood before the judge's table and swore his innocence by a roasted rooster, declaring that the bird would crow if he were innocent. As the noose was being fitted, the roast cock rose on the table and crowed; the pilgrim was freed, and he returned to Barcelos to raise a monument in thanks.
