Baba Yaga's Mortar and Pestle

tool · Russia · Slavic

Iron mortar and pestle ridden by Baba Yaga, the witch of Russian fairy tales, who flies over the forest in her mortar, sweeping away her trail with a broom and driving the mortar onward with the pestle. In Afanasyev's tales she is called 'Baba Yaga the Bone Leg' and travels in this way; the mortar, pestle and broom are among the most iconic props of Russian folk narrative. Folklorists note that the mortar echoes the work of grain-pounding and the boundary between the farmstead and the wild, fitting the witch who lives at the edge of the forest.
