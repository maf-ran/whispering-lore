Ginfaxi

rune stave · Iceland · Nordic

Ginfaxi is an Icelandic magical stave recorded in the Huld manuscript, compiled by Geir Vigfússon at Akureyri in 1860, and drawn to ensure victory over a rival in a contest or argument. In the tradition it is used together with the stave Gapaldur: Gapaldur is kept under the heel of the right foot and Ginfaxi under the toes of the left foot, so that the wearer wins bouts of Icelandic wrestling, glíma, and other competitions.
