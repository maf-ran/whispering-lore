Corona Civica

jewelry · Italy · Mediterranean

The corona civica, the civic crown of oak leaves, was Rome's highest honour for a citizen soldier, awarded to the man who saved a fellow citizen's life in battle and held the ground where he did it; Pliny the Elder in the Natural History and Aulus Gellius in the Attic Nights describe its rules.
