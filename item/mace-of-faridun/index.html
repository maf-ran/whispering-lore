The Mace of Faridun

weapon · Iran · Persian

The bull-headed mace (gurz) forged to the measure of the young prince Faridun, which Ferdowsi describes being made before his campaign against the tyrant Zahhak, so that only his hand could wield it. With it Faridun struck Zahhak's crowned head and split the serpents that had grown on the tyrant's shoulders, ending a thousand years of unjust rule and binding the defeated Zahhak in a cavern of Mount Damavand, where he is said to be chained to this day.
