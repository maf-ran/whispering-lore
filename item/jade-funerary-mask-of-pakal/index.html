Jade Funerary Mask of Pakal

religious object · Mexico · Mesoamerica

The funerary mask of K'inich Janaab' Pakal, the great seventh-century ruler of Palenque, is a mosaic of more than 340 pieces of green jadeite fitted over his face in the stone sarcophagus of the Temple of the Inscriptions. Discovered in 1952 by the Mexican archaeologist Alberto Ruz Lhuillier, the mask has eyes of shell and mother-of-pearl with pupils of black obsidian and stands about 25 centimetres tall.
