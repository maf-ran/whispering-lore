Prayer Wheel

ritual object · Tibet · East Asia and Pacific

The prayer-wheel is the cylindrical device of Tibetan Buddhism that is spun to release the mantras printed on the paper scrolls inside, each revolution sending the prayer for compassion into the world. Rows of large wheels line the walls of temples and stupas, and smaller hand-held wheels are turned by pilgrims at shrines and along circumambulation paths, the turning of the wheel standing for the turning of the dharma.
