The Bottle of the Ifrit

other · Iraq · Middle East

The sealed copper bottle cast up in the fisherman's net in the Thousand and One Nights tale of the Fisherman and the Jinni, from which a vast ifrit escaped in a pillar of smoke and, furious at his long imprisonment, threatened to kill the man who had freed him. The fisherman saved himself by doubting that so great a being could fit into so small a vessel, tricking the spirit back through its narrow mouth and sealing the bottle once more.
