Makonde Shetani Figure

ritual object · Tanzania · East Africa

The shetani carvings of the Makonde are dark hardwood figures of contorted, expressive spirits, carved from the ebony-like mpingo of the Tanzanian and Mozambican coast. Beginning in the 1950s, carvers turned from ancestor figures to the shetani, capricious bush and ancestral spirits, twisting human and animal forms into elongated, agonised, or playful shapes that seem to catch a spirit mid-transformation.
