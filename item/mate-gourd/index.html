Mate Gourd

container · Argentina · South America

The mate gourd is the dried calabash cup from which yerba mate, the leafy infusion of the Guaraní, is drunk through a metal straw called a bombilla, passed hand to hand around a circle of friends. The Guaraní of Paraguay, Brazil and Argentina prepared a drink of the herb before the Spanish arrived, and colonial accounts already describe it being shared; the Spanish word mate itself comes from the Quechua mati, for the gourd that holds it.
