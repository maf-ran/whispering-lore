Pachamanca

other · Peru · South America

The pachamanca is an Andean feast cooked in the earth: stones are heated in a dug pit, and layers of meat, potatoes, maize, beans and fragrant herbs are set over them, covered with leaves and earth, and steamed until done. The name comes from Quechua and means 'earth pot,' and the method is shared across the Peruvian and Bolivian highlands, where it is served at weddings, patronal fiestas, harvests and the raising of a new house.
