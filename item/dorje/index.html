Dorje

religious object · Bhutan · Asia

The dorje is the ritual thunderbolt of Bhutanese and Tibetan Buddhism, a bronze or gilt sceptre with a central grip and prongs that curve inward at each end, held in monastic hand rituals and placed on altars. The word is the Tibetan translation of the Sanskrit vajra, and it means lord of stones, hard as diamond and indestructible as the thunderbolt; the five prongs of the common form stand for the five Dhyani Buddhas and their wisdoms.
