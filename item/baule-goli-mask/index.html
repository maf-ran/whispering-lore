Baule Goli Mask

ritual object · Ivory Coast · West Africa

The goli is a round-faced, painted mask of the Baule people of the Ivory Coast, worn with a full costume during village celebrations, funerals, and the dances of the goli society. The word names a whole sequence of masks that appear in a fixed order, from the round kpan faces through animal forms to the large, frightening figured masks, a progression that retraces the Baule cosmos and the order of communal life.
