Silver Buckles

jewelry · Sweden · Swedish

Silver buckles and clasps worn with Swedish regional folk dress, on belts, shoes, and collars. Such silver, often inherited and re-used across generations, was engraved with initials and dates and served as both ornament and stored wealth. Wealthy folk wore heavy rectangular 'spännen'; poorer folk wore simpler clasps. The buckles are a documented part of the folk-costume tradition.
