The Broom King

tool · Sweden · Swedish

The broom king, the broom that rules all brooms, from the Swedish tale 'The Broom's Broom King of All Brooms'. In the tale a broom proves to be the king of its kind, sweeping away the story's dangers with a command that no other broom could match. The tale plays on the humble household object raised to kingship, a comic inversion in the spirit of the Scandinavian wonder-tales where the lowliest tool of the house shows the greatest power.
