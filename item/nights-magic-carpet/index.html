The Magic Carpet of the Nights

other · Iraq · Middle East

The flying carpet of the Thousand and One Nights cycle and of later oriental folklore, a woven rug that carried its riders through the air to far-off countries. Within the Nights corpus itself the carpet appears chiefly in the later tales, where Prince Husayn buys a magic carpet that bears its master wherever he wishes to go, and much of its modern fame comes from European retellings rather than from the medieval Arabic text.
