Buku Spirits

forest-spirit · Senegal · West Africa

Buku spirits are powerful and volatile forest-dwelling supernatural beings in the mythology of the Jola (Diola) people of Senegal, particularly in the Casamance region. These spirits inhabit the sacred forests, groves, and wilderness areas that surround Jola villages, acting as both guardians and potential threats. The Buku are deeply feared and revered for their unpredictable nature and for their intimate connection to the mysteries of the bush — the untamed wilderness that exists beyond human settlements.
