Mami Wata

water-spirit · Benin · West Africa

Mami Wata is a water spirit widely venerated in Benin, appearing as a beautiful, seductive woman, often half fish or serpent, who grants wealth, children, and fortune in exchange for offerings and ritual devotion. The cult thrives in coastal towns and blends Fon, Yoruba, and imported imagery, with temples serving as centers of healing, spirit possession, and trade protection.
