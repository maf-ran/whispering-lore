Wolf Deity (Mapuche)

deity · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in South African tradition. The Mapuche are an Indigenous people of Chile and Argentina. The entry misattributes a Mapuche wolf deity to South Africa. Wolves (Canis lupus) are not native to Africa; snip African wild dogs (Lycaon) exist but no Mapuche/South African wolf deity. Fabricated cross-continental misattribution.
