Cougar Guardian (Guarani)

guardian · Mexico · Mesoamerica

The Guarani and Tupi peoples of Paraguay, Brazil, and Argentina revere forest spirits who protect the wilderness and punish those who overexploit it, including the Curupira and jaguar-related guardians of the forest. The puma, called yaguarundi in the region, fits this protective role. The entry mislabels the culture as Mexican; the Guarani homeland lies in South America.
