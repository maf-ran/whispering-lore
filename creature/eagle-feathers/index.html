Eagle feathers

spirit · United States · North America

In many Native American traditions, particularly among Plains and Woodland tribes, eagle feathers are considered the most sacred of ceremonial objects, embodying the spirit and power of the eagle itself. Eagle feathers are not merely decorative items but are living objects that carry prayers to the Creator, witness truth, and confer honor upon those who earn them. The feathers are used in a wide variety of ceremonies including healing rituals, dances, peace ceremonies, and vision quests.
