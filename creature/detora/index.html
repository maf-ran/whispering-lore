Detora

spirit · Nauru · Pacific

Detora is the guardian hero of Nauruan tradition, a brave, protective spirit celebrated for superhuman strength and mastery of the outer reefs and coastal caves. His feats, passed down in Nauruan oral narrative, cast him as the defender of the homeland against the sea. He embodies Nauruan ideals of courage, resilience, and communal protection.
