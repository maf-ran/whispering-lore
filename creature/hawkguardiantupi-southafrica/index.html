Hawk Guardian (Tupi)

guardian · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested. The entry pairs the Tupi peoples of Amazonia with South Africa and imagines a hawk guardian carried across the Atlantic by 'Tupi descendants' during colonial history — a narrative with no documentary basis. No Tupi tradition records such a guardian, and no South African tradition names it; the culture/country mismatch is fabricated.
