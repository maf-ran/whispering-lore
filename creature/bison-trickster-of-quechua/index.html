Bison Trickster of Quechua

trickster · Peru · South America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Bison are not native to the Andes, and the puna-dwelling, lightning-linked trickster said to herald pachakuti is invented. Pachakuti (world-turning upheaval) and mountain apus are genuine Quechua concepts, but no bison being is recorded in Quechua tradition.
