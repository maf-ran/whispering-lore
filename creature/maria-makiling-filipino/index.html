Maria Makiling (Mariang Makiling)

spirit · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Maria Makiling is the most famous diwata (nature spirit/goddess) in Philippine mythology, the guardian spirit of Mount Makiling in Laguna. She is a breathtakingly beautiful young woman with long black hair and radiant white clothing who protects the mountain's forests, wildlife, and the people who depend on them. Pre-colonially venerated as the goddess Dayang Masalanta, she was sent by the supreme deity Bathala to aid mankind. She rewards those who respect nature and punishes those who harm it.
