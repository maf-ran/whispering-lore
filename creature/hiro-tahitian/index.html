Hiro

deity · French Polynesia · Pacific

The Tahitian trickster god of thieves, navigation, and the son of the supreme god Ta'aroa. Hiro was known for his cunning, cleverness, and magical abilities. He built a magical canoe that could sail faster than the wind. Using his enchanted vessel and his wits, Hiro traveled across the Society Islands performing daring thefts and outsmarting chiefs and gods alike. He stole fish from markets, food from temples, and once attempted to steal the island of Bora Bora itself. His exploits angered the other gods who unleashed a great storm to stop him.
