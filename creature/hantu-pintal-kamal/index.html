Hantu Pintal Kamal

ghost · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Hantu Pintal Kamal is a trickster ghost from Malaysian folklore, whose name roughly translates to 'Twisted Spirit of the Hearth' or 'All-Confusing Ghost.' This entity is known for its uncanny ability to walk on walls and ceilings, defying gravity and logic. Unlike more dangerous Malaysian hantu (spirits) that actively harm humans, Hantu Pintal Kamal is a mischievous household spirit that delights in creating confusion and disorder.
