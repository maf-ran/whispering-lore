Badger Ghost of Uzbek

ghost · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Uzbek tradition records a badger ghost. Uzbek folk belief includes jinn, devs, and paris, along with Turkic nature spirits, but the badger plays no documented spirit role; the wolf and eagle are the culturally prominent Turkic animals.
