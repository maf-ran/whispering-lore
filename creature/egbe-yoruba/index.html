Egbe

spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Egbe is the collective spirit of unborn children and the celestial society of souls in Yoruba cosmology. Before birth, every person belongs to a spiritual "club" or society called Egbe in the spirit world (Orun). Members of Egbe make a covenant to support each other in the earthly realm. Those who are born into the world carry their Egbe connection as a spiritual bond. Egbe is also known as the "Heavenly Society" or "Spiritual Playmates."
