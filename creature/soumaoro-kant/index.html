Soumaoro Kanté

spirit · Mali · Africa

Soumaoro Kanté (Unknown). is a spirit. Soumaoro Kante is a powerful sorcerer-king from the Epic of Sundiata, the Sosso ruler whose defeat by Sundiata Keita led to the founding of the Mali Empire.. Source: Mande oral tradition — Soumaoro Kanté is the sorcerer-king of Sosso, the antagonist in the Sundiata epic, documented in Mande griot tradition.
