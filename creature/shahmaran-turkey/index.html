Shahmaran

serpent · Turkey · Middle East and North Africa

Shahmaran (from Persian Shah-i-Maran, 'King of Snakes') is a mythical half-woman, half-snake creature revered in Azerbaijani, Turkish, and Kurdish folklore. She is the immortal queen of all serpents, possessing profound wisdom and magical healing powers. According to widespread legend, Shahmaran dwells in an underground realm beneath the ancient city of Tarsus, where intelligent and compassionate serpents live in peace.
