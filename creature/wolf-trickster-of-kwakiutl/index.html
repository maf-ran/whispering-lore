Wolf Trickster of Kwakiutl

trickster · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf trickster among the Kwakwaka'wakw. The trickster-creator of their mythology is Raven, who brought daylight, made the islands, and formed the first humans from wood and clay. The wolf is a respected crest and founding ancestor of chiefly lines, not a trickster figure.
