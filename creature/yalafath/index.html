Yalafath

deity · Micronesia · Pacific

Yalafath (Yälfaath the Elder, Yelafaz) is the supreme creator god of Yap in the Caroline Islands, regarded as the originator of the human race. He shaped the first man and woman, Ganan and Nik, from his hands and dirt, and on the following day made another couple before deciding it was faster to give humans sexual organs so they could reproduce. Though beneficent, he is indolent, dwelling in Falraman where the souls of the dead go, and he overcomes evil spirits when invoked.
