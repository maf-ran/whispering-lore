Imandwa Spirits

spirit · Burundi · East Africa

The Imandwa are the collective spirits and initiated members of the Kubandwa spirit cult in Burundi and Rwanda, forming a hierarchical pantheon of approximately forty distinct spirits. This spirit pantheon includes Kiranga (Ryangombe) as the supreme leader, his son and successor Binego, other sons like Ruhanga, legendary heroes like the Rwandan prince Kibogo, and various animal and nature spirits.
