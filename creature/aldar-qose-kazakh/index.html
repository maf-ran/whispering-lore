Aldar Qose

trickster · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Aldar Qose is the beloved trickster of Kazakh folklore, a poor wanderer in threadbare clothing whose unassuming look hides a sharp mind. Never resorting to violence, he relies on elaborate verbal tricks to outsmart the wealthy and powerful, exposing greed and hypocrisy. His tales are social satire championing wit over wealth and power across the Kazakh steppe.
