Bison Ancestor-Spirit (Guarani)

ancestor-spirit · Paraguay · South America

No bison spirit belongs to Guarani tradition: bison never inhabited South America. Guarani cosmology includes the creator Tupa, Arasy, Karai, Jakaira, and the serpent Mboi, but no bison ancestor-spirit. The entry invents a 'sacred bison' narrative that conflates plains-bison symbolism of North America with Guarani beliefs.
