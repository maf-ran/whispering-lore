Hawk Nature-Spirit (Tupi)

nature-spirit · Mali · West Africa

Hawk Nature-Spirit (Tupi) is a nature-spirit from Mali mythology. This region, with its harsh climate and sparse vegetation, demanded that the Tupi nature-spirit develop new strategies for protecting the people and the environment. For Tupi descendants who found themselves in the Sahel, this spirit became a symbol of resilience and adaptation to challenging conditions.
