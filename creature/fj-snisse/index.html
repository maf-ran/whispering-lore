Fjøsnisse

household-spirit · Norway · Nordic

Fjøsnisse (barn nisse) is a specialized type of Norwegian nisse that lives specifically in the cowshed (fjøs) and cares for the cattle. While the general nisse protects the entire farm, the fjøsnisse is devoted exclusively to the welfare of the cows, goats, and other barn animals. In Norwegian folk tradition, a well-kept barn with healthy, productive cows was a sign that the fjøsnisse was content. The fjøsnisse was known to braid the tails and manes of cattle, keep the barn clean, and help during calving.
