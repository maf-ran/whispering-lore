Umm Al Duwais

spirit · Bahrain · Middle East and North Africa

Umm Al Duwais ('Mother of the Toes') is a female jinn of Gulf Arab folklore, celebrated in Emirati and Bahraini tradition. She appears as a beautiful, perfumed woman who lures men near deserted places, then turns monstrous. Her legend is recorded in Gulf oral tradition and has inspired modern films and retellings. This entry duplicates the United Arab Emirates variant of the same figure.
