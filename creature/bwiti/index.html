Bwiti

spirit · Gabon · Central Africa

Bwiti is a complex spiritual discipline from Gabon and surrounding regions of Central Africa, centered on the ritual use of iboga (Tabernanthe iboga), a psychoactive plant root that induces profound visionary states. As one of Gabon's three official religions alongside Christianity and Islam, Bwiti represents a sophisticated system of spiritual knowledge, healing practices, and cosmological understanding that has survived centuries of colonial disruption.
