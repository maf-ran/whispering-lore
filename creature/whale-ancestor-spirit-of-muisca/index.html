Whale Ancestor-Spirit of Muisca

ancestor-spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Muisca tradition. Muisca are inland highland Andean people (Altiplano Cundiboyacense, 2600m elevation); whales do not exist in their territory or mythology. Muisca beings: Bachué, Bochica, Chía, Sué, Chibchacum, Goranchacha, Monster of Lake Tota (serpent/fish). Whale ancestor-spirit is a fabricated conflation.
