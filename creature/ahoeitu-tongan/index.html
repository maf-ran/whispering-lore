Ahoeitu

hero · Tonga · Pacific

Ahoeitu is the legendary first Tu'i Tonga (sacred king of Tonga), founder of the dynasty that ruled Tonga for over a thousand years. Born to a Tongan chiefess and the god Eitumatupua, who descended from heaven on a great tree, Ahoeitu was of both divine and mortal parentage. His jealous half-brothers murdered him and cut his body into pieces. When Eitumatupua discovered the crime, he restored Ahoeitu to life and punished the treacherous brothers.
