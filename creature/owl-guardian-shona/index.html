Owl Guardian (Shona)

guardian · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested as a named guardian in Shona tradition. Owls in Shona and wider Bantu culture are widely regarded as omens of death or witchcraft familiars (e.g., goba), not protective guardians of valleys. The entry uses a templated "guardian of valley" description contradicting actual cultural associations.
