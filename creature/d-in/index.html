Džin

spirit · Bosnia and Herzegovina · Slavic

Džin is the Bosnian variant of the jinn, supernatural beings in Islamic and pre-Islamic folklore that were created from smokeless fire and inhabit a parallel world alongside humans. In Bosnian tradition, heavily influenced by Ottoman-era Islamic culture, džins are powerful spirits that live in ruins, caves, abandoned wells, and other wild, desolate places. Unlike Western ghosts, džins are not spirits of the dead but distinct created beings with their own societies, hierarchies, and free will.
