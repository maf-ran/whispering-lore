Badger Giant of Blackfoot

giant · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Blackfoot source records a badger giant. Blackfoot tradition does include stories of earlier races and giant beings, but the badger has no documented role in them; attested Blackfoot spirit focus falls on Napi, the buffalo, and vision quests.
