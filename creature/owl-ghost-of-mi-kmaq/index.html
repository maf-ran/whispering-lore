Owl Ghost of Mi'kmaq

ghost · Canada · North America

No owl ghost is attested in Mi'kmaq folklore. The documented owl traditions of the Mi'kmaq are the celestial Owl, one of the star-hunters chasing the Great Bear, and the name kokokwes for the owl. Owls are not portrayed as ghosts or water spirits in Mi'kmaq stories; the entry is a template-generated invention.
