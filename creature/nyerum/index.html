Nyerum

water-spirit · Mali · West Africa

Nyerum are Bambara (Mali) water spirits inhabiting seasonal floodplains and ponds. They breathe water, immune to drought, and lure/drown intruders. Part of Bambara animistic tradition alongside Faro (river spirit) and pond genies. Documented in Bambara oral tradition and ethnographic surveys of Niger River communities.
