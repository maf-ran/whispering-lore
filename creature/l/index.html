Lá

forest-spirit · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Vietnamese folklore; 'Lá' is the Vietnamese word for leaf. The recorded mischievous plant spirit of forests and paddies matches the animist veneration of trees and plants found in Vietnamese village religion and upland traditions, but no documented entity named 'Lá' exists.
