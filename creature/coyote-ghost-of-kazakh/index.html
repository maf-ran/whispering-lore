Coyote Ghost of Kazakh

ghost · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Coyotes are not native to Kazakhstan, and no Kazakh failed-shaman 'coyote ghost' or 'Iyt-Köz' appears in any recorded tradition. Kazakh folklore reveres the wolf as legendary ancestor; the dog-eyed wanderer here is an invented figure.
