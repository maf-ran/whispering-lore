Fox Spirit (Zulu)

spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In the rich oral traditions of the Zulu people, this fox spirit is an embodiment of the duality of nature—simultaneously a playful trickster and a solemn guardian of the ancestral lands. It is believed to inhabit the intersection of the physical world and the realm of the Amadlozi (ancestors), acting as a mediator that carries the whispers of the living to the ears of the departed.
