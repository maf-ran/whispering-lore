Toad Guardian (Udmurt)

guardian · Russia · Slavic

Not independently attested: no toad guardian spirit appears in documented Udmurt tradition, which centers on household patrons such as korkamurt and vorshud. Toads carry folk associations with damp places and the underworld, but no specific Udmurt guardian figure is recorded.
