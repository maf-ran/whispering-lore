Degei (Ndengei)

serpent · Fiji · Pacific

Degei (also spelled Ndengei) is the great serpent god of Fiji, specifically venerated as the ancestor spirit who led the first humans from the Nakauvadra Mountains down to the coastal regions. This manifestation of Degei focuses on the god's role as an ancestral figure rather than the supreme creator. Fijian mythology is unique in the Pacific islands for featuring snake deities despite the complete absence of native snakes in Fiji's ecosystems, making Degei a remarkable cultural anomaly. The most detailed accounts of this tradition were documented by British colonial administrator Basil H. Tho
