Sgaana Supernatural Killer Whale Haida

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Sgaana, in Haida belief, are the supernatural beings of the sea, and the killer whale is their visible form in this world. Chiefs among the undersea people, the killer whales live in great houses beneath the ocean, rule the sea creatures, and can take human shape. The word and image figure in Haida crests and in Bill Reid's sculpture Skaana.
