Wasgo Sea Wolf Haida

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Wasgo, the sea wolf, is a powerful being of Haida tradition that moves between ocean and land, part wolf and part killer whale. Capable of seizing a whale and of storming the shore, it appears in crest carvings and pole legends as a creature of great supernatural strength linking the sea and the forest.
