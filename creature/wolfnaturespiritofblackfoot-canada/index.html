Wolf Nature-Spirit of Blackfoot

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf nature-spirit deity among the Blackfoot. Wolves appear as powerful helpers in Blackfoot tales, most famously in the Wolf Man story, where a pack saves a husband from his murderous wives; wolves are respected allies, but no wolf spirit-deity of nature is recorded.
