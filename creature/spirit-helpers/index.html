Spirit Helpers

spirit · Laos · Southeast Asia

Spirit helpers are the benevolent tutelary beings of Hmong shamanic tradition, assisting the shaman during trance journeys and aiding humans in daily tasks and healing. Each shaman relies on personal helping spirits (neeb) gained through initiation and maintained by offerings and respect. Documented in ethnographies of Hmong spirituality, they remain a functioning part of religious life.
