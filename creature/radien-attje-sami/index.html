Radien-attje

supreme-deity · Norway · Sami

Radien-attje (also Raedie or Veralden Olmai, meaning 'World Man') is the supreme high god in Sami mythology, the ruler of the cosmos and the celestial father. He is the creator and giver of life, sitting at the head of the divine family trinity alongside his wife Raedieahkka and their son Radien-pardne. In some traditions their daughter Rana Niejta is also included. He is more of an intellectual superior who maintains cosmic order rather than interfering directly — his son Radien-pardne performs practical tasks.
