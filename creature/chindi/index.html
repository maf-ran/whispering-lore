Chindi

ghost · United States · North America

Chindi (Navajo: ch'ijidii) is a concept in Navajo religious belief referring to the negative spiritual residue left behind after a person dies. Unlike a ghost in the Western sense, the Chindi is not the soul of the deceased but an accumulation of everything negative about their life including pain, fear, anger, disappointment, resentment, and rejection. According to Navajo tradition, the Chindi leaves the body with the person's last breath and lingers in the vicinity of the death site and the deceased's possessions.
