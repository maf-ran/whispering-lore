Moose Trickster of Tlingit

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a trickster of Tlingit folklore. The Tlingit trickster is Raven (Yéil), who stole the sun, the moon and the stars and shaped the world through his schemes. No source records a moose trickster on the Northwest Coast. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
