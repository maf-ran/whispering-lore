Vodník

water-spirit · Slovakia · Slavic

Vodník is the Slovak water spirit, nearly identical to the Czech Vodník. Inhabits Orava region lakes and rivers. Uses ribbons to lure humans, stores drowned souls in pots. His wife appears as a frog; a widespread tale has a woman serve as godmother to the vodník's frog-wife and free captive souls. Can be helpful to fishermen who offer tobacco.
