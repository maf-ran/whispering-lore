Los Chamos

goblin · Venezuela · South America

Not independently attested as a folkloric goblin. 'Los Chamos' is a famous 1980s Venezuelan boy band; 'chamo' is Venezuelan slang for 'kid/guy.' The goblin description appears to be a folk-etymology invention punning on the slang term. No Venezuelan ethnography records household goblins by this name.
