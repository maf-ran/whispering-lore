Dev

giant · Armenia · Caucasus

Dev is a type of giant or ogre in Armenian folklore, derived from the Persian word 'div' meaning demon or monster. These creatures appear throughout Armenian folktales and epic poetry, including the national epic 'Daredevils of Sassoun,' where they serve as formidable opponents for heroes. Armenian devs are characterized by their immense physical strength, limited intelligence, and generally brutish nature.
