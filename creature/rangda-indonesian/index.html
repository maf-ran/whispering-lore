Rangda (Demon Queen)

demon · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Rangda is the terrifying demon queen of the Leyaks in Balinese mythology, the eternal antagonist of Barong. She is the incarnation of Calon Arang, the legendary witch who wreaked havoc in ancient Java during the reign of King Airlangga in the 10th century. Historically linked to the exiled queen Mahendradatta, Rangda leads an army of evil witches and leyaks (black magic spirits) against the forces of good.
