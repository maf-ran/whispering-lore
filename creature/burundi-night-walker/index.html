The Night-Walker

spirit · Burundi · East Africa

The Night-Walker (Burundian). is a spirit. A spectral night-spirit that feeds on fear and dreams in Burundi. Source: Rural Burundian Folklore Found in Open valleys and the outskirts of rural Burundian villages during the night.. Documented in ethnographic records.
