Eagle Deity of Zapotec

deity · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In Zapotec mythology, the eagle is a powerful symbol associated with the sky, the sun, and warfare. The Zapotec civilization of Oaxaca, Mexico, regarded the eagle as a creature of great spiritual significance, often linked to their most important deity Cocijo, the lightning and rain god. Eagles were seen as messengers of the gods and guardians of the celestial realm. In Zapotec iconography, the eagle appears in ceramic art, sculpture, and architectural decoration, representing the warrior class and the nobility. The bird's association with height and vision made it a natural symbol for rulers
