Cougar Giant of Choctaw

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Choctaw emergence tradition holds that the people came from the underworld through the mound of Nanih Waiya, but no 'cougar giant' dwelling beneath it or causing earthquakes appears in any recorded tradition. The being is fabricated.
