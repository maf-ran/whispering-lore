Wakan Tanka

deity · United States · North America

Wakan Tanka (Great Mystery or Great Spirit) is the supreme sacred power in Lakota religion, the unified totality of all that is mysterious, powerful, and sacred. Unlike a personified deity in Western traditions, Wakan Tanka is better understood as the sum of all wakan (sacred) forces that permeate the universe. The term encompasses four primary deities: Wi (Sun), Skan (Sky), Maka (Earth), and Inyan (Rock), along with their associates. Everything in creation possesses wakan energy as part of Wakan Tanka
