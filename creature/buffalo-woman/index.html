Buffalo Woman

spirit · United States · North America

Buffalo Woman is a revered figure in Kiowa mythology, serving as a powerful symbol of the sacred relationship between the Kiowa people and the buffalo herds that sustained them on the southern Great Plains. In Kiowa oral tradition, Buffalo Woman appears as a liminal being who bridges the human and animal worlds, often bringing important teachings, prophecies, or warnings to the people. Her stories reinforce the Kiowa understanding that buffalo are not merely resources but relatives with their own intelligence, spirituality, and agency in the cosmic order
