Phi Pom

ghost · Laos · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in documented Lao folklore or academic sources as a distinct spirit named Phi Pom. The recorded traits—a pale figure near water that can be protective or vengeful, tied to unfinished business—resemble general Lao beliefs about the restless dead, but no dedicated Lao-language or scholarly source for this exact name was located.
