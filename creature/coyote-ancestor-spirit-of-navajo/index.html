Coyote Ancestor-Spirit of Navajo

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

Known as Mą'ii in the Navajo language, the Coyote ancestor-spirit is one of the most complex and revered figures in Diné Bahane', the Navajo creation story. Unlike lesser spirits, Mą'ii existed from the beginning of time, present in the First World of darkness and mist alongside First Man and First Woman. He is both a creator and a destroyer, a teacher and a trickster, whose ceremonial name is Áłtsé hashké or First Scolder.
