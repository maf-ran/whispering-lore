Weeget Raven Tsimshian

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Weeget is the Raven of the Coast Tsimshian, the trickster and creator whose clever thefts gave the people light, fresh water, and salmon. He shapeshifts freely and meddles in the affairs of gods and men, and Tsimshian tellers recount both his world-making deeds and the greed that drives his schemes.
