Anulap

deity · Federated States of Micronesia · Pacific

Anulap is the god of knowledge and magic in the mythology of Chuuk (Truk) in the Federated States of Micronesia, husband of the creator goddess Ligobubfanu and grandfather of the trickster Olifat. He taught humanity navigation, medicine, and sacred arts, and his wisdom was essential to voyagers reading stars, currents, and winds between the islands.
