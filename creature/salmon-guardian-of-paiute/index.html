Salmon Guardian of Paiute

guardian · United States · North America

Not attested in Paiute folklore. The Paiute of the Great Basin are known for the waterbaby spirit, the trickster Coyote, and their trout and cui-ui fisheries, but salmon were not native to their waters and no salmon guardian figure appears in their recorded traditions.
