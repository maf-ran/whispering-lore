Boumba

forest-spirit · Cameroon · Central Africa

Not independently attested as a spirit: 'Boumba' is chiefly a toponym in southeastern Cameroon, the Boumba River and Boumba-Bek National Park, and no Bantu forest guardian of that name appears in documented sources. The description is a generic filler portrait of a forest-protector spirit.
