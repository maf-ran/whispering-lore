Supay

god · Peru · South America

Supay is the Inca/Quechua/Aymara lord of Uku Pacha (the underworld), an ambivalent deity of death, shadows, and mineral wealth. Originally neutral (allisupa = good spirit, mana allisupa = bad), he was syncretized with the Christian Devil. Ruler of the dead's journey, master of mines, honored by miners as 'El Tío.' Brother of Inti, Illapa, Mama Quilla.
