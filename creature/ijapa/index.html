Ijapa (Unknown). is a animal-spirit. Ijapa (the Tortoise) is a trickster figure in Yoruba folklore. This clever tortoise uses his wits to survive and thrive, though his schemes often backfire in humorous ways.. Source: Yoruba oral tradition; West African folktales
