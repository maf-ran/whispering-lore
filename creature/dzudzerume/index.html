Dzudzerume

ogre · Georgia · Caucasus

Dzudzerume is a powerful witch from Georgian folklore, a shapeshifting female practitioner of dark magic who lives in remote mountain villages and wields formidable supernatural abilities. Unlike the typical European witch figure, Dzudzerume is deeply rooted in the unique mythology of the Caucasus region, combining elements of pre-Christian pagan beliefs with later folk traditions. She is known to ride bulls or wolves through the night sky, attending secret gatherings of witches on Mount Kazbek, one of Georgia highest peaks.
