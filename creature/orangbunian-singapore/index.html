Orang Bunian

spirit · Singapore · Southeast Asia

Orang Bunian (Hidden People) are supernatural beings from Malay folklore who are invisible to most humans except those with spiritual sight. They are a parallel race living in another dimension overlapping with the human world, particularly in forests, foothills, and cemeteries. In Singapore, Kubur Kassim Cemetery in Siglap contains tombs specifically dedicated to Orang Bunian. They are described as beautiful beings in ancient Southeast Asian attire, nearly identical to humans but sometimes lacking a philtrum.
