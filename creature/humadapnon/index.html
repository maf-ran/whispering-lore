Humadapnon

hero · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Humadapnon is the middle brother among three demigod heroes in the Hinilawod, the epic of the Suludnon people of Panay in the Philippines. Son of the goddess Alunsina and the mortal Datu Paubari, Humadapnon is characterized as a thoughtful strategist and skilled diplomat whose adventures balance his brother Labaw Donggon's brute strength and Dumalapdap's raw courage.
