Bison Nature-Spirit of Cherokee

nature-spirit · United States · North America

The underwater buffalo is a genuine mysterious being of Cherokee tradition, recorded by James Mooney: a lone buffalo seen rising from the waters of Cheowa River, which killed a man who threatened it and gave the place its name. The Buffalo Dance, performed in ceremony, further shows the buffalo as a powerful and honored presence in Cherokee life.
