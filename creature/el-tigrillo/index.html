El Tigrillo

animal-spirit · Costa Rica · Mesoamerica

El Tigrillo is a sacred jaguar spirit from Costa Rican folklore, revered as the guardian of the forests and a powerful omen of fortune. The legend is particularly strong in the mountainous regions of Costa Rica, including the Talamanca range and the Osa Peninsula, where jaguars (tigrillos) are the apex predators. In indigenous Bribri and Cabécar traditions, the jaguar is a sacred animal associated with shamans and spiritual power.
