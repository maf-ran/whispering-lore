Kitsunebi

monster · Japan · East Asia and Pacific

Kitsunebi, meaning "fox fire" in Japanese, are mysterious floating lights that appear in forests and rural areas of Japan, closely associated with the presence of kitsune fox spirits. These ethereal flames are believed to be a manifestation of the kitsune supernatural power, created when the spirits exhale or conjure fire as they move through the darkness. Witnesses describe the kitsunebi as small orbs of colored light that drift silently through the trees, sometimes appearing as a single light and other times as a long procession of flames stretching across the landscape.
