Asasabonsam

vampire · Ghana · West Africa

The Asasabonsam is a fearsome forest vampire from Akan folklore, closely related to the Asanbosam and Sasabonsam traditions found across Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Togo. This malevolent being dwells in the deepest, most inaccessible parts of the forest, particularly in ancient trees with sprawling canopies. The Asasabonsam is considered one of the most dangerous supernatural entities in West African folklore because it actively hunts humans for their blood rather than simply protecting territory.
