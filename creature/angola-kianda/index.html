Kianda

goddess · Angola · Central Africa

Kianda is the most beloved and widely recognized water goddess in Angolan mythology, a powerful mermaid-like deity who presides over the seas, lakes, rivers, and forests. In the Kimbundu language, 'kianda' translates to a fabulous mythological monster or water god, though she is far more complex than a simple mermaid figure. According to Mbundu tradition, every body of water—every river, lagoon, and coastal area—has its own Kianda, who takes the name of that particular water body.
