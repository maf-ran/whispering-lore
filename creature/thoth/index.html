Thoth

god · Egypt · North Africa

Thoth, the ibis-headed god of the moon, is the Egyptian lord of wisdom, writing, magic, and judgement. Scribe of the gods and arbitrator of their disputes, he stands beside Ra's solar barque and records the verdicts of the dead. His cult center was Hermopolis, and the Greeks identified him with Hermes; his name means he who is like the ibis.
