Psoglav

monster · Bosnia and Herzegovina · Slavic

Psoglav is a monster from Balkan / Slavic Mythology mythology. It is described as having a human body with horse legs, a dog's head with iron teeth, and a single cyclopean eye on the forehead. Psoglavs dwell in caves and a dark underground land rich with gemstones but without sun. They are ghoul-like beings who practice anthropophagy, eating humans and digging up corpses from graves to consume them. Their presence is considered a harbinger of death and misfortune.
