Crane Nature-Spirit of Apache

nature-spirit · United States · North America

No crane nature-spirit is attested in Apache folklore. Apache traditions include the Gaan (Mountain Spirits), Changing Woman, and the Jicarilla creation stories of Holy Boy, but cranes hold no recorded role as nature spirits. The sandhill crane's association with lightning belongs to Cheyenne tradition, not Apache, making this entry appear invented.
