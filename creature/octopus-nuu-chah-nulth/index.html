Octopus Nuu Chah Nulth

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

In Nuu-chah-nulth (Nootka) legend, the octopus appears in the tale 'Raven Annoys Octopus,' where Raven pesters the octopus people and learns a lesson in respect. An octopus woman with eight long braids digs on the shore with a stick, and the story teaches listeners about meddling and the boundaries of other beings.
