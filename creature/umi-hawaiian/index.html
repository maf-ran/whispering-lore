'Umi-a-Liloa

hero · United States · Pacific

A legendary Hawaiian chief of the island of Hawaii, known for his justice, wisdom, and military prowess. 'Umi-a-Liloa was the son of the great chief Liloa, born to a commoner woman. Despite his humble maternal lineage, 'Umi was recognized by his father and eventually became one of the greatest rulers of ancient Hawaii. He unified the island of Hawaii, reorganized the land divisions, established just laws, and protected the common people.
