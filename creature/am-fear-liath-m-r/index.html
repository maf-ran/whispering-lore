Am Fear Liath Mòr

cryptid · Scotland · Celtic

Am Fear Liath Mor, meaning the Big Grey Man in Scottish Gaelic, is a legendary cryptid said to inhabit the summit and slopes of Ben Macdui, the second-highest mountain in the British Isles, located in the Cairngorms range of Scotland. This mysterious entity has been reported by climbers and hikers since the late 19th century, with the most famous account coming from renowned physician and mountaineer Professor Norman Collie in 1891. The creature is associated with feelings of extreme fear and dread, footsteps following behind climbers in the mist, and occasional sightings of a tall grey figure
