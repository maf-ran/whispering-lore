Peri

spirit · Afghanistan · Central Asia

Peri are beautiful winged supernatural beings in Azerbaijani folklore, derived from Persian mythology and deeply integrated into Turkic folk tradition. These enchanting spirits are morally ambivalent but predominantly benevolent, often helping humans in distress and guiding heroes on quests. Peris live in elaborate underground kingdoms with gardens and palaces, organized into societies mirroring human structure.
