Coyote Trickster (Laotian)

trickster · Kenya · East Africa

Coyote Trickster (Laotian) is a trickster from Kenya mythology. In Lao folklore, trickster figures often blur the boundaries between the natural and supernatural worlds, teaching through mischief and paradox. This oceanic coyote spirit commands the tides and currents, shaping coastlines and carving river deltas with its playful movements. It represents the duality of the sea as both nourisher and destroyer, bringing abundant fish to coastal communities while also capsizing the boats of those who show arrogance or disrespect toward the waters
