Bison Spirit (Malagasy)

spirit · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested: no Malagasy buffalo spirit is documented in Kenyan traditions, and the description restates the fabricated 'bison spirit of Madagascar' while reassigning it to Kenya. Malagasy reverence centers on the ancestors (razana) and zebu cattle; the 'African buffalo guardian' figure described has no recorded basis in either country's folklore.
