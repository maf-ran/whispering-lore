Ifrit (Powerful Fire Jinn)

jinn · Algeria · North Africa

The Ifrit (plural: Afarit) is a powerful class of jinn in Algerian Islamic folklore, considered among the most dangerous and formidable of supernatural beings. Created from smokeless fire like all jinn, ifrits are distinguished by their immense strength, speed, and malevolent nature. In Algerian tradition, ifrits inhabit desolate places such as ruins, desert wastes, and cemeteries. They are particularly associated with the vast Sahara regions of southern Algeria.
