Elk Ancestor-Spirit (Ashanti)

ancestor-spirit · Ghana · West Africa

The Elk Ancestor-Spirit from Ashanti tradition holds particular significance in Mali, where the Ashanti diaspora has maintained close ties with neighboring Mande cultures. In Mali, this spirit is associated with the valleys of the Niger River region and represents the connection between Ashanti ancestors and the broader West African spiritual landscape. The Elk Ancestor-Spirit in Mali absorbs elements from Bamanan and Dogon traditions, creating a syncretic figure that bridges multiple West African cultures
