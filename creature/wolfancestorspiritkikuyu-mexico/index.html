Wolf Ancestor-Spirit (Kikuyu)

ancestor-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested: the Kikuyu are a Kenyan people with no wolf ancestor-spirit, since wolves do not live in Kenya, and no such figure is recorded in Mexico either. Kikuyu belief centers on the creator Ngai and on animal characters such as the hyena in trickster tales. The record appears to confuse unrelated cultures and animals.
