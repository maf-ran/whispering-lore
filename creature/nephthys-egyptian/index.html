Nephthys (Goddess of Protection and Mourning)

deity · Egypt · Africa

Nephthys is the Egyptian goddess of mourning, protection, and service. Daughter of Nut and Geb, sister of Isis, Osiris, and Set; snip she was the wife of Set but abandoned him due to his evil nature. She helped Isis search for the scattered pieces of Osiris's body and was a protector of the dead, usually paired with Isis at funerary ceremonies. She is associated with the night and the edge of the desert. She is the 'Mistress of the House' referring to the temple enclosure.
