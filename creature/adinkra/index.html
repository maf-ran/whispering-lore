Adinkra

spirit · Ghana · West Africa

Adinkra refers to a collection of West African symbols created by the Bono people of Gyaman, now Ghana, named after their original creator King Nana Kwadwo Agyemang Adinkra. Each Adinkra symbol represents concepts related to traditional wisdom, aspects of life, the environment, and philosophical ideas. One significant Adinkra symbol is Funtunfunefu Denkyemfunefu, representing Siamese crocodiles, which symbolizes democracy, cooperation, and unity among different groups.
