Stállo

giant · Norway · Sami

Stállo is a giant from Sami mythology. He is evil, strong, and dangerous but also clumsy, stupid, and easily tricked by clever Sami heroes who invariably outwit him using intelligence over brute force. He dresses in dark clothes with gold buttons and carries a rucksack on his back and a sharp knife. Stállo can be created by noaidis (shamans) from earth, wood, or turf, given part of the shaman's own life force, and sent to attack enemies.
