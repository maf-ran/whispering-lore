Le Zandolite

animal-spirit · Haiti · Caribbean

Le Zandolite is a Haitian spectral dragonfly appearing at night. If caught, it grants one wish before disappearing. A wish-granting animal spirit motif in Haitian folklore. Name may derive from French 'zandoli' (lizard/gecko) + diminutive, or creole variation. Not a major figure; minor wish-granting spirit.
