Hang Jebat (The Rebel Warrior)

hero · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Hang Jebat is the most complex and controversial figure in Malay folklore — a legendary warrior who rebelled against the Sultan of Melaka after his best friend Hang Tuah was unjustly executed. When the Sultan ordered Hang Jebat to kill Hang Tuah, Jebat instead revealed his own martial prowess and refused. He later went on a rampage in the palace, taking matters into his own hands against an unjust ruler.
