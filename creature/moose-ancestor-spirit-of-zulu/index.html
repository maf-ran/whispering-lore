Moose Ancestor-Spirit of Zulu

ancestor-spirit · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Zulu tradition. Moose are not native to Africa. The record appears fabricated, templated with "guardian of lakes" motifs. No ethnographic source documents a moose ancestor-spirit in Zulu or any Southern African culture. Closest real analogues: eland or antelope ancestral spirits in San/Bantu cosmology.
