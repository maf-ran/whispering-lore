Eagle Water-Spirit of Mapuche

water-spirit · Chile · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Mapuche tradition. Mapuche cosmology includes the ngen, spirits that own natural places, and water spirits associated with rivers and lakes, but no source describes an eagle owning the waters, controlling fish spawning, or assisting machi healing rituals. The entry composes a hybrid being from unrelated Mapuche elements.
