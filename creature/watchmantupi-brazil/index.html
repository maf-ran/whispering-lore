Watchman (Tupi)

guardian · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a named 'Watchman' in Tupi tradition. Tupi/Guaraní guardianship figures include Jurupari (men's secret cult deity), Tupã (thunder/creator manifestation), and forest owners, but no specific 'Watchman (Tupi)' entity. The generic 'shadowy humanoid at forest edge' description appears fabricated. May reflect conflation with Jurupari or general forest guardian concepts.
