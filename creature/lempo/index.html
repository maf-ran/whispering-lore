Lempo

spirit · Finland · Nordic

Lempo is a malignant forest spirit or devil in Finno-Karelian folklore, documented by Ganander in Mythologia Fennica (1789). Originally a distinct fire/demon entity associated with Hiisi, later conflated with the Christian devil — the Finnish word 'lempo' means 'damn.' Leads travelers astray, causes disease, and personifies destructive passion.
