Wolf Guardian (Cheyenne)

guardian · United States · North America

No standalone wolf guardian spirit is attested in Cheyenne folklore; the record inflates a real tradition. The wolf's central Cheyenne presence is the Wolf Warriors, a renowned military society honoring the wolf's wariness and skill, and wolves appear in tales as helpers and teachers. The animal is honored for endurance, not worshipped as a nature-spirit deity.
