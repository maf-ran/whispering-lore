Capotona

spirit · Cape Verde · West Africa

Capotona is a mournful coastal ghost in Cape Verdean folklore, haunting shorelines and abandoned lighthouses. It manifests as a spectral figure emitting mournful sounds, countered by sunrise. Represents the perils of the sea and lost sailors. Documented in Cape Verdean oral tradition alongside Catchorrona.
