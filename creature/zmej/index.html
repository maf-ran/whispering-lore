Zmej

dragon · Bulgaria · Slavic

Zmej is the Bulgarian dragon — a winged, scale-covered serpent, often with multiple heads (3–12). Unlike East Slavic malevolent zmey, the Balkan zmej is a benevolent guardian who fights the lamia/hala (drought demons). Can appear as a handsome man to woo human women; children of such unions become vampire hunters. Symbol of patriotic resistance. St.
