Bakai

hero · Kyrgyzstan · Central Asia

Bakai is the wise elder and counselor of the Kyrgyz epic of Manas, the voice of reason among the heroes. In the episode of his meeting with Manas, he advises the young leader to unite the Kyrgyz clans and return to their homeland of Ala-Too, restoring the people's strength and power; his counsel shapes the destiny of the epic's hero.
