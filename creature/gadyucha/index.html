Gadyucha

bird · Turkmenistan · Central Asia

The Gadyucha is a death-omen spirit from Turkmen folklore, taking the form of a gigantic owl that foretells mortality and misfortune. In Turkmen and broader Central Asian traditions, owls are considered potent omens associated with cemeteries, ruins, and the boundary between the living and the dead. The Gadyucha is most often described as female, reflecting the Central Asian belief that female spirits are particularly attuned to matters of life and death.
