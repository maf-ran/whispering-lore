Scorpion Ancestor-Spirit (Mari)

ancestor-spirit · Russia · Slavic

Not independently attested: scorpions do not occur in the temperate forests of the Mari El region, and no scorpion ancestor-spirit appears in documented Mari tradition. The entry appears to be an invention; Mari ancestor and nature worship centers on the forest, the household, and the sacred grove.
