Ligobubfanu

deity · Federated States of Micronesia · Pacific

Ligobubfanu is the creator goddess in the mythology of Chuuk, Micronesia, and the wife of the god Anulap. She is one of the supreme deities responsible for creating the world and all life within it. As a female creator, Ligobubfanu represents the fertile, nurturing aspect of the cosmos. She created the islands of Micronesia by scattering earth across the ocean and caused the first plants to grow. She is also associated with the tides, the moon, and the reproductive cycles of marine life.
