Fallow Doe (Ghoa Maral)

ancestor-spirit · Mongolia · Central Asia

Gua Maral (the Fallow Doe) is an ancestress of the Mongols in the Secret History of the Mongols: with the wolf Börte Chino she forms the legendary progenitor pair who crossed a lake to found the lineage of Chinggis Khan. Gentle and divine, she symbolizes blessing, fertility, and the mythic origins of Mongol royalty.
