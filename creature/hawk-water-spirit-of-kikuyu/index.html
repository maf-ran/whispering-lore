Hawk Water-Spirit of Kikuyu

water-spirit · Kenya · East Africa

In Kikuyu (Agikuyu) belief, sacred high places and water sources on the slopes of Kirinyaga (Mount Kenya) are tied to Ngai and the spirits guarding them. Mist, springs, and waterfalls are treated with reverence, and birds crossing between sky and water may signal divine presence. The entry combines hawk and water-spirit imagery into one guardian, a synthesis of attested beliefs.
