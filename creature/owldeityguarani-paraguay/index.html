Owl Deity (Guarani)

deity · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested in Guaraní tradition. The seven legendary monsters and major deities (Tupã, Arasy, Tau, Kerana, Angatupyry, Pombero, Kurupi, Jurupari, Abaangui) include no owl deity. Owls appear in general Guaraní bird lore but hold no deity status. Entity appears fabricated.
