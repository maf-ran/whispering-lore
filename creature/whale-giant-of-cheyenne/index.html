Whale Giant of Cheyenne

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Cheyenne tradition; the Cheyenne, a Plains people, have no recorded whale giant, and no source documents a whale in their mythology. The principal figures of Cheyenne tradition are the creator Maheo and the spider trickster Wihio. The entry appears to be an invented figure, one of a series of impossible whale attributions across desert and Plains nations.
