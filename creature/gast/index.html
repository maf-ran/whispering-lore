Gast

ghost · Sweden · Nordic

Gast is the general Swedish term for a ghost or returning dead person (gengångare), not a specific water spirit. The input record misclassifies it as a 'water spirit' with 'storm calling' abilities. In Swedish folk belief, gast/gengångare are restless dead who haunt specific places, cause cold spots, and can be laid to rest through proper burial rites or exorcism.
