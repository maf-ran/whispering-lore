Moose Guardian of Mapuche

guardian · Chile · South America

Not independently attested in Mapuche tradition. Duplicate of mooseguardianmapuche-chile with different habitat description ('undergrounds'). Moose are not native to South America. Mapuche underground spirits are the Ngen-mapu (earth owners) and Anchimayen (little people), not moose guardians.
