Duwende (Dwarf/Goblin)

spirit · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Duwende are dwarf-like goblins or earth spirits in Philippine folklore, closely related to the Nuno sa Punso (dweller of the anthill). These small, mischievous beings are believed to inhabit ant hills, termite mounds, old trees, and underground places. They are generally invisible but can choose to reveal themselves to humans. Duwende come in two types: white duwende (good, helpful spirits) and black duwende (mischievous or malevolent spirits).
