Sun Goat

beast · Chad · Central Africa

Among the Garap people of Chad, a myth explains the taboo on eating he-goat: a man found daylight personified as a he-goat in the forest but ate it instead of delivering it to the priest. The 'Sun Goat' is a figure in an aetiological tale transmitting moral knowledge about respect for property and authority, not a recurring beast or spirit.
