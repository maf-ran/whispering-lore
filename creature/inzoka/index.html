Inzoka

serpent · Rwanda · East Africa

Inzoka (Rwanda). is a serpent. Snake-like spirits dwelling in lakes and rivers, believed to wield power over droughts and floods. Source: Regional survey of East African indigenous traditions — Kikuyu, Luo, Ganda, Chaga, Malagasy, Maasai, Somali, Ethiopian, and related peoples as documen
