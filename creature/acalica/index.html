Acalica

nature-spirit · Bolivia · South America

The Acalica are mythological weather-controlling spirits from Bolivian folklore, sometimes called weather-fairies, with origins in indigenous Quechua and Aymara traditions of the Andean region. These elusive beings inhabit high-altitude underground caves and mountaintops, where they remain largely hidden from human sight in the rugged terrain of the Andes. The Acalica serve as subordinate guardians to Pachamama, the revered Mother Earth deity, acting as localized enforcers of her will by regulating weather patterns to maintain ecological balance and fertility.
