Jaka Tarub

human · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Jaka Tarub is the mortal protagonist of a famous Javanese folktale from Central Java. A young hunter, he discovers seven heavenly nymphs (bidadari) bathing in a forest lake and steals the shawl of the youngest and most beautiful, Nawang Wulan, preventing her return to heaven. They marry and have a daughter. Despite their happiness, Jaka Tarub breaks his wife's trust by lifting the cover of her magical cooking pot, shattering her spell.
