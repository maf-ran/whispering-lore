Cougar Nature-Spirit of Inuit

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Inuit oral traditions include nature spirits associated with various animals that inhabit or pass through northern territories. While the cougar is not a typical Arctic dweller, Inuit cosmology recognizes spirits that travel between worlds and may appear in forms unfamiliar to the physical landscape. These cave-dwelling spirits belong to the broader category of tuurngait, helping spirits that angakkuit call upon for guidance and protection. The cougar nature-spirit represents mysterious forces dwelling within limestone caves and ice-carved grottoes found along Arctic coastlines and river valle
