Salmon Spirit of Zulu

spirit · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Zulu tradition. Salmon (Salmo salar) are not native to southern African waters. The entry is fabricated with templated "guardian of cliffs" motif. Zulu water spirits include the mermaid-like njuzu and the serpent Inkanyamba, not salmon.
