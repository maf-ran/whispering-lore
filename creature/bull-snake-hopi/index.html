Bull Snake

spirit · United States · North America

Bull Snake is a character in the Hopi tale of Coyote and Bull Snake exchanging visits, recorded in the Traditions of the Hopi. Coyote finds Bull Snake's burrow beautiful and comfortable; the pair's exchange frames a homely lesson about envy and contentment. He is a narrative figure of a folktale, not a ceremonial spirit.
