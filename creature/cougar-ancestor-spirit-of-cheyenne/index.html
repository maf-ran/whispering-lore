ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

In Cheyenne cosmology, the mountain lion is respected as a powerful animal spirit associated with hunting prowess, stealth, and protection. The Cheyenne believe that the Great Creator Ma'heo'o imbued certain animals with special spiritual powers to assist humanity.
