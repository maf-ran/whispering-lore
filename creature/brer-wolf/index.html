Br'er Wolf

trickster · United States · North America

Br'er Wolf is a figure of African American folktale from the Uncle Remus cycle compiled by Joel Chandler Harris, a hungry and determined predator who hunts Br'er Rabbit but is repeatedly outwitted. Persistent yet lacking cunning, he loses to the rabbit's tricks, reinforcing the theme that wit defeats strength.
