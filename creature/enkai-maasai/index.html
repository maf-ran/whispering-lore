Enkai (Engai)

supreme-deity · Kenya · East Africa

Enkai, also known as Engai, is the supreme god of the Maasai people of Kenya and Tanzania. Enkai is neither male nor female but manifests in two distinct aspects: Enkai Narok (the Black God), who is benevolent and brings rain, grass, and prosperity, and Enkai Na-nyokie (the Red God), who is vengeful and brings drought, lightning, and famine. According to Maasai cosmology, Enkai once lived on earth with humanity but withdrew into the sky after being displeased.
