Se

spirit · Togo · West Africa

Se is divine law, destiny and cosmic order in Ewe cosmology: the moral and spiritual foundation set by the creator Mawu that governs all existence. Not a personified deity but the underlying balance between the spiritual and physical worlds, Se is honoured through ancestral rites, ethical conduct and respect for the natural order, and violating it brings misfortune.
