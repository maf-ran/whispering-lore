Nix

water-spirit · Unknown · Northern Europe

Nix is a water-spirit from Germanic/Nordic mythology. Similar to the Celtic kelpie, the Swiss Nix can assume multiple forms—most commonly a beautiful woman who lures men to watery deaths, but also a horse, fish, or even a log. According to Swiss folklore collected in central Switzerland, the Nix has complete control over water and weather, capable of causing floods or droughts at will. In some tales from Lake Lucerne, the Nix is a malevolent ghost that drowns anyone who pollutes or disrupts its underwater domain.
