Ko'komiki'somm

deity · United States · North America

Ko'komiki'somm, also known as Komorkis, is the Moon in Blackfoot tradition, one of the eldest of the sacred Sky People (Sspommitapiiksi). Wife of the Sun, Naato'si, and mother of the stars, she is the serene ruler of the night whose most important child is the Morning Star, Iipisowaahs; she is also the grandmother of the hero Star-Boy, and watches over lovers and those who travel after dark.
