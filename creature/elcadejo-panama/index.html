El Cadejo

spirit · Panama · Mesoamerica

El Cadejo is a spirit from Panama mythology. Unlike the dual white/good and black/evil Cadejos of other Central American countries, Panama's Cadejo is a single black creature. It is described as a large black dog with glowing red eyes, dragging heavy chains. Though monstrous in appearance, the Panamanian Cadejo is generally harmless and acts as a spectral companion on lonely roads. The legends trace back to Maya mythology and Nahua shamanistic traditions where dogs served as companions to the dead on their journey to the afterlife.
