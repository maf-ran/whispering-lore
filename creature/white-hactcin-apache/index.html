White Hactcin

spirit · United States · North America

White Hactcin is an important Hactcin supernatural being in Jicarilla Apache tradition. According to Apache mythology, White Hactcin possessed the sun. Holy Boy, guided by Cyclone the wind spirit, went to White Hactcin who gave him the sun, while Black Hactcin gave him the moon. White Hactcin and Black Hactcin held a ceremony with songs, and White Hactcin released the sun into the sky while Black Hactcin released the moon.
