Moose Trickster of Kikuyu

trickster · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested in Kikuyu tradition. Moose do not exist in Kenya. Kikuyu trickster figures include the hare (mukambi) and spider in some tales, but no moose. Sacred mugumo tree hosts ancestor spirits. This repeats the North American moose fabrication seen in the ancestor-spirit record.
