Yam-Nahar

deity · Syria · Middle East and North Africa

Yam-Nahar (literally 'Sea-River') is the god of the sea and rivers in ancient Syrian (Ugaritic) mythology, the personification of the destructive power of the waters. Known as Prince Yam and Judge Nahar, he is son of the supreme god El and the beloved of his father. Yam is depicted as a serpentine deity, often identified with the sea monster Lotan (Leviathan). In the Baal Cycle, Yam challenges the storm god Baal for kingship of the gods, sending messengers to demand Baal's tribute.
