Raven Water-Spirit of Blackfoot

water-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Blackfoot folklore. No water-spirit raven exists in Blackfoot tradition, where the waters are instead the home of the Horned Snake (Omahksoyisksiksina), a fearsome man-eating lake monster, and the tribe's trickster is Napi. A 'Raven Water-Spirit of Blackfoot' is a modern invention or misattribution.
