Pwint

forest-spirit · Myanmar · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in documented Burmese folklore or academic sources as a flower spirit named Pwint. While pwint is a common Burmese name meaning flower, and the padauk is Myanmar's national flower with deep symbolism, no source records a spirit called 'Pwint' living in padauk blooms. The entry appears to blend real flower symbolism with an unverified spirit figure.
