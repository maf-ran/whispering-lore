Kebo Iwa (Buffalo Toe)

giant · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Kebo Iwa (meaning 'Buffalo Toe') is a legendary giant and strongman in Balinese folklore, known for his immense size, bottomless appetite, and superhuman strength. According to legend, he was the son of a human mother and a celestial being, and his appetite was so enormous that he could drain entire lakes in a single drink. The villagers of ancient Bali struggled to feed him, but Kebo Iwa repaid their kindness by singlehandedly carving the island's famous rice terraces and irrigation systems using only his bare hands and feet.
