Hawk Nature-Spirit (Tupi)

nature-spirit · Brazil · South America

In the West African Sahel region, the Tupi Hawk Nature-Spirit manifests as a guardian of the transition zone between the Sahara Desert and the tropical savannas. This region, with its harsh climate and sparse vegetation, demanded that the Tupi nature-spirit develop new strategies for protecting the people and the environment. For Tupi descendants who found themselves in the Sahel, this spirit became a symbol of resilience and adaptation to challenging conditions.
