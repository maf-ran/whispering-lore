Bannick

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) figure called 'Bannick' appears in any source; the name resembles the Russian bannik, a bathhouse spirit. Documented Ojibwe spirits include manitou beings and Mishipeshu, but nothing named Bannick.
