Badger Giant of Ute

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No documented Ute tradition features a Badger Giant. Ute stories centre on Coyote the trickster and include earlier races of animal and giant beings, but the badger plays no attested role in them; the American badger is native to the region but absent from Ute myth.
