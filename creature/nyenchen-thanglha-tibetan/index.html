Nyenchen Thanglha

deity · China · Central Asia

Nyenchen Thanglha is the great mountain god of Tibet, lord of the Nyenchen Thanglha range and chief of the nyen spirits. A white-clad warrior who rides a white horse, he was a wrathful native deity before he bound himself by oath before Padmasambhava and became a protector of the Buddhist teaching, called upon for protection, good fortune and fair weather.
