Deer Deity of Cree

deity · Canada · North America

In Cree cosmology, the Deer Deity is a powerful spirit associated with the vast inland waters and oceanic realms, serving as a guardian of marine life and an intermediary between the human world and the underwater spirit domain. Known as a benevolent yet formidable force, this deity governs the abundance of sea creatures and holds sway over the weather patterns that affect coastal and inland communities.
