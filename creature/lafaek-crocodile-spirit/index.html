Lafaek (The Crocodile Spirit)

spirit · East Timor · Southeast Asia

The crocodile holds profound spiritual significance across East Timor's diverse cultures, appearing in multiple origin myths and as a totemic ancestral figure. In various Timorese traditions, the crocodile is both a creator figure and a sacred ancestor. The island of Timor itself is said by some traditions to be shaped like a crocodile, and the animal is deeply respected as a guardian of sacred waters and ancestral law. Crocodile spirits are associated with lulik potency and are often guardians of specific water sources and coastal areas. Some clans consider the crocodile their direct ancestor
