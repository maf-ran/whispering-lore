Dsonoqua

spirit · Canada · North America

Dsonoqua, also known as Dzunukwa or Tsonoqua, is a powerful and complex figure from the mythology of the Kwakwaka'wakw people of the Pacific Northwest Coast of Canada. Unlike many North American spirit figures, Dsonoqua is neither purely benevolent nor malevolent but embodies a wild, untamed power that demands respect and caution. She is often described as a giantess or wild woman of the forest who possesses both dangerous and beneficial aspects, capable of devouring children who disobey while also granting supernatural powers to those who prove worthy.
