Si'lah

shapeshifter · Saudi Arabia · Middle East and North Africa

The Si'lah (also Sila or Si'lat, plural Sa'aali) is a female shapeshifting jinn from pre-Islamic Arabian folklore, particularly associated with the Bedouin tribes of the Arabian Peninsula including modern-day Saudi Arabia. Unlike the more generic Ghul, the Si'lah is specifically female and known primarily for seduction and shapeshifting. Si'lahs are described as the most intelligent and talented shapeshifters among the jinn, able to perfectly mimic human appearance — unlike the Ghul with its telltale donkey hooves. They prefer living near water sources in remote desert areas and oases. Si'lahs
