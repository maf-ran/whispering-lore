Bear Ancestor-Spirit of Zapotec

ancestor-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Among the Zapotec people of Oaxaca, Mexico, bear ancestor-spirits appear in the rich tapestry of ancestral worship that characterizes their indigenous religion. The Zapotec, who built the magnificent city of Monte Alban, maintained a deep connection with the mountain forests where black bears once roamed. Bear ancestor-spirits are considered guardians of the mountain passes and sacred caves used for rituals and burials.
