Mouros

spirit · Spain · Mediterranean

Supernatural beings in Portuguese and Galician mythology, believed to be an ancient race that inhabited the land since the beginning of time before being forced underground for unknown reasons. The Mouros work gold, silver, and gemstones in subterranean forges beneath hills and ancient castro settlements, guarding enormous treasures protected by dragon-like cuélebres. They rarely emerge except to obtain food and during special liminal times like Midsummer (São João). Some appear as giants or warriors, while others take tiny forms called Mourinhos or Maruxinhos.
