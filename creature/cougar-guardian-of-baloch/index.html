Cougar Guardian of Baloch

guardian · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Cougars are not native to Balochistan. The genuine feline of Iranian-plateau tradition is the palang (leopard), a term rich in Persian literary symbolism (Rustam's leopard-skin), along with babr (tiger); the cave-guardian 'cougar' is invented.
