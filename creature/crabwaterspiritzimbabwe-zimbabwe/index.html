Crab Water-Spirit (Zimbabwe)

water-spirit · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested. The entry's description is set in the Seychelles — coral reefs, mangroves, coconut crabs — despite the slug and country naming Zimbabwe, an inland nation; the 'water spirits travelling through all bodies of water' rationale is invented. No Zimbabwean tradition records a crab water-spirit; Zimbabwe's water-lore centres on the Zambezi god Nyami Nyami.
