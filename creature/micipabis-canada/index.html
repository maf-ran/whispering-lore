Micipabis

spirit · Canada · North America

Micipabis (Mishipeshu, the Underwater Panther) is the great water being of Anishinaabe and Algonquian peoples: a panther-lynx with horns, copper scales and a spiked tail that dwells in the depths of the Great Lakes, raising storms, whirlpools and floods when angered. It guards copper deposits and must be appeased with offerings; it is the eternal opponent of the Thunderbirds.
