Simurgh

bird · Iran · Middle East and North Africa

Simurgh is a colossal benevolent mythical bird in Azerbaijani folklore, shared with Persian and broader Turkic mythology. This wise and ancient creature possesses profound healing powers and esoteric knowledge. The Simurgh nests atop the mythical Qaf Mountain at the edge of the world and is said to have lived so long that it witnessed the destruction of the world three times. In Azerbaijani tales, the Simurgh appears at moments of greatest despair to rescue heroes trapped in deep pits or remote mountains, carrying them to safety and imparting wisdom.
