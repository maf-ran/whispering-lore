Raven Trickster of Yurok

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Yurok folklore. The Yurok trickster is Coyote, as among the neighboring Karuk and Hupa of northwestern California; no Yurok tale makes Raven a river-guardian or trickster. The entry appears to be a misattribution of the Pacific Northwest Raven cycle to a people whose trickster traditions center on Coyote.
