Duende

spirit · Portugal · Mediterranean

Small, mischievous humanoid beings in Portuguese folklore, similar to goblins, elves, or leprechauns. Duendes are described as small-statured creatures wearing big hats who whistle an enchanting song through the forest to lure young children away from home, causing them to lose their way. The name derives from 'dueño de casa' (master of the house), suggesting they were originally household spirits. In Portuguese tradition, duendes inhabit forests, caves, and abandoned places.
