Heartman

spirit · Barbados · Caribbean

The Heartman is one of the most feared figures in Bajan folklore — a gruesome entity that preys on misbehaving children by carving out their hearts and offering them to the devil. Parents would invoke the Heartman's name to keep children in line, making him an instrument of social control through terror. The legend has deep roots in the island's African heritage blended with colonial Christian imagery.
