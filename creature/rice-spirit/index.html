Rice Spirit

spirit · Laos · Southeast Asia

The rice spirit of Lao and Thai tradition, closely tied to Mae Phosop (Mother Rice), holds the life force believed to inhabit the rice crop. Farmers propitiate her with rituals and offerings at planting and harvest, and the loss of her presence is thought to drain fields of vitality. The figure links agricultural practice with animist cosmology in mainland Southeast Asia.
