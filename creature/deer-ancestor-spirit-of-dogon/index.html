Deer Ancestor-Spirit of Dogon

ancestor-spirit · Mali · West Africa

The Deer Ancestor-Spirit of the Dogon people of Mali holds a significant place in the rich cosmological framework of Dogon mythology. The Dogon are renowned for their sophisticated astronomical knowledge and complex spiritual beliefs centered around the Nommo, primordial beings who shaped the world. This deer spirit represents the connection between the earthly realm and the ancestral world, serving as a messenger between living Dogon communities and their ancestors. It features in Dogon creation narratives and agricultural ceremonies tied to the rhythms of the seasons
