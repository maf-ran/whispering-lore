Masewi (Twin War God)

deity · United States · North America

Masewi is one of the Twin War Gods in Pueblo (specifically Acoma) tradition, brother to Oyoyewi. These twins are among the most important heroic figures in Pueblo mythology. They were born to a virgin mother who conceived from the Sun. The twins are credited with creating the world and all living creatures, slaying the giant monster that terrorized the Pueblo people, establishing the directions, bringing order to the world, and teaching people how to live.
