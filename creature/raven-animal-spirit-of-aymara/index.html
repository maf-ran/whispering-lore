Raven Animal-Spirit of Aymara

animal-spirit · Bolivia · South America

Not independently attested as a named Aymara spirit. Aymara cosmology includes animal spirits (fox, condor, llama) but no specific 'raven animal-spirit' guardian of oceans (ravens are rare/absent on Altiplano; condor fills the raptor role). The description borrows Pacific Northwest raven lore.
