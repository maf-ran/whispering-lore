Sang Kancil (The Mouse Deer)

animal · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Sang Kancil, the mouse deer, is the most famous trickster figure in Malay folklore. Despite being one of the smallest animals in the jungle, Sang Kancil uses extraordinary intelligence and wit to outsmart much larger and stronger adversaries — crocodiles, tigers, elephants, and humans. In the most famous tale, Sang Kancil tricks a river full of hungry crocodiles into lining up across the river by claiming the king has ordered a headcount of all crocodiles.
