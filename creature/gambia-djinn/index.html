Djinn

spirit · Gambia · West Africa

Djinn are invisible, shapeshifting spirits of Islamic folklore widely believed in the Gambia, where they can influence human fate, possess the unwary, or act as moral enforcers in desert and ruin contexts. Their power is invoked through charms, amulets, and the guidance of marabouts in Gambian Islamic folk practice.
