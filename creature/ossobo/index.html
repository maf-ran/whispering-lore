Ossobó

bird · Sao Tome and Principe · Central Africa

Ossobó is a mystical rain-bird in Forro (São Toméan) folklore. Inhabiting cloud forests and mountain peaks, it manipulates weather and heals through sonic powers — its melodic calls bring nurturing energy. A symbol of hope, renewal, and connection to ancestral spirits. The tale 'Gertrude's Bird' features it.
