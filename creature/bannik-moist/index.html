Bannik-Moist

water-spirit · Russia · Slavic

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The bannik, the Russian bathhouse spirit, dangerous after midnight and appeased with bread, salt, or an offering of water, is genuine Slavic folklore, but no 'Moist' variant appears in any source; the epithet and its supposed social character are invented.
