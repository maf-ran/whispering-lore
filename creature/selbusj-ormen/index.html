Selbusjøormen

monster · Norway · Nordic

Selbusjøormen is a legendary lake serpent said to inhabit Selbusjøen, one of the largest lakes in Trøndelag. Selbusjøen is located in Selbu municipality and is part of the Nea-Nidelva watercourse. The lake serpent has been reported for centuries by local farmers, fishermen, and travelers. Descriptions vary — some say it resembles a giant eel, others describe a serpent with multiple humps, and some claim it has a horse-like head.
