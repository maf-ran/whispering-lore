Cucafera

dragon · Spain · Mediterranean

Traditional Catalan dragon monster from the popular bestiary that has preceded Corpus Christi processions since the 14th century in cities of the former Crown of Aragon. First documented in Montblanc, the Cucafera is described as a wingless dragon with a turtle-like shell, crocodile head, and extendable neck. It represents idolatry or heresy in religious processions and is accompanied by giants and big-heads during the festive parade. Its fearsome appearance was used to frighten children into good behavior
