Coyote Ancestor-Spirit (Muisca)

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

Coyote Ancestor-Spirit (Muisca) is a ancestor-spirit from USA mythology. This variant represents the universal archetype of the cunning ancestor-spirit that exists across continents, adapted into Kenyan storytelling traditions through centuries of trade and cultural exchange across the Atlantic. Like the Muisca original that guards the boundary between worlds, this spirit patrols the liminal spaces between the savanna and the spirit realm, carrying with it the wisdom of both Andean highlands and African plains in a harmonious blend of trickster traditions from two continents
