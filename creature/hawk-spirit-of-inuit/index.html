Hawk Spirit of Inuit

spirit · Canada · North America

The Hawk Spirit holds a distinctive place in Inuit mythology, where the harsh Arctic environment gives special significance to any creature that can navigate the vast northern skies. In Inuit tradition, hawks and falcons are respected for their hunting prowess and their ability to survive in extreme conditions. The Hawk Spirit is considered a helper of shamans, assisting them in their spiritual journeys between worlds.
