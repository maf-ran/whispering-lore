Jätte

giant · Sweden · Nordic

Jättar (giants) are colossal humanoid beings from Swedish folklore, believed to be an ancient race that lived in the land long before humans. The word "jätte" derives from an Old Germanic verb meaning "to eat," reflecting their immense appetites. In Swedish folk tradition, jättar are most famously associated with features of the landscape: glacial erratic boulders called jättekast (giant's throws) were said to have been hurled by angry giants attempting to crush churches, whose bells they could not stand.
