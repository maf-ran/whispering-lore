Ankou

nature-spirit · England · Celtic

The Ankou is the death-messenger of Breton folklore, a skeletal or shrouded figure who rides or drives a cart, gathering the souls of the dying; the last person to die in a parish becomes the Ankou for the following year, and the creaking of his wheels is a mortal omen.
