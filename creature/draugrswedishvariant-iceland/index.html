Draugr (Swedish variant)

undead · Iceland · Nordic

Along Sweden's Baltic coast and archipelagoes, the draugr is the revenant of a fisherman or sailor lost at sea. This maritime variant guards sunken treasure, raises storms, and harries living boatmen, reflecting the sea as both livelihood and grave. It is distinct from the mound-dwelling draugar of the Icelandic sagas.
