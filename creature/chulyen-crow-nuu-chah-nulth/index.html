Chulyen Crow Nuu Chah Nulth

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

The name Chulyen belongs to the Dena'ina, an Athabaskan people of south-central Alaska, where Chulyen is the raven trickster and transformer of the Kenai Peninsula, not a Nuu-chah-nulth figure. Nuu-chah-nulth tradition gives the raven's role to its own culture hero, while the Dena'ina raven cycle tells how Chulyen stole fire and shaped the land.
