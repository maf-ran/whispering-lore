Eagle Trickster (Malagasy)

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a trickster of Malagasy folklore. In Malagasy tradition the eagle (voromahery) is a royal messenger of God, not a trickster; Malagasy trickster roles belong to the hare or the tortoise. The 'USA' framing also signals a fabricated diaspora entry with no documented source.
