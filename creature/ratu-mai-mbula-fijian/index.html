Ratu-Mai-Mbula

deity · Fiji · Pacific

Ratu-Mai-Mbula is a Fijian god associated with fertility, harvest, and agricultural abundance. As a deity of the land and its bounty, he governs the growth of crops, particularly yams and taro, which are staple foods in Fijian culture. Traditional ceremonies were held to honor Ratu-Mai-Mbula during planting and harvest seasons, with offerings made to ensure fertile soil and abundant yields. He is part of the agricultural cycle that connects the Fijian people to the land, and his favor was sought to prevent crop failures and famine.
