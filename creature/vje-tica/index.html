Vještica

ogre · Croatia · Slavic

Vještica is the Croatian/Serbian word for 'witch' (from 'vješti' — to know), not a distinct creature. The record appears to conflate the general term with a specific ogre. Actual Croatian witch beliefs involve night flight on wooden beams, causing storms, illness, and infertility.
