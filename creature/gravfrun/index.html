Gravfrun

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

Gravfrun (the grave lady) is a female guardian spirit of burial mounds, grave fields, and churchyards in Swedish folklore. She appears as a woman in white or black, sitting on ancient burial mounds at dusk or standing silently among the gravestones. Gravfrun is the protector of the dead — she wards off grave robbers, those who would disturb ancient burials, and anyone disrespecting the dead. She is particularly associated with Bronze Age and Iron Age burial mounds, which were considered sacred places inhabited by the dead and their guardians.
