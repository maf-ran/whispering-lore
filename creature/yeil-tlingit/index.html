Yeil (Raven)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Yeil is the Tlingit name for Raven, the central trickster and culture hero of Tlingit mythology. Yeil is responsible for many fundamental features of the world. He created the tides by making a woman under the earth to control the rise and fall of water. In the Tlingit flood narrative, Raven commanded the tide woman to raise the water. When Raven returned, he turned the remaining people and animals into stone and made new human beings from leaves, explaining why people die in autumn when leaves fall. Yeil is both creator and destroyer
