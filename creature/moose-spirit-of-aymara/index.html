Moose Spirit of Aymara

spirit · Bolivia · South America

Not independently attested in Aymara tradition. Moose are native to the northern hemisphere and absent from South America; no ethnographic source records a moose spirit in Altiplano cosmology. The closest Andean analogue is the deer or vicuña as herd animal, but no named moose spirit exists.
