Acqua Che Canta

water-spirit · Italy · Mediterranean

Acqua Che Canta, meaning singing water in Italian, is a water spirit from the folklore of northern Italy, particularly the Alpine and pre-Alpine regions including Veneto, Friuli, and the Dolomites. These creatures are known by many names including anguane, agane, vivane, subiane, and aquane. They are nature spirits akin to the nymphs of Roman mythology, blending characteristics of Germanic undines and Slavic rusalki.
