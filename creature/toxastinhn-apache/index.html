Toxastinhn (Water-old-man)

spirit · United States · North America

Toxastinhn ('Water Old Man') is a powerful water spirit of White Mountain Apache tradition, associated with rivers, lakes and springs. He appears in narratives alongside the Hero Twins and carries the sacred power of water in Apache cosmology. The figure is documented in Pliny Earle Goddard's White Mountain Apache texts published by the American Museum of Natural History.
