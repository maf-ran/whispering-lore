Jörmungandr (Faroese)

serpent · Faroe Islands · Nordic

In Faroese folklore, the Norse World Serpent Jörmungandr appears in a unique creation myth explaining the origin of the archipelago. After the World Serpent (child of Loki) was defeated in the final battles of the gods, a tooth fell from the dying serpent's enormous mouth into the ocean and became the Faroe Islands. This dramatic origin story connects Faroese identity directly to the great Norse mythological cycle, positioning the islands as a fragment of a cosmic being.
