Bear Ghost of Kazakh

ghost · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

In Kazakh folklore from the steppes of Central Asia, bear ghosts represent the spirits of shamans and warriors who have taken on bear form after death. While the wolf holds a more prominent place in Kazakh totemic tradition as the mythical ancestor of Turkic peoples, the bear is recognized as a powerful spirit animal in regions where bears inhabit the Tien Shan and Altai mountain ranges. Kazakh bear ghosts are believed to be the restless spirits of those who died with unfinished business, particularly warriors who fell in battle and shamans whose power was not fully transferred before death
