Splinter Foot Girl

culture-hero · United States · North America

Splinter Foot Girl is a miraculous culture hero in Arapaho folklore. A childless woman found a splinter embedded in her foot. To her amazement, the splinter grew and transformed into a baby girl. The girl grew rapidly and possessed supernatural abilities—she could communicate with animals and had the power to heal. She brought good fortune to her family and community, helping them find buffalo and warning them of danger. When kidnapped by a rival tribe, she used her powers to escape and return home.
