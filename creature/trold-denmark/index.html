Troll (Trold)

giant · Denmark · Nordic

Trold is the Danish spelling of troll — same entity as troll-denmark and troll-norway. This record duplicates the pan-Scandinavian troll with Danish orthography. The description (huge lumpy humanoids, rock-like skin, fear sunlight/church bells, hoard wealth) is accurate for the Danish tradition.
