The Rolling Cauldron

monster · Jamaica · Caribbean

The Rolling Cauldron is a Jamaican magical object — a cauldron that rolls along roads and crossroads at night. It contains either treasure or a curse depending on whether one touches it. A road-spirit motif warning against greed and nocturnal travel. Part of Jamaican 'duppy' (ghost) folklore complex.
