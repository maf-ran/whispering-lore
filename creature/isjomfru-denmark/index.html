Ice-Maiden (Isjomfru)

spirit · Denmark · Nordic

Isjomfru ('Ice Maiden') is a Danish winter spirit: pale, translucent skin, frost-haired, ice-gowned. Lures travelers with cold melody to freeze in snow. Inhabits frozen lakes and mountains. Vulnerable to fire, true love, spring sun. Parallels Snow Queen (Snedronningen); both stem from older winter-goddess motifs.
