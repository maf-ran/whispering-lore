Cap-a-Taille

demon · France · Mediterranean

Cap-a-Taille is a malevolent nocturnal spirit from French folklore, particularly associated with the forested regions of medieval and early modern France. Known as a cauchemar or nightmare spirit, this entity is closely related to the Germanic Alp and the English mare traditions, part of a widespread European belief in beings that torment sleepers by sitting on their chests and causing terrifying dreams.
