Boo Hag

spirit · United States · North America

The Boo Hag is a terrifying supernatural entity from Gullah Geechee folklore. Unlike European vampires that drink blood, Boo Hags drain their victims' breath and spiritual essence by 'riding' them while they sleep. According to legend, a Boo Hag appears as a beautiful woman by day — luring victims into marriage or trust — but at night, she removes her skin and transforms into a skinless, red, flying creature.
