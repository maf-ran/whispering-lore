Yulm First Carpenter Nuxalk

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Yulm is the eldest of the Four Carpenters, the culture-hero craftsmen who assisted the Nuxalk creator Alhkw'ntam in shaping the world. His name lives on in the making of the first bird: Yulm molded a qwaxw from charcoal and set it to fly, giving the Nuxalk the origin of birds and of carving.
