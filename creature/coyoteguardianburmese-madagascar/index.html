Coyote Guardian (Burmese)

guardian · Madagascar · East Africa

Coyote Guardian (Burmese) is a guardian from Madagascar mythology. This spirit reflects the historical connections between Southeast Asia and East Africa through the monsoon trade winds that carried not only spices and textiles but also stories and spiritual beliefs across the Indian Ocean. In Swahili folklore, the coyote guardian is known as the Mlinzi wa Barabara, or Guardian of the Path, and it is invoked by caravan leaders before they set out on journeys into the interior.
