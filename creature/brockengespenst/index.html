Brockengespenst

nature-spirit · Germany · Germanic

The Brockengespenst, or Brocken Spectre, is an atmospheric optical phenomenon observed on the Brocken mountain in the Harz range of Germany, where it has inspired folklore and legends for centuries. First scientifically described by Johann Silberschlag in 1780, the phenomenon occurs when a climber or observer stands on a ridge or peak with the sun behind them and mist or fog below, casting an enormous, magnified shadow onto the cloud layer.
