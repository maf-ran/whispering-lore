Crow Mother

deity · United States · North America

Crow Mother is an important Kachina spirit in Hopi tradition associated with rain, motherhood, and the nurturing aspects of nature. She is often depicted with a characteristic crow-like mask and is among the most respected Kachina figures. In Hopi ceremonies, Crow Mother appears during the Powamuya (Bean Dance) ceremony and is associated with bringing rain and fertility to the crops. She represents maternal authority and the life-giving forces of the natural world. She also appears in Zuni Kachi
