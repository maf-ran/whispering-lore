Zār

spirit · Sudan · North Africa

The zar is a class of possessing spirits in Sudan, Egypt, and the Horn of Africa, believed to seize people, most often women, causing illness, restlessness, and affliction. Each zar demands its own clothes, jewels, incense, and drumming, met through the healing ceremonies of the zar cult. Seven nations of zar spirits range from holy men to European officials.
