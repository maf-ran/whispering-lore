Wolf Water-Spirit of Inuit

water-spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf water-spirit in Inuit folklore. The attested wolf beings are land dwellers: Amarok, a gigantic lone wolf-spirit that hunts hunters abroad at night, and Amaguq, a wolf trickster. Waters are instead the domain of figures such as Sedna of the sea; no wolf spirit is tied to rivers.
