Hastrman

water-spirit · Czech Republic · Slavic

Hastrman is a malevolent water spirit from Czech and Slovak folklore, the male counterpart to the more famous Rusalka. This aquatic demon dwells in ponds, rivers, lakes, and millponds throughout Central Europe, luring unsuspecting humans to a watery grave. The name 'hastrman' is derived from the German word 'Wassermann' (water man), reflecting the strong cultural exchange between German and Slavic folklore in the region.
