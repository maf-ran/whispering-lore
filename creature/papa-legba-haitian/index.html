Papa Legba (Guardian of the Crossroads)

spirit · Haiti · Caribbean

Papa Legba is the foremost lwa (spirit) in Haitian Vodou, serving as the guardian of the crossroads, gateways, and communication between the human world and the realm of the spirits. No ceremony can begin without first invoking Papa Legba, who opens the spiritual gateway. He is an old, wise man who walks with a crutch or cane, often depicted as a humble elder leaning on a staff, wearing a broad-brimmed straw hat and smoking a pipe, carrying a sack or bundle of keys.
