Bes

guardian · Egypt · North Africa

Unique Egyptian dwarf god (likely of Somali/Nubian origin, absorbed into Egyptian pantheon). One of the FEW non-anthropomorphic Egyptian gods - actually looks like a dwarf. Protector OF Households, mothers, children, Birth - a home's fierce guardian. NOT worshipped in temples (no clergy), but Every home (Pharaoh to slave) had Bes images. Placed at gates, on bedroom furniture, unguent jars, headrests, beds, chairs, mirrors, knives, amulets. Also: god of humor, JOY, music, fertility, Warfare.
