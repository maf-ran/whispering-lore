Nanabolele

monster · Lesotho · Southern Africa

The Nanabolele are horrid, reptilian creatures from Basotho mythology that live underwater and underground in the Lesotho highlands. Their most distinctive feature is their ability to glow in the darkness, giving off a luminous light similar to that of the moon and stars. These creatures are often described as a type of water-dwelling reptile or serpent with bioluminescent properties, making them both beautiful and terrifying.
