Dwarf (Dvergr)

dwarf · Norway · Nordic

The Dvergr, or Norse dwarf, is a master craftsman and smith from the underground realm of Svartalfheim or Nidavellir in Norse mythology. These ancient beings were originally maggots that emerged from the decaying flesh of the primordial giant Ymir, later transformed by the gods into humanoid form with great intelligence and skill.
