Windigo

monster · United States · North America

The Windigo is a fearsome cannibalistic ice monster of Ojibwe and Anishinaabe mythology, known throughout the Algonquian-speaking peoples of North America. A Windigo was once human but was transformed through an act of cannibalism or overwhelming greed into a giant, emaciated creature with a heart of ice. It has an insatiable hunger for human flesh and grows larger the more it eats, never satisfied. Windigos are associated with winter, famine, and the cold.
