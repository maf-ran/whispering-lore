Nāga

serpent · India · South Asia

Nāga is the diacritically-marked Sanskrit form of naga (slug: naga), not a distinct entity. Identical to the naga entry: divine half-human, half-serpent beings, children of Kashyapa and Kadru, dwelling in Patala, guarding waters and treasures, rivals of Garuda.
