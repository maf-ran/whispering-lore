Qwaxw Raven Nuxalk

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Qwaxw is the Raven of Nuxalk (Bella Coola) tradition, made from charcoal by the youngest of the Four Carpenters, who cried 'qwaxw, qwaxw, qwaxw' when he came to life. He flew up to Nusmata, tricked the granddaughter of the creator, and carried away the box of daylight, so that the world below gained light, fire, and abundance.
