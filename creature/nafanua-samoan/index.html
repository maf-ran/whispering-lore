Nafanua

deity · Samoa · Pacific

The greatest warrior goddess of Samoa, daughter of Saveasi'uleo (god of Pulotu, the spirit underworld) and Tilafaiga. Born a female, Nafanua was hidden in the forest by her mother out of shame at giving birth to a girl. She emerged as a fierce warrior when Samoa was oppressed by tyrannical rulers. She disguised her chest so enemies would not know she was a woman. Nafanua led rebellions that freed the people of Savai'i from bondage, then brought peace after winning the wars between eastern and western districts. She established the matai (chiefly) system of governance and is Samoa's most revere
