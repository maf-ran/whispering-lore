Aisha Qandisha

spirit · Morocco · North Africa

Aisha Qandisha is a female mythological figure in Moroccan folklore, a jinn-like entity with a distinct personality who is both feared and revered. She is associated with bodies of water such as rivers, lakes, and the sea, and is known to use her beauty to seduce men before driving them mad or killing them.
