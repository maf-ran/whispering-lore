Wildcat Guardian (Shona)

guardian · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested in Brazilian tradition. MISATTRIBUTION: The Shona are a Bantu people of ZIMBABWE (Southern Africa), not Brazil. Shona mythology includes Mwari (creator), vadzimu (ancestral spirits), and njuzu (water spirits), but no 'wildcat guardian' in Brazil. Entity appears to be a complete fabrication conflating African and South American elements.
