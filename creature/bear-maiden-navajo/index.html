Bear Maiden

spirit · United States · North America

The Bear Maiden is a figure from Navajo tradition about a young woman who transformed into a bear after being mistreated by her family or community. As she wandered into the wilderness in despair, her hands and feet slowly transformed into bear claws and her body grew fur. She later encountered hunters from her village who recognized her by remnants of her human appearance.
