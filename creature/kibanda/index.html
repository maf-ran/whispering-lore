Kibanda

water-spirit · Uganda · East Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Bunyoro or Ugandan source documents a water spirit called 'Kibanda', and 'Lake Bunyoro' does not exist; the python-and-hippo master-of-the-fishery is generic filler. The genuine water-god of the region is Mugizi, associated with Lake Albert and the Cwezi kubandwa spirit cult.
