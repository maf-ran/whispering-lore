Hawk Trickster of Paiute

trickster · United States · North America

No hawk trickster appears in Paiute tradition; the trickster of the Great Basin peoples, including the Paiute, is Coyote, often malevolent, lecherous, and sometimes a fire-bringer. No source documents a hawk in that role, and the entry's meditation on 'questioning received wisdom' is invented.
