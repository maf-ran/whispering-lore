Mama D'Leau

water-spirit · Dominica · Caribbean

Mama D'Leau, also known as Mama Glo or Mama D'Lo, is the Mother of Water in Dominican and wider Caribbean folklore. She is the wife of Papa Bois and the fierce guardian of rivers, streams, and water creatures. Described as having a beautiful woman's upper body and the lower body of a massive serpent, she uses her enchanting beauty and hypnotic song to lure poachers, trespassers, and those who harm her aquatic domain close before ensnaring them with the powerful coils of her tail.
