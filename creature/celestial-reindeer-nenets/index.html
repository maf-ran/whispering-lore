Celestial Reindeer (Seven Reindeer)

beast · Russia · Central Asia

Among the Nenets of Siberia the bright northern stars are read as a celestial reindeer or elk pursued across the sky, a tale told by forest Nenets reindeer herders to mark the turning of the seasons. Because the reindeer stands at the heart of Nenets life, its image in the heavens carries both practical and sacred meaning.
