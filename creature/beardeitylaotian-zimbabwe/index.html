Bear Deity (Laotian)

deity · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Zimbabwean tradition. The description matches Laotian 'Moon Bear' animist beliefs (lunar cycles, rice agriculture, nocturnal protection), not Shona or Ndebele folklore. Zimbabwe has no native bear species. The record misplaces a Laotian deity into southern Africa, likely via erroneous cultural conflation.
