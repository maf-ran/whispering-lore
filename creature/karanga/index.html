Karanga

spirit · Burkina Faso · West Africa

The Karanga are masked spirits that appear during ceremonial performances in the cultures of Burkina Faso and neighboring regions of West Africa. These sacred figures play an essential role in traditional African religious practices, serving as intermediaries between the human world and the ancestral realm. The Karanga masquerades are deeply rooted in the spiritual traditions of the region, where masked dancers embody the spirits of ancestors, nature deities, and supernatural forces during important community ceremonies. These performances are not merely entertainment but are considered sacred
