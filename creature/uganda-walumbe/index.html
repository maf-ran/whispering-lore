Walumbe

spirit · Uganda · East Africa

Walumbe is the personification of death in Baganda mythology, the brother of Nambi and son of the sky god Ggulu. In the creation myth, Walumbe is forbidden by his father from knowing about Kintu—the first man—and his sister Nambi's marriage. When Kintu and Nambi prepared to descend to Earth, Ggulu warned them to leave immediately without returning for anything forgotten. But Walumbe tricked them, causing Nambi to forget grain for their journey.
