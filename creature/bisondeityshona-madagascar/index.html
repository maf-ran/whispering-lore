Bison Deity (Shona)

deity · Madagascar · East Africa

Not independently attested: Shona religion belongs to Zimbabwe, not Madagascar, and venerates Mwari as supreme being with the mhondoro (lion) ancestral spirits guarding the land; there is no bison deity. The buffalo (mhofu) appears in Shona totemic naming, and zebu cattle are valued in Madagascar, but the described bison deity is a fabricated cross-cultural mash-up with no source.
