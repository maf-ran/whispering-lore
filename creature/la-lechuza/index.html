La Lechuza

shapeshifter · Mexico · North America

La Lechuza is a terrifying shapeshifting witch from Mexican and Texas border folklore who transforms into a giant owl — seven feet tall with a fifteen-foot wingspan and the face of an old woman. According to legend, she was once a human woman who made a pact with the devil or practiced black magic, gaining the power to become an enormous owl that hunts at night. She preys primarily on drunk men and children, luring victims by mimicking the cry of a human infant or whistling.
