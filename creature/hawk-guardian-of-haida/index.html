Hawk Guardian of Haida

guardian · Canada · North America

Among the Haida people of Haida Gwaii (the Queen Charlotte Islands), the Hawk Guardian is a prominent crest figure representing the power of the skies and the noble status of Eagle clan lineages. In Haida culture, organized into two primary matrilineal clans (Raven and Eagle), the hawk is closely associated with the Eagle clan and appears on totem poles, house front paintings, and ceremonial objects as a symbol of authority and spiritual vision. The Hawk Guardian is believed to possess the power of far-sighted wisdom, able to see across both the physical realm and the spirit world. Haida chief
