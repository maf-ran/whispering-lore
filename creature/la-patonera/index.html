La Patonera

monster · Colombia · South America

La Patonera (also known as La Patuda or simply 'Big Foot') is a legendary creature from the folklore of Argentina, Uruguay, and southern Brazil, particularly associated with the vast wetlands of the Esteros del Iberá in Corrientes, Argentina, and the Paraná River delta. The name derives from 'pata' (foot/leg), referring to the creature's most distinctive feature: enormous, oversized feet that leave massive tracks in the mud of riverbanks and swamps.
