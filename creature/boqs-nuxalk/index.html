Boqs (Cannibal Giant)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Boqs is the hairy wild man of the woods of the Nuxalk (Bella Coola), a giant sasquatch-like being of the British Columbia coast whose hands and face are human. He walks stooping, can raise the very land by supernatural power, and was said to still haunt King Island; Boas recorded detailed first-hand accounts.
