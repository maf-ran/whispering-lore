Wolf Ancestor-Spirit (Kikuyu)

ancestor-spirit · Myanmar · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a Kikuyu or Myanmar ancestor-spirit. The Kikuyu of Kenya worship the high god Ngai (Mwathani) at Mount Kenya under the mugumo fig tree; their documented religion contains no wolf ancestor-spirit. The record mislabels culture and region, combining a Kenyan people with a Myanmar setting; it appears to be a generic spirit template without documentary basis.
