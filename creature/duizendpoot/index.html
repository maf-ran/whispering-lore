Duizendpoot

beast · Netherlands · Germanic

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Duizendpoot is simply the modern Dutch word for a centipede (literally 'thousand-foot') and names no mythological being in Dutch tradition, where the nearest domestic or forest spirits are the kabouter and the klabauterman. The present description is a copy-pasted Belize duende text.
