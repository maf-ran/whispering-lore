Ko'wituma (Twin War God)

deity · United States · North America

Ko'wituma (Twin War God) is one of the sacred twin deities in Zuni mythology, the elder of the two warrior brothers who protect the Zuni people from evil spirits and monsters. Together with his brother Pautiwa, Ko'wituma drove the great monsters underground during the time of creation, clearing the world for human habitation. He is associated with the east and the rising sun, and carries a bow of lightning and arrows of flint. Ko'wituma is invoked before hunts and during times of conflict, and his sacred stone images are kept in the medicine kivas
