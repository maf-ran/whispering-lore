Soungoula

trickster · Seychelles · East Africa

Soungoula (Seychellois Creole). is a trickster. Soungoula is the most important trickster figure in Seychellois Creole folklore, the clever hare who outwits stronger animals and teaches moral lessons.. Source: Island Hopper Guides - Seychelles Folklore; The Creole Melting Pot; Folklore.unisey.ac.sc; Seychelles Research Journal
