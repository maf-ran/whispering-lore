Toad Boy

spirit · United States · North America

The Toad Boy is the son of Toad Woman (Machak Wuhti) in Voth's Hopi story 'The Toad and the Snow Kachinas': he lives at Katchinavala, wears a wildcat-skin robe, paints his face to watch the Snow Kachinas practice, and is teased by the children, who take him to an anthill because he likes to eat ants. He brings the red piki he begs home to his mother.
