Moose Nature-Spirit of Cheyenne

nature-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a nature-spirit of Cheyenne folklore. No source records a moose spirit among the Cheyenne, whose Plains traditions center on Maheo the Creator, the Sacred Arrows, and the buffalo as the people's life-giver. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
