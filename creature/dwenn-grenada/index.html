Dwenn

spirit · Grenada · Caribbean

The douen (dwen) is a spirit of Grenadian and wider Trinidadian folklore: the soul of a child who died unbaptized, appearing as a faceless child under a wide straw hat, with feet turned backwards. It lures living children into the forest by calling or asking for help, and its reversed footprints make it impossible to track.
