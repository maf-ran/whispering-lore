Fox Guardian of Inuit

guardian · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a guardian of Inuit folklore. The documented Inuit fox figures are the fox-woman of the Labrador legend, who becomes a hunter's wife, and the trickster fox of northern tales; neither acts as a guardian spirit. No Inuit fox-guardian is recorded in the available sources.
