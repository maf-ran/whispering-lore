Badger Nature-Spirit of Passamaquoddy

nature-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not native to Passamaquoddy territory, and no such nature-spirit is documented. Passamaquoddy tradition instead features the culture hero Glooskap, the storm-bird Pamola, and wilderness spirits such as Kiwahq; the wild-spirit twin is the closest attested analogue.
