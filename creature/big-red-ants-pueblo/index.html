Big Red Ants

spirit · United States · North America

Big Red Ants are spirit beings in Pueblo mythology associated with industriousness, community cooperation, and the power of the earth. They are credited with teaching the Pueblo people the value of organized labor and collective effort, showing how small creatures working together can accomplish great feats — moving seeds, carrying water, and building vast underground colonies. During certain Pueblo ceremonies, red ant motifs appear in sandpaintings and pottery as symbols of hard work and earth energy.
