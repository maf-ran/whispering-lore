The Fish-Wife of Lake Toba

spirit · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

The Fish-Wife of Lake Toba is the tragic spirit-woman at the center of North Sumatra's most famous origin myth. A poor fisherman named Toba caught a magical golden fish that transformed into a beautiful woman. She agreed to marry him on one condition: he must never reveal her fish origins. They had a son named Samosir, and lived happily for years. But one day, enraged by his son's laziness, Toba shouted 'You son of a fish!' — revealing his wife's secret. The curse was broken.
