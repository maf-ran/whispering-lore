Fox Deity of Ojibwe

deity · United States · North America

In Anishinaabe (Ojibwe) tradition of the Great Lakes, the fox is a clan animal (doodem) linked to cleverness and diplomacy and appears in tales as a shrewd trickster. The fox is a respected minor spirit figure in medicine practice, not a formal deity; no dedicated fox deity is documented.
