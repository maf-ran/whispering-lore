Suv Peri (Water Fairy)

spirit · Turkmenistan · Central Asia

Suv Peri (Water Fairies) are mythical water spirits from Turkmen folklore inhabiting the Caspian Sea coast and rivers such as the Amu Darya. According to Turkmen tradition, the Amu Darya mermaids are tender, sweet-voiced, and mysterious, following traditional Central Asian standards of beauty. However, the water maidens of the Caspian Sea and southern rivers are treacherous, dangerous, and have a frightening dark coloration. Suv Peri are guardians of water resources who can permit or forbid fishing and sea travel. They embody the protective role of water spirits in Turkmenistan's arid environm
