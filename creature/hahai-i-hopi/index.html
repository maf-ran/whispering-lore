Hahai-i

spirit · United States · North America

Hahai-i Wuhti is the Katsina Mother of Hopi tradition, mother of all katsinam, the spirit messengers who bring rain, gifts, and teachings to the people. Carved kachina dolls of her image have been collected at Oraibi on Third Mesa since the late nineteenth century, and she holds a central place in the Hopi ceremonial year.
