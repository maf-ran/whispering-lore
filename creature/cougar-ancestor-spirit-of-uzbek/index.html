ancestor-spirit · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

Although cougars are not native to Uzbekistan, the concept of powerful feline ancestor-spirits blends with local traditions of snow leopard and tiger spirits found across Turkic mythology. In Uzbek folk belief, ancestors could reincarnate as powerful animals or manifest as guardian spirits to protect their descendants.
