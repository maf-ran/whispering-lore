Salmon Water-Spirit of Shoshone

water-spirit · United States · North America

Not attested in Shoshone folklore. The Shoshone of the Great Basin have no salmon in their territory or mythology; their stories feature Coyote, Water Baby and local animal spirits. No 'salmon water-spirit' appears in the recorded Shoshone corpus.
