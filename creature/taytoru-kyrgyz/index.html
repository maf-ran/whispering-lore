Taytoru

mythical-horse · Kyrgyzstan · Central Asia

Taytoru is the magical steed of Semetey in the Kyrgyz Epic of Manas, a sleek, fiery-eyed horse of supernatural speed and endurance. She carried Semetey from Bukhara across the mountains to the Talas valley so he could avenge his father Manas's death. Loyal and shrewd, she is the archetype of the epic hero's faithful mount.
