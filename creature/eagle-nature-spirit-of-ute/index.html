Eagle Nature-Spirit of Ute

nature-spirit · United States · North America

In Ute cosmology, the eagle nature-spirit is a powerful embodiment of the natural world's sacred intelligence, representing the highest spiritual authority among the animal beings. The Ute people believe that all living things possess supernatural power, and the eagle sits at the apex of this spiritual hierarchy. The eagle nature-spirit is not merely a supernatural bird but the living embodiment of the sky's power manifest in the natural world.
