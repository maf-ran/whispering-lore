Chase Vault

monster · Barbados · Caribbean

The Chase Vault mystery is one of the most famous and enduring paranormal legends of the Caribbean, centered on a family tomb in the Christ Church cemetery of Barbados. Between 1812 and 1820, the heavy lead-lined coffins interred in the Chase family vault were repeatedly found disturbed, moved from their original positions, and in some cases overturned, despite the vault being sealed with a marble slab and showing no signs of forced entry.
