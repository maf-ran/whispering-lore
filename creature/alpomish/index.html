Alpomish

hero · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

Alpomish is a legendary hero from Turkic folklore, particularly prominent in Uzbek, Kazakh, and other Central Asian epic traditions. He is the central figure of the Alpomish epic, one of the most important examples of Turkic oral literature, dating back to ancient times and passed down through generations of storytellers. The epic tells the story of Alpomish, a heroic warrior who overcomes numerous challenges, battles monsters and enemies, rescues his beloved, and proves his worth through courage, strength, and cunning.
