Gueno

deity · Mali · West Africa

Gueno (also spelled Guéno) is the eternal creator god of the Fulani people, the supreme being who existed before all things. He created the universe and entrusted the care of cattle and the land to Koumen, the primordial shepherd. Gueno is associated with the sky, celestial order, and the spiritual essence that animates all life. In pre-Islamic Fulani tradition, Gueno was worshipped alongside 28 lesser deities tied to natural forces.
