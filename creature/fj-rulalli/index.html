Fjörulalli

beast · Iceland · Nordic

Fjörulalli (Beach Walker) is a smaller variant of the skeljaskrímsli in Icelandic folklore, about the size of a medium dog to a calf. Fjörulalli is covered with shells or lava fragments and has a small rounded head. It is generally considered harmless to humans but has a nasty habit of tearing the udders off sheep it encounters on the beach. Pregnant women were warned to avoid fjörulalli sightings. The creature walks along beaches at night, its shell coating making a distinctive rattling sound. Fjörulalli has been sighted on all Icelandic coasts, with particularly common reports from the Westf
