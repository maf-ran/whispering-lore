Hinn

spirit · Saudi Arabia · Middle East and North Africa

The Hinn are a class of pre-Adamite beings in Islamic and Arabian tradition. Ibn Kathir's exegesis of Qur'an 2:30 relates that the Hinn (also written hinn and binn) shed blood on earth before humanity, prompting the angels' question to God about creating Adam. Later cosmology groups hinn with binn, timm, and rimm among jinn sub-races.
