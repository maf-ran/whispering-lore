Al-Khidr

spirit · Egypt · North Africa

Al-Khidr, meaning 'The Green One,' is an immortal prophet and mystic figure deeply revered in Jordanian and Levantine folklore. He is described in the Quran (Surah Al-Kahf) as a righteous servant of God possessing divine wisdom and secret knowledge. In Jordanian tradition, Al-Khidr is believed to be a wandering immortal who appears to those in distress, particularly travelers, sailors, and seekers of spiritual wisdom. He symbolizes renewal, greenery, and life, and is associated with springs, rivers, and fertile lands. His shrines are found throughout Jordan and the Levant, making him a bridge
