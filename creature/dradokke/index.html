Dradokke

monster · Norway · Nordic

A dradokke (dra-doll, dragedokke) is a doll-like object of Norwegian folk belief that 'draws' wealth to its owner. Sources in the Norsk Folkeminnesamling record that it was sent out at night to bring back milk, grain, clothes, gold, and silver, sometimes from drowned men at the seabed; recipes for making one could call for stolen cloth or a dead thief's right thumb.
