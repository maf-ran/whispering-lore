Bà Triệu

hero · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Bà Triệu, known as Lady Triệu or Triệu Thị Trinh, is a legendary Vietnamese warrior heroine who led a rebellion against the Wu Chinese occupation in the 3rd century AD. According to folklore, she possessed supernatural strength and the ability to grow to giant size in battle, making her an invincible force against enemy armies. Born in Quan Yên village (present-day Thanh Hóa province), she gathered a force of over 1,000 warriors, including many women, and fought numerous battles against the occupying forces.
