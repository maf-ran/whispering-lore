Lizard Deity (Tibetan)

deity · Ethiopia · East Africa

Not independently attested in Ethiopian tradition. The 'Lizard Deity (Tibetan)' is a Tibetan Buddhist protector (e.g., lizard-headed deity guarding hidden treasures in Dzogchen terma) misassigned to Ethiopia. Ethiopia's traditions include Atete (Oromo fertility goddess), Waaqa (supreme deity), zar spirits—no Tibetan lizard deity.
