Holy Boy

spirit · United States · North America

In Jicarilla Apache creation accounts, Holy Boy is the restless spirit of the dark underworld who drove creation forward. When the Black Hactcin had shaped the animals and the world, Holy Boy grew weary of the darkness and pressed for the sun, moon, and light to be made, so that the people could live above in a bright world.
