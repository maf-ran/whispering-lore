Adroanzi

guardian · Uganda · East Africa

Adroanzi is a guardian spirit from the mythology of the Lugbara people of Uganda and the Democratic Republic of Congo. In Lugbara cosmology, Adroanzi are nature spirits associated with specific locations such as rivers, rocks, trees, and forests, serving as protective entities of the natural landscape. They are considered lesser spirits compared to the supreme creator god Adroa, but they play an essential role in mediating between the human world and the divine realm.
