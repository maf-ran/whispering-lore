Pocong

ghost · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

The pocong is a ghost of Indonesian folklore: a humanoid figure wrapped entirely in white burial cloth, tied at the head and feet, that moves by hopping or, in some accounts, teleporting because its legs are bound. It appears in cemeteries and abandoned houses, sometimes asking people to untie its shroud, and underscores the importance of completing Islamic burial rites properly.
