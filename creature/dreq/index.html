Dreq

dragon · Albania · Balkan

Dreq, from Latin draco, is a dragon-demon of Albanian folk belief that rises from lakes and freshwater to attack livestock and people. Closely related to the multi-headed kulshedra, it takes a more draconic, vampiric form. Albanian tradition ties the dreq to dangerous waters, explaining drownings and mysterious deaths near lakes and rivers.
