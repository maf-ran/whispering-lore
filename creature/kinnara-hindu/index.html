Kinnara (Celestial Musician)

spirit · India · South Asia

Kinnaras are half-human, half-bird celestial beings from Hindu and Buddhist mythology, renowned as divine musicians and lovers. Described in the Mahabharata, they are eternal lovers who never separate, never bearing children but forever embracing. They come from the Himalayas and are known for their exquisite music, dance, and poetry. In Hindu iconography, they are depicted with a human torso and head atop a bird's lower body, or sometimes as half-horse, half-human centaur-like beings.
