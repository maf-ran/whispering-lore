Lamassu

guardian · Iraq · Middle East and North Africa

The Lamassu is a colossal protective deity from ancient Mesopotamian religion, depicted as a majestic hybrid of a human head, the body of a bull or lion, and the wings of an eagle. Originally a protective goddess called Lamma in Sumerian times, the Lamassu evolved during the Neo-Assyrian Empire (c. 883–612 BCE) into monumental stone sculptures that guarded the entrances of palaces, temples, and cities.
