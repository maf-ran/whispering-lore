Salmon Ancestor-Spirit of Inuit

ancestor-spirit · Canada · North America

Not attested as an Inuit ancestor-spirit. Inuit mythology centers on sea-mammal spirits such as Nuliajuk (Sedna) and the moon god; salmon and char are food fish but do not appear as ancestral spirits in the recorded corpus. 'Salmon Ancestor-Spirit' does not occur in Inuit tradition and is a modern coinage.
