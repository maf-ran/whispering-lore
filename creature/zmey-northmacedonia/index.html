Zmey

spirit · North Macedonia · Slavic

Zmey is a spirit from Macedonian Slavic Mythology mythology. In Macedonian tales and folk songs, Zmey are described as extraordinarily intelligent beings with hypnotizing eyes, possessing a human-like form with a snake's tail and golden wings. They live in caves or on remote mountain tops and are extremely wise. The Zmey has a dual nature: they are territorial guardians who protect villages and fight destructive female Lamia in the skies during storms, yet they also fall passionately in love with human women, often kidnapping them to their mountain lairs.
