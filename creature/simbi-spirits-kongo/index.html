Simbi Spirits

water-spirit · Democratic Republic of the Congo · Central Africa

The Simbi are powerful water and nature spirits in Kongo mythology, ancient beings existing since before humanity. They inhabit rivers, streams, sacred pools, forests, mountains, and crossroads throughout the Kongo region. They are guardians of esoteric knowledge and natural balance. Simbi take several forms: Simbi d'Maza (Water Simbi) are healers invoked for purification and fertility; Simbi dia Nkita (Land Simbi) guard forests and agriculture; Simbi Nzila (Path Simbi) protect travelers.
