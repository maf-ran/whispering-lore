Steel Donkey

beast · Barbados · Caribbean

The Steel Donkey is a terrifying demonic donkey made of metal that roams the back roads of Barbados. Its eyes burn like fire, its mouth emits flames, and it drags heavy chains that clank loudly to announce its approach. The legend dates to the early 20th century and was believed to be summoned by malevolent individuals to carry out curses and inflict torment on enemies. Sightings are often accompanied by inexplicable events like stones pelting rooftops, livestock falling ill, or sudden hoofprints appearing on dusty paths.
