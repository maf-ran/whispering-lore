Elverfolk

elf · Denmark · Nordic

Elverfolk, also known as elver or elvefolk, are a staple of Danish ballad tradition, most famously appearing in the medieval ballad Elveskud (The Elf-Shot). These forest beings are beautiful but perilous, representing the dangerous allure of the wild in contrast to the safety of Christian villages. Danish folklore distinguishes elverfolk from the church-going human community by their lack of salvation — they are pagan spirits who will never see heaven and thus envy mortal souls.
