Eagle Spirit (Guarani)

spirit · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Guarani tradition. The input's description is wholly about the Aztec founding myth of Tenochtitlan (the eagle on a cactus), which belongs to Mesoamerica, not to Guarani-speaking Brazil. No source documents an eagle spirit among the Guarani, and the conflation of Guarani with Mexican eagle symbolism is unfounded.
