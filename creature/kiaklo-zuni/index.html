Kiaklo

spirit · United States · North America

Kiaklo is the Zuni rain-priest hero who, sent by the kachina priest Ka'wimosa, journeyed to find the Middle Place (itiwana) where the Zuni would settle. According to Zuñi origin myth, Duck carried him to the council hall of the gods, where he learned the story of creation and the sacred dances; his quest brought the kachina ceremonies to the people.
