Laibon Spirits

spirit · Kenya · East Africa

The Laibon (plural: Laiboni or Oloiboni) are the hereditary ritual leaders and spirit mediums of the Maasai people, believed to possess supernatural powers passed down through the Inkindongi clan. Laiboni serve as intermediaries between the Maasai and Enkai, wielding powers of prophecy, rainmaking, healing, divination, and blessing. After death, powerful Laiboni become ancestral spirits who continue to guide and protect their descendants. The position of Laibon is the highest spiritual office in Maasai society.
