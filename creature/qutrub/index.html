Qutrub

shapeshifter · Saudi Arabia · Middle East and North Africa

The Qutrub (also spelled Qutrub) is a type of werehyena from Arabian folklore, a shape-shifting creature that combines human and hyena traits. According to the 10th-century scholar al-Mas'udi, the Qutrub was created from a split egg of a female jinn — its counterpart being the Marid (powerful sea jinn). The Qutrub is described as resembling a cat-like creature that haunts graveyards, eating corpses.
