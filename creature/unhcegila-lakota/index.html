Unhcegila

serpent · United States · North America

Unhcegila (Unk Cekula) is a gigantic serpentoid creature from Lakota mythology responsible for many unexplained disappearances and deaths. Described as a massive dragon-like serpent with impenetrable scales, eyes of fire, and a fanged mouth shrouded in smoke or cloud. Her only weakness was a seventh spot on her body where her heart lay burning within. She emerged from primordial waters, was fought by the Thunderbird Wakinyan, and her shattered bones became the Badlands.
