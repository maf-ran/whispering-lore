Gàidhrí

guardian · Nepal · South Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. 'Gaidhri' is a Gaelic-looking name with no attested Newari or Nepali tradition; the description is fabricated. The genuine Nepalese spirit of this household-forest type is the Newari Khyah, a large hairy household guardian.
