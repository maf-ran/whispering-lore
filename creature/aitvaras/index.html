Aitvaras

dragon · Lithuania · Baltic

The Aitvaras is a nature spirit in Lithuanian mythology, also known as Atvaras, Damavykus, Pukis, Sparyzius, Koklikas, Gausinelis, Zaltviksas, and Spirukas. It is identical to the Latvian Pukis. This creature is a household spirit that hatches from an egg laid by a nine-to-fifteen-year-old rooster. Once an Aitvaras lodges itself in a house, it most often refuses to leave and becomes a permanent resident.
