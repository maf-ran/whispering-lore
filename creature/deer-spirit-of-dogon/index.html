Deer Spirit of Dogon

spirit · Mali · West Africa

In Dogon tradition from Mali, deer spirits are associated with the sacred springs and water sources that sustain life in the arid Bandiagara Escarpment region. The Dogon people, renowned for their complex cosmology and astronomical knowledge, recognize animal spirits as intermediaries between the creator god Amma and the human world. Deer spirits specifically guard water sources, which are precious in the dry Sahel, and are linked to the Nommo water spirits that feature prominently in Dogon creation mythology. These spirits ensure that springs remain pure and that those who approach them do so
