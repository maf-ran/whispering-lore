Siagtut

spirit · Greenland · North America

The Siagtut (Siasgiut) are lost hunters of Greenland Inuit tradition who froze to death on the Arctic ice and whose souls were placed in the sky, where they now shine as the three stars of Orion's Belt. They serve as an eternal warning to hunters about the dangers of the wilderness, and travelers use the belt to keep their bearings. They can only watch, fixed forever in the night sky.
