Crane Water-Spirit of Inuit

water-spirit · Canada · North America

Powerful water spirit from Inuit tradition, embodied in the cranes that migrate across the vast Arctic tundra each spring. This being governs the thawing of frozen rivers and lakes, deciding when the ice will break and release the life-giving waters. Inuit elders tell stories of this spirit guiding migrating birds to safe nesting grounds and ensuring the Arctic watersheds remain pure. It occupies a central role in Inuit cosmology as a bridge between the frozen winter world and the brief but vibrant Arctic summer
