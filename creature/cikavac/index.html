Cikavac

bird · Serbia · Slavic

The Cikavac is a mythical winged creature from Serbian folklore, born from an egg laid by a black hen that must be carried under a woman's armpit for forty days. During this gestation period, the woman must not confess, cut her nails, wash her face, or pray, adhering to strict taboos that imbue the egg with magical properties. The resulting creature serves its owner by stealing milk from neighbors' cattle and honey from their beehives, bringing these goods back to its master.
