Whale Ancestor-Spirit of Shoshone

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Shoshone tradition; the Shoshone, a Great Basin people, have no recorded whale ancestor-spirit, and the description of a guardian of the springs matches no documented being. The real water guardians of the Shoshone are the water babies, known as nu-numbi, who watch over springs and lakes. The entry combines an ocean creature with a desert water spirit.
