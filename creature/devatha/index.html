Devatha

deity · Sri Lanka · South Asia

Devatha refers to a class of divine beings in Sri Lankan Buddhist and animist traditions, encompassing nature spirits, guardian deities, and ancestral spirits that protect villages, forests, and waterways. The concept blends pre-Buddhist animist beliefs with Theravada Buddhist cosmology, where devathas are benevolent beings who have achieved a favorable rebirth through merit but remain within the cycle of samsara.
