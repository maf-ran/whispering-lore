Wolf Trickster of Cheyenne

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf trickster in Cheyenne folklore. The Cheyenne trickster is Veeho, a spider who takes human form and is both transformer-hero and fool; his name, often misread as 'White Man,' actually means 'Spider.' Cheyenne wolves instead belong to the Wolf Warrior society, an honored order, not trickster tales.
