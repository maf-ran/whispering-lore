Turtle Giant of Baloch

giant · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in Baloch folklore. Baloch oral tradition (shahr, dastan) features heroes like Mir Chakar Rind, Hammal Jiand, and desert djinn (Mujna), but no turtle giants. Turtles are not prominent in arid Balochistan mythology. The record appears fabricated, possibly conflating Hindu Kurma avatar with Baloch culture.
