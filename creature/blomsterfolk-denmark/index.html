Flower-people (Blomsterfolk)

spirit · Denmark · Nordic

Not independently attested in Danish tradition as a named being. Danish folklore has generic nature spirits (ellefolk, huldrefolk) and flower-associated beliefs (e.g., elder tree mother), but 'Blomsterfolk' as tiny flower-born guardians appears to be a modern literary invention or conflation. The closest analogue is the general Scandinavian vättar/huldrefolk who inhabit natural features.
