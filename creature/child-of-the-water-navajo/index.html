Child of the Water (Tóbájíshchíní)

spirit · United States · North America

Child of the Water (Tóbájíshchíní) is one of the Navajo Hero Twins, younger brother of Monster Slayer. Born to Changing Woman and fathered by the Sun, he accompanied his brother on the quest to rid the world of the Anaye (monsters). Together they journeyed to their father the Sun, passing deadly tests.
