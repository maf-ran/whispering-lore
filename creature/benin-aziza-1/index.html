Aziza

forest-spirit · Benin · West Africa

The Aziza are benevolent forest spirits in Fon and Dahomean mythology of Benin. Described as diminutive 'little people,' these beings inhabit anthills, hollow trees, and sacred groves within the deep forests. Unlike many spirits in West African folklore that are feared, the Aziza are known for their wisdom and generosity toward humans who respect the forest. They are credited with teaching humanity essential survival skills including the use of fire, knowledge of medicinal herbs, hunting techniques, and tracking of animals.
