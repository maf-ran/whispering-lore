Salt Woman (spirit)

spirit · United States · North America

Salt Woman is a spirit in Hopi tradition who became angry when people violated taboos involving salt — being wasteful, disrespectful, or using salt improperly during ceremonies. In response, she moved the salt deposits far away to the Grand Canyon and other distant locations. Ever since, the Hopi have had to undertake long, dangerous journeys to gather salt, which is a sacred substance used in ceremonies and daily life. The story teaches respect for natural resources and the consequences of taki
