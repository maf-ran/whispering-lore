Crane Animal-Spirit of Haida

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

No crane animal-spirit is attested in Haida tradition. In the Haida story of 'Kwaie'tek, The Sea Gull' the crane appears as a minor character set against Sea Gull by Raven, but it carries no spirit role. The great beings of Haida mythology are Raven, Eagle, Bear and Killer Whale, not the crane.
