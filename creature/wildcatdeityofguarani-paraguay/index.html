Wildcat Deity of Guarani

deity · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested in Guaraní tradition. No wildcat deity exists in Guaraní mythology (seven legendary monsters, major deities). Wildcats (jaguarundi, margay, ocelot) inhabit the region but hold no deity status. Entity appears fabricated, possibly conflating Kurupi (fertility/sexuality god, sometimes feline-associated) or the Celestial Jaguar myth.
