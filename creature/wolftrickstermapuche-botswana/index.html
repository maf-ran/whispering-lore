Wolf Trickster (Mapuche)

trickster · Botswana · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Botswana or Mapuche tradition. Mapuche (Chile/Argentina) mythology features the fox-like trickster Colocolo and the shapeshifting Kalku, but no wolf trickster. Wolves are absent from both southern Africa and Patagonia. The record misattributes a South American culture to Botswana and invents a wolf figure where none exists.
