Kupe

hero · New Zealand · Pacific

The great Polynesian navigator and chief who discovered Aotearoa (New Zealand) according to Maori tradition. Kupe sailed from Hawaiki in his canoe Matahourua, pursuing the giant octopus Te Wheke-a-Muturangi which had been stealing his bait. His wife Hine-te-Aparangi was the first to sight land, calling out 'He ao, he ao!' (A cloud, a cloud!) - giving the name Aotearoa (Land of the Long White Cloud).
