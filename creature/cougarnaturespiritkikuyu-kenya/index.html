Cougar Nature-Spirit (Kikuyu)

nature-spirit · Kenya · East Africa

Within Kikuyu oral traditions the cougar nature-spirit represents one of many territorial guardians assigned by Ngai to protect specific ecological zones across the central Kenyan highlands. These spirits belong to a complex hierarchy of supernatural beings including ancestral spirits, nature deities, and the distant high god. The cougar spirit specifically oversees transition zones where cultivated fields meet wild forests, acting as a regulator of the relationship between human settlement and untamed nature.
