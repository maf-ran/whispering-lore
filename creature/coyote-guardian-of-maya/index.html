Coyote Guardian of Maya

guardian · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

In Maya mythology the coyote guardian associated with rivers belongs to the complex pantheon of animal spirits and wayob that inhabit the natural world. The Maya recognize coyote as a creature of the twilight boundary between day and night, a being that moves between cultivated fields and wild forests along river corridors. In the Maya calendar the day sign Oc represented the dog, but also carried associations with coyotes and the underworld journey of the sun through Xibalba.
