Salmon Spirit of Paiute

spirit · United States · North America

Not attested in Paiute folklore. No Paiute story records a salmon spirit; the Paiute's sacred and trickster beings are Coyote, the waterbaby, and the animal spirits of the Great Basin. Salmon did not inhabit their territory, and the figure is a modern invention.
