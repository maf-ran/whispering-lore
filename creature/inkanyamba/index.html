Inkanyamba

water-spirit · Eswatini · Southern Africa

Inkanyamba is a Zulu and Xhosa serpentine water spirit associated with Howick Falls (Umgeni River, KwaZulu-Natal). Its name means "snake cloud" — it causes tornadoes and seasonal storms. Described as a multi-headed serpent with horselike head, dorsal fins, and fish tail. Appeased for rain and fertility; snip angered by corrugated roofs mimicking water.
