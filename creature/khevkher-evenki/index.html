Khevkher

deity · Russia · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a distinct deity of Evenki folklore. No source confirms a creator or guardian named 'Khevkher'; the Evenki supreme creator is Buga, with shamanic spirit masters of forest and river, but no figure by this name appears in ethnographic literature. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
