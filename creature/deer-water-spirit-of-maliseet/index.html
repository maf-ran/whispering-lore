Deer Water-Spirit of Maliseet

water-spirit · Canada · North America

In Maliseet tradition from the Maritime provinces of Canada, the deer water-spirit is a guardian of mountain springs and highland lakes. The Maliseet (Wolastoqiyik) people of the Wabanaki Confederacy hold deep spiritual connections to the waterways that define their traditional territory, including the Saint John River watershed. Deer water-spirits are believed to inhabit the mountain sources of these rivers, protecting the purity of the water that flows downstream to the villages.
