Giant Cloud-swallower

giant · United States · North America

Häki Suto, the Giant Cloud-Swallower, is a Zuni ogre who brought drought by drinking the clouds from the sky, so the rains failed and the people starved. In Zuni folk tales the War Twins pursued and slew him, freeing the clouds so the waters returned to the land, explaining drought and the restoration of rain through the twins' heroism.
