Raven Giant of Passamaquoddy

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Passamaquoddy folklore. No raven giant appears in Passamaquoddy tradition, whose culture hero is Koluskap (Glooscap); ravens and crows play no major role, the notable birds being Culloo, the giant bird that carries off children, and the Petakiyik thunderers. This entry appears to be a modern fabrication.
