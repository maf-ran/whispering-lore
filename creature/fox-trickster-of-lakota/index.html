Fox Trickster of Lakota

trickster · United States · North America

The Fox Trickster of the Lakota people is a venerated spirit being that roams the hills and prairies of the Great Plains, serving as both a teacher and a cautionary figure. In Lakota cosmology, the fox is associated with keen observation, strategic thinking, and the ability to adapt to changing circumstances. This trickster appears in oral traditions passed down through generations, often in stories that explain natural phenomena or reinforce community values.
