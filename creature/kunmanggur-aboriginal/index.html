Kunmanggur

serpent · Australia · Pacific

Kunmanggur is the Rainbow Serpent figure in the mythology of the Murinbata people of the Daly River region in the Northern Territory, Australia. As a powerful creator being, Kunmanggur is associated with the waterholes, rivers, and rain that sustain life in the region. The serpent plays a central role in the Murinbata understanding of the landscape, with its movements creating the waterways and geological features of the Daly River area.
