Eintal

water-spirit · Norway · Nordic

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Norwegian water-spirit named 'eintal' appears in folklore collections, and the proposed ein plus tal ('lone song') etymology is invented. The genuine lake-dwelling musician who enchants listeners with fiddle music is the nokken, and Telemark accounts of such a being belong to that figure.
