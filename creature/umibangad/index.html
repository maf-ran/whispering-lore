Umibangad

bird · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in documented Philippine folklore; searches locate no omen bird called 'Umibangad.' The recorded huge, owl-like night bird whose cry heralds death or disaster fits the attested Philippine omen-bird tradition of the tigmamanukan, whose song foretells fortune, and the owl-like kulawo, but no entity bears this name.
