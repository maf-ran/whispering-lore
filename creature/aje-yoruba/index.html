Aje

spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Aje is the Yoruba orisha of wealth, prosperity, and economic success. Also known as Ajé Shaluga or Aje Olokun (associated with the deity of the ocean, Olokun), Aje represents the spiritual force behind financial abundance and commercial success. Aje is particularly associated with the marketplace, trade, and business acumen. Those who honor Aje through proper rituals and ethical business practices are blessed with prosperity. Aje is not merely material wealth but the spiritual principle of abundance itself — the concept that wealth flows through proper spiritual alignment. Markets in Yorubalan
