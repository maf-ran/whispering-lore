Raven Qwaxw Nuxalk

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Raven, called Qwaxw in Nuxalk, is the culture hero of Bella Coola mythology, a revered transformer who shaped the world for the people. Benevolent in his great acts, he is also a trickster, and many Nuxalk stories tell of his frivolous, ill-considered behavior landing him in trouble.
