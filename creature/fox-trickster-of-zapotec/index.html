Fox Trickster of Zapotec

trickster · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Zapotec oral tradition of Oaxaca includes humorous tales of clever animals, among them the fox, told at community gatherings as warnings against vanity and greed. The entry contrasts a playful fox trickster with a solemn fox spirit; while both moods exist in regional tales, the specific division and the cave-dwelling framing are the editor's synthesis, not a fixed figure.
