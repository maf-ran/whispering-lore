Bear Guardian of Cree

guardian · Canada · North America

In Cree (Nehiyaw) tradition the bear is one of the most powerful animal beings, honored as a teacher, healer, and source of medicine power who shares knowledge of medicinal plants and hunting with those who approach with humility and respect. Regarded as a relative who walks between worlds, the bear guides dreamers and vision-fasters, revealing life paths during vision quests.
