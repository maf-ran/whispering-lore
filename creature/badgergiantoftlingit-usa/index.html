Badger Giant of Tlingit

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Tlingit source records a badger giant; the badger is not a crest animal, and the documented giant-spirit category 'Nah' includes no badger figure. Tlingit crest animals are the Raven, Eagle, Wolf, Bear, and Killer Whale.
