Dangun

monster · South Korea · East Asia and Pacific

Dangun Wanggeom is the legendary founder of Gojoseon, the first Korean kingdom, and the central figure of Korea's national origin myth. According to the Samguk Yusa (13th-century Korean chronicle), Dangun was born from the union of Hwanung, the heavenly prince who descended to Mount Taebaek, and Ungnyeo, a bear who transformed into a woman after enduring a grueling trial of garlic and mugwort in a cave for one hundred days.
