Iara da Praiana

water-spirit · Brazil · South America

Iara da Praiana is a Brazilian coastal variant of the famous Iara legend, specifically associated with the seashores and coastal waters rather than the freshwater rivers of the Amazon. While the Amazonian Iara is a freshwater mermaid who lures fishermen, this saltwater variant sings to sailors and navigators along Brazil's extensive coastline. The Iara da Praiana represents the adaptation of the indigenous Tupi legend to coastal environments, blending elements of European mermaid mythology with local Brazilian folklore.
