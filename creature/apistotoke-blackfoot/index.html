Apistotoke

creator-deity · United States · North America

Apistotoke (also known as Ihtsipatapiyohpa, 'Source of Life') is the great creator god of the Blackfoot people. A divine spirit with no human form or attributes, Apistotoke is entirely gender-neutral and never personified in traditional folklore. He created the Sky Beings (Naato'si the Sun, Ko'komiki'somm the Moon, and the stars), then Ksahkomitapi (Earth) as mother to all creation. Na'pi (Old Man) was created as the first man and demigod who shaped the world. Apistotoke takes a minimal role in folklore, with Na'pi and Kipitaakii performing the hands-on work of creation and teaching
