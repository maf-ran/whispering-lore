Badger Giant of Tlingit

giant · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not a Tlingit crest animal, and no Tlingit badger giant is documented. Tlingit clan crests belong to the Raven and Eagle moieties (Wolf, Bear, Killer Whale), and 'Nah' refers to giant spirits, but no source connects these to a badger.
