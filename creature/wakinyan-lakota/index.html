Wakinyan Lakota

spirit · United States · North America

Wakinyan is the Thunderer of the Lakota, a powerful sky spirit that dwells in the west and appears as a giant bird whose wingbeats roll thunder and whose eye blinks lightning. Wakinyan brings the life-giving rains and, as the enemy of the evil water monsters, keeps the world in balance.
