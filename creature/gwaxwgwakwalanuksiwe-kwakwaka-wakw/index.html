Gwaxwgwakwalanuksiwe Kwakwaka Wakw

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Gwaxwgwakwalanuksiwe' is the man-eating raven of the Kwakwaka'wakw Hamatsa, the most prestigious role among the supernatural man-eating birds that serve the Man-Eater at the North End of the World. As a servant of the cannibal spirit, his masked dance holds the highest station of the Cannibal Society's winter ceremony.
