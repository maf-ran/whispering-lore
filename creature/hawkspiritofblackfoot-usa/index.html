Hawk Spirit of Blackfoot

spirit · United States · North America

The Hawk Spirit of the Blackfoot people in Canada carries the same sacred significance as in the American context, but with particular emphasis on the vast prairie landscapes of southern Alberta and Saskatchewan. Among the Blackfoot Confederacy's Canadian members, particularly the Kainai, Siksika, and Piikani nations, the Hawk Spirit is intimately connected to the seasonal rhythms of the northern plains.
