Abonsam

spirit · Ghana · West Africa

Abonsam is a malevolent spirit from the traditional beliefs of the Akan people of Ghana and the Gold Coast region of West Africa. The name derives from Akan terms denoting evil thrown about in disorder, referring to a malevolent force that embodies fundamental opposition to moral and social harmony. Unlike physical forest monsters such as the Sasabonsam, Abonsam is an intangible, spectral presence that manifests through subtle yet ominous phenomena.
