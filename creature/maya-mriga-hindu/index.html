Maya Mriga (Golden Deer)

monster · India · South Asia

The Maya Mriga (Golden Deer) is a pivotal illusionary creature from the Ramayana, created by the rakshasa Maricha at Ravana's command. To enable Ravana's abduction of Sita, Maricha transformed himself into a mesmerizing golden deer with silver spots and jewel-like horns that appeared to be made of precious metals and gems. The deer's magical beauty was so captivating that Sita, seeing it near their forest hermitage, asked Rama to capture it for her.
