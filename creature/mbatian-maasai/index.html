Mbatian

culture-hero · Kenya · East Africa

Mbatian was the most powerful laibon (spiritual leader/prophet) in Maasai history, a legendary figure of the late 19th century descended from the sacred Inkindongi clan (Oloiboni clan). His prophetic powers were unrivaled -- he could predict droughts, locate lost cattle, communicate directly with Enkai, and control the weather. Mbatian served as the supreme spiritual authority for the Maasai during a period of great upheaval. As he grew old, his younger son Lenana tricked a messenger and usurped his elder brother Senteu's birthright, sparking a rivalry that divided the Maasai. Mbatian died aro
