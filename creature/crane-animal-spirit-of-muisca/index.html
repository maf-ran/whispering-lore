Crane Animal-Spirit of Muisca

animal-spirit · Colombia · South America

The Crane Animal-Spirit of the Muisca people emerges from the high-altitude wetlands and sacred lakes of the Altiplano Cundiboyacense in the Colombian Andes. The Muisca, renowned for their sophisticated goldwork and the legend of El Dorado, venerated water birds as messengers between the earthly realm and the spirit world. In Muisca cosmology, the crane is associated with the goddess Chía (the Moon) and the sacred lakes of Guatavita, Iguaque, and Tota, where the spirits of ancestors dwell beneath the dark waters.
