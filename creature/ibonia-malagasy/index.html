Ibonia

hero · Madagascar · East Africa

Ibonia (Iboniamasiboniamanoro, 'He of the Clear and Captivating Glance') is the great epic hero of Madagascar. Conceived through divine intervention, he began speaking from his mother's womb, demanding betrothal to Iampelasoamananoro ('Girl of Grace'). After birth, Girl of Grace was abducted by Ravato ('Stone Man'). Ibonia defeated the Great Echo in a verbal duel, fought powerful animals including the Crocodile and the Bull, and disguised himself in an old man's skin to infiltrate Stone Man's village.
