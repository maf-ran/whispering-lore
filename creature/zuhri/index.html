Zuhri

chimera · Morocco · North Africa

The zuhri is a being of Moroccan belief, born of a human mother and a jinn father, who can see buried treasure beneath the earth. Marked by a slanting gaze, the zuhri is sought by treasure hunters, while the treasures themselves are said to strike the greedy dead. The belief is so potent that it has been invoked in real, violent crimes.
