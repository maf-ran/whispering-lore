Skan

deity · United States · North America

Skan (Taku Skanskan) is the sky spirit and supreme judge in Lakota cosmology as recorded by James R. Walker. Born of the waters that Inyan could not hold, Skan sets motion and law for all things, arbitrates among the four primary Wakan Tanka spirits, and judges souls after death. He is associated with the blue of the sky.
