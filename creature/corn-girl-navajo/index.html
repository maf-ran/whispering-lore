Corn Girl

deity · United States · North America

Corn Girl is a Navajo Holy Person associated with the sacred mountain Tsoodził (Mount Taylor), the southern boundary mountain of the Navajo world set with turquoise and female rain. She dwells there with Turquoise Boy, guarding corn and its growth, and stands with them as an emblem of fertility and the Navajo corn-growing cycle.
