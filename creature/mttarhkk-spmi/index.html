Máttaráhkká

goddess · Sweden · Nordic

Máttaráhkká is a goddess from Sápmi mythology. She lives in the ground beneath the goahti, opposite the door in the maadtere (women's sacred space). She is the great ancestress who receives the human soul from Radien-attje and gives it life before passing it to her daughter Sáráhkká who places it in the womb. She makes female humans and animals fertile. Her name literally means 'origin woman' or 'ancestral mother.'
