Egungun

ancestor-spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Egungun are the collective spirits of the ancestors in Yoruba religion, manifested through masked masquerades in Nigeria and the African diaspora. During egungun festivals, costumed performers channel ancestral presence, offering blessings, guidance, and judgment to the living. The tradition preserves genealogical memory and honors the continuing role of the dead in community life.
