Teke Teke

ghost · Japan · Asia

Teke Teke (Unknown). is a ghost. Teke Teke is a modern Japanese urban legend about the ghost of a woman who was cut in half by a train. She drags herself on her elbows through the streets, making a "teke teke" sound, and will cut her. Source: Japanese urban legend; contemporary folklore
