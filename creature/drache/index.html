Drache

dragon · Germany · Germanic

Drache is the German word for dragon. In Germanic folklore the Drache is a fire-breathing serpent or reptile guarding treasure in mountains, caves, and forests, with regional forms such as the Lindwurm. In the Nibelungenlied, Siegfried slays the dragon and bathes in its blood to become invulnerable.
