Bigorn

serpent · Seychelles · East Africa

'Bigorn' is not attested as a treasure-guarding serpent in Seychellois tradition. The name recalls La Bigorne, a beast of medieval French folktale, and an eighteenth-century corporal in the history of Ile Sainte-Marie; the modern 'treasure snake' framing appears in tourist guides and derivative retellings. Genuine Seychellois lore preserves figures such as Tizan, but this serpent is unattested.
