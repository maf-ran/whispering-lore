Deer Trickster (Mixtec)

trickster · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In Mixtec tradition from Oaxaca, Mexico, the deer trickster is a complex figure that embodies both the sacred and the mischievous aspects of the forest. Mixtec codices, which were painted on deer hide in an accordion-like format, frequently depict deer in mythological scenes alongside gods, rulers, and supernatural events. The deer trickster uses its intelligence and knowledge of the forest to outwit larger, stronger opponents, teaching lessons about cunning over brute force. It is associated with the wind that moves through the trees, carrying secrets and whispers between the worlds of humans
