Bison Nature-Spirit (Aztec)

nature-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The entry itself admits the spirit is a post-contact syncretic invention. Real Aztec nature powers — the tlaloqueh rain/earth spirits and Xipe Totec's renewal rites — are documented, but no bison nature-spirit appears in the Florentine Codex or other sources.
