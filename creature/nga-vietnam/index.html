Ông Địa

nature-spirit · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Ông Địa is the earth god of the Vietnamese household, depicted as a jovial bald old man with a long beard who resides beneath the home, in gardens, or fields. He protects the family from misfortune and brings prosperity in exchange for offerings and reverence. His cult blends Buddhist practice with indigenous folk religion, and he is among the most widely honored domestic deities in Vietnam.
