Cougar Water-Spirit of Zapotec

water-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In Zapotec cosmology from Oaxaca, Mexico, the cougar water-spirit is associated with underground water sources and cavern systems that characterize the region's karst topography. The Zapotec people, whose civilization flourished at Monte Alban and Mitla, believed that underground water was controlled by powerful spirits connected to the earth deity Cocijo, the god of rain and lightning. This cougar water-spirit operates in the liminal space between the surface world and the subterranean water systems, serving as a guardian of the hidden rivers that flow beneath the valleys of Oaxaca.
