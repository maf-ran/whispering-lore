Mitsina (Hermit)

spirit · United States · North America

Mítsina is the Hermit of Zuni folklore, who dwelt all alone in the Canyon of the Pines southeast of Zuni. Because he was unwise and evil in his ways, the gods bade him live apart until, through much happening, he should become worthy to dwell among them; hunters passing by heard the sounds of his solitary gaming.
