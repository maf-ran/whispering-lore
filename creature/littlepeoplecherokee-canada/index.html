Little People (Cherokee)

fairy · Canada · North America

Not attested as a being of Canadian folklore. The Cherokee Little People (Yûñwï Tsunsdi') belong to the Cherokee of the southeastern United States, whose homeland is the Southern Appalachians, not Canada. This record duplicates littlepeoplecherokee-usa with a mistaken country field; Canada's own traditions feature distinct little-folk such as the Anishinaabe little people.
