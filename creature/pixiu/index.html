Pixiu

guardian · China · East Asia and Pacific

Pixiu (貔貅) is an auspicious chimera of Chinese mythology, usually a winged, lion-like beast with a dragon's head, sometimes split into male (pí) and female (xiū). Because it feeds on wealth and has no anus, it is believed to gather and hold fortune without releasing it, making it a popular talisman for business and prosperity, worn as jade pendants and placed in homes and offices.
