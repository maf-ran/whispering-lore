Panther Trickster (Laotian)

trickster · Laos · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named figure of Lao folklore; searches locate no panther trickster in Lao tradition. Lao oral literature features animal tales, such as the crow-and-peacock legend, and the clever-human trickster Xieng Mieng, but no panther deity or trickster appears in documented sources, marking this entry as an invented name.
