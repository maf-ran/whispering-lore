Wolf Guardian of Muisca

guardian · Colombia · South America

The 'Wolf Guardian of Muisca' is not attested in Muisca tradition. Wolves never existed in South America. Muisca guardianship is associated with Bochica (civilizer hero), the zipa/zaque rulers, and the sacred lakes (Guatavita, Iguaque). No wolf figures appear in Muisca mythology documented by Ocampo López.
