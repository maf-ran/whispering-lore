Wildcat Giant of Quechua

giant · Peru · South America

Not independently attested in Quechua/Inca tradition. Inca/Quechua sacred animals: condor, puma, serpent. No wildcat giant in Huarochirí Manuscript, Viracocha myths, or Inka bestiary. Andean wildcats (pampas cat, Andean cat) exist but hold no giant mythic status. Entity appears fabricated.
