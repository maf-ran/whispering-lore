Cougar Giant of Kikuyu

giant · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Cougars are not native to Africa, and no Kikuyu tradition describes giant spirit-cats guarding the lakes of Mount Kenya. Kikuyu cosmology centers on Ngai, the supreme god of Kirinyaga; the marozi, a spotted-lion cryptid of the Aberdares, is a separate and unverified notion.
