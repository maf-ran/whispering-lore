The Forest Phantom

spirit · Uganda · East Africa

Not independently attested as a named entity in Ugandan folklore. 'Forest Phantom' / 'Green Shadow' / 'Way-Wanderer' appears to be a generic compilation of widespread Ugandan forest trickster motifs (hare/rabbit, leopard, mimicry spirits in Bwindi).
