Deer Animal-Spirit of Guarani

animal-spirit · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Guarani tradition. While teko porã ('the good way of being') is a genuine Guarani ethical concept and deer appear in South American hunting lore, no source records a deer animal-spirit as a pan-Guarani nature guardian. The entry generalizes a common ecological teaching into a specific being that the Guarani corpora (Cadogán, Colmán) do not name.
