Qals Mink Coast Salish

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Mink is the trickster of the Coast Salish, told of among the Saanich, Comox, and Lummi. Compared with Raven he is a negative hero, greedy, reckless, and vain, and his adventures — being swallowed by a whale, running across the sky as the Sun's son, tussling with Devilfish — are cautionary tales of how not to behave.
