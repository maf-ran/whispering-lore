Sinti Lapitta

serpent · United States · North America

Sinti Lapitta is the great horned serpent of Choctaw tradition, a giant water snake with two horns on its head, in some accounts a panther head, that lives in the deep waters. It possesses great power and wisdom and was said to visit unusually wise young men, granting them knowledge and strength.
