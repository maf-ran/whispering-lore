Berggeist

nature-spirit · Liechtenstein · Germanic

Berggeist ('mountain spirit') is a general term in Germanic and Alpine tradition for spirits who dwell in and guard mountains, most famously the Bergmonch, a monk-like figure seen in mines and high passes. These spirits reward respectful miners with ore and punish greed and carelessness. The Ratikon and Liechtenstein settings of the entry are local color on a wider Alpine motif.
