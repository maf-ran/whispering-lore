Long

dragon · China · East Asia and Pacific

Long (龍) is the Chinese dragon: a benevolent rain-bringer with a long serpentine body, antlers, four legs, and the power to fly without wings while controlling water, clouds, and weather. It dwells in rivers, lakes, seas, and sky, symbolizing imperial power, yang energy, and good fortune. Dragon dances, dragon-boat races, and rain prayers honor it.
