Miraculous Well of Sidi Bou Said

spirit · Tunisia · North Africa

The Miraculous Well of Sidi Bou Said is a legendary blessed water source in the famous blue-and-white Tunisian town of Sidi Bou Said, near Tunis. According to local legend, the well was blessed by Sidi Bou Said (also known as Abu Said al-Baji), a 13th-century Sufi saint who lived and taught in the area. The well is said to possess miraculous healing powers, with locals and visitors alike claiming that drinking water from it can cure various ailments, from simple headaches to more serious diseases.
