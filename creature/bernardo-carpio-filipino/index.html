Bernardo Carpio

giant · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Bernardo Carpio is the Filipino equivalent of Hercules — a legendary giant with superhuman strength believed to be trapped between two great rocks in the mountains of Montalban (Rizal province). According to folklore, he was a Spanish-Filipino of immense size and power who single-handedly fought entire armies. When the Spanish authorities could not defeat him, they tricked him into a cave where a magical spell trapped him between two massive boulders. Every time he struggles to free himself, the earth trembles, causing earthquakes in the Philippines. The legend became a powerful symbol of Fili
