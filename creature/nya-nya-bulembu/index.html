Nya-nya Bulembu

monster · Eswatini · Southern Africa

Nya-nya Bulembu is a Swazi (Eswatini) monster haunting deep pools, described as a massive moss-green beast with fur like living seaweed. It preys on the careless, transforms victims into monsters, and punishes ritual violations. The name suggests a pool-dwelling entity in siSwati. Limited documentation outside local Swazi folklore references.
