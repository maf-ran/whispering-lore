Wildcat Deity (Maya)

deity · United States · North America

This entry is wrong on two counts: the Maya are a Mesoamerican people, not of the modern United States, and their feline deity is the jaguar, not a generic 'wildcat.' The Jaguar God of the Underworld, associated with the night sun, caves, and the underworld, is a central Maya deity. This record appears to be a jaguar entry garbled and mislabeled as 'wildcat' and 'USA.'
