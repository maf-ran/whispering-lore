Fox Trickster of Creek

trickster · United States · North America

The Fox Trickster of the Creek (Muskogee) nation is a revered figure in Southeastern Native American folklore, known for his clever schemes and transformative encounters with humans. In Creek tradition, the fox is considered one of the most intelligent beings in the animal world, possessing knowledge of hidden springs, medicinal roots, and the language of birds. This trickster figure appears in stories told during winter months, teaching children and adults alike about the consequences of greed, dishonesty, and disrespect toward nature.
