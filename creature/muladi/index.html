Muladi

demon · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a distinct entity in Brazilian folklore. The name resembles Afro-Brazilian entities (Exu, Pombagira) but no ethnographic source documents 'Muladi' as a specific demon. Likely a conflation of 'mulata' or a garbled Exu epithet.
