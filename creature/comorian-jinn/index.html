Comorian Jinn (Mashetani)

jinn · Comoros · East Africa

In the Comoros Islands, belief in jinn (locally called mashetani) is deeply ingrained in the culture, representing a syncretic blend of Islamic theology with indigenous African and Malagasy spirit beliefs. Comorian jinn are invisible spirits created from smokeless fire who inhabit a parallel world to humans. They are believed to dwell in specific locations such as ancient baobab trees, volcanic craters, coral reefs, and abandoned wells.
