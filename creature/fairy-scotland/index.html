Fairy

nature-spirit · Scotland · Celtic

Scottish fairy tradition encompasses the Seelie and Unseelie Courts, trooping fairies (Sídhe), and solitary spirits like the brownie, glaistig, and each-uisge. Not a single being but a class of nature spirits inhabiting knolls (sídhe), lochs, and glens.
