Kallikantzaros

goblin · Greece · Mediterranean

The Kallikantzaros is a malevolent goblin-like creature from Greek folklore believed to dwell underground for most of the year, tirelessly sawing at the great tree that holds up the earth. These mischievous demons emerge onto the surface during the twelve days of Christmas, from December 25 to January 6, when they run rampant through villages causing chaos and destruction.
