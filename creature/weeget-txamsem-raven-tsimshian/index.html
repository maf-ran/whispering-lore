Weeget Txamsem Raven Tsimshian

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Weeget Txamsem is the raven trickster-creator of the Tsimshian, a shapeshifter whose voracious appetite drives his adventures. He steals the sun to light the world, releases the first salmon, and orders the tides and seasons, while his greed and mischief teach the lessons of the adaawx.
