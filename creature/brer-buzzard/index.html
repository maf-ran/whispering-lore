Br'er Buzzard

trickster · United States · North America

Br'er Buzzard is a trickster character in African American folklore, particularly in Gullah Geechee traditions. As a buzzard (vulture), he is associated with death, transformation, and the sacred. In Gullah belief, the buzzard is not merely a carrion eater but a sacred bird that carries souls back to Africa. This connects directly to the Flying Africans legend, where the Igbo captives who drowned at Dunbar Creek were said to have transformed into buzzards and flown home. Br'er Buzzard's high-soaring perspective makes him a witness to events that others miss
