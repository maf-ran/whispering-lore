Arọ̀ni

forest-spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Arọ̀ni is a Yoruba forest spirit known as "one having a withered limb," credited with teaching herbal medicine while guarding the secrets of Osain, the orisha of healing and herbs. Often shown as a small bird or animal, Arọ̀ni is a powerful and mysterious presence who aids only those who show proper respect to the forest and its pharmacopoeia.
