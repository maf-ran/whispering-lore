Turtle Nature-Spirit of Muisca

nature-spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Muisca tradition as a named 'turtle nature-spirit guardian of swamps'. Muisca cosmology (Bochica, Chía, Sué) centers on solar/lunar deities and culture heroes; snip animal spirits are not prominently documented as named guardians. The turtle appears in some Chibcha art but not as a distinct spirit entity with this title.
