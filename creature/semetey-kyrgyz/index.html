Semetey

hero · Kyrgyzstan · Central Asia

Semetey is the son of the great hero Manas in the Kyrgyz epic trilogy: the second generation who must reclaim his father's legacy. Raised in hiding, he grows into a powerful warrior whose righteous fury is turned against the traitors who wronged his father, seeking vengeance while protecting his people across the Manas-Semetey-Seytek cycle.
