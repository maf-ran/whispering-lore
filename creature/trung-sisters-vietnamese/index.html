The Trung Sisters (Hai Bà Trưng)

hero · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

The Trung Sisters — Trưng Trắc and Trưng Nhị — are Vietnam's most celebrated legendary heroines who led a rebellion against Chinese Han occupation in 40-43 CE. Daughters of a Lạc lord in Mê Linh, they were educated in martial arts and military strategy by their father. When Trưng Trắc's husband Thi Sách was executed by Han officials for plotting rebellion, the sisters raised an army of over 80,000 soldiers, many of them women led by female generals.
