Ts Uu Jaas Cedar Woman Haida

human · Canada · North America

Ts'uu Jaas, the Cedar Woman, is a spirit of Haida tradition who is bound to the cedar tree. Cedar gave the Haida their homes, canoes, clothing, and totem poles, and Cedar Woman is honored as 'every woman's sister,' a reminder that the people and the tree live together in a relationship of use and gratitude.
