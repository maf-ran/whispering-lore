Talking Fox

animal-spirit · Sweden · Nordic

Not independently attested as a named entity in Nordic folklore. Talking foxes appear in fairy tales (ATU 370 'The Fox as Shepherd') and fables, but not as a recurring folkloric being with a specific name or role. The closest analogue is the fox as trickster in pan-European tales, not a Nordic-specific spirit.
