Deer Guardian (Mixtec)

guardian · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in South African tradition. The Mixtec are an Indigenous people of Oaxaca, Mexico. The description explicitly describes a Chilean pudu deer guardian of alerce forests in the Andes, copied from a Chilean/Mapuche context and mislabeled as South African/Mixtec. Fabricated cross-continental misattribution.
