Asdiwal Tsimshian

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Asdiwal is the hero of a celebrated Tsimshian myth studied by Claude Lévi-Strauss. Son of Ho, who made him magic snowshoes, Asdiwal chases a white bear up a ladder into the sky and finds it is the daughter of the Sun; he wins her through impossible trials and marries her, before his boastfulness brings him ruin.
