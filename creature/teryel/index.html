Teryel

ogre · Algeria · North Africa

Teryel is the most famous ogress in Kabyle (Algerian Amazigh) mythology — a terrifying female monster with an anthropophagic (man-eating) appetite. According to a foundational Kabyle myth, when humanity was created as fifty men and fifty women, 98 chose to settle and build houses, but one man and one woman chose to live in the wild. The man became a lion (izem) and the woman became Teryel, the first ogress. Teryel represents the untamed, wild feminine that rejects domesticity and social norms.
