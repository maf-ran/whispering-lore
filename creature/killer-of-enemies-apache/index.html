Killer-of-Enemies

spirit · United States · North America

Killer-of-Enemies is the chief culture hero of the Lipan and other Apache peoples, son of the Sun and of White Painted Woman (Changing Woman), and brother of Child-of-the-Water, son of the Moon. With his brother he slew the monsters that plagued the people and taught the skills of survival; in Lipan tradition he is identified with the Sun himself.
