Huldufolk

nature-spirit · Iceland · Europe

Huldufolk (Unknown). is a nature-spirit. The Huldufólk ("hidden people") are elf-like beings in Icelandic folklore who live in rocks and hills. They are nearly identical to humans but invisible to most people, living parallel lives in the Ic. Source: Icelandic folklore; contemporary Icelandic tradition
