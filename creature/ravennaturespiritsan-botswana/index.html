Raven Nature-Spirit (San)

nature-spirit · Botswana · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in San (Khoisan) tradition of Botswana. While San folklore includes avian spirits (e.g., ostrich, vulture in /Xam narratives documented by Bleek & snip Lloyd), a specific "Raven Nature-Spirit of the valley" lacks verification. Ravens are rare in the Kalahari; snip the entry appears templated.
