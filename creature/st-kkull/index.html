Stökkull

sea-monster · Iceland · Nordic

Stökkull (the Jumper) is the most feared of all the illhveli (evil whales) in Icelandic folklore, despite not being the largest. Stökkull has a distinctive rounded body, dark above and white below, 8-20 meters long, with a battering-ram snout. Its most remarkable feature is the flaps of skin (blinders) that cover its eyes — it must leap completely out of the water to see. According to Navigatio Sancti Brendani, Saint Brendan prayed to God to give Stökkull these blinders to limit its destructive power.
