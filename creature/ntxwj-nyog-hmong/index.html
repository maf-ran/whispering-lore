Ntxwj Nyog (Spirit of Death and Sickness)

spirit · Laos · Southeast Asia

Ntxwj Nyog (also Ndu Nyong or Ntxwj Nyug) is the primordial spirit of sickness, death, and evil in Hmong shamanic mythology. According to Hmong creation myth, Ntxwj Nyog emerged from an unburned egg when the original creators failed to properly destroy all evil in the world. He descended to earth and began devouring human souls, causing sickness and death. The high god Yawm Saub sent the first shaman Siv Yis to combat Ntxwj Nyog, beginning an eternal struggle between the shaman and the death spirit.
