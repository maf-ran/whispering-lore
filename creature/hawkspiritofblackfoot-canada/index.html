Hawk Spirit of Blackfoot

spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Blackfoot (Niitsitapi) source documents a 'Hawk Spirit' guiding people to buffalo or water; the description is generic filler ending with an empty 'In Canada folklore' clause. Blackfoot traditions do feature the beaver in creation narratives, but a 'Hawk Spirit' as described is unattested.
