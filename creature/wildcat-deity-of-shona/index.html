Wildcat Deity of Shona

deity · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Shona tradition. The entry claims a "wildcat deity" guarding oceans — but Zimbabwe is landlocked. African wildcats (Felis lybica) exist but no Shona source documents a wildcat ocean deity. Fabricated templated entry with "guardian of oceans" motif misapplied to landlocked country.
