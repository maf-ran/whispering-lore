Tui Delaivugalei

deity · Fiji · Pacific

Tui Delaivugalei is a significant Fijian deity associated with fishing, the sea, and abundance. As a sea god and guardian of fishermen, he presides over the bounty of the ocean and ensures successful catches for those who show proper respect. Offerings and rituals were traditionally made to Tui Delaivugalei before fishing expeditions, particularly important voyages to deep-sea fishing grounds. He is part of the complex pantheon of Fijian sea deities that govern the relationship between coastal communities and the ocean.
