Cat Sídhe

fairy · Ireland · Celtic

The Cat Sídhe, also known as Cat-sìth in Scottish Gaelic, is a fairy creature from Celtic mythology that resembles a large spectral black cat with a distinctive white spot on its chest. This being spans both Scottish and Irish folklore, though it is more prominent in Scottish Highland traditions. Unlike ordinary fairy creatures, the Cat Sídhe occupies an ambiguous space between fairy, witch, and omen of death, reflecting the complex relationship between the Celtic otherworld and the mundane realm.
