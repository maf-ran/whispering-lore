Hawk Deity of Ute

deity · United States · North America

Among the Ute people of the Colorado Plateau and Great Basin regions, the Hawk Deity stands as a powerful spiritual being closely associated with vision, protection, and the sacred mountains. Ute traditions teach that hawks serve as messengers between the human world and the Creator Sinawav, carrying prayers upward on their soaring flights. The Ute people have always lived in the mountains of Colorado and Utah, and they believe that the Hawk Deity watches over them from the high peaks, particularly around sacred sites like Bears Ears and the La Sal Mountains.
