Coyote Ancestor-Spirit (Muisca)

ancestor-spirit · Colombia · South America

In the rich tapestry of cross-cultural folklore, the coyote ancestor-spirit of the Muisca tradition finds an unexpected parallel in East African oral narratives, where trickster figures like the hare and jackal hold similar roles. This variant represents the universal archetype of the cunning ancestor-spirit that exists across continents, adapted into Kenyan storytelling traditions through centuries of trade and cultural exchange across the Atlantic. Like the Muisca original that guards the boundary between worlds, this spirit patrols the liminal spaces between the savanna and the spirit realm
