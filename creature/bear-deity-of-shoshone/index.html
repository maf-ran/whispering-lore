Bear Deity of Shoshone

deity · United States · North America

Among the Shoshone people of the Great Basin and Rocky Mountains, the bear is revered as a powerful spiritual being with deep connections to the landscape and healing traditions. The Shoshone, ranging across Nevada, Utah, Idaho, and Wyoming, encountered grizzly and black bears throughout their vast territory and incorporated the animal into their mythology as a being of great medicine power. The bear deity is particularly associated with the Wind River Mountains of Wyoming, where Shoshone medicine people went on vision quests seeking bear spirit guidance.
