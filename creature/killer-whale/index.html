Killer Whale

spirit · Canada · North America

The Killer Whale, known as Sgaana in the Haida language, is a profoundly significant supernatural being in Haida mythology and the broader Indigenous folklore of the Pacific Northwest coast of Canada. These majestic creatures are not mere animals but are considered powerful spirit beings, the rulers of the ocean, and the ancestors of the Haida people transformed into their present form. In Haida tradition, Killer Whales live in immense underwater villages organized just like human communities, with houses, chiefs, and potlatch ceremonies conducted beneath the waves.
