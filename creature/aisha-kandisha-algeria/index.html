Aisha Kandisha

spirit · Algeria · North Africa

Aisha Kandisha is the most feared jinn in Algerian and North African folklore, a beautiful but deadly female water spirit of Berber origin. She is described as a stunningly beautiful woman from the waist up but with goat or camel legs, carrion hands, and poultry claws. Her skin is said to be extremely white, with long black or red hair that flows down to her hips. Aisha Kandisha is associated with water sources including rivers, springs, wells, and the sea, and emerges at night to seduce and kill men who disrespect women or venture alone into desolate areas.
