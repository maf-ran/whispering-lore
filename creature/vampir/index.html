Vampir

vampire · Serbia · Slavic

Vampir is the Serbian vampire — the etymological root of the English word 'vampire'. An animated corpse rising from the grave at night to drink blood of the living. Created by suicide, excommunication, caul birth, or animal jumping over corpse. The 18th-century cases of Petar Blagojevich and Arnold Paole sparked the European vampire controversy. Warded by garlic, crosses, holy water.
