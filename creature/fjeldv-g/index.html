Fjeldvīg

giant · Denmark · Nordic

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Fjeldvig is a Norwegian place-name and surname, not a Danish folklore being, and the description grafts generic bjergfolk (hill-folk) lore onto the name. Genuine Danish mound-folk belong to the bjergfolk complex related to the nisse and trold; no being called Fjeldvig is recorded.
