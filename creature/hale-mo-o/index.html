Hale (Mo'o)

serpent · Hawaii · Pacific

Hale is a mo'o, a powerful water lizard spirit from Native Hawaiian mythology, revered as an 'aumakua (ancestral guardian spirit) associated with freshwater sources across the Hawaiian Islands. Mo'o are amphibious reptilian deities that embody both the life-giving and death-dealing properties of water, living primarily in fishponds, freshwater pools, waterfalls, and streams. They can shapeshift into monstrous reptiles, tiny geckos, or stunningly beautiful human women.
