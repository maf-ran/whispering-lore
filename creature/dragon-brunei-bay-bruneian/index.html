Naga Berani (Dragon of Brunei Bay)

dragon · Brunei · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Brunei legend names a 'Naga Berani' guarding Brunei Bay, and the celestial-guardian and sunken-kingdom details are unattested. Naga serpent-dragon motifs are real across Malay folklore and appear in Brunei's epic of Awang Semaun, but not as this specific bay dragon.
