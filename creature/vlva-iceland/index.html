Völva Iceland (Unknown). is a witch. A Völva was a female seer and shaman in Norse and Germanic tradition who practiced seiðr magic and prophesied the future. The most famous völva recounts the creation and end of the world in the Völusp. Source: Poetic Edda; Norse sagas; archaeological evidence
