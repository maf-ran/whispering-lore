Dokkaebi

spirit · North Korea · East Asia and Pacific

Dokkaebi are goblin-like spirits of Korean folklore, born from old household objects imbued with spiritual energy. Neither wholly good nor evil, they reward kindness and punish greed, wield the wish-granting bangmangi club, excel at ssireum wrestling, and can be warded off by horse blood or a hanging sieve.
