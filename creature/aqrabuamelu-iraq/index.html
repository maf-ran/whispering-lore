Aqrabuamelu

hybrid · Iraq · Middle East and North Africa

Aqrabuamelu (also known as Girtablilu) is a hybrid creature from Mesopotamian mythology combining the torso, arms, and head of a human with the lower body, legs, and tail of a scorpion. These scorpion-people appear most famously in the Epic of Gilgamesh, where a pair of them, a male and a female, guard the entrance to the Mashu Mountains—the gateway through which the sun god Shamash passes each day and the threshold to the underworld realm of Kurnugi.
