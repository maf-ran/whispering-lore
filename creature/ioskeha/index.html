Ioskeha

demigod · United States · North America

Ioskeha is the Good Twin and culture hero of Huron (Wyandot) tradition. With his evil brother Tawiscaron he shaped the world after their mother Aataentsic's death; Ioskeha created the sun and moon, slew monsters, and gave the people corn, tobacco and the arts of living. He is the model of order, light and benevolent leadership.
