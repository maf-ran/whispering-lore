La Garita del Diablo (The Devil's Sentry Box)

folk-hero · Puerto Rico · Caribbean

La Garita del Diablo (The Devil Sentry Box) is the most famous supernatural legend of Old San Juan, Puerto Rico. The story centers on a solitary sentry box (garita) at Castillo San Cristobal, perched on a rocky cliff above the crashing Atlantic. In the late 1600s, a Spanish soldier named Sanchez, nicknamed Flor de Azahar (Orange Blossom) for his pale white skin, was assigned to the farthest, most isolated sentry box.
