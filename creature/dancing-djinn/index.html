Dancing Djinn

jinn · Tunisia · North Africa

Dancing Djinn are playful supernatural spirits from North African folklore, particularly associated with the vast desert landscapes of Tunisia and the Maghreb region. Djinn (or jinn) are beings of smokeless fire created alongside humans and angels in Islamic cosmology, possessing free will and the ability to choose good or evil. These particular djinn are known for their love of music, dance, and revelry under the moonlight.
