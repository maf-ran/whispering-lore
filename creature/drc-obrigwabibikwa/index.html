Obrigwabibikwa

shapeshifter · Democratic Republic of the Congo · Central Africa

Obrigwabibikwa is a mystical dwarf and shapeshifter from the mythology of the Mbuti Pygmy people of the Democratic Republic of Congo. This enigmatic figure possesses the ability to transform into a reptile, most commonly a lizard, which holds sacred significance in Mbuti cosmology. The lizard is considered the divine messenger of Khonvoum, the supreme creator god of the Mbuti, who uses these creatures to communicate with the mortal world.
