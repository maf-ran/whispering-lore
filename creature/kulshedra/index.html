Kulshedra

dragon · Albania · Balkan

Kulshedra is a colossal multi-headed female dragon from Albanian folklore, one of the most feared and powerful creatures in Balkan mythology. This monstrous serpentine being is associated with water, storms, and fire, commanding the primal forces of nature and bringing either drought or flood to the land depending on her mood. Kulshedra is the ancient enemy of the drangue, a heroic winged warrior who protects humanity from her depredations.
