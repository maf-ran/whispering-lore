Kilimanjaro Mountain Serpent

serpent · Tanzania · East Africa

The Mountain Serpent is a legendary guardian creature of Kilimanjaro in Chaga mythology, dwelling in caves and tunnels beneath the mountain. According to tradition, the serpent was placed by Ruwa to guard the sacred inner chambers where ancestral spirits gather. The serpent is as old as the mountain itself, its body coiling through volcanic tunnels honeycombing Kilimanjaro's interior. During times of great change or danger, the serpent stirs, causing tremors. It is associated with the mountain's water sources -- its movements create springs emerging from the mountain's sides.
