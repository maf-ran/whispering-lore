Raven Water-Spirit of Kazakh

water-spirit · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a distinct water-spirit of Kazakh folklore. No source confirms a raven water-spirit guarding mountains or causing drownings; attested Kazakh water lore centers on the lake-serpent Aidakhar and the wind-camel Jhelmaia, with no such raven figure in ethnographic records. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
