Legba

deity · Benin · West Africa

Legba is the trickster god and divine intermediary in Fon Vodun cosmology of Benin, serving as the essential gatekeeper who opens communication between the human and spirit worlds. No ceremony, prayer, or offering can reach the other Vodun deities without Legba's permission and assistance. He stands at the cosmic crossroads, speaking all human languages, and is the master of doors, pathways, and communication.
