Halmasti

beast · Pakistan · South Asia

Halmasti, also known as the Celestial Hound, is a terrifying wolf-like creature from Chitrali folklore in northern Pakistan. Its name derives from the Khowar language and means 'thunder,' reflecting its association with destructive lightning and violent celestial forces. Halmasti is deeply connected to liminal moments in life, appearing during births and deaths when spiritual vulnerability is highest.
