Wolf Spirit

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Wolf Spirit is a powerful animal spirit and guardian figure in Comanche mythology. In the story How the Buffalo Were Released, a Wolf spirit guided a young Comanche warrior to the cave where buffalo were hidden, transformed him into a buffalo calf to infiltrate the herd, and taught the people how to hunt the buffalo with respect. In vision quest stories, Wolf appears as a spirit guide granting strength, courage, and tracking ability to warriors who fast and seek visions. Wolf is considered a brother and protector to the Comanche people
