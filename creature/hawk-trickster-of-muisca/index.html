Hawk Trickster of Muisca

trickster · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Muisca tradition. Muisca cosmology, recorded by colonial chroniclers (Simón, Lugo) and modern scholars, includes deities such as Bochica, Bachué, Sué and Chía, but no hawk trickster who mocked chieftains appears in the sources. The narrative of a humorous challenger to Muisca authority is invented; the goldwork and hierarchy are real, the hawk is not.
