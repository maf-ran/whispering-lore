Owl Nature-Spirit (San)

nature-spirit · Botswana · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in San (Khoisan) tradition of Botswana. While /Xam San narratives (Bleek & snip Lloyd) document avian spirits including ostrich and vulture, a specific "Owl Nature-Spirit of the lake" lacks verification. Owls in San folklore are less prominent; snip the entry appears templated.
