Kääk

spirit · Luxembourg · Germanic

'Kaak' is not attested in Luxembourgish, Estonian, or Finno-Ugric folklore, and the entry itself mixes an Estonian framing with a Luxembourgish country tag. Genuine Estonian forest spirits (metsavaimud) such as the metsik have documented names; 'Kaak' does not. The name appears invented, and no oral or literary source records it.
