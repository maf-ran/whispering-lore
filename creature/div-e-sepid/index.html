Div-e Sepid

demon · Afghanistan · Central Asia

Div-e Sepid, the White Demon, is a legendary creature from Persian and Afghan mythology, best known as the chieftain of the divs of Mazandaran and the penultimate adversary in Rostam's Seven Labors from Ferdowsi's Shahnameh. The White Demon is a being of colossal size and power, commanding armies of lesser divs from his cave fortress in the mountains. He is described as having white skin or fur, a black face, and immense strength.
