Bear Deity of Maya

deity · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

In Maya cosmology the bear appears in the mythology of highland Maya regions where the spectacled bear historically roamed. While jaguars dominate lowland Maya animal symbolism, Maya communities of the Guatemalan highlands incorporated the bear into their celestial understanding. The Maya associated bears with the god of the Pleiades constellation, recognizing the bear's connection to the sky through Ursa Major.
