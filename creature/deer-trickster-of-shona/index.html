Deer Trickster of Shona

trickster · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

In Shona mythology from Zimbabwe, the deer trickster is a swift and clever figure that inhabits the granite kopjes and cliff faces of the Zimbabwean plateau. Shona folklore is rich with trickster animals that teach moral lessons through their cunning adventures, and the deer trickster stands out for its ability to use speed and intelligence to overcome challenges. Unlike the more common hare or tortoise tricksters of African folklore, the deer trickster represents the union of physical grace and mental agility.
