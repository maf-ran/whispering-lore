Vård

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

Vård (Swedish 'warden') is a nature guardian spirit of a specific place — sacred tree (vårdträd), rock, spring, forest, field. Personifies land's genius loci. Manifests as white deer, snake, bird, light, or elderly humanoid; often felt as invisible presence. Protects those who respect land and leave offerings; punishes desecrators with misfortune, illness.
