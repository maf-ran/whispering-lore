Aiyy

deity · Russia · Central Asia

Aiyy are the benevolent celestial deities of Yakut (Sakha) cosmology, radiant beings of light often pictured as majestic humanoid figures in white robes, though some appear as horses, eagles, or swans. Benevolent yet distant, they respond to prayers and sacrifice rather than intervening directly, guiding chosen heroes to preserve cosmic balance against the chaotic Abaasy.
