Bolukun

deity · Nigeria · West Africa

Note: This entry is being added with slug bolukun-yoruba but may be a variant of Olokun, who already exists in the database. Bolukun is a regional Yoruba variant name for the orisha of the ocean depths. Bolukun represents the unfathomable depths of the sea and the mysterious, untamed aspects of water. As the ruler of the great waters, Bolukun controls the tides, marine life, and the hidden treasures of the ocean floor.
