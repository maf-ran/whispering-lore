Jiuku Tiannu

deity · China · East Asia and Pacific

Not independently attested as a goddess of Chinese religion. No source documents a 'Jiuku Tiannu' (救苦天女) who descends during epidemics to cure disease. The name appears to conflate documented figures: Taiyi Jiuku Tianzun (太乙救苦天尊), the Daoist Lord of Salvation, and Jiutian Xuannü (九天玄女). Neither is attested as a plague-curing goddess under this name.
