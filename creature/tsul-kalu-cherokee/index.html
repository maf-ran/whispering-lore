Tsul 'Kalu

spirit · United States · North America

Tsul'kalu, the Slant-Eyed Giant, is the Great Lord of the Game in Cherokee tradition, owner of the wild game of the mountains and master of hunting rites. He dwells on Tanasee Bald in North Carolina at Tsunegûñ'yï; Judaculla Rock bears markings attributed to his hands. His name means 'he has them slanting.'
