Gwyllion

nature-spirit · Wales · Celtic

The Gwyllion is a type of fairy or spirit from Welsh folklore, specifically associated with the mountainous regions of Wales such as Snowdonia. The name "Gwyllion" is derived from the Welsh word "gwyll" meaning dusk, twilight, or gloom, linking these spirits directly to the dim hours of evening and early night. They are considered mountain fairies that haunt lonely passes, rocky crags, and desolate moors, often misleading travelers who venture out after dark.
