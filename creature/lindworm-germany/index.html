Lindworm

dragon · Germany · Germanic

The Lindworm (Old High German lintrache, Middle High German lintwurm, Old Norse linnormr) is a flightless serpentine dragon of Germanic and Norse folklore. In Swedish tradition, it is a limbless forest serpent with a mane or dorsal fins, spitting blinding milk, swallowing its tail to roll as a wheel.
