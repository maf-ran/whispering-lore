Daini

ogre · Bangladesh · South Asia

In Bengali folklore, the Daini (derived from Sanskrit 'Dakini') is a feared witch who practices black magic and dark sorcery. Unlike ethereal spirits, the Daini is a living human woman, typically elderly, who is rumored to wield supernatural powers passed down through secret traditions. She is believed to kidnap children and drink their blood to sustain her unnaturally prolonged life, sometimes living for centuries.
