Sphinx

guardian · Egypt · North Africa

The Egyptian sphinx is a guardian of sacred ground, carved with the head of a king or god upon the body of a lion. From the Great Sphinx of Giza to the ram-headed criosphinxes lining temple avenues, it stood watch over tombs and processional ways. It embodies royal power, the horizon god Horemakhet, and the silent strength of the desert edge.
