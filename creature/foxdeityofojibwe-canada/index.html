Fox Deity of Ojibwe

deity · Canada · North America

In Anishinaabe (Ojibwe) tradition, the fox is one of the animal clan figures (doodem), representing cleverness and diplomacy, and appears in storytelling as a shrewd helper or trickster. Animal powers are respected in Anishinaabe medicine practice, and the fox is a real but minor spirit figure rather than a named deity.
