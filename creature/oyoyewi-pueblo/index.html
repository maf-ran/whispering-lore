Oyoyewi (Twin War God)

deity · United States · North America

Oyoyewi is one of the Twin War Gods in Pueblo (specifically Acoma) tradition, brother to Masewi. Together, these twins are the most important heroic and protective figures in Pueblo mythology. Born to a virgin mother who conceived from the Sun, they were responsible for creating order in the world, slaying monsters, establishing the directions, and teaching humanity the proper way to live.
