Yeil Raven Tlingit

animal-spirit · United States · North America

Yeil, the Raven, is the great trickster and creator of the Tlingit, who stole the sun, moon, and stars in nested boxes and released them into the sky. He shaped the landscape, brought salmon to the rivers, and gave fire to the people; his lineage, the Raven moiety, is one of the two primary divisions of Tlingit society.
