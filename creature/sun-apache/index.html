Sun

celestial · United States · North America

In Chiricahua and Mescalero Apache tradition, the Sun is the great creator deity who brought light and order to the world. Their creation story begins with a great flood that destroyed a malevolent world, and the creator, closely linked to the Sun, shaped the world, the gods, and all living things through his power.
