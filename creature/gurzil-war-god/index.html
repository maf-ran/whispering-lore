Gurzil (Bull-Headed War God)

deity · Algeria · North Africa

Gurzil was an important ancient Berber war god, primarily associated with the Laguatan people of the Tripolitania region (spanning modern Libya and southeastern Algeria). He is depicted as a bull-headed deity, the son of the god Ammon (Amun) conceived from a wild heifer. According to historical accounts from the 6th-century Roman poet Corippus, the Laguatan carried a representation of Gurzil in the form of a bull into battle when they revolted against the Romans in AD 546.
