Zankallala

trickster · Ivory Coast · West Africa

Zankallala is a remarkable trickster figure from Hausa folklore, found across the Sahel region including northern Ivory Coast. Despite being only the size of two clenched fists, Zankallala is one of the most powerful beings in Hausa mythology, carrying a snake as a walking stick, wearing a pair of scorpions as spurs, and a swarm of bees as a hat. He rides a jerboa into battle and wherever he goes, he is followed by birds that sing his praises and attack his enemies.
