Robin Chinook

animal · United States · North America

Robin is the long-suffering elder brother of Blue Jay in Chinook myth as recorded by Franz Boas. Patient and obedient, Robin endures Blue Jay's tantrums, sings at his shamanic cures, and helps gather the payments of roots, meat, and grease that the brothers bring home after healing the duck and the wolf girl.
