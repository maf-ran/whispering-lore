Mandukhai Khatun

hero · Mongolia · Central Asia

Mandukhai Khatun, 'Mandukhai the Wise,' is the legendary warrior-queen of 15th-century Mongolia, a noble queen in traditional headdress and armor who wields twin swords while riding horseback. A warrior who led armies into battle and a wise counselor in statecraft, she fought to reunify the Mongol nation alongside Dayan Khan and remains a lasting symbol of female power.
