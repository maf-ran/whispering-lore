Hawk Animal-Spirit of Quechua

animal-spirit · Peru · South America

The Hawk Animal-Spirit is a respected spiritual being in Quechua tradition of the Andean region of Peru, where the soaring condors and hawks of the high mountains hold deep spiritual significance. In Quechua cosmology, the world is divided into three realms: Hanan Pacha (the upper world of the gods and celestial spirits), Kay Pacha (the earthly world of humans), and Ukhu Pacha (the inner world of ancestors).
