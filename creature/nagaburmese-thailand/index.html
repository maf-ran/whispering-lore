Naga (Burmese)

serpent · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Same naga tradition as the Myanmar entry: in Thailand the naga (phaya nak) is a serpent deity of rivers, lakes, and the underworld, guarding water sources and treasures and appearing with multiple heads. It aids or opposes humans, and is central to Buddhist legends, temple iconography, and the Naga Fireballs. The input duplicates the Burmese figure under a Thai attribution.
