Eagle Guardian (Zulu)

guardian · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Eagle Guardian (Zulu) is a guardian from Guatemala mythology. The eagle is revered as the king of birds, a symbol of royal authority, courage, and spiritual vision. Eagle feathers are reserved for leaders and warriors, conferring the bird's power of clear sight and connection to the divine. The eagle guardian watches over communities, carries prayers to the spirit realm, and protects against evil forces. Its image appears in ceremonial regalia and is invoked during important rituals and community gatherings
