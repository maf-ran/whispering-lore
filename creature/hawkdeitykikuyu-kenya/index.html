Hawk Deity (Kikuyu)

deity · Kenya · East Africa

In Kikuyu (Agikuyu) tradition the hawk belongs to the birds regarded as intermediaries between humanity and Ngai, the supreme god of Mount Kenya (Kirinyaga). This entry generalizes that role into a 'hawk deity' marked by vigilance and keen sight. Its claim of a diaspora-specific variant is unsupported; attested usage keeps the raptor within Ngai's broader messenger cosmology.
