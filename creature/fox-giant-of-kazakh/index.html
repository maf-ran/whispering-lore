Fox Giant of Kazakh

giant · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested: while the fox (tülki) is a familiar trickster in Kazakh tales, such as those of Aldar Köse and in the Korkut-ata tradition, no 'giant fox' being is recorded in Kazakh folklore. The description inflates the fox's attested trickster role into a fabricated primordial giant. The closest genuine figure is the cunning fox of the folktales, not a supernatural giant.
