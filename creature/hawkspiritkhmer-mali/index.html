Hawk Spirit (Khmer)

spirit · Mali · West Africa

Not independently attested and internally contradictory: the Khmer are a Cambodian people, and the entry, attributed to Mali, describes a hawk spirit tied to 'Cambodian people who resettled in Canada' and to comfort 'during the long winter months.' No such figure appears in Malian or Khmer tradition; the entry appears fabricated.
