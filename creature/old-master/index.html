Old Master

folk-hero · United States · North America

Old Master is a recurring character in African American folklore, particularly in the 'trickster slave' narrative cycle. He is the plantation owner (the enslaver) whom clever enslaved protagonists consistently outwit. These stories — collected by Zora Neale Hurston, Julius Lester, and others — portray Old Master not as a benign paternalist but as a figure of arbitrary power who is repeatedly humiliated by the intelligence and wit of those he considers his inferiors.
