Tumatauenga

deity · New Zealand · Pacific

God of war, hunting, warfare, and human activities. Tumatauenga is the fierce son of Ranginui and Papatuanuku. After the separation of his parents, he was the one who proposed killing them rather than separating them. When his proposal was rejected, he remained silent during the debate. After the separation, when Tawhirimatea attacked all his brothers, only Tumatauenga stood his ground. He defeated all his siblings - creating snares to catch Tane's birds, nets to catch Tangaroa's fish, and digging implements to uncover Rongo and Haumia from the earth. He is the patron of humanity and all human
