Bat Animal-Spirit (Udmurt)

dragon · Russia · Slavic

In Udmurt animistic tradition, the bat animal-spirit is a nature guardian associated with the transitional spaces between day and night, earth and sky. The Udmurt people, a Finno-Ugric ethnic group from the Volga region of Russia, traditionally hold deep reverence for animal spirits that inhabit the natural world. Bats, with their ability to fly and dwell in caves, are seen as mediators between different realms of existence.
