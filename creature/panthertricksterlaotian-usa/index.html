Panther Trickster (Laotian)

trickster · United States · North America

Not attested in North American folklore. This entry misplaces a Laotian panther trickster in the United States: no USA tradition records such a being, and the Laotian origin is itself unsupported by any named source. North American trickster roles belong to Coyote, Raven and other animals; a panther trickster is not part of any recorded USA tradition.
