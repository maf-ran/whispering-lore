Wildcat Giant of Guarani

giant · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested in Guaraní tradition. No wildcat giant exists in Guaraní mythology (seven legendary monsters include no giants). Teju Jagua (cavern lord) is lizard-like; Mbói Tu'ĩ is serpentine. Wildcat giants appear fabricated, possibly conflating feline folklore with giant motifs.
