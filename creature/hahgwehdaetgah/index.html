Hahgwehdaetgah

demigod · United States · North America

Hahgwehdaetgah, also known as Flint or the Left-Handed Twin, is the malevolent twin grandson of Sky Woman in Iroquois mythology. He forced his way out through his mother's armpit during childbirth, killing her. From her buried body grew the sacred plants — tobacco from her heart, strawberries from her feet. Flint created everything dangerous and destructive in the world: steep cliffs, monstrous animals, poisonous plants, winter, darkness, and death. He constantly tried to undo his brother's good work
