Hawk Animal-Spirit of Mi'kmaq

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

The Hawk Animal-Spirit is a respected spirit being in Mi'kmaq (Mi'kmaw) tradition, representing the qualities of vision, guardianship, and connection between the sky world and the earth. In Mi'kmaq cosmology, all animals possess spiritual essence and certain species serve as clan totems or personal spirit guides (buoin). The hawk, with its aerial mastery and precise hunting skills, is honored as a powerful spirit that protects the valleys and forested landscapes of Mi'kma'ki (the traditional Mi'kmaq territories spanning the Maritime provinces of Canada).
