Camahueto

beast · Chile · South America

Camahueto is a Chilote aquatic bull from Chiloé Archipelago, Chile. Born in rivers with a single spiral horn like a unicorn, it grows massive and migrates to the sea. Destructive on land: destroys crops, creates valleys and floods. Only a machi can lasso it with kelp and return it to the ocean. Its horn shavings are prized medicine.
