Wolf Nature-Spirit of Baloch

nature-spirit · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in Balochi folklore as a named entity 'Wolf Nature-Spirit of Baloch'. Balochi oral tradition includes nature spirits, jinns, and animal helpers (e.g., in the epic of Mir Chakar Rind), but no specific wolf spirit by this name. The 'guardian of the swamps' and 'central nature-spirit' description appears fabricated.
