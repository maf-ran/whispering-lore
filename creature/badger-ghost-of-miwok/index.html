Badger Ghost of Miwok

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Miwok tradition records a badger ghost. Miwok creation is led by Coyote and his grandson, the falcon Wek-Wek, with other animals playing supporting roles; Miwok beliefs about the dead do not single out any badger spirit.
