Raven Deity of Ashanti

deity · Ghana · West Africa

Not independently attested: ravens do not occur in West African fauna and no raven deity appears in Ashanti (Akan) religious sources. The figure is likely a mistranslation or conflation, possibly with the vulture associated with Nyame or with eagle imagery in Akan royal regalia.
