Coyote Guardian (Burmese)

guardian · Myanmar · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Coyotes are not native to Myanmar, and no Burmese coyote guardian exists; the given description is a Swahili coastal invention ('Mlinzi wa Barabara'). Genuine Burmese guardian spirits are the nats, not coyote beings.
