Sheikh of the Nile (Nile River Spirit)

water-spirit · Egypt · Africa

The Sheikh of the Nile is a protective river spirit in Egyptian folk belief, a powerful guardian who inhabits the Nile River. He is a wali (saint) who oversees the river's bounty and dangers, controlling the annual flood, protecting fishermen and sailors, and punishing those who pollute or disrespect the Nile. The figure blends pre-Islamic Nilotic worship with Islamic saint veneration. The Offering of the Bride (Arusa al-Nil) ceremony, an annual ritual, was historically performed to appease him by throwing a decorated doll into the river.
