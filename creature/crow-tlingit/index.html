Crow Tlingit

animal-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a separate crow being in Tlingit folklore. The raven-crow narrative role of Tlingit myth belongs to Yéil, the Raven culture hero and world-maker whose cycle of adventures includes stealing the light and shaping the world; no distinct female crow trickster is recorded for the Tlingit.
