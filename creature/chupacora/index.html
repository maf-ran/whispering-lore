Chupacora

chimera · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested in folklore literature. 'Chupacora' is an unattested variant of the chupacabra, a livestock-killing cryptid first reported in Puerto Rico in 1995 and since reported across Latin America. No distinct Guatemalan 'Chupacora' with its own characteristics is documented.
