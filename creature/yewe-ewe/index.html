Yewe

deity · Togo · West Africa

Yewe (also known as Hevioso, Xevioso, or Sogbo) is the powerful Ewe god of thunder, lightning, rain, and divine justice. One of the major trɔwo (lesser deities descended from Mawu), Yewe is one of the most feared and respected spirits in the Ewe pantheon. His sacred cult, the Yewe society, is one of the most exclusive and highly developed spiritual organizations in Eweland, with elaborate initiation ceremonies involving seclusion, sacred drumming (Sowu), and ritual dances.
