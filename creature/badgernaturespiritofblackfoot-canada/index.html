Badger Nature-Spirit of Blackfoot

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

No Blackfoot source documents a 'badger nature-spirit.' Blackfoot tradition centers on figures such as Napi (Old Man) and on personal spirit-helpers gained through vision quests, but the badger does not appear as a named nature-spirit in the record.
