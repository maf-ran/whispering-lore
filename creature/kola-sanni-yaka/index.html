Kola Sanni Yaka

demon · Sri Lanka · South Asia

Kola Sanni Yaka is a fearsome demon of disease and spirit possession from Sri Lankan folklore, belonging to the Sanni Yakka pantheon of eighteen demons collectively responsible for causing specific illnesses and afflictions. This particular yaka is associated with severe physical and mental ailments, with its name deriving from kola meaning leaf or herbal medicine and sanni meaning disease or affliction.
