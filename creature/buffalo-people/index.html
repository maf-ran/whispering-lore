Buffalo People

spirit · United States · North America

The Buffalo People are significant spirit beings in Cheyenne mythology and oral traditions, representing the deep spiritual connection between the Cheyenne people and the American bison, which was central to their way of life on the Great Plains. These sacred beings are not merely animals but intelligent, spiritual entities with their own society, customs, and medicine power. The Buffalo People taught the Cheyenne important ceremonies and hunting practices, and their stories emphasize the reciprocal relationship between humans and buffalo, where respect and gratitude must be shown for the buff
