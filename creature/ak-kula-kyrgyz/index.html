Ak-Kula

mythical-horse · Kyrgyzstan · Central Asia

Ak-Kula is the magical white stallion of Manas, greatest hero of the Kyrgyz epic, a white horse whose coat glows like moonlight and whose eyes betray human understanding. Born from the sea, it speaks with a human voice, giving prophetic warnings and fighting beside him, untameable by the unworthy. It is as iconic as Manas, embodying the bond between heroes and steeds.
