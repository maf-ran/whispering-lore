Kinkirsi (Kinkirga)

nature-spirit · Burkina Faso · West Africa

Kinkirsi (singular: Kinkirga) are the powerful nature spirits of the Mossi people of Burkina Faso, invisible beings that inhabit the untamed wilderness beyond village boundaries. According to Mossi cosmology, these spirits are 'as numerous as the stars' and occupy all spaces not inhabited or cultivated by humans — sacred woods, hills, rivers, rocky outcrops, and wild plains.
