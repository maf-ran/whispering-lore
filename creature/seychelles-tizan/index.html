Tizan

spirit · Seychelles · East Africa

Tizan (Ti-Zan, 'Little John') is the clever poor boy who outwits monsters, giants, and the mighty throughout Seychellois Creole tales, many gathered in the University of Seychelles folklore database. In stories such as 'Tizan and the Seven-Headed Monster' he wins by wit rather than strength. The input's red-eyed dwarf image better fits the soungoula; Tizan is the boy-hero, not a monster.
