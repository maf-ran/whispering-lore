Wildcat Nature-Spirit of Apache

nature-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat nature-spirit in Apache tradition. Apache mythology is built around figures like White Painted Woman and the warrior twins Killer of Enemies and Child of the Water, with animals such as the mountain lion and coyote appearing in tales; no wildcat or bobcat nature-spirit is recorded. The entry appears to be a generic feline misattribution to the Apache.
