Kuveni (Yakshini Queen of Lanka)

spirit · Sri Lanka · South Asia

Kuveni (also known as Kuvanna or Sesapati) was a Yakshini (demoness) queen ruling in Lanka when Prince Vijaya arrived from India. According to the Mahavamsa, the ancient Pali chronicle of Sri Lanka, she was a shape-shifting demoness who could assume the form of a beautiful sixteen-year-old maiden. She initially imprisoned Vijaya's men through magical means, but when Vijaya overpowered her, she swore loyalty and helped him conquer the Yakkha city of Sirisavatthu.
