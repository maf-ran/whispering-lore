Deer Water-Spirit of Ashanti

water-spirit · Ghana · West Africa

In Ashanti mythology from Ghana, the deer water-spirit is an aquatic guardian that inhabits the cave springs and hidden water sources of the forest. The Ashanti people, part of the larger Akan ethnic group, have a rich pantheon of water spirits that protect rivers, lakes, and springs. The deer water-spirit specifically guards the underground water systems that feed the sacred rivers, including the Tano River which is itself personified as a deity.
