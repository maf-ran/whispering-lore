Badger Deity of Apache

deity · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No documented Apache tradition features a badger deity; the badger is neither a prominent Apache animal figure nor a clan or ceremonial spirit. Genuine Apache spirit beings include the Gan (Mountain Spirits) of the Crown Dance, Coyote, and the owl, which carries death associations.
