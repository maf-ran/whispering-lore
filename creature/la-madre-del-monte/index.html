La Madre del Monte

nature-spirit · Colombia · South America

La Madre del Monte (Mother of the Mountain) is a powerful nature spirit from the folklore of Costa Rica, Panama, and Colombia, particularly among the indigenous Bribri, Cabécar, and Ngäbe peoples. She is the protective goddess of forests, mountains, and wild animals, serving as the guardian of biodiversity and natural balance. La Madre del Monte is one of the most respected and feared spirits in Central American folk tradition because she controls the abundance of game animals, the fertility of the forest, and the safety of those who enter her domain.
