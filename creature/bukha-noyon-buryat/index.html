Bukha Noyon

deity · Russia · Central Asia

Bukha Noyon is the celestial bull of Buryat tradition, a totemic progenitor who descended from the sky to create and defend the western Buryat clans. Fought and beloved, he is said to have shaped the land with his body and to have become the ancestor of the Bulagat tribe. Sacred sites in Buryatia still receive offerings to this powerful bull-god.
