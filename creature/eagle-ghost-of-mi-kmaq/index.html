Eagle Ghost of Mi'kmaq

ghost · Canada · North America

In Mi'kmaq mythology, the eagle is a powerful spirit being associated with the sky realm and the Great Spirit (Glooscap/Klu skap). The Mi'kmaq people of eastern Canada regard the eagle as a messenger and protector who carries prayers to the Creator. The eagle ghost specifically refers to the spirit of an eagle that has passed into the spirit world but continues to watch over and guide the living. In Mi'kmaq tradition, the eagle is considered the chief of the birds and is deeply respected for its wisdom, strength, and connection to the upper world.
