Moose Nature-Spirit (Mixtec)

nature-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested, and geographically impossible: the Mixtec are a Mesoamerican people of Oaxaca, Mexico, where moose do not occur, and this record mislabels them as USA folklore. Mixtec tradition reveres deities such as Dzahui, the rain god, and the earth lord. No moose nature-spirit appears in any source. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
