Kushtaka

monster · United States · North America

The Kushtaka, meaning "Land Otter Man" in the Tlingit language, is a shape-shifting creature from the folklore of the Tlingit and Haida peoples of the Pacific Northwest coast of North America. These are hybrid beings with both otter and human characteristics, created when humans are captured and transformed by the Land Otter spirits that inhabit the dense coastal forests. The Kushtaka are among the most complex supernatural beings in Northwest Coast mythology, possessing a deeply dual nature that makes them both terrifying and benevolent, depending on the circumstances.
