Bear Giant of Quechua

giant · Peru · South America

In Quechua oral traditions from the Andean highlands of Peru and Bolivia, the bear giant appears in stories as a hybrid creature combining human and bear characteristics, known as ukuku or paucar. The Quechua people, descendants of the Inca civilization, tell stories of giant bear-like beings that once roamed the high puna grasslands and cloud forests. These bear giants are portrayed as solitary creatures interacting with humans through a combination of menace and foolishness, appearing in cautionary tales teaching children to stay close to their communities
