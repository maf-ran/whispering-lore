Eagle Deity of Yoruba

deity · Nigeria · West Africa

In Yoruba mythology, the eagle is associated with the orisha (deity) Ogun and with the concept of ase (divine authority and power). The eagle's ability to soar to great heights and see with clarity makes it a symbol of vision, leadership, and spiritual insight. Among the Yoruba people of Nigeria, the eagle is considered a royal bird, associated with kingship and the authority of the oba (ruler). Eagle feathers are used in the crowns and regalia of Yoruba chiefs, signifying their connection to the divine realm and their role as intermediaries between the people and the orishas
