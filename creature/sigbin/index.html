Sigbin

beast · Philippines · Southeast Asia

The sigbin is a creature of Visayan folklore, a hornless, dog-like beast with oversized hind legs and enormous flapping ears that walks backwards with its head between its legs. It emerges during Holy Week to suck blood through shadows and is said to hunt children for their hearts; it is tied to the darker arts of the usar witch tradition.
