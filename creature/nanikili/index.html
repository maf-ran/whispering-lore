Nanikili

demon · Samoa · Pacific

Nanikili is a sea demon of Samoan belief, an aitu (spirit of the dead or supernatural being) that lurks in coral reefs and rocky shores, attacking swimmers and fishermen. Tradition holds that it can be slain but returns to life unless it is properly destroyed, and its strikes are invoked to explain drownings and missing fishers. It reflects the perils Samoans associate with the sea.
