Yocahu (Taino Supreme God of Fertility)

supreme-deity · Puerto Rico · Caribbean

Yocahu (Yucahu Bagua Maorocoti) was the supreme deity and masculine spirit of fertility in Taino mythology -- the greatest of all zemis. His full name, recorded by the Spanish missionary Fray Ramon Pane in 1498, carries multiple meanings: Yucahu means giver of cassava (the staple crop), Bagua means master of the sea, and Maorocoti means conceived without male intervention -- born solely from his mother Atabey, the goddess of fresh water.
