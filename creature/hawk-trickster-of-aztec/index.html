Hawk Trickster of Aztec

trickster · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Hawks and eagles carry strong symbolism in Aztec (Mexica) culture as creatures of the sun, war, and the warrior orders, and raptors were watched for omens. A humorous 'hawk trickster' who exposes hypocrisy is not attested in Mexica sources; the entry projects a trickster template onto Aztec raptor symbolism. The genuine associations are martial and augural, not comedic.
