Nai Tiki

chimera · Fiji · Pacific

Nai Tiki is a forest creature of Fijian mythology, a half-human, half-beast being said to combine animal strength with human cunning. Possessed of extraordinary power and speed, it can command the weather, calling rain and sun over the Fijian wilderness it inhabits. It captures the Fijian understanding of the boundary between the human and animal worlds.
