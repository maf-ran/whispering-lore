Gramya Devata

deity · India · South Asia

Gramya Devata or Gramadevata are village deities from Bengali and wider Hindu folk tradition, serving as local guardian spirits that protect specific hamlets, fields, water sources, and communities across the Indian subcontinent. The Sanskrit term combines grama meaning village and devata meaning deity, referring to the localized nature of these gods whose authority is tied to particular geographic areas. In Bengal, these deities form a crucial layer of popular Hinduism that coexists with the greater pantheon of Puranic gods, representing the grassroots expression of religious devotion. Gramya
