Bones Together

monster · United States · North America

Bones Together is a fearsome monster in Crow mythology, a skeletal being with impenetrable bone armor. Seven Bison and their human companion fought against Bones Together when the monster threatened them. Each bison charged but broke their legs against Bones Together's bony hide. Finally the human companion, blessed by an eagle with the power to transform into a feather, faced the monster. Bones Together charged four times, but the man turned into a feather and wafted out of harm's way.
