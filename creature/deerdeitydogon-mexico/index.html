Deer Deity (Dogon)

deity · Mexico · Mesoamerica

The Deer Deity of the Dogon tradition as carried to Mexico represents the adaptation of West African spiritual traditions in the African diaspora communities of Mexico. During the colonial era, enslaved Africans from the Mali region, including Dogon people, were brought to Mexico, carrying their spiritual traditions with them despite brutal suppression. This version of the Dogon Deer Deity blended with indigenous Mexican deer symbolism, particularly the reverence for deer in Aztec and Maya traditions, creating a unique syncretic spiritual being that honors both African and American roots In Me
