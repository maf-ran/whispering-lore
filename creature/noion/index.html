Noion

household-spirit · Tibet · East Asia and Pacific

Not independently attested as a figure of Tibetan folklore. No source documents a household spirit named 'Noion' tied to Manjushri, the bodhisattva of wisdom, who answers questions in dreams. The recorded traits resemble generic depictions of Manjushri, a well-attested figure, but the name and role appear in no Tibetan or scholarly source. Likely a modern invention.
