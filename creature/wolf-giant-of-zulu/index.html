Wolf Giant of Zulu

giant · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Zulu tradition. No 'wolf giant' appears in Bantu oral records; wolves are absent from sub-Saharan Africa. The record likely conflates the Zulu river-goddess Mamlambo (a serpentine water spirit) with foreign wolf motifs. Zulu myth features sky spirits like Umvelinqangi and the shape-shifting Nomkhubulwane, but no wolf-beings.
