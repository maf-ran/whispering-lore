Moose Ancestor-Spirit (Ashanti)

ancestor-spirit · Canada · North America

A garbled entry combining 'Ashanti' with a Canadian moose ancestor spirit; no Ashanti (Ghana) tradition is associated with the moose, a species absent from West Africa. The nearest genuine parallels are Algonquian and Blackfoot traditions in which the moose appears as a provider or spirit being. Not independently attested; flagged as a data-merge error.
