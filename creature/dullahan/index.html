Dullahan

spirit · Ireland · Celtic

Headless Rider - Irish 'Dubhlachan' (from dubh 'black'). Also called 'Colainn Gan Cheann' (body without head). NOT related to the Banshee (her lineage): the Dullahan is a solitary death Bringer, NOT a herald. The Banshee Warnings before death, but the Dullahan Brings the death himself. No parents, no family - he's a force, not a person. Rides the 'Coiste Bodhar' (Soundless Death Coach) made from Coffins, Bones, Funerary remnants. The coach axle is Spine, wheel-spokes are thigh bones. He carries his own Severed Head under his arm as a lantern - the dead eyes glow like coals, the grin has razor'
