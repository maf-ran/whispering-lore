Invunche

guardian · Chile · South America

Invunche (Imbunche) is a Chilote monster from Chiloé Island, Chile: a kidnapped infant deformed by a brujo (warlock) — leg twisted over back, tongue forked, skin blue, fed on black cat's milk and human flesh. Guards the warlock's cave entrance, acts as advisor and instrument of curses. Created through ritual torture of a firstborn under nine days old.
