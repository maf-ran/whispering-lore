Dame Blanche

ghost · France · Mediterranean

The Dame Blanche (White Lady) is a famous female spirit from French mythology and folklore, reported across regions including Lorraine, Normandy, and the Pyrenees mountains. These spectral figures are described as a type of fée (fairy) of less benevolent character, lurking in narrow ravines, forests, bridges, and caverns. They likely descend from ancient pre-Christian goddesses known as the Matres or Dominae, guardian deities of home and hearth.
