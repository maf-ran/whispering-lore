Panther Spirit (Dogon)

spirit · Chile · South America

Not independently attested. The Dogon are a West African people (Mali); panthers (leopards) and Dogon cosmology are absent from Chile. The described 'lake-embodying panther spirit' has no basis in Mapuche or Chilote mythology. Complete cross-continental fabrication.
