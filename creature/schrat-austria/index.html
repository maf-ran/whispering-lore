Schrat

forest-spirit · Austria · Germanic

The Schrat (plural Schratln) is a versatile and regionally diverse German legendary creature, appearing as either a wood sprite, a domestic kobold, or a nightmare demon depending on location and tradition. The term is ancient, entering Slavic languages as skrat, skrzat (Polish), skřítek (Czech), and even Estonian kratt — a testament to its deep Indo-European roots. In Bavaria and Austria, the Waldschrat is a solitary forest-dwelling spirit, small and mischievous, who plays malicious pranks on woodcutters and hikers.
