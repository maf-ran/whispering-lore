Lizard Spirit (Bhutanese)

spirit · Bhutan · South Asia

Not independently attested as a named 'Lizard Spirit' in Bhutanese texts. Bhutanese folklore includes lizard/deity associations (lu/khu spirits in rocks, trees), but no specific 'Lizard Spirit'. The 'protector of the savannas' claim is erroneous — Bhutan has no savannas (Himalayan mountains/forests). Likely a fabricated entry conflating generic nature spirits with incorrect habitat.
