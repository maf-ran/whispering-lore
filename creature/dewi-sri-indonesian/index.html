Dewi Sri (Rice Goddess)

goddess · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Dewi Sri is the revered Javanese, Sundanese, and Balinese Hindu goddess of rice and fertility, still widely worshipped across Indonesia. According to Sundanese myth, she was born from a miraculous egg laid by the naga god Antaboga. She grew into a princess of such beauty that the supreme god Batara Guru became dangerously attracted to her. To prevent scandal in heaven, the gods poisoned her, and her innocent body was buried.
