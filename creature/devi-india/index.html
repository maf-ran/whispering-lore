Devi

ogre · India · South Asia

Devi, the Great Goddess of Hinduism, is the supreme feminine power, Shakti. Her gentle forms include Parvati, Uma, and Gauri; her fierce forms include Durga, the ten-armed warrior who slays the buffalo demon Mahishasura, and Kali. The Devi Mahatmya of the Markandeya Purana recounts these victories.
