Karakondzula

nature-spirit · Bulgaria · Slavic

Karakondzula is a winter demon from Balkan folklore, closely related to the Karakondzho of Bulgaria and the Karakoncolos of Turkey. This malevolent spirit is said to appear during the coldest months of the year, particularly around the Christmas and New Year period when the boundaries between the human and spirit worlds are believed to thin. The Karakondzula is part of a broader family of similar creatures found throughout the Balkan Peninsula, all sharing characteristics of hairy, demonic beings that emerge during winter to harass humans.
