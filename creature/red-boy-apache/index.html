Red Boy

spirit · United States · North America

In the Jicarilla Apache creation narrative, Holy Boy (the record's 'Red Boy') is the younger companion of Killer-of-Enemies. At the dawn of the world he traveled to obtain the sun from White Hactcin and the moon from Black Hactcin, bringing light into the darkened world. His deeds are central to the Jicarilla origin cycle.
