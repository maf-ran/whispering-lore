Klabautermann

ghost · Germany · Germanic

The Klabautermann is a ship spirit of German, Frisian, and Scandinavian maritime lore: a small sailor-specter who lives aboard a vessel, working unseen at night — mending ropes, pumping leaks, polishing — and guarding the ship, but who may turn malicious toward a slovenly or blasphemous crew, and whose visible appearance foretells shipwreck.
