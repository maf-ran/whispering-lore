Pygmalion

human · Cyprus · Europe

Pygmalion (Unknown). is a human. Pygmalion is a figure from Greek mythology, a sculptor from Cyprus who fell in love with his own ivory statue of a woman. The goddess Aphrodite brought the statue to life, and Pygmalion married her.. Source: Metamorphoses (Ovid, 8 CE); Greek mythological tradition
