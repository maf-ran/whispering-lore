Bakh

forest-spirit · Sudan · North Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No source records a Sudanese 'Bakh' forest spirit. The Nuba peoples of South Kordofan do keep traditions of ancestral and nature spirits, including kujur rainmaker-mediums, but no forest being by this name appears in the ethnographic record.
