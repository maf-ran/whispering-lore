Nasnās

humanoid · Saudi Arabia · Middle East and North Africa

Nasnās is a monstrous creature from Arabian folklore, described as half a human being with half a head, half a body, one arm, and one leg, hopping with agility. According to Arab tradition, the Nasnās was believed to be the offspring of a Shiqq (a half-formed jinn) and a human. In the Hadramaut region of Arabia, Nasnās were said to be eaten by locals who considered their flesh sweet. One was reportedly brought alive to Caliph al-Mutawakkil, described as resembling a man with only half a face located on its chest and a sheep-like tail. In Shia sources, the Nasnās lived on earth before humanity
