Asase Yaa

deity · Ghana · West Africa

Asase Yaa is the great earth goddess of the Akan people, second only to Nyame in the pantheon. As the wife of Nyame (or Nyankopon), she is the mother of all life and the foundation of the material world. Asase Yaa has two aspects: Asase Yaa (the elder, representing the dry/barren earth and the realm of the dead) and Asase Afua (the younger, representing the fertile earth and agricultural abundance).
