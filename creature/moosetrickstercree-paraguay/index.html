Moose Trickster (Cree)

trickster · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested in Paraguay or Guarani tradition. The Cree are an Algonquian people of subarctic Canada; moose trickster figures (e.g., Wisahkecahk as moose) belong to Cree/Anishinaabe lore, not Paraguay. No moose exist in Paraguay.
