Sisiutl

spirit · Canada · North America

The Sisiutl is the double-headed sea serpent of the Kwakwaka'wakw and neighboring Northwest Coast peoples, with a serpent head at each end of its body and a human-like face at the centre, all three horned. Seeing it means death to the unprepared, but a warrior who faces it may gain superhuman strength and armor from its blood and hide. Its image guards houses, canoes and warriors.
