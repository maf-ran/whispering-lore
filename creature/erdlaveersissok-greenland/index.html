Erdlaveersissok

spirit · Greenland · North America

Erdlaveersissok, 'the Entrail-Seizer,' is a terrifying being of Greenlandic Inuit tradition who haunts the celestial path to the moon. Travelers who laugh or lose their composure on that journey have their entrails torn out by it; the tale teaches the value of discipline, mental strength and restraint on the spiritual journey.
