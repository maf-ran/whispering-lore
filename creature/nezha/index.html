Nezha

deity · China · East Asia and Pacific

Nezha (哪吒) is the fiery third son of the Pagoda-Bearer Li Jing in Chinese mythology, central to Fengshen Yanyi and Journey to the West. Born after three years in the womb, he wields a fire-tipped spear, the cosmic ring Qiankun Quan, and the Wind-Fire Wheels he rides beneath his feet. He rebels, dies, and is reborn from a lotus as a powerful young guardian of heaven.
