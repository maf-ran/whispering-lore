Mujna

jinn · Pakistan · South Asia

Mujna (or Mujina) is a desert djinn (jinn) in Pakistani Baloch/Sindhi folklore. Appears to lost travelers in the desert, grants wishes but demands payment (often tragic). Part of Islamic-influenced folk belief in Balochistan and Sindh. Similar to Persian 'Mujina' (badger-shapeshifter) but localized as a desert wish-granting spirit. Documented in regional folk collections and oral accounts.
