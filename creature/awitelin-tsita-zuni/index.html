Áwitelin Tsíta (Earth Mother)

deity · United States · North America

Awitelin Tsita (Áwitelin Tsíta) is the Earth Mother of Zuni creation, formed together with the Sky Father Ápoyan Ta'chu from the mists that lay over the great waters; she conceived all living things in her four-fold womb. In Frank Hamilton Cushing's Zuni Genesis, the All-Father Awonawilona sets this creation in motion. She is a central generative deity of Zuni cosmology.
