Nk (or Nkanyin)

spirit · Congo · Central Africa

Nkornkanyin (Nk or Nkanyin) is a Congolese forest guardian spirit (Republic of Congo). Identical to the DRC entry — a tall, leaf-clad humanoid with glowing eyes that shapeshifts into gorilla or leopard, curses taboo-breakers, and heals. It watches forest protocol and punishes overhunting. The duplicate slugs reflect the shared culture across the Congo River border.
