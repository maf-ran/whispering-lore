Xhindet

spirit · Albania · Balkan

Xhindet are the jinn-like trickster spirits of Albanian folklore, beings of illusion and stealth that haunt mountain passes and old ruins. Mischievous and deceptive rather than overtly malevolent, they can be countered with iron or clever riddles. Their stories remind listeners that the world holds unseen forces which must be negotiated with wit, a recurring theme across the Balkans.
