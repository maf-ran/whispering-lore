Deer Water-Spirit of Yurok

water-spirit · United States · North America

In Yurok tradition from the Pacific Northwest coast, the deer water-spirit is a guardian of the springs and streams that flow through the redwood forests of northern California. The Yurok people have a deep spiritual relationship with the Klamath River and its tributaries, which they consider the center of their world. Water spirits in Yurok tradition are powerful beings that must be treated with great respect, as they control the salmon runs, the health of the forest, and the prosperity of the villages.
