Nanabush

spirit · United States · North America

Nanabush, known in his many nations as Nanabozho or Wenabozho, is the culture hero and trickster of the Anishinaabe and neighboring Algonquian peoples whose lands span the Great Lakes on both sides of the United States-Canada border. Through his adventures he remade the world and taught the people to live. His stories, told when snow covers the ground, mix comedy and sacred instruction.
