Cadejo

spirit · Belize · Mesoamerica

Cadejo is a spirit from Central American mythology. The legend has pre-Columbian indigenous origins, where dogs were believed to help humans pass into the afterlife. With the arrival of Christianity, the story evolved into a dualistic tale. The White Cadejo, with blue eyes, is a protective spirit that guards drunkards, lost travelers, and innocent children from harm. The Black Cadejo, with burning red eyes, is a malevolent entity that preys on evildoers, drunks, and those with impure intentions — stalking them through village streets and stealing their souls. The Salvadoran writer Manlio Argue
