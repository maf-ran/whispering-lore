Moose Trickster (Cree)

trickster · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested as a moose trickster of Cree folklore, and the Mexico country tag is wrong. The Cree are a North American people; their trickster is Wisakedjak (Wesakechak), not a moose. Moose do appear in northern Algonquian stories, but no moose trickster is recorded, and Cree tradition belongs to Canada and the northern US, not Mexico. The record conflates unrelated traditions.
