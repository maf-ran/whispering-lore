Goblin

goblin · England · Northern Europe

A goblin is a small, grotesque, mischievous spirit of English folklore, from the family of household and wilderness spirits that includes hobgoblins and boggarts. Goblins are chiefly pranksters who cause annoyance rather than real harm. The word likely comes from the French gobelin or medieval Latin gobelinus, and the figure has spread widely through literature and fantasy.
