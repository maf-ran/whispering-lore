Blue Heron (Táłtłʼááh álééh)

hero · United States · North America

Blue Heron (Táłtłʼááh álééh) is a hero figure in Navajo mythology known for his role in the emergence stories and his gift of water to the people. He is one of the animal spirits who accompanied the Holy People during the journey through the underworlds, and his patience and determination in fishing the first water from the depths made life possible for humans on the surface world. Blue Heron is a symbol of stillness, focus, and self-reliance, and he is invoked by hunters and fishers who need patience and precision.
