Faraa Kankurang (Wuleng Wuleng)

spirit · Gambia · West Africa

Faraa Kankurang, also known as Wuleng Wuleng (meaning 'red, red') or Ifangbodi, is one of the three original Kankurang masquerade forms in Mandinka tradition of The Gambia, distinguished by its powerful mystical identity and connection to the spirit world. Unlike other Kankurang forms primarily associated with social discipline, Faraa Kankurang carries a more deeply spiritual and supernatural character.
