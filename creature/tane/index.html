Tane

forest-spirit · France · Mediterranean

Tāne is the Polynesian god of forests and birds, best known in Māori tradition as Tāne Mahuta, who separated earth and sky and gave humanity the trees of the forest. The record identifies him as a Tahitian forest deity honored through carved wooden figures in temples. Note that the figure belongs to Polynesian, not French, tradition despite the country tag on this record.
