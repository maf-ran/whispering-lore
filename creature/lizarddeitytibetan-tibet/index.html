Lizard Deity (Tibetan)

deity · Tibet · East Asia and Pacific

Not independently attested as a distinct figure of Tibetan folklore. No source documents a named lizard deity guarding treasures and sacred knowledge in Tibet's rivers and lakes. The recorded traits match no specific deity; Tibetan water and underground spirits are the serpentine 'klu' (nāga-like beings), not lizards. The entry appears to derive from conflation or modern invention.
