El Cuchubo

ogre · Panama · Mesoamerica

El Cuchubo is a fearsome two-headed giant from Panamanian folklore, said to dwell in the dense jungles of the Darién Gap and the remote forests of the Azuero Peninsula. This monstrous ogre is one of several giant legends found across Panama, representing the dangers that lurk in the unexplored wilderness. According to tales passed down through generations of indigenous and rural communities, El Cuchubo is a territorial being that devours anyone who enters its domain without permission.
