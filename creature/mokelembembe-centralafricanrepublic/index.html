Mokele-mbembe

dragon · Central African Republic · Central Africa

Mokele-mbembe is a legendary water-dwelling entity believed to inhabit the Congo River Basin, with traditions extending into the Central African Republic among the Bayaka pygmy peoples. The name derives from the Lingala language and translates as 'one who stops the flow of rivers', referring to the creature's supposed preference for nesting in river bends. Descriptions vary widely but commonly depict a large quadrupedal creature with smooth skin, a long neck and tail, and a small head resembling a sauropod dinosaur.
