Crane Guardian of Ojibwe

guardian · Canada · North America

No 'crane guardian' spirit is attested in Canadian Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) tradition. The crane is instead the Ajijaak doodem, the leadership clan that, with the Loon Clan, guided the Anishinaabe and produced hereditary chiefs along the Great Lakes. This entry appears to be an AI-generated invention built from genuine clan symbolism.
