Moose Nature-Spirit (Mixtec)

nature-spirit · Madagascar · East Africa

Not independently attested in Malagasy tradition. Mixtec are Indigenous people of Oaxaca, Mexico—not Madagascar. Moose absent from both Madagascar and Mesoamerica. Malagasy nature spirits include kalanoro (forest dwarf), kokolampo (water spirits), razana (ancestors). Cross-regional fabrication conflating Mexican Mixtec, North American moose, and Madagascar geography.
