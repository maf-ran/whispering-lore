Crane Nature-Spirit of Shoshone

nature-spirit · United States · North America

No crane water-spirit is attested in Shoshone tradition. While Shoshone oral literature includes beings tied to water and lakes, a crane spirit that guards alpine lakes or replenishes fish does not appear in documented sources. This entry duplicates the unattested crane-spirit template and appears to be a fabricated invention.
