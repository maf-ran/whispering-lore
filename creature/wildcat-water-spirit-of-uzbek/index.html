Wildcat Water-Spirit of Uzbek

water-spirit · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a water-spirit in Uzbek folklore. Cats in Central Asian folk belief are domestic creatures whose favor or displeasure is read in omens, but no feline spirit of rivers or lakes appears in Uzbek tradition. The record's ocean habitat and storm-calling powers are modern inventions built on the wildcat's secretive character.
