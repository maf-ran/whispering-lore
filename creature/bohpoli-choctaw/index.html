Bohpoli

spirit · United States · North America

Bohpoli, meaning the Thrower, also called Kowi Anukasha, the Forest Dweller, are little people of the Choctaw who live alone in the thick, dark woods. About two feet tall, they throw sticks at travelers and are mischievous, yet they are said to share medicine knowledge with those who treat the woods with respect. Documented by Swanton.
