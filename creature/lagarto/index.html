Lagarto

dragon · Portugal · Mediterranean

The Lagarto is a dragon-like creature from Portuguese folklore, deeply rooted in the country's medieval mythological traditions. The name derives from the Portuguese word for 'lizard,' and these creatures are often described as large reptilian monsters that dwell in caves, rivers, and remote mountains throughout Portugal. Unlike the fire-breathing dragons of northern European tradition, the Portuguese Lagarto is more closely associated with water and is often depicted as a giant lizard or crocodile-like beast.
