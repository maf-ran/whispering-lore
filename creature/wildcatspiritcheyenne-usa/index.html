Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat spirit in Cheyenne tradition. Cheyenne mythology knows no wildcat spirit or deity; its sacred figures are the culture hero Sweet Medicine and the trickster Veeho (Wihio) the spider, along with the spirits of the buffalo, wolf, and eagle. This 'Cheyenne wildcat' record is a duplicate misattribution with no basis in recorded Cheyenne folklore.
