Salmon Trickster of Shona

trickster · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Shona tradition. Salmon do not exist in Zimbabwe. The entry is fabricated with templated "guardian of caves" motif. Shona trickster figures are hare (tsuro) or baboon; snip water spirits are njuzu (mermaids).
