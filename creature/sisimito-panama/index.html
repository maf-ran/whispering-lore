Sisimito

humanoid · Panama · Mesoamerica

The Sisimito is a cryptid from Panamanian and Central American folklore, described as a bipedal gorilla-like humanoid with long unkempt fur and a human-like head. In Panama, the creature is known among the Ngäbe and Kuna indigenous peoples of the Darién Gap and the dense forests of western Panama. The Sisimito is a guardian of the deep forest, often associated with the protection of sacred lands and ancestral territories. Legends depict it as shy but dangerous when provoked.
