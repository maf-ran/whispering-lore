Cougar Guardian of Aymara

guardian · Bolivia · South America

In Aymara cosmology from the Andean highlands of Bolivia, Peru, and Chile, the cougar guardian (puma) is a sacred animal spirit associated with mountain springs, water sources, and the pastoral economy that sustains Aymara communities. The Aymara people, who have inhabited the altiplano around Lake Titicaca for millennia, consider the puma one of the most spiritually significant animals in their religious system.
