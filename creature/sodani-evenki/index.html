Sodani

human · Russia · Central Asia

Sodani is the hero of an Evenki heroic legend recorded in the scholarly series Monuments of the Folklore of the Peoples of Siberia and the Far East as 'The Brave Hero Sodani.' A bogatyr of the taiga who defends his people through strength and courage, he belongs to the Evenki tradition of heroic storytelling rather than to the divine pantheon.
