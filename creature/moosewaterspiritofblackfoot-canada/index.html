Moose Water-Spirit of Blackfoot

water-spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a water-spirit of Blackfoot folklore. No source records a moose water-spirit among the Siksika; Blackfoot water and underground traditions concern medicine power and sacred bundles rather than moose. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
