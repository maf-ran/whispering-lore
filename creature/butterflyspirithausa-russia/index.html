Butterfly Spirit (Hausa)

spirit · Russia · Slavic

No butterfly spirit is attested in Hausa tradition, and the entry is mislabeled as Russian: the Hausa are a West African people. The genuine Hausa spirit world is the bori system of iskoki, organized in the twelve houses of Jangare, but no butterfly iskoki is documented. The description mixes an invented desert-guardian narrative with garbled text.
