Coyote Spirit of Tlingit

spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a Tlingit spirit. Coyote is a Plains and Plateau figure and does not appear in Tlingit oral tradition; Tlingit creation and transformation roles belong to Raven, who stole the sun and set the world in order. No Tlingit coyote spirit is recorded in documented sources.
