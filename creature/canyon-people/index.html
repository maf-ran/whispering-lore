Canyon People

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature as spirit beings. 'Canyon People' (Tseyi Ndee) is the name of an Apache band, not a class of guardian spirits; the entry invents deep-gorge inhabitants and ancestor-mediator roles with no named narrative. Genuine Apache place-spirits exist in oral tradition, but none is recorded under this name.
