Krasue

ghost · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Thai floating head Ghost - 'กระสือ' (krase). Head detaches and Flows with trailing Organs (entrails). Famous in Thai horror. Lived normal human life before - became Krasue after death. Active at Night - seeks blood/raw flesh. Whistling cry is signature. Warnings: used to warn pregnant women (fear maternal death). Cursed being - eternally Hungry. Daytime: appears normal, lives normal. Night: transforms. Can appear also in Laos ('Krasue'), Cambodia ('Krasu'). Connected to feminine vulnerability and death
