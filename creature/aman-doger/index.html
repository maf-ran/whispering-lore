Aman Doger

sea-monster · Sudan · North Africa

Aman Doger is a sea monster from Javanese and Indonesian mythology, specifically from the folklore of Java, Indonesia. The name combines aman meaning safe or peaceful and doger referring to a type of mythical creature. This sea monster is said to inhabit the waters around the Indonesian archipelago, particularly the Java Sea. In Indonesian maritime folklore, sea monsters like Aman Doger represent the dangers of the ocean and serve as explanations for shipwrecks, disappearances at sea, and the mysterious forces that sailors must contend with.
