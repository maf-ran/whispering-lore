Crop Eaters

spirit · Canada · North America

Mysterious spirit beings from Blackfoot (Niitsitapi) tradition in the Canadian plains that appear during harvest season to consume crops in the fields. Serving as a spiritual check on human greed, the Crop Eaters ensure that the Blackfoot people remember their obligation to share abundance with the community. They are considered messengers of the Creator, testing whether individuals will honor the traditional value of generosity or succumb to selfishness In Canada folklore, this figure is known among the Canada people.
