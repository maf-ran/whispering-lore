Gnefro

spirit · Italy · Mediterranean

The Gnefro is a legendary household spirit from the folklore of the Umbria region of central Italy, particularly associated with the Valnerina valley, the Marmore Falls area, Lake Piediluco, and the territory between Terni and Rieti. These diminutive creatures are deeply rooted in Italian rural folk tradition, representing a local variation of the widespread European belief in domestic spirits. The gnefro shares characteristics with Roman lares and penates, Slavic domovoi, and Germanic kobolds, suggesting a common Indo-European origin for household spirit beliefs.
