Bison Trickster (Olmec)

trickster · Brazil · South America

No Olmec or Brazilian bison trickster exists: bison are absent from Mesoamerica and South America, and the original text itself concedes the figure is not Olmec, describing a fabricated 'South African synthesis.' The entry is a mislabeled invention with no documentary basis.
