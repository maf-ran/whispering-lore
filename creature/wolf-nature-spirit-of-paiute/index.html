Wolf Nature-Spirit of Paiute

nature-spirit · United States · North America

Wolf is a creator and culture hero in Paiute tradition, called Isa, who usually appears in human form and made the world and its people in the beginning; Coyote, his younger brother, follows him and brings folly, death, and change. Wolf is the elder and wiser of the pair, respected as the one who set the world in order.
