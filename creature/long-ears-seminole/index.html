Long-Ears

monster · United States · North America

Long-Ears is a monster of Seminole tradition, a four-footed, wolf-headed creature the size of a donkey with long, pointed ears and an insatiable hunger. It hunts people in the swamps and forests of Florida, and its great speed, cunning, and tracking ability make it one of the most feared beings in Seminole legend.
