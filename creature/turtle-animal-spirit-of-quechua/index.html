Turtle Animal-Spirit of Quechua

animal-spirit · Peru · South America

Not independently attested in Quechua tradition as a named 'turtle animal-spirit guardian of swamps'. Andean cosmology includes animal spirits (apus, wak'as) but no specific turtle guardian of swamps is documented in ethnographic literature (Métraux, Urton, Salomon). The turtle appears in some origin myths but not as a distinct named entity with this role.
