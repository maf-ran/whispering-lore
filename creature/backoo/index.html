Backoo

spirit · Guyana · South America

The Backoo is a unique entity from Guyanese folklore, blending African, Indigenous, and colonial influences into a singularly strange creature. This spirit is described as having a body that is half wood and half flesh, with an abnormally large head and missing kneecaps. The Backoo is bound to serve those who know the correct rituals to summon and control it, typically through a contract or pact that exchanges lifelong servitude for some form of payment, often the owner's soul.
