Bodu Naga (Maldivian Sea Serpent)

serpent · Maldives · South Asia

Bodu Naga is a great serpent spirit in Maldivian folklore that inhabits lagoons, reefs, and deep channels between the Maldivian atolls. The name means 'Great Serpent' in Divehi (Bodu = big/great, Naga = serpent). In pre-Islamic Maldives, nagas were revered as powerful nature spirits associated with the waters that surrounded the atolls. Bodu Naga is described as a guardian of the sea, protecting certain channels, passes, and reefs.
