Wolf Ghost of Choctaw

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf ghost in Choctaw folklore. The attested Choctaw spirit beings include Nalusa Falaya, a shadowy figure who slips along trails to attack travelers, and the little forest people Bohpoli and Kowi Anukasha; no wolf specter or wolf ghost appears in the recorded corpus documented by Swanton.
