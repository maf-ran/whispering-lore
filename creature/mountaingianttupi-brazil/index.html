Mountain Giant (Tupi)

giant · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a named entity in Tupi-Guarani tradition. Tupi mythology includes forest giants like Curupira (red-haired dwarf), Caipora, and Mapinguari (giant sloth), but no specific 'Mountain Giant' deity by that name. The generic description suggests a conflation of multiple beings.
