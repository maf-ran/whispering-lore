Mộng

spirit · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Vietnamese folklore; 'Mộng' is simply the Vietnamese word for dream. The recorded ability to send prophetic or frightful dreams matches the attested Vietnamese belief in báo mộng, a dream message—most often from an ancestor or deceased relative seeking help, atonement, or a ritual act.
