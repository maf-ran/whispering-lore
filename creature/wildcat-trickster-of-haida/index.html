Wildcat Trickster of Haida

trickster · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat trickster in Haida tradition. The Haida trickster and transformer is Raven (Xhuuya), a shapeshifter who stole the sun, moon, and stars and whose adventures shaped the world. No wildcat or bobcat occupies this role in Haida mythology. The record is a generic trickster misattribution, since Northwest Coast trickster lore belongs to Raven, not to any wildcat.
