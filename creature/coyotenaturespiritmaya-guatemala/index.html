Coyote Nature-Spirit (Maya)

nature-spirit · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Among Maya communities of the Yucatan Peninsula the coyote nature-spirit occupies a distinct role as a protector of aluxob, the small forest spirits inhabiting unspoiled wilderness. These forest guardians work in hierarchical relationship: aluxob tend to immediate needs of specific trees and plants while the coyote nature-spirit oversees larger territories and coordinates multiple aluxob under its jurisdiction.
