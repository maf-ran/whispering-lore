Rolling Calf

spirit · Barbados · Caribbean

The Rolling Calf is a feared Barbadian and Jamaican duppy (malevolent spirit) taking the form of a headless bull or goat with blazing red eyes, trailing chains, and foul stench. It roams villages at night, chasing and terrifying travelers. A classic duppy variant documented in Caribbean folklore collections.
