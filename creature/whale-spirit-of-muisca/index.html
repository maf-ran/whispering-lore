Whale Spirit of Muisca

spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Muisca tradition. Muisca are inland highland Andean people; whales do not exist in their territory or mythology. Muisca water beings: Lake Iguaque (origin), Lake Guatavita (El Dorado ritual), Monster of Lake Tota. No whale spirit in Ocampo López or chroniclers.
