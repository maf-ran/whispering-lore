Owl Giant of Yurok

giant · United States · North America

No owl giant appears in Yurok folklore. Yurok traditional narratives collected by Kroeber and others center on figures such as the Bear and her fawns, the theft of fire, and the origin of salmon; owls play no giant or guardian role. The entry is a template invention with no basis in Yurok tradition.
