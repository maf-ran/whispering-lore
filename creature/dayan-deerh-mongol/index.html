Dayan Deerh

deity · Mongolia · Central Asia

Dayan Deerh is a shamanic deity of fertility and protection from the Khövsgöl region of northern Mongolia, venerated in both yellow and black shamanism. Worshipped as a mountain god turned shaman-spirit and community guardian, his cult centers on Tsagaannuur and the Deerkhiin Khüree shrine, where his statue and renewed ongons receive offerings for cattle and children.
