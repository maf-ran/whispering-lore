Waaq

deity · Somalia · East Africa

Waaq (Somali). is a deity. Waaq is the pre-Islamic supreme sky god of the Cushitic peoples of Somalia and the Horn of Africa, associated with rain, fertility, and the natural forces of the sky.. Source: Somali oral tradition, pre-Islamic Somali mythology, Wikipedia - Somali mythology
