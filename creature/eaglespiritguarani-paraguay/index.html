Eagle Spirit (Guarani)

spirit · Paraguay · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Guarani tradition. This Paraguay variant repeats the misassigned Aztec founding myth of Tenochtitlan and adds the unfounded claim of 'Guarani-influenced traditions of Mexico' exchanging eagle beliefs; no Guarani eagle spirit is documented in Paraguay. The entry is a Mesoamerican legend mislabeled as Guarani.
