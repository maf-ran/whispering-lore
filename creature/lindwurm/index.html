Lindwurm

dragon · Germany · Germanic

The Lindwurm is the German variant of the Lindworm dragon, a great serpentine beast that haunts the forests, caves, and river valleys of the Germanic lands. In German folklore, Lindwurms are often depicted as guardians of ancient hoards or as the cursed forms of human beings punished for their sins. The city of Linz, Austria, takes its name from a Lindwurm legend in which a great dragon terrorized the Danube valley until slain by a brave knight.
