Set (God of Chaos and Storms)

deity · Egypt · Africa

Set is the Egyptian god of chaos, storms, violence, and the desert. He murdered his brother Osiris out of jealousy and fought his nephew Horus for the throne of Egypt for 80 years. Depicted as a mysterious animal with a curved snout (the Set animal), he was associated with the barren desert, foreign lands, and upheaval. Despite being a god of chaos, Set was not purely evil in Egyptian thought; snip he served a necessary role. He stood at the prow of Ra's solar barque each night, using his spear to defeat the chaos serpent Apophis. During certain periods, especially under the Hyksos and Ramessi
