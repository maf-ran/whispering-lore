Gewi

deity · South Africa · Africa

Gewi (Unknown). is a deity. Gewi (also spelled Gcwi) is the second son of Kaang (Cagn), the supreme creator god in San (Bushman) mythology. He appears alongside his elder brother Cogaz in the creation myths of the Maluti Bushmen. Source: San (Bushman) mythology; Specimens of Bushman Folklore (Bleek & Lloyd, 1911)
