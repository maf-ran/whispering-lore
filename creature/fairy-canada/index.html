Fairy

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Not attested in Canadian folklore. The input's description is purely Polish-Slavic material — rusałka, boginka, dziwożona, południca and świtezianka — mistakenly filed under 'Canada.' Indigenous Canada has genuine 'Little People' figures (Wabanaki mikumwess, Ojibwe/Cree memegwesi, Mi'kmaq puglatmu'j), but no generic European fairy belongs to Canadian tradition.
