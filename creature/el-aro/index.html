El Aro

ghost · Honduras · Mesoamerica

El Aro is a terrifying phantom from Honduran folklore, described as a misty, wheel-shaped apparition that roams the countryside at night. Its name derives from the Spanish word for 'hoop' or 'ring,' referencing its distinctive shape. According to legends from the departments of Comayagua and Francisco Morazán, El Aro is a tormented spirit that appears to solitary travelers on dark roads. Some versions say it is the soul of a man who committed terrible sins, condemned to roll endlessly through the night until it frightens enough sinners to repentance.
