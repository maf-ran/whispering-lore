Badger Trickster of Aymara

trickster · Bolivia · South America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is absent from the high Andes and plays no role in Aymara tradition, whose trickster is the fox. Andean cosmology's three worlds, hanan pacha, kay pacha, and ukhu pacha, structure Aymara belief, but no badger figure appears in it.
