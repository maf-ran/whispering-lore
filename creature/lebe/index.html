Lebe

spirit · Mali · West Africa

Lebe is an ancestral spirit and earth deity of the Dogon people of Mali. According to Dogon cosmology, Lebe was the first ancestor, who died and was reborn as a serpent and whose body is identified with the earth. Linked with the sacred serpent and cycles of regeneration, he is honoured through offerings and rites in the Dogon ritual year.
