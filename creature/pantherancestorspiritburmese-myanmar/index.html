Panther Ancestor-Spirit (Burmese)

ancestor-spirit · Myanmar · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Burmese folklore; no source documents a 'Panther Ancestor-Spirit.' The underlying idea is real: Karen and Tangshang (Naga) traditions hold that ancestor spirits may appear as big cats, and nats ride tigers, but the big-cat ancestor figures of Myanmar are tigers, and no panther figure bears this name.
