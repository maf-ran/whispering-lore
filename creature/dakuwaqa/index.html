Dakuwaqa

animal-spirit · Fiji · Pacific

Dakuwaqa is the powerful shark deity of Fijian mythology, a shape-shifting sea god greatly respected by fishermen and navigators across the archipelago. His name derives from the Fijian words 'daku' (back) and 'waqa' (canoe), evoking the image of a shark guardian positioned astern of boats. According to legend, Dakuwaqa was once a prideful and ambitious god who sought to conquer all of Fiji's islands, challenging every guardian spirit he encountered. After a humbling defeat by the giant octopus goddess Rokobakaniceva at Kadavu Island, he swore an oath to protect fishermen and became a benevole
