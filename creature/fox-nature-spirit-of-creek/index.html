Fox Nature-Spirit of Creek

nature-spirit · United States · North America

No fox nature-spirit is attested in Creek tradition. The fox appears in the Creek story of 'The Fox and the Fry Bread' as a clever character, but it carries no spirit role. Creek spiritual beings are tied to fire, the Green Corn Ceremony and beings such as the tie-snakes; a fox nature-spirit is not documented.
