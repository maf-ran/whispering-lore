Shurpanakha (Rakshasi)

demon · India · South Asia

Shurpanakha ('she whose fingernails are like winnowing fans') is a rakshasi (demoness) in the Hindu epic Ramayana, sister of the Lankan king Ravana and daughter of sage Vishrava and the rakshasi Kaikeshi. Her role in the epic, though small, is pivotal — her encounter with Rama and Lakshmana in the Panchavati forest triggered the chain of events leading to Sita's abduction and the great war of Lanka.
