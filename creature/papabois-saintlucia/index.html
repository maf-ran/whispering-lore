Papa Bois

nature-spirit · Saint Lucia · Caribbean

Papa Bois, 'Father of the Wood' in French Creole, is the guardian of the forest in Saint Lucian and wider Caribbean lore: an old, muscular man with cloven hooves, horns and a beard of living leaves who warns animals with a cow's horn and punishes wasteful hunters, sometimes by shapeshifting into a deer to lead them astray.
