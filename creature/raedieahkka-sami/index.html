Raedieahkka

goddess · Norway · Sami

Raedieahkka (also Radien-akka) is the supreme goddess in Sami mythology, the wife of the high god Radien-attje. Together with her husband, she created the world and the human soul — they are described as the two divinities who jointly created the cosmos. She is the mother of Rana Niejta (the spring goddess) and Radien-pardne (the son who executes his father's will). She forms part of the Sami divine trinity alongside her husband and son.
