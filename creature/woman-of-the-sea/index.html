Woman of the Sea

spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as 'Woman of the Sea' in Haida or generic Canadian folklore. Coastal Northwest Coast traditions do include sea-connected women, such as the Haida Ocean Woman of recent retellings and Property Woman, who grants wealth, but no named 'Woman of the Sea' spirit appears in the ethnographic record. The entry is vague and unattested.
