Penyimbang Laut (Water Spirit of Brunei)

spirit · Brunei · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in Bruneian folklore. No scholarly or primary source documents a water spirit called 'Penyimbang Laut'; the figure appears only in a blog source. Brunei's documented river legend is Jong Batu, the tale of a son turned to stone.
