Pontianak (Singapore)

ghost · Singapore · Southeast Asia

The Pontianak holds a unique place in Singaporean culture as the most iconic supernatural figure across the island's multi-ethnic population. She is the vengeful spirit of a woman who died during childbirth, depicted with long black hair, a white dress, and a terrifying true form with fangs and claws. The 1957 Cathay-Keris film 'Pontianak' premiered at midnight and ran for nearly three months in Singapore cinemas, becoming a cultural phenomenon that bridged Malay, Chinese, and Indian communities
