Famadihana

ancestor-spirit · Madagascar · East Africa

The Famadihana is not a creature but the honored ancestor spirit at the center of the Malagasy "turning of the bones" ceremony, a funerary tradition practiced primarily by the Merina and Betsileo peoples of Madagascar's central highlands. Known as razana (ancestors), these spirits remain intimately involved in the daily lives of their descendants long after death.
