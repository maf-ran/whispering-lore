Butu

spirit · Sierra Leone · West Africa

Butu is a masked spirit figure from the RaGbenle secret society of Sierra Leone, representing the ancestral spirits and supernatural forces that underpin traditional social order. The RaGbenle society is a powerful regulatory institution among the Mende and related peoples, responsible for maintaining law, conducting initiations, and acting as intermediaries between the living and the spirit world.
