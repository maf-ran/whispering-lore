Barmanou

cryptid · Pakistan · South Asia

The Barmanou is a bipedal humanoid primate cryptid reported in the mountainous Chitral and Karakoram regions of northern Pakistan. Shepherds and locals have documented sightings for generations, describing a creature that straddles the line between ape and wild man. Spanish zoologist Jordi Magraner conducted extensive field research in the 1990s collecting over fifty firsthand sighting accounts that all identified the Barmanou as an apelike hominid distinct from known primate species.
