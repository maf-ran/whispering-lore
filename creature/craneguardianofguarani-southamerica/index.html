Crane Guardian of Guarani

guardian · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested in Guaraní tradition. The seven legendary monsters (Teju Jagua, Mbói Tu'ĩ, Moñái, Jasy Jatere, Kurupi, Ao Ao, Luison) include no crane guardian. Cranes (jabiru, wood stork) inhabit the Pantanal/Chaco but hold no specific guardian role in Guaraní mythology. Entity appears fabricated; duplicate of Paraguay entry with Brazil country assignment.
