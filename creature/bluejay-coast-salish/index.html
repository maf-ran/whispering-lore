Bluejay Coast Salish

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Blue Jay is the trickster of Coast Salish and southern Salishan tradition, a clever but reckless bird-being whose adventures cross between the human and spirit worlds. Told by the Saanich and neighboring peoples, he is helpful yet foolish, and his brushes with the supernatural carry warnings about proper conduct.
