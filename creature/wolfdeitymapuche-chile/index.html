Wolf Deity (Mapuche)

deity · Chile · South America

The 'Wolf Deity (Mapuche)' is not attested in Mapuche tradition. Wolves are absent from South America; snip the Mapuche have no wolf deity. Mapuche cosmology features Ngünechen, the ngen (nature owners), wekufe spirits, and animal spirits like the fox (Ngürü) and puma. The record misapplies Northern Hemisphere wolf motifs.
