Raven Ghost of Kikuyu

ghost · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested in Kikuyu tradition. Ravens (Corvus corax) are not native to Kenya; the region has pied crows and white-necked ravens, but no raven ghost tradition. Kikuyu avian spirits involve the sacred mugumo tree and Ngai atop Mount Kenya. Appears fabricated, possibly conflating Norse/Celtic raven lore (Odin's Huginn/Muninn) with Kikuyu spirituality.
