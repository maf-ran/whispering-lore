Trollkjerring

troll · Norway · Nordic

Trollkjerring (Norwegian: 'troll wife/witch') is a female troll figure in Norwegian folklore, a specific subtype of the broader troll tradition. She is an ugly, magical troll woman associated with high mountain peaks, known for prophecy and stone manipulation. Not a distinct species — the term specifies gender within the troll tradition.
