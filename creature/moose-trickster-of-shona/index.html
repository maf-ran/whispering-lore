Moose Trickster of Shona

trickster · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Shona tradition. Moose are not native to Africa. This trickster entry repeats the fabricated moose template with "guardian of caves" motif. Shona trickster figures are typically hare (tsuro) or baboon, not moose.
