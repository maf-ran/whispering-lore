Crane Spirit of Shona

spirit · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

The Grey Crowned Crane is Zimbabwe's national bird and holds cultural significance in Shona tradition as a messenger between the living and ancestors (midzimu) on the granite hills. While not a formal deity, crane spirits appear in Shona cosmology as intermediaries carrying prayers to Mwari. The specific named entity lacks direct ethnographic citation but draws on attested avian symbolism.
