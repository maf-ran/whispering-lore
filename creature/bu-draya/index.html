Bu Draya

sea-monster · Bahrain · Middle East and North Africa

Bu Draya (also Bu Daryā, meaning 'Father of the Sea' from Persian) is a terrifying sea jinn from Bahraini and Arabian Gulf folklore. Believed to be a malevolent water spirit, Bu Draya appears in the darkest nights to prey on pearl divers and sailors. Described as a huge half-human, half-amphibious creature, it summons winds and controls waters to capsize boats. Its most notorious trick is mimicking drowning cries at night — when a sailor jumps in to rescue what sounds like a comrade, Bu Draya seizes and devours them.
