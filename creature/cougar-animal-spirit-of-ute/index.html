Cougar Animal-Spirit of Ute

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Cougar Animal-Spirit from Ute tradition is a revered guardian figure in the spiritual beliefs of the Ute people of the Rocky Mountain region, spanning parts of present-day Colorado, Utah, and New Mexico. The Ute, who have inhabited the Southern Rocky Mountains for centuries, hold the mountain lion in high regard as a powerful spirit animal representing leadership, courage, and strategic thinking.
