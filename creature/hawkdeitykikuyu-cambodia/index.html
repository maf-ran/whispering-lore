Hawk Deity (Kikuyu)

deity · Cambodia · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested. The entry mismatches the Kikuyu people of Kenya with Cambodia and claims the hawk is a sacred intermediary to Ngai, a status no Kikuyu source supports. Kikuyu cosmology centres on Ngai (Mwene-Nyaga) dwelling on Mount Kenya, while birds appear only in minor folktales: the attested tale 'The Hawk and the Hen' explains why hawks prey on chickens. No hawk deity exists.
