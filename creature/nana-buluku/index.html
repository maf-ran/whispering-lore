Nana Buluku

deity · Benin · Africa

Nana Buluku (Unknown). is a deity. Nana Buluku is the supreme creator god in the Fon and Ewe cosmologies of Benin and Togo. This androgynous deity gave birth to the twin gods Mawu (moon) and Lisa (sun) who govern creation.. Source: Fon and Ewe cosmology; Dahomean mythology
