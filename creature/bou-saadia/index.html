Bou Saadia

spirit · Tunisia · North Africa

Bou Saadia (Father of Saadia) is a tragic folkloric figure from Tunisian tradition, particularly associated with the medina of Tunis and Stambeli ritual performances. According to legend, Bou Saadia came from sub-Saharan Africa in search of his daughter Saadia, who was sold into slavery in Tunis. He is described as a man who went mad with grief, wandering door to door in the medina, humming and dancing, searching for his lost child.
