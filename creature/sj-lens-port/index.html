Själens port

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

Själens port (Soul Door/Gate) is not a spirit but a Swedish folk belief concept: the soul can leave the body during deep sleep, illness, or trance, traveling to other places or meeting the dead. If the body is moved while the soul is away, the person dies. Documented by Ebbe Schön as a framework for dreams, sleep paralysis, and near-death experiences. A belief, not a being.
