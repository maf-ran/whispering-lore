Coyote Spirit of Muisca

spirit · Colombia · South America

In Muisca mythology from the Altiplano Cundiboyacense of Colombia the coyote spirit of the mountains belongs to the hierarchy of nature spirits inhabiting sacred peaks and paramo highlands. The Muisca people, famous for the legend of El Dorado, recognized mountains surrounding the Bogota savanna as dwelling places of powerful spirits governing weather, water flow from heights, and soil fertility in valleys below.
