Bison Ancestor-Spirit (Guarani)

ancestor-spirit · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested: the entry assigns a Guarani spirit to Kenya and describes bison, animals of North America absent from both Guarani and Kenyan traditions. Guarani cosmology centers on figures such as Tupã and the twin culture heroes, while East African ancestors are honored through ngoma spirits.
