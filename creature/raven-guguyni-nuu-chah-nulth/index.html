Raven Guguyni Nuu Chah Nulth

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Raven is the great culture hero of the Nuu-chah-nulth (Nootka), a benevolent transformer and trickster who gave the people fire, light, and the shape of the world. In Nuu-chah-nulth tradition he works alongside the Twin Transformers Kwe'kustepsep, who changed people, animals, and the land into their present forms.
