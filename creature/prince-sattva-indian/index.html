Prince Sattva (Mahasattva Jataka)

spirit · India · South Asia

Prince Sattva (also known as Prince Mahasattva) is the Bodhisattva in one of the most famous Jataka tales — the story of the starving tigress, from the Jataka-mala (Garland of Birth Stories) by Aryasura. In this past life, the future Buddha was born as Prince Sattva, the youngest son of a king. While wandering in the forest with his two elder brothers, they discovered a starving tigress near a mountain gorge.
