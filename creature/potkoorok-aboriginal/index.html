Potkoorok

spirit · Australia · Pacific

Potkoorok is a water spirit from Aboriginal Australian mythology, particularly known among the peoples of coastal and riverine regions. This freshwater spirit dwells in billabongs, lagoons, and slow-moving rivers, where it is said to create the distinctive bubbling and swirling patterns seen on the water's surface. Potkoorok is generally considered a mischievous but not malevolent spirit, known for playing tricks on people who venture too close to its watery home. It may create ripples to lure curious onlookers or cause floating logs to spin in circles. In some traditions, Potkoorok is a guard
