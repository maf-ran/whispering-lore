Fox Spirit of Muisca

spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No fox messenger or divination figure appears in recorded Muisca mythology; the claimed role as messenger of Sue and Chia and the links to Lake Guatavita and El Dorado are invented. Muisca tradition preserves no named fox spirit.
