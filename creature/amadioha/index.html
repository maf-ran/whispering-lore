Amadioha

deity · Nigeria · Africa

Amadioha (Unknown). is a deity. Amadioha is the god of thunder and lightning in the Igbo pantheon of Nigeria. He is considered one of the most powerful deities, serving as the arbiter of justice who punishes wrongdoers with thunderb. Source: Igbo mythology; Nigerian oral tradition
