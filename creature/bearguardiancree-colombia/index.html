Bear Guardian (Cree)

guardian · Colombia · South America

The bear (maskwa) is a powerful medicine being in Cree tradition, a spirit of healing and ceremony and a guide encountered in dreams and visions; legends such as Mistahi-Maskwa tell of a great bear devoted to medicine and the sharing of knowledge. The entry's metadata mislabels the Cree as Colombian, and its lake-guardian framing is unsupported.
