Dievas

god · Lithuania · Baltic

Dievas is the primordial supreme sky god in Baltic mythology, worshipped by ancient Lithuanians, Latvians, and Old Prussians as the creator of the universe and ruler of all deities. His name derives from the Proto-Indo-European sky father *Dyēus, making him cognate with the Vedic Dyaus Pita, Greek Zeus, and Roman Jupiter. Dievas is the god of light, sky, prosperity, and wealth, and alongside Perkūnas (thunder god) and Potrimpo, forms the supreme triad of Baltic deities.
