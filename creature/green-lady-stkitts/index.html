Green Lady

spirit · Saint Kitts and Nevis · Caribbean

The Green Lady is a spirit from St. Kitts folklore who manifests as the legendary Green Flash — a rare optical phenomenon seen at sunset when a bright green light flashes for a brief second over the horizon. According to local legend, the Green Lady was once a beautiful and kind-hearted woman who lived on the island centuries ago but was tragically separated from her true love by societal norms and family feuds.
