El Añá

serpent · Argentina · South America

El Añá is a legendary lake serpent from the folklore of the Andean lakes region of Argentina, particularly in the provinces of Neuquén and Río Negro. The name derives from Mapuche and Guarani mythologies, where it refers to malevolent water spirits. Unlike the more famous Nahuelito, El Añá is described as a hybrid creature combining serpentine and cervine features.
