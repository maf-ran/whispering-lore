Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)

spirit · Paraguay · South America

The 'Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)' is not attested in Paraguayan or Guaraní tradition. The Cheyenne are a North American Plains people (Montana, Oklahoma) with no historical presence in Paraguay. The closest Guaraní analogue is the Yaguareté-Abá (jaguar shapeshifter) or forest guardian spirits (Yvyraija) documented by Métraux.
