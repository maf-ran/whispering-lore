Deer Deity of Zapotec

deity · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Among the Zapotec people of Oaxaca, the Deer Deity is a river guardian closely tied to the life-giving waters that flow through the mountain valleys of southern Mexico. This benevolent spirit governs the freshwater ecosystems that sustain both agriculture and wildlife, acting as an intermediary between the Zapotec people and the great rain god Cocijo. The deer has particular significance in Zapotec culture as a symbol of the forest bounty and the sacred connection between the human world and the spirit realm that dwells in the misty cloud forests
