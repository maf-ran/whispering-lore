Gara Yakka (Sinhalese Demon of Disease)

demon · Sri Lanka · South Asia

Gara Yakka is a prominent demon in Sinhalese folklore, part of the Daha Ata Sanniya (the eighteen maladies) — a group of eighteen diseases believed to be caused by demonic possession. He is portrayed as a fearsome Yaksha (demon) who was once a mortal wrongdoer transformed into a malevolent spirit. Gara Yakka is particularly associated with causing childhood illnesses, fevers, convulsions, and nightmares.
