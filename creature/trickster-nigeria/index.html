Trickster

spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Not independently attested as a distinct figure. The source record is a generic placeholder that repeats text about Kiowa tradition while labelled Nigeria, an apparent copy-paste error. In Nigerian folklore the classic tricksters are the tortoise (Ijapa among the Yoruba, Mbe among the Igbo) and Gizo the spider in Hausa tales; no separate 'Trickster' entity exists.
