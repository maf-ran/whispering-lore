Buda

shapeshifter · Ethiopia · East Africa

Buda (Ge'ez: ቡዳ) is a powerful and feared figure in Eritrean and Ethiopian folk religion, representing the supernatural power of the evil eye combined with the ability to shapeshift into a hyena. The belief is deeply rooted in the highland cultures of Eritrea, particularly among Tigrinya-speaking communities. Buda is believed to be a power held and wielded by specific individuals, often associated with marginalized social groups such as metalworkers (täbbäb), potters, tanners, and weavers.
