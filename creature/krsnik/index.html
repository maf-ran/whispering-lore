Krsnik

hero · Slovenia · Slavic

Krsnik is a vampire hunter from Slovenian and Croatian folklore, a special individual born with supernatural abilities that enable them to see, track, and destroy vampires and other undead creatures. The name krsnik derives from krs meaning cross or baptism, reflecting the Christian folk beliefs mixed with older pagan traditions that characterize these warriors. Krsniks are believed to be born with a caul or born on a Saturday, signs that mark them as chosen for this sacred duty.
