Isis (Goddess of Magic)

deity · Egypt · Africa

Isis is the greatest goddess of Egyptian mythology, known as the goddess of magic, motherhood, healing, and protection. Daughter of Geb and Nut, sister and wife of Osiris, mother of Horus. When her brother Set murdered Osiris and scattered his body, Isis tirelessly searched for the pieces, reconstructed him, and briefly revived him to conceive Horus. She is the greatest magician in Egyptian mythology; she tricked Ra into revealing his secret name, gaining power over all creation.
