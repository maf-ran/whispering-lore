Uchtsiti (Sun Father)

celestial · United States · North America

Uchtsiti (Sun Father) is the supreme creator deity in Acoma Pueblo tradition. He created the world from a clot of his own blood and created Tsichtinako to guide the first beings. Uchtsiti is the father of Iatiku and Nautsiti, and through them all of creation. He is associated with light, life, and the generative masculine principle.
