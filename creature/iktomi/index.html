Iktomi

trickster · United States · North America

Iktomi is the spider trickster of Lakota mythology, a complex figure who is both clever and foolish. Born fully grown with the body of a spider and the cunning of a man, Iktomi is known for his clever schemes and predictions. However, his selfishness and mischievous nature often backfire—in one tale he tried to keep fish soup for himself rather than share; in another he angered a boulder and was crushed, only to be freed by bats who were turned into stones.
