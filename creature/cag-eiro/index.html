Cagüeiro

shapeshifter · Cuba · Caribbean

Cagüeiro is a shape-shifting outlaw figure from Cuban folklore, particularly associated with the rural countryside and Afro-Cuban folk traditions. Unlike many supernatural creatures that are inherently magical or mythological in origin, the Cagüeiro is a human who has gained the power to transform into any animal or object through the use of magical incantations and pacts with dark forces.
