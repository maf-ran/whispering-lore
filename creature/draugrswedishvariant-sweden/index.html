Draugr (Swedish variant)

undead · Sweden · Nordic

The Swedish variant of the Draugr shares many characteristics with its Norse cousins but has developed distinct features shaped by Sweden's unique coastal geography and maritime traditions. In Swedish folklore, the Draugr, also known regionally as drög or draug, is specifically associated with the Baltic Sea coast and the archipelagoes of Sweden, where it appears as the undead spirit of sailors and fishermen who died at sea.
