Coyote Spirit of Tlingit

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a Tlingit spirit. Coyote is unknown in Tlingit tradition, whose great beings are Raven, Eagle, Bear and Killer Whale; the trickster-transformer is Raven. The USA duplicate repeats the unattested coyote claim, which appears in no documented Tlingit narrative.
