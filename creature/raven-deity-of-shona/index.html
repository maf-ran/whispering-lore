Raven Deity of Shona

deity · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested as a deity in Shona tradition. Ravens occur in southern Africa but no ethnographic source documents a "Raven Deity" guarding lakes. The description appears templated. Shona avian spirits include the fish eagle (hungwe) as Mwari's messenger, not a raven lake deity.
