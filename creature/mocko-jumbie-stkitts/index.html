Mocko Jumbie

spirit · Saint Kitts and Nevis · Caribbean

The Mocko Jumbie is a towering stilt-walking spirit dancer and a central figure in St. Kitts and Nevis Carnival and folklore traditions. The name combines 'Moko' (a West African deity who guards, heals, and delivers retribution) and 'Jumbie' (a Caribbean spirit). Dressed in colorful flowing costumes and balancing on 6-to-8-foot stilts, Mocko Jumbies are believed to be ancestral spirits who walk among the living, providing protection and warding off evil.
