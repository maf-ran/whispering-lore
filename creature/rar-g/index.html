Raróg

nature-spirit · Poland · Slavic

Raróg is a fire spirit of Slavic folklore, often appearing as a falcon or a fiery whirlwind, associated with the hearth and the sky. In Polish and Czech folk belief Raróg can be summoned or kept as a household familiar, bringing luck or serving its keeper, and some scholars connect his name to the legendary Obodrite stronghold of Reric.
