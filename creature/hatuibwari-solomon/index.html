Hatuibwari

deity · Solomon Islands · Pacific

Hatuibwari is a powerful dragon-like being from the mythology of the Solomon Islands, particularly among the people of Guadalcanal. Described as a massive serpent with the features of a dragon, Hatuibwari is a creator and cultural hero who shaped the islands and taught humans essential skills. In some accounts, Hatuibwari is a dual-sex being who created the first humans from clay and gave them fire, tools, and knowledge of agriculture.
