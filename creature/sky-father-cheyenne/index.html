Sky Father (Cheyenne)

deity · United States · North America

The Cheyenne creator Maheo, also called Heammawihio, 'the one who lives above,' is the supreme being who made the world from the void, molding the earth brought by a diving duck into the land. He is a spirit without human form, rarely personified, and in some tellings he first made people immortal before changing death to its present form.
