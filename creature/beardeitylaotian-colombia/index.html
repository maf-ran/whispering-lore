Bear Deity (Laotian)

deity · Colombia · South America

The 'Bear Deity (Laotian)' is not attested in Colombian or Muisca tradition. The description describes a Laotian (Southeast Asian) moon bear deity tied to lunar cycles and rice agriculture—completely unrelated to Colombia. The Muisca had no bear deities; snip spectacled bears (ukumari) exist in the Andes but lack a moon-bear lunar deity in Muisca sources.
