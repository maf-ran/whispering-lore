Hawk Guardian (Tupi)

guardian · Ghana · West Africa

Not independently attested and geographically incoherent: the Tupi are an indigenous South American people, and no 'Hawk Guardian' of Tupi origin appears in Ghanaian tradition or documented diaspora history. The entry's account of 'Tupi descendants' reaching Africa through colonial history and carrying a hawk guardian is unsupported and appears fabricated.
