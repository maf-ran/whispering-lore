Panther Deity (Guarani)

deity · Paraguay · South America

Partially attested but misnamed. Guarani mythology includes the Celestial Jaguar (Jaguarete, Jaguareté) — a primordial jaguar deity associated with the sky, twins, and ancestral souls — but 'panther' (leopard) is an Old World term. No 'Panther Deity' as distinct entity; the Celestial Jaguar fills this role.
