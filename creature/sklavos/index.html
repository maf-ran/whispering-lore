Sklavos

ghost · Albania · Balkan

Not independently attested in documented Albanian folklore or academic sources. No reference describes an Albanian ghost 'Sklavos,' a shrouded figure haunting the living until buried; searches return only Greek σκλάβος ('slave') and the Albanian kukudh, a different undead being. The recorded traits resemble general Balkan burial beliefs and appear to derive from modern speculation.
