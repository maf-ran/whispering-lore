The Great Nile Serpent

spirit · Uganda · East Africa

The Great Nile Serpent is a colossal, iridescent-scaled serpent spirit guarding the Victoria Nile's deep trenches in Ugandan Nilotic/Bantu oral tradition. It emerges during lunar eclipses or ecological crisis, controlling currents and demanding tribute. Associated with Ruhanga (creator) and the underworld ruler.
