Fox Deity (Ashanti)

deity · Ghana · West Africa

Not independently attested. No fox deity appears in documented Ashanti (Akan) cosmology or folklore; the Ashanti trickster is Anansi the spider, and the fox plays no recorded role in Akan religion. The entry's account of offerings made to a 'fox deity' for discretion and careful planning is unsupported and appears invented.
