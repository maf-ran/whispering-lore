Vindrå

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

The Vindrå is the ethereal spirit of the wind, a volatile and invisible entity that governs the currents of the air. Unlike the grounded rån, the Vindrå is an entity of pure movement and change. In Swedish lore, the Vindrå is responsible for the sudden gusts that blow out candles or the gentle breezes that carry scents from distant lands. She is often sought after by sailors and farmers who wish to influence the wind for their own benefit, though she is notoriously fickle and demands unusual offerings in exchange for her cooperation
