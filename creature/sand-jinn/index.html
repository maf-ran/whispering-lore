Sand Jinn

jinn · Mauritania · West Africa

Sand Jinn are desert spirits in Mauritanian folklore, believed to be made of smokeless fire who dwell beneath the dunes and protect lost travelers or lead them astray. The belief reflects Islamic cosmology adapted to the Sahara, where survival depends on the mercy of the desert and its unseen inhabitants.
