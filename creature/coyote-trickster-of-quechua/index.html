Coyote Trickster of Quechua

trickster · Peru · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Quechua tradition. Coyotes are a North American species absent from the Andes, so no Quechua coyote trickster exists; the genuine Andean canid of Quechua tales is the fox (atoq), who appears in stories such as the fox and the condor. The input's framing of lakes as Ukhu Pacha portals is real Andean cosmology, but no coyote guards them.
