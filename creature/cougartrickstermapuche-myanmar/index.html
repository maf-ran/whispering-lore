Cougar Trickster (Mapuche)

trickster · Myanmar · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested. No documented tradition records a 'cougar trickster' among the Mapuche, and none connects such a figure to Myanmar: the entry crosses a South American people (Mapuche) with a Southeast Asian country, and its 'trapial wekufe' name and Antu culture-hero narrative are unsupported. Cougars do not occur in Myanmar, and no Burmese source names this being.
