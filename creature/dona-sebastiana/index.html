Doña Sebastiana

spirit · United States · North America

Doña Sebastiana is a New Mexican Hispano death figure — a skeletal woman with bow and arrows in a wooden cart, whose rattling is heard before a death. Carved as bultos in the santero tradition, she is identified with Santa Muerte in some stories and in others a separate figure who grants sinners a final confession.
