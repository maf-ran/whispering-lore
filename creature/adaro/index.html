Adaro

spirit · Solomon Islands · Pacific

The Adaro are among the most fearsome sea-spirits of Melanesian mythology, originating from the Arosi people of Makira, Solomon Islands. They are described as human-headed fish-men with gills behind the ears, a long tail-fin, a dorsal horn, and a spear-like projection emerging from the head. Adaro are malevolent beings who prey upon fishermen who venture too far or violate cultural taboos. They have the power to launch poisonous flying fish at victims and can traverse rainbows and waterspouts, symbolic pathways between ocean and sky.
