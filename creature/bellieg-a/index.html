Belliegħa

water-spirit · Malta · Mediterranean

Belliegħa, also known as Il-Belliegħa, is a female water spirit from Maltese folklore that inhabits wells, springs, and underground water sources across the Maltese islands. In Maltese tradition, which blends ancient Mediterranean beliefs with Christian elements, Belliegħa is a dangerous but fascinating figure who guards the freshwater sources that are precious in the dry Mediterranean climate of Malta.
