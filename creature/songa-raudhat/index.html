Songa Raudhat

spirit · Comoros · East Africa

'Songa Raudhat' appears only in a single modern online retelling of a Comorian tale about djinns and a hidden sea garden; the name is unattested in collections of Comorian oral literature. Comorian tradition does preserve djinn and sea-spirit lore, but this named figure has no documented or ethnographic record and reads as a recent invention.
