Mulan (Unknown). is a human. Hua Mulan is a legendary figure from Chinese folklore who disguised herself as a man to take her father's place in the army. Her story appears in the Ballad of Mulan, composed during the Northern Wei . Source: Ballad of Mulan (Northern Wei period); Chinese literary tradition
