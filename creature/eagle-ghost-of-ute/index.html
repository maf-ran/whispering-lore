Eagle Ghost of Ute

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a ghost of Ute folklore. The eagle is deeply sacred to the Ute — eagle feathers carry prayer and eagle spirits are honored in ceremony — but no Ute eagle-ghost is documented. Ute traditions of the dead involve the spirits of ancestors, not a supernatural eagle revenant.
