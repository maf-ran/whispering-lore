Hawk Spirit of Zapotec

spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In Zapotec tradition of Oaxaca, Mexico, the Hawk Spirit (known as something akin to a sacred raptor being) occupies an important place in the spiritual landscape of the region. The Zapotec people, whose civilization flourished in the Valley of Oaxaca for millennia, have long regarded hawks as sacred messengers connected to the mountain peaks that surround their ancestral homeland. The Hawk Spirit is intimately tied to the concept of spiritual vision and foresight, believed to carry the prayers of the people to the cloud-shrouded peaks where the old gods dwell.
