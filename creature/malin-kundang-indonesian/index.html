Malin Kundang

human · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Malin Kundang is the tragic protagonist of West Sumatra's most famous folktale, a cautionary story about filial ingratitude. A poor boy from a fishing village, Malin Kundang leaves his widowed mother to seek fortune overseas. After years of hardship, he becomes a wealthy merchant, marries a princess, and returns home in a grand ship. His elderly mother, overjoyed, rushes to embrace him, but Malin Kundang, ashamed of his humble origins in front of his wife and crew, cruelly denies knowing her and orders her driven away.
