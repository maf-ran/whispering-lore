Lagarfljótsormur

monster · Iceland · Nordic

The Lagarfljótsormur, also known as the Lagarfljót Worm, is a legendary lake monster said to inhabit Lake Lagarfljót near the town of Egilsstaðir in eastern Iceland. First recorded in 1345 AD in the Icelandic Annals, this creature is one of Iceland's most famous cryptids, comparable to the Loch Ness Monster. According to the folk tale collected by Jón Árnason in 1862, the worm began as a small heath worm or slug that was placed with gold by a young girl hoping to make it grow.
