Guabance

goddess · Puerto Rico · Caribbean

Guabancex is the supreme storm deity of the Taíno people, the indigenous inhabitants of Puerto Rico, Hispaniola, Jamaica, Cuba, and other Caribbean islands. She is a zemi (deity) of chaos and disorder, controlling hurricanes, storms, volcanoes, and earthquakes. Guabancex is considered the violent and destructive aspect of the mother goddess Atabey, representing natures raw, untamed power. Her name is derived from the Taíno words "guaba" (hurricane) and "cex" (center), emphasizing her connection to the most tempestuous weather phenomena.
