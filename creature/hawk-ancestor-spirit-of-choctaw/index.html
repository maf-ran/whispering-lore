Hawk Ancestor-Spirit of Choctaw

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

In Choctaw tradition the hawk is honored for its keen sight and hunting prowess, qualities tied to vision and perception. Choctaw cosmology holds that each person carries a shilup (soul or spirit) and that the natural world is filled with spirit beings. The hawk figures as a symbol of sharp perception; the historic Choctaw homeland was the forested Southeast, not desert or prairie.
