Eagle Animal-Spirit of Cree

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

The Eagle Animal-Spirit holds a position of profound importance in Cree mythology as the sacred guardian of remote cliff faces and high rocky outcrops across the traditional Cree territories of what is now Canada. In Cree spiritual tradition, the eagle is the most revered of all birds, serving as a messenger between humans and the Great Spirit (Kise Manito). The Eagle Animal-Spirit is specifically tasked with protecting cliff habitats and teaching humans the wisdom of living in balance with nature.
