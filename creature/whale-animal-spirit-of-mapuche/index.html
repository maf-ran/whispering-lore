Whale Animal-Spirit of Mapuche

animal-spirit · Chile · South America

Not independently attested in Mapuche tradition. Mapuche territory includes Chilean coast, but 'whale animal-spirit guardian of deserts' is fabricated — Mapuche are temperate rainforest/Andean, not desert dwellers. Mapuche beings: ngen (nature owners), wekufe, Pillan, Coi Coi-Vilu (sea serpent), Pincoya. No whale spirit in Bacigalupo, Faron, or Guevara.
