Raven Giant of Cheyenne

giant · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Cheyenne folklore. No source records a Cheyenne raven giant or spring-guardian; the Cheyenne trickster and transformer is Veeho (Wihio), the spider, who appears as a man in the tales. A 'Raven Giant of Cheyenne' is almost certainly a modern invention or a mistaken transplant of Pacific Northwest Raven material.
