Turtle Trickster of Choctaw

trickster · United States · North America

In Choctaw oral tradition, turtle (Loksi) is a figure who relies on patience, a hard shell and quick wits to outsmart larger animals, a role paralleled in turtle tales across the Southeastern woodlands. Choctaw legend materials document Loksi stories. The record's 'shapeshifting trickster' framing overstates a role that sources describe as clever and persistent rather than deceptive.
