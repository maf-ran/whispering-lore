Yatoka (Sun Father)

celestial · United States · North America

Yatoka, the Sun Father, is the Zuni celestial deity, the form taken by Áwonawílona, the All-Container, when he made himself into the sun. From the mist clouds of the Sun Father gathered the Great Waters from which Earth Mother (Áwitelin Tsíta) and Sky Father (Ápoyan Tá'chu) emerged, beginning the Zuni creation.
