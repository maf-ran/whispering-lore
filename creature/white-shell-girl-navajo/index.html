Yoołgaii Asdzą́ą́ (White Shell Girl)

spirit · United States · North America

Yoołgaii Asdzą́ą́ (White Shell Girl) is the original form of Changing Woman in Navajo tradition. She was born in the Fourth World and later matured into Changing Woman. She was sought in marriage by the Sun (Jóhonaa'éí), who disguised himself as a handsome warrior. After many trials, she agreed to marry him and traveled to his house in the eastern horizon made of turquoise and white shell. She bore the Sun's children who became the Hero Twins.
