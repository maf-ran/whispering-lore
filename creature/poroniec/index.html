Poroniec

demon · Poland · Slavic

Poroniec is a Polish demon born from stillborn or unbaptized infants. It haunts crossroads and graveyards, causing stillbirths and infant mortality. If buried under the house threshold, it becomes a protective kłobuk house spirit instead. Related to the Scandinavian myling. Reflects Christian anxiety about unbaptized souls.
