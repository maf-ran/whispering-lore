The Helpful Fish

animal-spirit · Qatar · Middle East and North Africa

The helpful fish of Qatari coastal lore is a benevolent water spirit that guards tide pools and the shallow Gulf waters, guiding honest fishermen to good catches. Often identified with the two-banded porgy known locally as al-fisaikra, it embodies foresight and clear speech, rewarding respect and punishing greed. Tales of the guide fish remain part of Gulf oral heritage.
