Nainas

spirit · Norway · Sami

Nainas is a Sami mythological figure associated with the Northern Lights (Aurora Borealis). In Sami tradition, the Northern Lights are sometimes seen as warrior spirits or celestial beings, and Nainas embodies this concept. In the romantic Sami folk tale 'The Daughter of the Moon and the Son of the Sun,' Nainas leads a band of Northern Light warriors who enter an empty house by the sea at dusk. When the Sun sees Nainas and pins him with a heat shaft, the Moon's daughter Niekia shields Nainas and is seized by the Sun.
