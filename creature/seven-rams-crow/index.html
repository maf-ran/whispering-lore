Seven Rams

spirit · United States · North America

Seven Rams are magnificent spirit animals in Crow mythology. A Crow man of immense ability and power once encountered them in the mountains. Confident in his own strength, he thought he could overpower them. But the Seven Rams taught him humility—despite his great power, the man learned he must respect the Sweat Lodge and its ceremonies, for the lodge's power was greater than any individual's. The Rams demonstrated that true strength comes from spiritual purity and connection to the Creator, not from physical prowess
