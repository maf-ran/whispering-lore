Nei Tituaa

spirit · Kiribati · Pacific

Nei Tituaa, also recorded as Nei Tituaabine or Titua'abine, is a creator spirit of Kiribati (Gilbertese) mythology, a being of the celestial realm tied to weather and life-giving power. One tradition casts her as a lightning goddess with red skin and blue eyes; another as a tree spirit. She is counted among the first divine beings shaped by Nareau's creative work.
