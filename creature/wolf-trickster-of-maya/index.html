Wolf Trickster of Maya

trickster · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested: wolves never lived in the Maya lowlands, and the Maya trickster and nawal is the coyote, not a wolf. No Maya story features a wolf trickster guarding the hills. The record misattributes a generic wolf-trickster template to the Maya.
