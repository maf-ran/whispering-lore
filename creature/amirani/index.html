Amirani

monster · Georgia · Caucasus

Amirani is a culture hero and demigod from Georgian mythology, comparable to the Greek Prometheus or the Armenian Mher. He is the son of a hunter and a goddess, blessed with superhuman strength from birth. The Amirani epic is one of the most important cycles in Georgian mythology, telling the story of a hero who challenged the gods and suffered eternal punishment. According to the myth, Amirani was chained to a rock in the Caucasus Mountains as punishment for stealing divine fire and revealing its secrets to humanity.
