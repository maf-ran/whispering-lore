Zin Kibaru

spirit · Niger · West Africa

Zin Kibaru is a water spirit from Songhay-Zarma mythology of the Niger River region. The term Zin refers to a class of genies inhabiting water bodies, and Kibaru specifically designates a powerful water demon that demands respect and offerings. These spirits are associated with the Holeys, possession dances of the Songhay-Zarma people. Zin Kibaru are believed to inhabit the deepest parts of the Niger River and its tributaries.
