Pacific Northwest

spirit · United States · North America

Not a genuine mythological being. 'Pacific Northwest' is a geographic region, not a creature of Tlingit or any other tradition, and no source names a spirit by this title. The real Tlingit spiritual world is rich with animal guardians, the land-otter people and spirits of forest and coast, but the entity described in this record does not exist.
