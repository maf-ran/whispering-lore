Underjordiske

elf · Norway · Nordic

Underjordiske (the underground/hidden people) is a collective term in Norwegian folklore for the various supernatural beings who live beneath the earth or inside mountains and hills. This category includes huldrefolk, haugfolk, tusser, and other hidden beings. The underjordiske live in a parallel world that mirrors human society - they farm, fish, trade, marry, have children, and hold celebrations.
