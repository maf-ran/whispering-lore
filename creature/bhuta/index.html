Bhuta

ghost · India · South Asia

A supernatural creature and restless ghost from the Indian subcontinent, deeply rooted in Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions that span thousands of years. The Bhuta is the spirit of a deceased person who, due to a violent death, unfulfilled desires, improper funeral rites, or unfinished earthly business, remains trapped between worlds instead of proceeding to the afterlife or reincarnation.
