Hi'iaka

deity · United States · Pacific

The youngest and most beloved sister of Pele, the volcano goddess. Hi'iaka is the goddess of hula, medicine, and healing. Her full name Hi'iakaikapoliopele means 'Hi'iaka in the bosom of Pele' as she was born from Pele's thoughts. She is known for her epic journey to Kauai to retrieve Pele's lover Lohiau, during which she demonstrated her power and compassion. Hi'iaka is a shapeshifter who can travel at supernatural speed, control the forests, and commune with spirits. She is a skilled hula dancer and is considered the patron goddess of hula practitioners
