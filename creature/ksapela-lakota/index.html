Ksapela

spirit · United States · North America

Ksapela (Little Wisdom) is a minor spirit in Lakota mythology, also known as Gnaski. He is the son of Iya (the destructive storm monster) and Unk (the spirit of contention). Ksapela managed to outwit and perfectly mimic Ksa (the spirit of wisdom) to the point that the two were indistinguishable, earning him the name 'little wisdom.' He appears in the Great Race story as the wise one who proposed the competition between the four-legged animals and the two-legged birds to determine who would eat whom
