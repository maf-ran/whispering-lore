Great Buzzard

spirit · United States · North America

In the Cherokee account of creation, the Great Buzzard flew over the soft, newly dried earth. Each downward beat of his enormous wings created a valley, each upward stroke a mountain, shaping the rolling landscape of the Cherokee homeland. The story, told in Mooney's 'How the World Was Made', explains the mountains and valleys of Cherokee country.
