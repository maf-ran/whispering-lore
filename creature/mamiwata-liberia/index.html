Mami Wata

water-spirit · Liberia · West Africa

Mami Wata of Liberia appears as a beautiful water spirit who grants wealth, beauty, and luck in exchange for offerings and sexual celibacy. Her cult blends indigenous water deities with Hindu, Islamic, and colonial European imagery, making her one of the most complex and widely recognized water spirits along the West African coast.
