Eagle Deity of Passamaquoddy

deity · United States · North America

In Passamaquoddy mythology, the eagle is a revered bird of great spiritual significance, serving as a messenger between humans and the Creator. The Passamaquoddy people of Maine and the Maritime provinces regard the eagle as a guardian figure associated with the sky world and the upper realms. Eagles are seen as wise beings who taught humans important lessons about respect for nature and balance in life.
