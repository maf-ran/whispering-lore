High John the Conqueror

folk-hero · United States · North America

High John the Conqueror is a legendary figure in African American folklore, a trickster hero and spirit of liberation who moved invisibly among the enslaved. According to traditions collected by Zora Neale Hurston, High John was an African prince sold into slavery whose spirit could never be broken. When the whip fell, John would 'take the spirit of his people out of their bodies' so they could not feel the pain.
