Kuçedra

dragon · Albania · Balkan

Kuçedra is the multi-headed dragon of Albanian folklore, an enormous fire-breathing serpent of deep caves and mountain peaks that brings storms and chaos. Demanding tribute and battling heroes and spirits alike, it personifies destructive natural forces that saints, weather-lords, and heroes must overcome. The name is an older form of kulshedra, the dragon-queen of Albanian oral tradition.
