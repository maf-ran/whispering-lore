Kamapua'a

trickster · Hawaii · Pacific

Kamapua'a, the 'hog child,' is a Hawaiian kupua, a demigod of dual human and pig nature, born on Oahu to Hina and the chief Kahikiula. Associated with Lono and agriculture, he is defiant and untamed, famous for his shapeshifting between human, hog, fish, and plant forms and for his stormy pursuit of the fire goddess Pele.
