Cannibal Giants

giant · United States · North America

Cannibal Giants are fearsome creatures from the oral traditions of various First Nations peoples across Canada, particularly among the Algonquian and Athabaskan-speaking tribes of the Subarctic and northern woodlands. Known by different names in different languages, these beings share a common archetype: giant humanoid monsters driven by an insatiable hunger for human flesh that embody the harsh and unforgiving nature of the northern wilderness.
