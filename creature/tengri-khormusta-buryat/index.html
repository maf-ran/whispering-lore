Tengri Khormusta

deity · Russia · Central Asia

Khormusta, king of the western heavens, is the supreme sky deity of Buryat Tengriism and father of the hero Abai Geser. A radiant warrior-king amid celestial hosts, he wages eternal war against the eastern gods of darkness. His scattering of the slain Atai Ulan's body, meant to prevent resurrection, instead seeded the demons that plague the world.
