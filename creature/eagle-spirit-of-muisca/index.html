Eagle Spirit of Muisca

spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Muisca tradition. The Temple of the Sun at Sogamoso and the deities Sué and Chía are genuine elements of Muisca cosmology, but no source records an 'Eagle Spirit' presiding over that sanctuary or serving as a celestial messenger. The entry grafts a generic Andean eagle figure onto documented Muisca institutions.
