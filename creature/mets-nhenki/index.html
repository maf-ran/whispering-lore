Metsänhenki

spirit · Finland · Nordic

Metsänhenki (Finnish: 'forest spirit' or 'forest self') is not a distinct creature but the animistic concept of the forest's living essence in Finnish tradition. It can manifest as a person, light, or presence, misleads travelers, and is the forest itself — not a being within it. Central to the Finnish relationship with nature.
