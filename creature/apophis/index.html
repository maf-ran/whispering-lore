Apophis

serpent · Egypt · North Africa

The giant serpent of Egyptian chaos, known as Apophis or Apep, who represents the primal forces of darkness, disorder, and non-being that constantly threaten the cosmic order established by the sun god Ra. This immense entity dwells in the Duat, the Egyptian underworld, and is the eternal enemy of Ma'at, the principle of truth, balance, and cosmic order. According to myth, Apophis existed before creation in the primeval waters of Nun and was born from Ra's umbilical cord or from the saliva of the goddess Neith.
