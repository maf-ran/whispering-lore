Skeljungur

sea-monster · Iceland · Nordic

Skeljungur (Shell-Whale) is a unique illhveli (evil whale) in Icelandic folklore distinguished by being covered in rattling shells and being the only edible wicked whale. Skeljungur is 20-45 meters long, fat-bodied, with short flippers and no dorsal fin. Its body is covered with shells that rattle loudly as it moves — the sound gives it away. It sleeps vertically with its head above water. The shells make it constantly itchy, so it rubs its head against coastal rocks. Skeljungur actively blocks ships and flips them with its fins.
