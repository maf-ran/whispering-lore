Turtle Animal-Spirit of Kazakh

animal-spirit · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested in Kazakh folklore. This figure is described as a turtle animal-spirit and guardian of the cliffs, tied to forests and the Central Asian wilderness, protecting the land and teaching strength. Kazakh tradition centres on the horse, the eagle, and the wolf rather than any turtle guardian, and no source documents such a being.
