Hang Tuah (The Invincible Warrior)

hero · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Hang Tuah is the most legendary warrior in Malay history and folklore, the paragon of Malay chivalry and loyalty. According to the Malay Annals (Sejarah Melayu), he served as the Laksamana (admiral) of the Melaka Sultanate during its golden age in the 15th century under Sultan Mansur Shah. Hang Tuah was unmatched in silat, diplomacy, and statesmanship. Together with four other legendary warriors (Hang Jebat, Hang Kasturi, Hang Lekir, and Hang Lekiu), he defended Melaka's sovereignty.
