Little Giver

spirit · United States · North America

Little Giver, also called Fastachee, is the corn spirit of the Miccosukee and Seminole, who usually appeared as a small dwarf and gave the people the gift of corn and of medicine. He was thereafter always honored at the harvest, and the Green Corn Ceremony recalls his generosity.
