Jólakötturinn

beast · Iceland · Nordic

Jólakötturinn (the Yule Cat) is a gigantic black cat from Icelandic Christmas folklore, the pet of Grýla and the Yule Lads. The Yule Cat roams the Icelandic countryside on Christmas Eve, eating anyone who has not received new clothes before Christmas. The tradition is tied to the Icelandic wool industry — farmers would give new clothes to workers who finished processing the autumn wool before Christmas.
