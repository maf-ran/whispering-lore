Nian

beast · China · East Asia and Pacific

Nian is the lion-like monster of Chinese folklore said to emerge each Lunar New Year's Eve to attack villages, its wide mouth and sharp teeth terrifying the countryside. Fearless of predators, it is driven away by the color red, firecrackers, and loud noises — the origin of red decorations and fireworks in Chinese New Year celebrations. Its legend anchors the festival's most visible customs.
