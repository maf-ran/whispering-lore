Bai Ze

beast · China · East Asia and Pacific

Bai Ze is a legendary chimeric creature from Chinese mythology, first recorded during the Tang Dynasty, known for its extraordinary wisdom and supernatural perception. According to ancient Chinese texts, Bai Ze appeared to the Yellow Emperor, Huangdi, during his reign around 2600 BCE, revealing the secrets of all the supernatural beings in the world. This encounter resulted in the creation of an encyclopedia of spirits and demons, a text that became foundational for Chinese demonology and exorcism practices.
