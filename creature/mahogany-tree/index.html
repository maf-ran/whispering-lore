Mahogany Tree

deity · Antigua and Barbuda · Caribbean

The Mahogany Tree in Antiguan folklore is a sacred tree believed to be the dwelling of protective ancestral spirits. A powerful chief sacrificed himself, transforming into the tree to guard his people. Symbolizes strength, endurance, and protection. Reflects West African tree-spirit beliefs (e.g., Iroko, Silk Cotton) transplanted to the Caribbean.
