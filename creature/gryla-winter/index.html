Winter Grýla

ogre · Iceland · Nordic

Winter Grýla appears to be a modern seasonal elaboration of the Icelandic ogress Grýla, emphasizing her winter activity. No independent traditional source distinguishes a 'Winter Grýla' as a separate being from the main Grýla figure, who is already intrinsically winter-associated (Christmas). This likely reflects a modern categorization rather than a distinct folk variant.
