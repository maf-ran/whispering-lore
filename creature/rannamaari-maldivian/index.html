Rannamaari (Sea Demon of the Maldives)

demon · Maldives · South Asia

Rannamaari is a terrifying sea demon from Maldivian folklore that rose from the ocean each month to demand a sacrifice of a virgin girl. According to legend, before the Islamization of the Maldives in 1153 CE, the Buddhist inhabitants of Malé were terrorized by this demon. Each month, a virgin was dressed in fine clothes and left alone overnight in a temple on the eastern seafront of Malé — by dawn, she was found violated and dead.
