Wadlim

water-spirit · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested as a Guatemalan water spirit. No source records a jungle river guardian named 'Wadlim' who keeps the fish and drowns offenders; searches surface only Maine surnames and place names. The nearest attested Maya and Central American water beings that punish disrespect for rivers include the Siguanaba and the guardian serpent-spirits of rivers and cenotes.
