Cyclone (wind spirit)

spirit · United States · North America

Cyclone is a primordial wind spirit in Jicarilla Apache creation accounts, present in the darkness before the world was lit. Cyclone spied on the Hactcin, the ancestral spirits, and told Holy Boy that White Hactcin held the sun; with that knowledge Holy Boy obtained the sun and the moon, and daylight came into being.
