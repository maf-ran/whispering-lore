Hirsiz İb

spirit · Turkey · Middle East and North Africa

'Hırsız İb' ('Thief İb') is not attested in Turkish folklore. Web research returns only dictionary and lexical results for 'hırsız' (thief); no oral-tradition source, folkloric collection, or scholarly work documents a spirit or patron of thieves by this name. The entry appears to be an invented figure built from the Turkish word for thief.
