Cadejo Chico

animal-spirit · El Salvador · Mesoamerica

Cadejo Chico is the smaller, more mischievous counterpart to the Cadejo Grande in Central American folklore, particularly prevalent in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, and Costa Rica. Unlike its larger, malevolent relative, the Cadejo Chico is known for its playful and sometimes helpful nature, though it can still be dangerous to those who mistreat it. Rooted in Maya and Nahua mythology, the cadejo legend stems from ancient beliefs in animal spirit companions known as naguals.
