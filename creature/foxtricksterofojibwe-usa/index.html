Fox Trickster of Ojibwe

trickster · United States · North America

This Canadian variant of the Ojibwe fox trickster reflects the distinct traditions of Ojibwe communities living north of the Great Lakes in Ontario and Manitoba. While sharing many characteristics with its American counterpart, this northern manifestation is more closely tied to the extensive network of Canadian Shield waterways and the long, harsh winters that define the region. The trickster appears in stories from Ojibwe elders in communities along the shores of Lake Superior and Lake Huron, where the relationship between humans and the spirit world is mediated through the seasonal rhythms
