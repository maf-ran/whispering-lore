Moose Trickster of Blackfoot

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a trickster of Blackfoot (Siksika) folklore. The Blackfoot trickster is Napi, Old Man, the bumbling yet powerful being who shaped the world and its customs through his deeds. No source records a moose trickster in Blackfoot tradition. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
