Phi Kam

ghost · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Thai folklore; searches locate no 'Phi Kam.' The recorded suicide ghost who haunts the site of a self-inflicted death matches the attested phi tai hong, spirits of those who die suddenly or violently—including by suicide—whose unfinished lives leave them restless, but no documented figure bears this name.
