Kongamato

monster · Zambia · Southern Africa

The Kongamato is a legendary flying creature from Zambian folklore, described as a large, bat-like monster that inhabits the swamps and rivers of the remote regions of Zambia, Angola, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. The name kongamato means "overwhelmer of boats" in the Kaonde language, reflecting the creatures reputation for attacking and capsizing watercraft. This cryptid has captured the imagination of both local populations and Western explorers, with reported sightings dating from the colonial era through the present day. The Kongamato is often compared to the prehistoric pterosaur
