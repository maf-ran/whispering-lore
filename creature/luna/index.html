Luna

spirit · Belgium · Germanic

Luna is a Walloon (Belgian) lunar spirit — a woman who walks the countryside at full moon, granting blessings and curses. She reflects the moon's sway over human behavior (lunacy). The name comes from Latin luna; the concept echoes Romance/Celtic lunar lore in Wallonia. Not a forest spirit — the input's archetype is wrong. She is a nocturnal wanderer of moonlight and rural paths.
