Termite Animal-Spirit (Komi)

animal-spirit · Russia · Slavic

Not independently attested: termites do not occur in the boreal forests of the Komi Republic, and no termite ancestor-spirit appears in documented Komi tradition. The entry likely conflates Komi nature guardians with an unrelated insect totem; no real analogue is recorded.
