Kapre

giant · Philippines · Southeast Asia

The Kapre is a towering tree giant from Philippine mythology, one of the most well-known supernatural beings in Filipino folklore. These massive creatures are said to inhabit ancient trees such as acacias, mangoes, bamboo, and balete trees throughout the Philippines. While often appearing frightening, kapre are not necessarily evil, but rather complex beings that can be either benevolent or mischievous depending on how they are treated.
