Fox Giant of Aztec

giant · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No fox giant exists in Aztec (Mexica) myth; the text itself conflates the fox with the tlacuache (opossum) and invents Tezcatlipoca associations and underworld-guide roles. These claims trace to AI content farms, not to Mesoamerican sources.
