Anqa (Mythical Bird of Arabia)

mythical-bird · Saudi Arabia · Middle East and North Africa

The Anqa is a golden, fabulous female bird in pre-Islamic Arab mythology, described as immensely beautiful and colorful with a long neck, a human-like face, four pairs of wings, and a whiteness in its neck. Created by God with all perfections, she lives alone on Mount Qaf, flies far away, and only appears once in ages. According to Zakariya al-Qazwini's 'Wonders of Creation', the Anqa lives for 1,700 years, mates at 500 years of age, and its chick remains in the egg for 125 years.
