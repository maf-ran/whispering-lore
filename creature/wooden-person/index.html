Wooden Person

spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a 'Wooden Person' spirit in Haida folklore. In related Kwakwaka'wakw tradition, Raven fashions the first man and woman from wood and clay, and Haida Raven likewise fashions people; these are episodes within Raven's creation work, not a distinct spirit. The record appears to distill that motif without a tribal basis.
