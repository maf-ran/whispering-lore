Pah

deity · United States · North America

Pah is the Moon god in Pawnee mythology, placed in the heavens by Tirawa as one of the last divine creations. Along with Shakuru (the Sun), Pah governs the night sky. The Sun and Moon produced a son who was placed on Earth as the ancestor of the Pawnee people. Pah is associated with the night, women's cycles, and the rhythm of time. In some traditions, the Moon is considered female, representing the feminine principle of creation alongside the feminine Evening Star. The Moon's phases mark the timing of ceremonies
