Bison Trickster of Zulu

trickster · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested. Bison are a North American animal absent from Southern Africa, and no Zulu tradition records a bison trickster or a cliff-guardian of that name; the Drakensberg account is generic template prose. Zulu trickster-lore centres on the child uHlakanyana, the jackal and the hare, while the African buffalo (inyathi) marks chiefly power, not spirit.
