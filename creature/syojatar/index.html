Syöjätär

monster · Finland · Nordic

Syöjätär is a Finnish ogress from runic songs and Ganander's Mythologia Fennica (1789). Mother of snakes, lizards, wolves, wasps, and diseases; her spit births serpents, stones are her 'heart's core'. In folktales she replaces royal children with animals and is burned in a sauna trap.
