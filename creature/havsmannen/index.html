Havsmannen

mermaid · Sweden · Nordic

The Havsmannen is the elusive male counterpart to the Sjöjungfru, a powerful merman who rules the deeper currents of the Swedish coast. Unlike the seductive sirens, the Havsmannen is often portrayed as a stern herald of the sea, possessing the ability to predict weather patterns and shipwrecks. He is a guardian of the ocean's secrets, often interacting with humans only to warn them of impending doom or to demand respect for the maritime laws. His presence is often marked by a sudden change in current or the appearance of unusual fish near the shore
