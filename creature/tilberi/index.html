Tilberi

monster · Iceland · Nordic

Tilberi (also Snakkur) is a worm-like magical creature created by women in Icelandic witchcraft traditions to steal milk from neighbors' cows. The tilberi was made from a stolen dead man's rib and grey wool, hidden between the woman's breasts and fed with stolen communion wine for three Sundays. Once created, it was sent to suckle from the woman's inner thigh, creating a wart-like mark where it fed.
