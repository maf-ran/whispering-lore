Bear Trickster (Inuit)

trickster · Canada · North America

There is no bear trickster in Inuit tradition; the Arctic trickster figures are Raven and Fox, while the polar bear (Nanuk) is a revered, powerful being in its own right. The entry's description even concedes this, framing the 'Bear Trickster' as a composite that does not correspond to any attested Inuit figure. Polar-bear respect is real; this named figure is not.
