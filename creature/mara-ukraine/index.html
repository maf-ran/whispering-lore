Mara

spirit · Ukraine · Slavic

Mara (mora) is a Slavic night spirit who torments sleepers, pressing on their chests and causing nightmares and sleep paralysis. In Ukrainian folk belief she appears as a shadowy woman or animal and can be warded off with prayers, salt, or blessed objects; her name is linked to the crushing motion of the night and to the winter goddess Marzanna.
