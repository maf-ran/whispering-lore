Pet Nat

ghost · Bangladesh · South Asia

Not independently attested in Bengali tradition as 'Pet Nat'. The name likely conflates 'petni' (female ghost of childbirth/violent death) with generic 'pet' (spirit). Bengali folklore documents petni, pishach, and churail as distinct categories. The record's sudden-death ghost haunting riverbanks matches petni or generic bhoot, not a distinct entity called 'Pet Nat'.
