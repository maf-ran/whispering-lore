Krakonoš

giant · Czech Republic · Slavic

Krakonoš, known in German as Rübezahl, is the mountain spirit of the Krkonoše (Giant Mountains) on the Czech-Polish border, guardian of the range and its people. He rewards honesty, hard work, and respect for nature while punishing greed and arrogance with wry, inventive retribution; he remains one of the most beloved figures in Czech folklore.
