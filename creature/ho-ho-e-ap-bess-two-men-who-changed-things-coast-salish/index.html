Ho Ho E Ap Bess Two Men Who Changed Things Coast Salish

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Although often filed under Coast Salish, the Ho-ho-e-ap-bess are the Makah Twin Transformers, the Two Men Who Changed Things, who in Makah legend traveled the world reshaping beings and landscape into their present forms. Brothers of the sun and the moon, their journeys explain the world as it is today.
