Rounce

nature-spirit · Saint Vincent and the Grenadines · Caribbean

The Rounce is a malevolent spirit in the folklore of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, said to grunt like a pig and carry people away if they are out at night. Tradition holds that someone seized by a Rounce must be beaten with a stick, counting the blows, since passing the set count summons a rival Rounce. It is classed among the island's night-terror spirits.
