Kirkelam

ghost · Denmark · Nordic

Kirkelamet (the Church Lamb) is a ghostly lamb that appears in Danish churchyards and rural roads as a death omen. Unlike other church foundation spirits, the Kirkelam represents Christ as the Lamb of God, yet its appearance brings ill tidings rather than comfort. The Kirkelam would appear from the churchyard at dusk and run toward a specific house. If it rubs against your leg, you or someone in your immediate family will die within three days.
