Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)

spirit · Chile · South America

The 'Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)' is not attested in Chilean or Mapuche tradition. The Cheyenne are North American Plains people with no presence in Chile. Mapuche tradition features the Ngürü (fox spirit) and ngen (nature owners), but no wildcat spirit matching Cheyenne cosmology. The record appears fabricated.
