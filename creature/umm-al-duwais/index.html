Umm Al Duwais

spirit · United Arab Emirates · Middle East and North Africa

Umm Al Duwais is the seductive, deadly jinniyyah of Emirati folklore: a stunningly beautiful woman in rich silks and gold whose feet are donkey hooves. Appearing in the desert or near abandoned houses, she captivates men with perfume and beauty and kills them with a single touch. The tale warns against surface beauty and unguarded desire in Emirati oral tradition.
