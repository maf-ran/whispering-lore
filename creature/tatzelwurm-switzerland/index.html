Tatzelwurm

dragon · Switzerland · Germanic

A legendary dragon-like lizard creature from Alpine folklore, also called Stollenwurm, Bergstutz, or Stutzn depending on the region. The Tatzelwurm is described as having the face of a cat with a long serpentine body, four short legs (or sometimes only two forelegs), ranging from one to seven feet in length. Local names in Styria and Tyrol include Bergstutz ('mountain stump'), reflecting its stubby appearance.
