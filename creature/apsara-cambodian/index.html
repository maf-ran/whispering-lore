Apsara (Celestial Dancer)

spirit · Cambodia · Southeast Asia

The Apsara (Khmer: អប្សរា) is a celestial dancer goddess from Khmer mythology, born from the churning of the Ocean of Milk — the Hindu creation myth deeply embedded in Cambodian culture. According to legend, when the devas (gods) and asuras (demons) churned the cosmic ocean using Mount Mandara and the serpent Vasuki, the Apsaras emerged as exquisitely beautiful celestial maidens whose purpose was to entertain the gods with heavenly dance.
