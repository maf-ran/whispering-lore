Gullah Frog

trickster · United States · North America

The Gullah Frog is a trickster figure in Gullah Geechee folklore, smaller and more humble than Br'er Rabbit but equally clever. In many tales, the frog uses his small size and familiarity with the swamp to outwit larger predators. The Gullah Frog appears in stories as a voice of wisdom from the marsh, often offering advice that saves other animals from disaster. His croaking is said to contain messages for those wise enough to understand. The frog is associated with the boundary between land and water, a liminal creature that can move between worlds.
