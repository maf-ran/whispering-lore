Akbaatatdia

creator-deity · United States · North America

Akbaatatdia (One Who Has Made Everything), also known as Iichíkbaalee (First Doer) and Isáahkawuattee (Old Man Coyote), is the supreme Creator in Crow religion. This singular omnipotent god created the entire universe. The Crow believe the universe consists of three worlds: the physical world (the smallest), the spirit world (the intermediate realm), and the third world where God alone dwells. Akbaatatdia is omnipresent and omnipotent. Because God created everything, all of nature is sacred, and all religions are considered valid paths to the same Creator
