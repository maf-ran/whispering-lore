Wildcat Spirit (Cheyenne)

spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Not independently attested in Nigerian tradition. 'Cheyenne' refers to a Native American Plains people (Montana/Oklahoma); wildcat spirits in Cheyenne tradition relate to cave-dwelling guardians. No Cheyenne wildcat spirit exists in Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa, or any Nigerian folklore. Record is a complete cross-continental misattribution.
