Hawk Ghost of Ute

ghost · United States · North America

No hawk ghost is documented in Ute tradition. Ute oral traditions concern the culture hero Sinawav and spirit beings tied to their Great Basin and Plateau homelands, but no spectral hawk guardian of canyons or springs appears in published ethnography. The entry appears to be an invented composite of generic motifs.
