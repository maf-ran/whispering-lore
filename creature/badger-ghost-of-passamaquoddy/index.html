Badger Ghost of Passamaquoddy

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not native to Passamaquoddy territory, and no badger ghost appears in their tradition. Documented Passamaquoddy spirits include the culture hero Glooskap, the storm-bird Pamola, and Kiwahq, the wild twin brother of Glooskap; guardian spirits are sought in dreams but never as a badger.
