Stone Coat

giant · United States · North America

The Stone Coats, or stone giants, are terrible giants of Iroquois tradition, taller than any man and covered in a skin of rock-hard scales (native names include Genonsgwa). They wander the winter lands of the Adirondacks and Alleghenies, and heroes defeat them by striking their one soft spot.
