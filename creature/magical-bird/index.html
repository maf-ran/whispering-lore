Magical Bird

bird · Sweden · Nordic

Refers to Grip, the magical talking bird from the Danish literary fairy tale 'The Bird Grip' (Fuglen Grip), collected by Svend Grundtvig and E.T. Kristensen. Grip aids the hero with guidance and warnings. While talking birds appear in Nordic oral tales (e.g., the raven in Icelandic legends), Grip is specifically a literary fairy-tale figure, not a folkloric being with independent tradition.
