Chut

household-spirit · Belarus · Slavic

Chut is a spectral entity from Scottish Highland folklore, particularly associated with the Orkney and Shetland Islands as well as parts of the northern Scottish mainland. This mysterious being is classified among the many nature spirits and apparitions that populate the rich Celtic and Norse-influenced folklore of northern Scotland. The Chut is considered a harbinger of misfortune, often appearing before storms, accidents, or deaths in fishing communities.
