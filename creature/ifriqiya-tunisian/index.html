Ifriqiya (Tunisian Serpent of Wisdom)

serpent · Tunisia · Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. 'Ifriqiya' is the medieval Arabic name of the North African province (from the Roman Africa, named for the Berber goddess Ifri and the Afri people), not a serpent of Tunisian legend. No Tunisian source describes a guardian dragon of that name; the earthquake and 'city built over its head' details are invented.
