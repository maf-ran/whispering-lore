Atum (Primordial Creator God)

deity · Egypt · Africa

Atum is the primordial creator god of Egyptian mythology, the first deity of the Heliopolitan Ennead. He created himself from the primordial waters of Nun, then masturbated to create Shu (air) and Tefnut (moisture). His tears created humanity. Atum is both male and female (the Great He-She), representing the totality of creation. He is associated with the setting sun (as Atum-Ra). In later periods, he merged with Ra as the sun god.
