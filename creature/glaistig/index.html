Glaistig

fairy · Scotland · Celtic

The Glaistig is a haunting spirit from Scottish mythology, classified as a type of fuath or supernatural being that haunts the Scottish Highlands and islands. Also known as Maighdean Uaine or the Green Maiden, this ambivalent creature appears in legends across Scotland with both benevolent and malevolent traits. The name derives from Scottish Gaelic glas meaning gray, wan, or pale green, reflecting her characteristic coloring.
