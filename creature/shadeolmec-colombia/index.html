Shade (Olmec)

ghost · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Colombia. The Olmecs were a Mesoamerican civilization (Veracruz/Tabasco, Mexico, 1400-400 BCE), not Colombian. Colombia's pre-Hispanic cultures include Muisca, Quimbaya, Tairona, San Agustín. 'Shade' as a ghost type is European; no Olmec ghost tradition exists in Colombia.
