Ayida-Weddo

serpent · Benin · West Africa

Ayida-Weddo (also known as Aido Hwedo) is the cosmic rainbow serpent of Fon/Dahomey mythology who supports the entire world. According to the Fon creation myth, Ayida-Weddo served as Mawu's mount during creation — his winding body shaped the mountains, valleys, and rivers of the earth. After creation, he coiled beneath the earth to support its immense weight, holding his own tail in his mouth (forming an ouroboros).
