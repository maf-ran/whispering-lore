Chullachaqui

shapeshifter · Peru · South America

Chullachaqui, also known as Shapishico, is a mythical forest creature from the Peruvian and Brazilian Amazon jungle, prominent in the folklore of the Quechua, Shipibo, and other indigenous peoples of the Amazon Basin. The name derives from the Quechua words chulla meaning one or odd and chaki meaning foot, referring to the creature's most distinctive physical trait of mismatched feet. The Chullachaqui is a guardian spirit of the rainforest, protector of trees and animals, and punisher of those who break taboos in the forest.
