First Man (Áłtsé Hastiin)

spirit · United States · North America

First Man (Áłtsé Hastiin) is a primordial figure in Navajo creation mythology. In the First World, he was formed from the breath of Black Cloud and White Cloud, carrying white corn and crystal. He made a fire with crystal and joined with First Woman after seeing her light from across the darkness, establishing the pattern of life.
