Awang Semaun (The Legendary Hero)

hero · Brunei · Southeast Asia

Awang Semaun is the legendary culture hero and founding father of Brunei, celebrated in the Brunei Darussalam national epic. According to the Silsilah Raja-Raja Brunei (Genealogy of Brunei Kings), Awang Semaun was a figure of superhuman strength and wisdom who helped establish the Brunei Sultanate. He was one of 14 brothers born from a magical womb, each with extraordinary abilities. Awang Semaun was renowned for his physical strength, seafaring skills, and ability to communicate with animals and spirits.
