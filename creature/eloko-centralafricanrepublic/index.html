Eloko

spirit · Central African Republic · Central Africa

Eloko is a spirit from Central African Republic mythology. These forest-dwelling creatures are believed to be the restless spirits of ancestors who died with unresolved grudges, condemned to haunt the darkest, densest parts of the rainforest. Unlike benevolent spirits, the Eloko are fiercely territorial and vengeful, guarding their forest treasures including game animals and rare plants. They are known to cast spells on trespassers using enchanted bells that paralyze their victims before they attack.
