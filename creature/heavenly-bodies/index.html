Heavenly Bodies

spirit · United States · North America

In Miwok mythology of California, the concept of Heavenly Bodies encompasses a class of spirit beings that inhabit the sky and exert influence over earthly affairs. The Miwok people, who traditionally lived in the Sierra Nevada and California coastal regions, developed a rich celestial mythology in which stars, planets, and other heavenly bodies were understood as living spirits with distinct personalities and powers.
