Deer Ancestor-Spirit (Zapotec)

ancestor-spirit · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Brazilian tradition. The Zapotec are an indigenous people of Oaxaca, Mexico, not of Brazil, and no source records a 'Zapotec deer ancestor-spirit' preserved among Brazilian communities; the diaspora narrative is invented.
