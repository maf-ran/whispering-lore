Olobit

mythical-serpent · United States · North America

The olobit, also called the 'sharp-breasted snake,' is a monstrous serpent of the Natchez, a people of the lower Mississippi. Described in John R. Swanton's field research, it is a supernatural reptile closely related to the Uktena of the Cherokee and other great serpents of the Southeast.
