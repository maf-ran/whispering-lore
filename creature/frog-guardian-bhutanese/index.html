Frog Guardian (Bhutanese)

guardian · Bhutan · South Asia

The Frog Guardian of Bhutanese tradition is a protective nature spirit associated with the kingdom's pristine valleys and mountain streams. In Bhutanese Buddhism, which blends Mahayana Buddhist teachings with ancient Bon animist beliefs, the frog is considered an auspicious creature symbolizing fertility, abundance, and the cleansing power of water. This guardian deity watches over specific valleys and watersheds, ensuring that villagers maintain harmony with the natural world.
