Brahma Rakshasa

demon · India · South Asia

The Brahma Rakshasa is a powerful and dangerous demon from Indian Hindu mythology, born either from the yawn of the god Brahma or from the cursed soul of a Brahmin who lived a wicked life. Unlike ordinary rakshasas, the Brahma Rakshasa possesses immense knowledge of the Vedas, mantras, and sacred texts, making it both highly intelligent and exceptionally dangerous. It is considered one of the most formidable types of rakshasas in Hindu folklore, feared for its supernatural powers and insatiable hunger for human flesh
