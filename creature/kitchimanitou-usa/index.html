Kitchi-Manitou

spirit · United States · North America

Kitchi-Manitou (Gitche Manitou, Gichi-manidoo) is the Great Spirit of Algonquian-speaking peoples such as the Ojibwe, Potawatomi, and Menominee: the supreme creative power behind all life and spiritual authority, including the Midewiwin (Grand Medicine Society). The entry is condensed to the attested Great Spirit, and the empty filler naming 'the USA people' is removed.
