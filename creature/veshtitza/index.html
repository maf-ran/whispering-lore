Veshtitza

witch · Montenegro · Slavic

Veshtitza is a Montenegrin witch-vampire whose soul leaves her body at night in the form of a black hen or moth to scout homes with children. She returns to drain blood and consume hearts. By day she appears as an ordinary woman. A South Slavic variant of the striga/shtriga, blending witch and vampire traits. Feared as a night predator of infants.
