Fox Spirit of Ashanti

spirit · Ghana · West Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No fox spirit acts as messenger of Asase Yaa or keeper of sacred groves in Akan belief; those claims are invented. The Akan spirit world centers on figures such as Asase Yaa, Nyame, and the trickster Ananse, with no attested fox spirit.
