Wolf Ghost of Hopi

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf ghost in Hopi tradition. The Hopi katsinam include Kweo, a wolf kachina who is a great hunter and pathfinder guiding villages to game, and wolves appear in the world-age account of the second world; a malevolent wolf specter is not part of the ceremonial record.
