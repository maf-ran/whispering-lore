Kintaro

human · Japan · Asia

Kintaro (Unknown). is a human. Kintarō ("Golden Boy") is a folk hero from Japanese folklore, a child of superhuman strength who was raised by a mountain witch on Mount Ashigara. He is said to have wrestled bears and befriended wood. Source: Japanese folklore; kabuki tradition
