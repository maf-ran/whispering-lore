Lamia

spirit · Vatican City · Mediterranean

Lamia is a spirit from Roman Christian mythology. Originally a figure from classical Greek mythology - a lover of Zeus who turned into a monster after Hera killed her children - Lamia was adopted into Roman and later Christian demonology. In Vatican tradition, Lamia represents the demonic forces that threaten innocent souls, particularly unbaptized infants. Medieval Christian writers described Lamia as a female demon who lures and devours children.
