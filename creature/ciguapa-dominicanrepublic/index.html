Ciguapa

nature-spirit · Dominican Republic · Caribbean

The Ciguapa is a nocturnal wild woman of Dominican mountain folklore whose backward-turned feet baffle trackers. The earliest written account is Francisco Javier Angulo Guridi's 1866 story 'La Ciguapa.' Scholars debate a possible Taíno origin against European parallels such as mermaids and the churel.
