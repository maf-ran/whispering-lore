Kamata

hero · Vanuatu · Pacific

Kamata is a culture hero in Vanuatu mythology, particularly among the people of Ambrym and Malekula islands. He is credited with bringing fire, agriculture, and essential knowledge to humanity. Kamata journeyed across the islands performing great deeds, shaping the landscape, and teaching people how to cultivate yams, build houses, and conduct proper ceremonies. He is associated with the volcano spirits of Ambrym, the region known for its active volcanic activity. Kamata's exploits often involve tricking dangerous spirits and defeating monstrous beings.
