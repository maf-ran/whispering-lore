Apwoyo (Hare)

animal · Kenya · East Africa

Apwoyo is the clever hare trickster of Luo folklore, a small but extraordinarily cunning creature who constantly outsmarts larger and stronger animals through pure wit. In one famous story, Apwoyo tricked a mighty elephant and a huge python into a tug-of-war, pitting them against each other. In another tale, he escaped being eaten by the lion by claiming to be Were's messenger, sent to inspect the animals' behavior. When the hyena tried to copy Apwoyo's tricks, disaster always followed. Apwoyo stories teach Luo children that intelligence and quick thinking are more valuable than brute strength
