Tidzi

spirit · Senegal · West Africa

Not independently attested in Senegalese folklore. No published source records a Senegalese bush spirit named Tidzi; searches return only Tidzi, a rural commune in Essaouira Province, Morocco, and related place names. The claim that a 'Senegalese bush spirit tests travelers' resembles the Rab spirits of Wolof and Lebou belief, but no such entity named Tidzi is documented.
