Octopus Water-Spirit (Wolof)

water-spirit · Senegal · West Africa

Not independently attested as a distinct named entity in Wolof tradition. Wolof water spirits (jinés/jinn) inhabit rivers and coasts but no specific 'octopus water-spirit' appears in ethnographic records (Diop, Faye). The octopus motif may reflect Atlantic marine influence but lacks attestation.
