Rồng Hồ

dragon · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Vietnamese folklore; no source documents a golden lake dragon called 'Rồng Hồ.' The Hoàn Kiếm legend that anchors the entry centers on the Golden Turtle, Thần Kim Quy, not a dragon: the turtle received Lê Lợi's sword and returned it to the deep, giving the lake its name.
