Mörksugga

demon · Sweden · Nordic

Mörksugga (Swedish: 'dark sow') is a legendary pig-like creature from Dalarna, Sweden — a black wild boar with glowing eyes and large tusks, used as a bogeyman to frighten children from staying out after dark. Traditionally fearsome, it was reimagined in modern times (Verner Molin's art, Andrzej Sapkowski's Witcher) as a guardian against darkness.
