Singh Dhan

beast · India · South Asia

Singh Dhan (Golden Lion/Deer) appears in Rajasthani folk tales as a divine golden deer granting wishes. Related to Hindu 'Mrigashira' motif and golden deer (Mareecha) in Ramayana that lures Rama. Rajasthani folklore includes divine animal helpers (Pabuji's mare, Tejaji's serpent). 'Singh Dhan' may be a local name for a wish-granting forest deity.
