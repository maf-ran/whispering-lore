Hou Yi

human · China · Asia

Hou Yi (Unknown). is a human. Hou Yi is a legendary archer from Chinese mythology who saved the world by shooting down nine of the ten suns that were burning the earth. He is also associated with the story of Chang'e, the moon god. Source: Classic of Mountains and Seas; Chinese mythological tradition
