Bison Giant of Mapuche

giant · Chile · South America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Bison are not native to South America, and the volcano-dwelling, Pillan-linked bison giant is invented. Mapuche cosmology includes real ngen (masters of nature) and Pillan spirits of volcanoes and lightning, and the Nguillatun ceremony, but no bison being figures in it.
