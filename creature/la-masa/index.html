La Masa

monster · Honduras · Mesoamerica

La Masa (The Mass) is a bizarre and terrifying entity from Venezuelan folklore, particularly from the central plains (llanos) and Andean regions. The name 'La Masa' literally means 'The Dough' or 'The Mass,' referring to the creature's amorphous, dough-like appearance. Despite its unusual name, La Masa is considered one of the most dangerous supernatural entities in Venezuelan oral tradition, capable of crushing, suffocating, or absorbing its victims into its gelatinous body.
