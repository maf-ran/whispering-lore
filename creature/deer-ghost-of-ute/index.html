Deer Ghost of Ute

ghost · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a ghost of Ute folklore. Deer are central to Ute life and ceremony, but no Ute deer-ghost is documented in the sources. Ute spirit traditions concern shamans, animal helpers and the dead who return in dreams; a deer revenant guarding the mountains is not attested.
