Moose Deity (Inuit)

deity · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a deity of Inuit folklore. Moose are absent from most Inuit lands, and no source records a moose god in Inuit cosmology, which centers on beings such as the sea goddess Sedna, the Moon Man (Anningan), Sila and Qailertetang. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
