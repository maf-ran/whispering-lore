Fox Ancestor-Spirit of Aztec

ancestor-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No sacred fox ancestor-spirit occurs in Aztec religion; the description conflates the fox with coyote figures such as Huehuecoyotl, the aged coyote trickster god, and invents messenger and calendar associations. The fox is not a significant figure in recorded Mexica mythology.
