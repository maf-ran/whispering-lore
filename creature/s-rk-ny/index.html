Sárkány

dragon · Hungary · Balkan

The legendary dragon in Hungarian folklore, originating from Turkic roots (Proto-Turkic *sāŕgan meaning 'carp, snake, dragon,' related to Cuman sazagan). Originally conceived as humanoid figures with multiple heads representing souls—a pre-Christian belief where each head embodied a spiritual essence. By the medieval period, evolved into the classic scaly, winged serpentine beast, heavily influenced by Christian demonology.
