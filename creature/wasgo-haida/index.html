Wasgo (Sea Wolf)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Wasgo, the Sea Wolf, is a Haida supernatural being combining wolf and killer-whale features — wolf head and body with the fins and tail of a whale. A crest figure for Haida clans, he crosses between ocean and forest worlds, and his stories teach the fluid boundaries between animal beings in the Haida worldview.
