Stihi (or Stihini)

giant · United States · North America

Not attested as a Pueblo being. The name 'Stihi' is Albanian, naming a fire-breathing storm demon related to the kulshedra, and no 'Stihi/Stihini' giant appears in Pueblo or New Mexico folklore. The closest real Pueblo giant is Tsahveeyo of the Tewa, a huge being who dwelt on a mesa and preyed on children in the pueblos. The record appears to be a fabricated or mislabeled entry.
