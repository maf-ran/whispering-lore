Conjure Doctor

folk-hero · United States · North America

The Conjure Doctor (also known as a root doctor, hoodoo doctor, or two-headed doctor) is a powerful figure in African American folk belief — a practitioner of conjure, hoodoo, and rootwork who possesses supernatural knowledge and the ability to influence events through magical means. Conjure Doctors serve as community healers, spiritual advisors, and protectors against evil. They use herbs, roots, bones, and personal items to create mojo bags, cast spells, or provide protection. Essential tools include John the Conqueror root (for luck and protection), goofer dust (for cursing), black cat bone
