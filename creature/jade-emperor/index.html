Jade Emperor (Unknown). is a deity. The Jade Emperor (Yu Huang) is the supreme ruler of Heaven in Chinese folk religion and Taoist mythology. He governs the celestial bureaucracy and decides the fates of mortals.. Source: Taoist canon; Chinese folk religion; Journey to the West (Wu Cheng'en, 16th century)
