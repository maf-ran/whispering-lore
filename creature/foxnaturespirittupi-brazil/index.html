Fox Nature-Spirit (Tupi)

spirit · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a named Tupi entity. Tupi-Guarani mythology includes fox tricksters (e.g., Aguara in Guarani) but no specific 'Fox Nature-Spirit' with the described crystalline, celestial attributes. The elaborate description appears to be a modern literary invention.
