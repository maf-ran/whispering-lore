El Boyo

ghost · Nicaragua · Mesoamerica

El Boyo is a malevolent water spirit from Nicaraguan folklore, specifically associated with Lake Nicaragua (Cocibolca) and the surrounding rivers. Described as the ghost of a drowned man, it rises from the depths to drag the living down to join it in the watery abyss. The legend is particularly strong in the cities of Granada and Rivas, where lake drownings were historically common. The tale served as a cautionary story to warn children and young people about the dangers of swimming alone or at night in the lake's unpredictable currents and the bull sharks that inhabit its waters
