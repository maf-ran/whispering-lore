Mythic Animals

spirit · United States · North America

Not a single attested being: 'Mythic Animals' is a catch-all category, not a figure of Blackfoot folklore. Genuine Blackfoot mythic animals include the buffalo and the Iniskim (buffalo stone), Napi's animal companions, and the underwater people of springs. No source records a Blackfoot entity named 'Mythic Animals.' The recorded traits appear to derive from modern speculation.
