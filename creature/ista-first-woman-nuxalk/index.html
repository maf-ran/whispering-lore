Ista First Woman Nuxalk

human · Canada · North America

Ista is the First Woman of the Nuxalk (Bella Coola) origin account, who descended to the land at the beginning of the world and became mother of the people. Her story, told as a smayusta, explains the beginnings of human life and of Nuxalk society and belongs to the creation narratives of the Bella Coola.
