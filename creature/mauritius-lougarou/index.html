Lougarou

shapeshifter · Mauritius · East Africa

The Lougarou is a notorious figure in Mauritian folklore, essentially a werewolf that blends French and African mythological influences. It is a human who has the ability to transform into a beast, typically appearing during the full moon. The Lougarou is used as a cautionary figure to scare children into behaving and staying indoors at night. It is believed that the Lougarou brings trouble and chaos to the local population, and the only way to protect oneself is to consult a local sorcerer or witch doctor
