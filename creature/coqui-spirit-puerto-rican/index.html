Coqui (Sacred Frog Spirit of Puerto Rico)

nature-spirit · Puerto Rico · Caribbean

The Coqui is a tiny tree frog (Eleutherodactylus coqui) native to Puerto Rico that has transcended biology to become a sacred cultural and mythological symbol. In Taino mythology, the Coqui distinctive two-note nocturnal call Co-kee! has a divine origin. According to legend, a beautiful Taino goddess fell in love with Coqui, the chief son. They met in secret, but the evil god Juracan (the spirit of chaos and hurricanes) snatched Coqui away.
