Bergr

spirit · Iceland · Europe

Bergr is an Old Norse word used as a personal name meaning 'mountain' or 'rescuer, helper' (from bjarga). It is not independently attested in Icelandic folklore as a named spirit or supernatural being; references likely stem from confusion with saga personal names or with the bergrisar ('mountain giants') of Norse legend. Uncertainty should be assumed for this entry.
