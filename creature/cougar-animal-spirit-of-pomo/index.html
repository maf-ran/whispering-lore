Cougar Animal-Spirit of Pomo

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Cougar Animal-Spirit from Pomo tradition is a powerful guardian being in the spiritual beliefs of the Pomo people of northern California. The Pomo, who lived in the region north of San Francisco Bay, traditionally inhabited areas where mountain lions were common and respected as powerful spirits. In Pomo cosmology, the world is filled with spirit-beings known as Xa'nis, and powerful animals like the cougar were believed to possess especially strong spiritual power.
