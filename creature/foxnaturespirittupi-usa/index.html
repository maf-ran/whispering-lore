Fox Nature-Spirit (Tupi)

spirit · United States · North America

Not attested in USA or Tupi folklore. The fox trickster Aguara belongs to the Guarani and Chané peoples of Paraguay, Argentina and Brazil, not the Tupi, and not to North America. In Guarani stories Aguara stole algarroba seeds from Viscacha, outwitted the jaguar, and captured the vulture to obtain its rubber. The North American framing is a misattribution.
