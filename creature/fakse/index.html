Fakse

monster · Norway · Nordic

Fakse is a type of carved wooden cult idol from the Setesdal region of Sørlandet, representing a rare survival of pre-Christian worship practices into the 19th century. The most famous fakse figures were Fakse-Brokke (from Brokke farm in Valle) and another from Rygnestad — male wooden figures about one meter tall, carved from pine. Farmers offered beer, butter, and food to the fakse, believing it would bring prosperity, protect livestock, and ensure good harvests.
