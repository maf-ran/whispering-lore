Moose Giant of Baloch

giant · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in Baloch folklore. Moose are not native to Balochistan (arid Pakistan/Iran/Afghanistan). Baloch oral tradition features tribal heroes (Mir Chakar Rind, Hammal Jiand), desert djinn (Mujna), and mountain spirits, but no moose giants. The record appears fabricated, possibly conflating North American moose mythology with Baloch culture.
