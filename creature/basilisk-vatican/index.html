Basilisk

dragon · Vatican City · Mediterranean

Basilisk is a dragon from Roman Christian mythology. Described by Pliny the Elder and later Christian bestiaries, the Basilisk is a small but deadly serpent crown-crested creature whose mere gaze causes death. It is said to be hatched from a cock's egg incubated by a serpent or toad. In Christian symbology adopted by the Vatican, the Basilisk represents the power of sin and heresy that can be overcome by faith.
