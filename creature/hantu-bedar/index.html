Hantu Bedar

demon · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Hantu Bedar is a fearsome demon from Malaysian folklore, particularly known in rural communities of Peninsular Malaysia. The name 'Hantu Bedar' translates roughly to 'bulky/large ghost,' describing its enormous stature. This malevolent forest spirit is one of many hantu (ghosts or demons) in Malay animistic tradition, which blends indigenous beliefs with Islamic cosmology. Hantu Bedar is primarily a cautionary figure used by parents to warn children against wandering into the deep forest alone, especially at night. The legend is most common among Malay communities living near dense jungle area
