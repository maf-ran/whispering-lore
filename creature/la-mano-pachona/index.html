La Mano Pachona

spirit · Mexico · North America

La Mano Pachona is a chilling legend from northern Mexico and the US Southwest about a disembodied, hairy hand that attacks travelers at night. The hand is described as large, dark, and covered in thick hair, with long fingernails. It wanders along lonely roads, scratching at windows and car doors, or reaches up from beneath vehicles to grab unsuspecting victims. Some versions claim the hand belongs to a man who was cursed for his evil deeds, while others say it is the hand of a murdered criminal that continues to seek revenge.
