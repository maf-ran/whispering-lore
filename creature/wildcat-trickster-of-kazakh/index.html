Wildcat Trickster of Kazakh

trickster · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a trickster in Kazakh folklore. The sly-animal role in Turkic Central Asian tales belongs to the fox, while the wild cats of the steppe appear as emblems of strength, power and freedom rather than as tricksters. No wildcat trickster is recorded in Kazakh sources; the claimed shapeshifting traits are unsupported.
