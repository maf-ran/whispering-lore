Kitchi-Manitou

spirit · Canada · North America

Kitchi-Manitou, also known as Gitche Manitou or Gichi-manidoo, is the Great Spirit in the spiritual traditions of Algonquian-speaking Native American peoples of the United States, including the Ojibwe, Potawatomi, Menominee, and other tribes of the Great Lakes region. As the supreme creative force in the universe, Kitchi-Manitou is understood as the source from which all life, energy, and spiritual power emanate.
