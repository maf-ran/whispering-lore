La Tulivieja

spirit · Panama · Mesoamerica

La Tulivieja is one of Panama's most famous legendary figures, a vengeful ghost transformed into a hideous creature. According to the legend, a beautiful young woman had a secret relationship that resulted in pregnancy. To hide her sin, she drowned her baby in a river. As punishment from God, she transformed into a monster with a face full of holes sprouting tangled hair, bat wings, chicken legs, and a distinctive tule hat.
