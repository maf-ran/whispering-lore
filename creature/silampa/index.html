La Silampa

spirit · Panama · Mesoamerica

La Silampa is a legendary white specter from Panamanian folklore, described as a ghostly white sheet-like creature that soars over roads, deserted areas, and fields, blending into the fog. According to legend, La Silampa was originally a beautiful indigenous princess who fell in love with a forbidden warrior. When Spanish soldiers killed her lover and her father murdered him, the spirit of the night entered her body, transforming her into an evil spiritual being. She devoured her father, leaving only his skeleton. Since then, La Silampa roams Panama's roads and fields seeking victims to devour
