Hoklonote

spirit · United States · North America

Hoklonote is a malevolent spirit from Choctaw tradition, described in 19th-century ethnographic accounts collected by Henry S. Unlike many supernatural beings in Choctaw mythology, Hoklonote is unequivocally evil — a shapeshifting entity with the terrifying ability to read human thoughts. The Choctaw believed that Hoklonote could assume any form it desired, making it nearly impossible to recognize until it was too late.
