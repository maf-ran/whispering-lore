Rồng Biển

sea-monster · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Vietnamese folklore; no source documents a sea dragon called 'Rồng Biển.' Vietnam's attested water beings are the dragon-like thuồng luồng (a riverine crocodile-serpent), maritime deities such as Hải Long Vương, and the whale-spirit veneration of fishing villages, but no named sea dragon matches the entry.
