Nang Phisuea Samud (Sea Ogress)

ogre · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Nang Phisuea Samud (Thai: นางผีเสื้อสมุทร) is a powerful sea ogress from the Thai national epic Phra Aphai Mani by Sunthorn Phu. She is a giant ogress of the deep ocean who kidnapped Prince Aphai Mani after hearing his enchanted flute playing. Despite her terrifying appearance as a sea monster, she fell deeply in love with the prince and kept him imprisoned in her undersea cave palace for eight years.
