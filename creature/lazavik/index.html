Lazavik

guardian · Belarus · Slavic

Lazavik (lazovik) is a gentle spirit of Belarusian folklore who lives in the flax fields, guarding the crop and hiding among the fibers. He is described as a small, old, harmless being who flees when the flax is harvested, a personification of the plant essential to Belarusian rural life.
