Changó

deity · Cuba · Caribbean

Changó, also spelled Shangó or Xangô, is one of the most powerful and revered orishas in the Yoruba religion and its Afro-Caribbean derivatives including Santería, Candomblé, and Lukumí. As the orisha of thunder, lightning, fire, war, dance, and justice, Changó embodies masculine power, passion, and royal authority. Originally a historical king of the Oyo Empire in what is now Nigeria, he was deified after death and became the第四 king of the Yoruba pantheon.
