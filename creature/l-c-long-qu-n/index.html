Lạc Long Quân

dragon · Vietnam · Southeast Asia

Lạc Long Quân, the 'Dragon Lord of Lac,' is the divine ancestor of the Vietnamese people in the national foundation myth recorded in the Lĩnh Nam chích quái. A dragon prince of the sea, he wed the fairy Âu Cơ, who bore a hundred sons: half followed him to the waters, while half followed their mother into the mountains and became the Hùng Kings.
