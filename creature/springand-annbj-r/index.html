Springand-Annbjør

ghost · Norway · Nordic

Springand-Annbjør (Running Annbjør) is a restless running spirit from Setesdal folklore in Sørlandet. She is described as a female undead or wraith that runs endlessly through the Setesdal valley, never stopping, never resting. The name Annbjør is a female name common in Setesdal, and the legend likely originated as a ghost story about a specific woman condemned to eternal running as punishment for a grave sin.
