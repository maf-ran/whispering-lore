Wolf Deity of Haida

deity · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wolf deity in Haida folklore. Haida sacred narratives center on the Raven (Xhuuya/Nankil'slas), the culture hero and transformer who shaped the world and freed the sun and the tides; the wolf appears chiefly as a crest and clan emblem carved on poles, never as a worshipped god. The water-spirit archetype is a template artifact.
