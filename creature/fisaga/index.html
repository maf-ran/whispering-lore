Fisaga

nature-spirit · Samoa · Pacific

Fisaga is a gentle wind spirit from Samoan mythology, personifying the light and pleasant breeze associated with fine weather. In the Samoan language, fisaga refers specifically to a gentle, pleasant wind that brings good conditions. The most famous legend involving Fisaga comes from the tale of Ti'iti'i who imprisoned the winds one by one in his canoe or calabash to control their power over the land.
