Letao

spirit · Marshall Islands · Pacific

Letao is the trickster god of Marshallese mythology, a mischievous spirit of winds and ocean currents who delights in confounding navigators and bending the sea to his will. Grandson of the creator Lowa and brother of Jemeliwut, he reflects the ocean's volatility, and his pranks are countered only by the navigational knowledge of Marshallese wayfinding.
