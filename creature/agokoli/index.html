Agokoli

human · Togo · Africa

Agokoli (Unknown). is a human. Agokoli is a legendary tyrannical king of Notsé (in present-day Togo) from Ewe oral tradition. His oppressive rule drove the Ewe people to escape through a hole in the city wall, leading to their dias. Source: Ewe oral tradition; Ghanaian/Togolese historical narratives
