Trasgo

trickster · Spain · Mediterranean

El Trasgo is a household and trickster spirit of Galician and wider Iberian folklore, a small mischievous shapeshifter that haunts barns, kitchens, and settlements, stealing food and playing pranks on residents. Legends recount how it may be expelled or tamed by outwitting it. It belongs to the family of Iberian domestic goblins associated with rural life.
