Magical Boy Nuxalk

human · Canada · North America

The Magical Boy is the young hero of a Bella Coola (Nuxalk) legend, a child of humble birth who, aided by spirit power, confronts a man-eating monster that has terrorized the people. Where strength fails, his courage and cleverness prevail, and the tale stands as a hero-child story of the Nuxalk.
