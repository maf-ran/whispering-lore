Tunisian Ghoul

demon · Tunisia · North Africa

The ghoul of Tunisian folklore is a shape-shifting desert demon of the jinn, haunting ruins, cemeteries, and lonely roads. It appears as a beautiful woman or a hunched crone to waylay travellers, whose flesh it devours. Unlike the Western grave-robber, the Tunisian ghoul is above all a deceiver of the wilderness, warded off by iron and the name of God.
