Povitrulya

nature-spirit · Ukraine · Slavic

Povitrulya is a Ukrainian wind spirit from the Carpathian Mountains, daughter of the mountain winds. She appears as a winged, flower-clad maiden who flies without touching ground. She helps lovers and shepherds; a man who steals her wings can marry her, but she will flee if she recovers them. A positive, benevolent spirit of the air.
