Áwonawílona (All-Father)

deity · United States · North America

Áwonawílona (All-Father, Maker and Container of All) is the supreme primordial deity in Zuni creation mythology. In the beginning, only Áwonawílona existed. He made himself into the Sun Father (Yatoka). The mist clouds formed into the Great Waters, from which Earth Mother (Áwitelin Tsíta) and Sky Father (Ápoyan Ta'chu) emerged.
