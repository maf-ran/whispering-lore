Badger Nature-Spirit of Blackfoot

nature-spirit · United States · North America

No Blackfoot source documents a 'badger nature-spirit.' Blackfoot tradition knows Napi (Old Man) and spirit-helpers acquired through vision quests, but the badger is not attested as a named nature-spirit. This entry is the same template fabrication as the Canada variant and adds no independent tradition; the Blackfoot homeland spans Montana and Alberta.
