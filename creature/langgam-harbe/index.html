Langgam Harbe

animal-spirit · Philippines · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in documented Philippine folklore; searches locate no spirit called 'Langgam Harbe,' and 'langgam' is the Tagalog word for ant. The recorded insect-linked traits fit the wider Philippine class of small, mound-dwelling spirits such as the nuno sa punso, spirits of termite mounds and anthills that demand respect and offerings.
