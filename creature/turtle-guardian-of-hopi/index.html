Turtle Guardian of Hopi

guardian · United States · North America

The Turtle Kachina is one of the many katsinam of Hopi tradition, spirit beings impersonated in ceremonies and carved as dolls to bring rain, health and blessings; turtle imagery in the Pueblo world carries associations of earth, longevity and protection. Museum collections document the Hopi Turtle Kachina. A specific 'guardian of lakes' role, however, is not attested in Hopi sources.
