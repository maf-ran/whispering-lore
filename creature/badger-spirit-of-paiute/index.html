Badger Spirit of Paiute

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No documented Paiute tradition features a badger spirit, though badgers are native to the Great Basin. Paiute stories centre on Coyote the creator-trickster and, in some groups, Wolf; the badger has no attested role in Paiute cosmology or storytelling.
