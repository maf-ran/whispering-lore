Sumbo

spirit · Burkina Faso · West Africa

Sumbo refers to the ritual masks of the Bwaba (Bobo) people of Burkina Faso, which are believed to embody powerful spirits that connect the human world with the invisible realm of ancestors and bush spirits. In Bwaba cosmology, the Sumbo masks represent a complex spiritual system that mediates between two categories of invisible entities: the ancestors and the bush spirits (genies de brousse).
