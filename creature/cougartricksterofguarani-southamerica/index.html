Cougar Trickster of Guarani

trickster · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Guarani tradition. This Brazilian variant repeats the Paraguay entry's fabrication that a cougar ranks among the seven monstrous sons of Tau and Kerana; the documented sons are Teju Jagua, Mbói Tu'i, Moñái, Jasy Jatere, Kurupí, Ao Ao and Luisón, none a cougar.
