Leppalúði

ogre · Iceland · Norse

Leppalúði (Icelandic). is a ogre. Leppalúði is the lazy and gluttonous third husband of the ogress Grýla in Icelandic folklore, father of the Yule Lads, who contributes little to their child-snatching activities.. Source: Icelandic folklore, Jón Árnason's collections
