Loupo

werewolf · France · Mediterranean

The loupo is the werewolf of Occitan and Alpine French tradition, a loup-garou variant recorded in Savoie and Dauphiné, where belief in human-wolf metamorphosis was widespread. Local accounts describe men who changed shape under a curse and roamed the mountains at night, illustrating pre-Christian shapeshifting belief in southern France, distinct from the northern loup-garou.
