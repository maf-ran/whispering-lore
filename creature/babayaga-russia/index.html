Baba Yaga

ogre · Russia · Slavic

Baba Yaga is the iconic East Slavic witch: a gaunt crone with iron teeth who dwells in a hut on chicken legs deep in the forest. Flies in a mortar, steers with a pestle, sweeps tracks with a broom. Tests visitors with three impossible tasks; rewards cleverness with magical gifts, devours the failures. Commands forest creatures.
