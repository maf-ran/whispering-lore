Moose Water-Spirit of Muisca

water-spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Muisca tradition. Moose are absent from South America; the Muisca of the Colombian Altiplano had no moose in their cosmology. Their water beings include the Monster of Lake Tota (serpent) and lake-dwelling spirits, but no moose.
