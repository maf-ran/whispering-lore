Eagle Animal-Spirit of Lakota

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Eagle Animal-Spirit, known as Waŋblí in the Lakota language, is one of the most sacred beings in Lakota mythology and spirituality of the Great Plains. The eagle is considered the strongest and bravest of all creatures, serving as the primary messenger between humans and Wakan Tanka, the Great Spirit or Great Mystery that pervades all of creation. In Lakota tradition, eagle feathers are given as the highest honor, representing bravery, wisdom, and spiritual attainment. The Waŋblí carries prayers from the earth up to Wakan Tanka on its powerful wings, and its feathers adorn the sacred pipe
