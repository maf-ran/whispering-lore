Akkedem

nature-spirit · Algeria · North Africa

Akkedem is a nature spirit from the folklore of the Philippines, belonging to the rich tradition of diwata and nature spirits in Filipino mythology. These spirits are believed to inhabit natural features such as trees, rivers, mountains, and forests, acting as guardians of the natural world. In Filipino indigenous belief systems, nature spirits like Akkedem are respected and appeased through offerings and rituals to ensure harmony between humans and the environment.
