Bura

demon · Azerbaijan · Caucasus

Bura is a malevolent wind spirit from Azerbaijani and Caspian Sea folklore, known for its sudden and devastating appearances that sweep away everything in its path. This destructive elemental entity is particularly feared by shepherds, nomads, and maritime communities whose livelihoods depend on calm weather and stable conditions. Bura is associated with violent storms, tornadoes, and dust devils that arise without warning, tearing through settlements, flattening tents, uprooting trees, and churning the Caspian Sea into lethal waves.
