Biton

spirit · South Sudan · North Africa

'Biton' or 'Betón,' poetically called the 'Obsidian Butterfly,' is not attested in Dinka mythology. Its only source is an AI-content site; genuine Dinka tradition has no death spirit of this name or image. The attested Dinka cosmos centers on Nhialic, Deng, Abuk, and Garang, with ancestral Jok spirits and the great serpent Piath.
