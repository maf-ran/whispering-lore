Cougar Animal-Spirit of Inuit

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

The Cougar Animal-Spirit from Inuit tradition represents the powerful animal guardian spirits that form the foundation of Inuit spiritual beliefs across the Arctic regions of Canada, Alaska, and Greenland. While cougars are not found in the far north, Inuit mythology includes the concept of powerful predator spirits such as the Amarok, a giant wolf spirit that hunts alone. This cougar spirit should be understood within the broader context of Inuit animism, where all animals possess souls and powerful spirits can manifest in forms appropriate to their purpose.
