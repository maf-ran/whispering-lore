May and Gilan

human · Qatar · Middle East and North Africa

Mayy and Ghīlān are the legendary founding couple of Qatari tradition — a beloved hikâya told for generations about a heroic pair whose story is celebrated as a foundational myth of the peninsula. Cast as brave, protective figures of courage and unity, they embody the protector spirit of the Qatari people in oral storytelling, song and popular memory.
