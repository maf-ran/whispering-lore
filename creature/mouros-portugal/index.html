Mouros

spirit · Portugal · Mediterranean

The Mouros are supernatural beings in Portuguese and Galician folklore, an ancient race forced underground. They work gold and silver in forges beneath hills and castro ruins, guarding treasure with dragon-like cuélebres, and surface mainly at liminal times such as Midsummer. The Moura Encantada is their best-known form, and their legends have guided archaeology.
