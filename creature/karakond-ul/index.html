Karakondžul

demon · Bulgaria · Slavic

Karakondzul is the Serbian variant of the Balkan Christmas demon, closely related to the Bulgarian Karakondzho and the Greek Kallikantzaros. This malevolent creature is deeply rooted in Serbian folk tradition, where it is believed to appear during the twelve days of Christmas, a liminal period when supernatural forces are thought to roam freely among the living. According to Serbian folklore, the Karakondzul dwells in dark, hidden places for most of the year but emerges during the winter solstice season to harass, frighten, and sometimes harm humans.
