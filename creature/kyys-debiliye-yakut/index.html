Kyys Debiliye

hero · Russia · Central Asia

Kyys Debiliye is a warrior heroine of the Yakut (Sakha) olonkho epic tradition: a tall, strong woman famed for archery and combat mastery who battles the heroes of the middle world. Battle-hardened and protective, she exemplifies the female warrior archetype of olonkho and the martial values of the Sakha heroic worldview.
