Butterfly Nature-Spirit (Tongan)

nature-spirit · Tonga · Pacific

No butterfly spirit or guardian of sacred mountains is attested in Tongan tradition; Tongan mythology centers on Maui, the sky and sea god Tangaloa, and the divine descent of the royal lineage. The entry's description is an invented nature-spirit narrative with no documentary basis.
