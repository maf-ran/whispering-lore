Hamingja

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

The Hamingja is the physical personification of a family's luck and prosperity. Unlike a ghost or a monster, the Hamingja is a benevolent ancestral spirit that clings to a bloodline. In Swedish lore, luck was not seen as a random occurrence but as a finite resource that could be inherited, grown, or even stolen. The Hamingja manifests as a protective aura or a subtle guiding presence that ensures crops grow, cattle remain healthy, and children survive. If a family's Hamingja leaves them, it is a sign of inevitable ruin and social collapse
