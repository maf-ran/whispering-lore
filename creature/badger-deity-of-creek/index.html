Badger Deity of Creek

deity · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not a documented Muscogee (Creek) deity or clan animal; Muscogee clans include Bear, Deer, Wind, and Bird. Genuine Muscogee spirit figures include the Master of Breath, the Tie-Snake, and the Little People, and the Green Corn ceremony opens the ceremonial year.
