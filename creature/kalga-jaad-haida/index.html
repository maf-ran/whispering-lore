Kalga Jaad (Ice Woman)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Kalga Jaad (Ice Woman) is a supernatural being of Haida tradition, counted among the Sgaana Kiidads and appearing in the Raven Traveling narratives recorded by John R. Swanton. She commands ice and winter storms, pairing with Gaayhllga Jaad (Flood-Tide Woman) to represent the balance of seasonal forces in Haida cosmology.
