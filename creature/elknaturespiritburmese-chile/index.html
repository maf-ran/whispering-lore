Elk Nature-Spirit (Burmese)

nature-spirit · Chile · South America

Not independently attested. The Burmese (Myanmar) and elk (Cervus canadensis) are absent from Chile. Chile's native cervids are huemul (Hippocamelus bisulcus) and pudú (Pudu puda). The described being (longyi, headwrap, bamboo staff, water buffalo drum) is explicitly Burmese, transplanted to Chile without basis.
