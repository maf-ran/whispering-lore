Domovoy (Slavic House Spirit)

household-spirit · Russia · Slavic

The Domovoy, known as Domovoi or Domovik in various Slavic languages, is a household spirit deeply rooted in Slavic mythology across Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Poland, Serbia, and other Eastern European nations. The name derives from the Proto-Slavic root 'dom' meaning house, establishing this entity as the master and guardian of the home who protects the family and oversees domestic harmony.
