Mamlambo

water-spirit · South Africa · Southern Africa

Mamlambo is a fearsome shapeshifting water deity of the Zulu and Xhosa peoples, often described as a giant serpent with a horse's head or a beautiful woman who lures men to their doom. She controls the waters, causing floods when angered and droughts when she withdraws. Those who seek her for wealth must make terrible sacrifices, and the riches she grants always bring ruin. In 1997, nine deaths near the Mzintlava River were attributed to her, with victims found with their brains sucked out, earning her the name 'brain sucker.'
