Singing Well Spirit

spirit · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a distinct spirit of Uzbek folklore. No source confirms a singing well-spirit prophesying fate in Khiva or Khorezm; the attested Kheyvak well legend involves the boy Shem, not a prophetic singing spirit. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
