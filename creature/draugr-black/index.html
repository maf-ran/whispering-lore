Black Draugr

undead · Iceland · Nordic

Not independently attested in Icelandic tradition. 'Black Draugr' appears a modern elaboration conflating standard draugr (corporeal undead of Norse folklore) with literary tropes. No saga or ethnographic source describes a distinct 'black' variant with scorched skin, freezing blue eyes, orchestrated misfortunes.
