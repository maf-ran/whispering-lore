Salmon Ghost of Cheyenne

ghost · United States · North America

Not attested in Cheyenne folklore. No Cheyenne story records a salmon ghost; as a Plains people the Cheyenne hunted buffalo and had no salmon in their waterways or mythology. The entry appears to be an AI-generated variant of the salmon motif that is genuine only among Pacific Northwest peoples.
