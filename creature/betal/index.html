Betal

vampire · India · South Asia

A vampiric undead being from Indian folklore, particularly prominent in the Konkan region of Goa and Maharashtra. The Betal is a complex figure that hangs upside down from trees near cremation grounds and ancient battlefields, guarding occult knowledge and secrets of the dead. Unlike common ghosts, the Betal possesses a cunning intelligence and delights in riddles and philosophical debates. It is known to test the bravery and wisdom of those who dare approach it, sometimes rewarding clever individuals with supernatural gifts or hidden truths about the universe.
