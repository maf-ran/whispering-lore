Hawk Animal-Spirit of Ojibwe

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Hawk Animal-Spirit is a revered guardian in Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) tradition in Canada, where the vast northern forests and lakes of Ontario, Manitoba, and Quebec provide the backdrop for this powerful spirit's domain. In Ojibwe cosmology, animal spirits serve as teachers and protectors, each carrying specific lessons and powers. The hawk spirit teaches the value of perspective: seeing the big picture from a height while maintaining focus on important details below.
