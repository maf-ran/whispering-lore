Cadejo

spirit · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

El Cadejo is a legendary spirit from Salvadoran folklore that manifests as two spectral dogs — a White Cadejo and a Black Cadejo — representing the eternal struggle between good and evil. The legend has pre-Columbian indigenous origins, where dogs were believed to help humans pass into the afterlife. With the arrival of Christianity, the story evolved into a dualistic tale. The White Cadejo, with blue eyes, is a protective spirit that guards drunkards, lost travelers, and innocent children from harm.
