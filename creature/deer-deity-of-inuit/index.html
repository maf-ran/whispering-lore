Deer Deity of Inuit

deity · Canada · North America

Among the Inuit of the Arctic regions, the Deer Deity is a powerful caribou-spirit being that oversees the great herds upon which human survival depends. Unlike southern deer spirits, this entity is intrinsically tied to the harsh realities of tundra life, controlling the migration patterns of caribou and the abundance of game animals. Shamans, known as angakkuit, seek the favor of this deity through drum ceremonies and amulet crafting, believing that a respectful relationship with the Deer Deity ensures the continuation of life in the frozen north
