Eagle Spirit

animal-spirit · United States · North America

Eagle Spirit is a powerful guardian and teacher in Crow mythology. In the Bones Together story, the Eagle blessed the human companion with the power to transform into a feather, which allowed him to evade the monster's charges and defeat Bones Together. Eagles are considered sacred birds in Crow culture, associated with the sky, the sun, and divine power. Eagle feathers are used in ceremonies and headdresses as symbols of spiritual authority. Eagle Spirit guides warriors and vision seekers, granting them the power of keen sight and spiritual elevation
