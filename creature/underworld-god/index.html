Underworld God

deity · Greece · Mediterranean

This entry is a generic archetype label rather than a single attested figure; within the database's Mediterranean frame its concrete referent is the Greek underworld deity Hades (Plouton), ruler of the realm of the dead. Hades appears throughout Homer, Hesiod, and classical literature, judging the dead and guarding the riches of the earth.
