Mami Wata

water-spirit · Gambia · West Africa

Mami Wata of the Gambia is a beautiful mermaid water spirit who grants wealth, luck, and fertility in exchange for offerings and sexual celibacy. She appears in rivers and the sea, holds protective maritime powers, and her cult blends indigenous beliefs with colonial-era imagery along the Gambia coast.
