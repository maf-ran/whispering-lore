Crane Animal-Spirit of Cree

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

In Cree tradition the whooping crane is a respected being whose story explains why cranes have long legs and a red crown. The crane taught the Cree people patience, observation and balance, standing watch by the water as a quiet teacher. This origin story is told in Cree cultural teaching materials, honoring the crane as an animal of spirit and wisdom.
