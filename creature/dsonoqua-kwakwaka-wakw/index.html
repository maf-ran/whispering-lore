D'Sonoqua (Dzunukwa)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Dzunuḵ̓wa (Tsonoqua, D'Sonoqua) is the Wild Woman of the Woods in Kwakwaka'wakw tradition: a naked ogress with pendulous breasts, tangled hair, and pursed red lips whose Hu! call rides the wind. She steals children in her basket, yet also brings wealth through her child, and her geekumhl mask appears at the close of the potlatch.
