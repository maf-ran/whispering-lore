Leuk (Hare)

spirit · Senegal · West Africa

Leuk is the clever hare trickster of Wolof folklore from Senegal, the embodiment of intelligence triumphing over brute force. In countless Wolof tales, Leuk uses his wit, cunning, and patience to overcome larger, stronger adversaries — most often Bouki the Hyena, who is greedy, foolish, and easily tricked. The Leuk-Bouki cycle is the most famous body of Wolof folklore, with Leuk consistently outwitting his larger companion.
