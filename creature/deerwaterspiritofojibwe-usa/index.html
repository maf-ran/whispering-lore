Deer Water-Spirit of Ojibwe

water-spirit · United States · North America

In Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) tradition from Canada, the deer water-spirit similarly protects mountain waters but with specific connection to the Canadian Shield watersheds that feed Hudson Bay. This variant of the spirit is closely tied to the rugged terrain of northern Ontario and Manitoba, where countless lakes and rivers are born from ancient bedrock. The Canadian Ojibwe traditions emphasize different aspects of the deer water-spirit, particularly its role in winter survival and its connection to the northern lights. It shares the same essential nature as the southern variant but with adaptation
