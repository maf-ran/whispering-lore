Anarteq

spirit · Greenland · North America

Anarteq is a figure of Greenlandic Inuit legend, a hunter taken into the sea by a sea spirit who adopted him and taught him to live beneath the waves, granting him power over the sea animals. On returning to his village he shares knowledge of hunting and of the sea. The tale is recorded in Knud Rasmussen's Eskimo Folk-Tales.
