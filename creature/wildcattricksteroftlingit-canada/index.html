Wildcat Trickster of Tlingit

trickster · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat trickster in Tlingit tradition. The Tlingit culture hero and trickster is Raven (Yéil), the transformer who stole the sun and brought light to the world; no wildcat figure plays this role. This Canada-flagged record is a duplicate of the same misattribution, since Tlingit trickster lore centers on Raven across both Alaska and Canada.
