Brahma Raksha

guardian · India · South Asia

The Brahma Raksha is a sacred guardian spirit from Indian Hindu folklore, believed to be the benevolent spirit of a deceased Brahmin priest who watches over and protects Brahmin families and their households. Unlike the more dangerous Brahma Rakshasa, the Brahma Raksha is a positive, protective entity bound by dharma to safeguard its charges. This spirit represents the concept of ancestral guardianship, where the soul of a righteous individual continues to fulfill its duties even after death, serving as a bridge between the living and the divine
