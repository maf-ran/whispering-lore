Gatekeeper (Maya)

guardian · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

In Maya mythology and folklore, Gatekeepers are powerful guardian spirits known as Balam or Balaam, which means jaguar in the Maya language. These tutelary beings serve as protectors of villages, agricultural fields, and sacred spaces throughout the Maya region spanning modern-day Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, and Honduras. The balam are considered spiritual guardians who defend their communities from evil spirits and natural threats, most active during the night hours in reflection of the jaguar's nocturnal nature.
