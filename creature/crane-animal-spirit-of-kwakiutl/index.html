Crane Animal-Spirit of Kwakiutl

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

The Crane Animal-Spirit of the Kwakiutl (Kwakwaka'wakw) people emerges from the rich ceremonial traditions of the Pacific Northwest Coast, where the heron and crane are revered as spirit beings associated with the forest edge and the shoreline. In Kwakiutl cosmology, the crane is a transformer figure, appearing in the great Winter Ceremonies (Tseka) as a spirit that initiates young dancers into the Hamatsa society.
