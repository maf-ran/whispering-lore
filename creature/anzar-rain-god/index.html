Anzar (Berber Rain God)

deity · Algeria · North Africa

Anzar is the ancient Berber rain god, one of the most important deities in pre-Islamic Berber mythology of Algeria. He is a powerful figure associated with rainfall, fertility, and agricultural prosperity. The name Anzar (also spelled Anẓar) is derived from the Berber root meaning 'to see' or 'to foresee,' relating to his ability to see the needs of his people and send rain accordingly. In Kabyle and Chaoui traditions of Algeria, Anzar was venerated through specific ceremonies and rituals designed to invoke his favor during times of drought.
