Tulafale

ghost · Samoa · Pacific

Tulafale is a ghost from Samoa folklore. Samoan ghost of an orator-chief who died without completing speeches. Haunts meeting houses, whispers unfinished oratory. Can be helpful if someone co. Oratory tradition very important in Samoa. Ghosts of failed orators remain restless
