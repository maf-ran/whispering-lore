Tigilau

hero · Samoa · Pacific

A powerful Samoan chief and warrior from ancient legend, Tigilau is remembered for his strength, leadership, and exploits. In Samoan mythology, Tigilau sailed on voyages across the Pacific and was known for his encounters with supernatural beings. He is sometimes associated with the figure of Tinilau, a god or culture hero known across several Polynesian traditions. Tigilau's stories emphasize the values of Samoan chiefship - courage, wisdom, and generosity.
