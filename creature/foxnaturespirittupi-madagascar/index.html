Fox Nature-Spirit (Tupi)

spirit · Madagascar · East Africa

Not independently attested in Malagasy tradition. Tupi are Indigenous people of coastal Brazil—not Madagascar. The 'celestial fox spirit of Atlantic rainforests' describes a Brazilian Tupi-Guaraní figure misassigned to Madagascar. Malagasy fox-like spirits don't exist (no native foxes). Cross-regional error placing a Brazilian Amazonian figure in the Indian Ocean.
