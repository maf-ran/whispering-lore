Cougar Animal-Spirit of Apache

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Cougar Animal-Spirit of the Apache people is a powerful guardian being in Apache spiritual traditions of the American Southwest. In Apache cosmology, the mountain lion or cougar is respected as a spirit animal associated with hunting prowess, leadership, and protection. Apache warriors sought the cougar's power through vision quests and ceremonies, believing that the spirit would grant them stealth, courage, and strategic wisdom.
