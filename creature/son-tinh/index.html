Son Tinh

deity · Vietnam · Asia

Son Tinh (Unknown). is a deity. Sơn Tinh (the Mountain God) is a deity from Vietnamese mythology who competed with Thủy Tinh (the Water God) for the hand of Princess Mỵ Nương. His victory over the annual flooding explains the monsoo. Source: Vietnamese mythology; The Legend of Son Tinh and Thuy Tinh
