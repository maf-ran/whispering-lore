Rahko

spirit · Finland · Nordic

Rahko is a Finnish lunar spirit who paints the moon's phases with tar, documented by Ganander in Mythologia Fennica (1789) as a being who covers the moon during eclipses. A marginal figure in runic songs, sometimes identified with the waning moon or a disease spirit. Ganander's account is the primary source; later folklorists treat him as a minor lunar demon.
