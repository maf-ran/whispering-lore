Gizo

trickster · Nigeria · West Africa

Gizo is the spider trickster of Hausa folklore, a clever but overconfident schemer whose plans often backfire spectacularly. In one well-known tale, Gizo tries to outwit the Wisdom Bird but fails, teaching that intelligence without humility leads to ruin. Another story tells of him building a luxury lagoon home through trickery by convincing other animals to build it for him, claiming a magical competition.
