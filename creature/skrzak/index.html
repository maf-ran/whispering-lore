Skrzak

goblin · Poland · Slavic

Skrzak (or skrzat) is a household spirit of Polish and West Slavic folklore, a small goblin-like being who lives in the home or its garden, brings luck and prosperity while the household is virtuous, and hides or turns spiteful when mistreated. He belongs to the wider Slavic domovoi family of hearth guardians.
