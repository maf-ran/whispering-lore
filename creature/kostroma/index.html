Kostroma

nature-spirit · Russia · Slavic

Kostroma is an East Slavic spring-and-summer fertility figure of Russian folk ritual, honored during Semik as a straw effigy or a disguised girl who is first revered and then burned or drowned to end the season's rites. Her name derives from kostyor, 'bonfire.' The entry's claim that she is a plague goddess is unsupported; her genuine role is seasonal and agricultural.
