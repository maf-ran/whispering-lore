Veyis Baba

spirit · Turkmenistan · Central Asia

Veyis Baba is the protective spirit (iye) of camels in Turkmen mythology and folklore. Originally based on the historical figure Veysel Karani, a Yemeni Muslim camel herder known for his love of the Prophet Muhammad, Veyis Baba became the mythological guardian of camels among Turkmen and other Turkic peoples. In Turkmen culture, where camels were essential to nomadic life in the Karakum Desert, Veyis Baba is revered as the patron who protects camel herds from disease, predators, and misfortune.
