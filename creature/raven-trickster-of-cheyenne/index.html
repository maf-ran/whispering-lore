Raven Trickster of Cheyenne

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested in Cheyenne folklore. The Cheyenne trickster is Veeho (Wihio), the spider, who appears as a man and whose foolish, self-serving adventures teach lessons; ravens are not tricksters in Cheyenne tales. This entry is a misattribution, almost certainly transplanting the Raven trickster of the Pacific Northwest.
