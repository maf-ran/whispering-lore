Gardvord

household-spirit · Norway · Nordic

Gardvord (farm guardian) is the older, original Norwegian farm guardian spirit, predating the nisse in Norwegian folk tradition. The gardvord was believed to be the first person buried on a farm or the founding ancestor whose spirit remained to protect the farmstead. Unlike the bearded nisse, the gardvord was often described as a small, naked child or a tiny old man who lived in a tree on the farm, usually an ash or elm. Offerings were made at this tree.
