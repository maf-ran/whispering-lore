Nang Kwak (Cat-Calling Prosperity Goddess)

deity · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Nang Kwak (Thai: นางกวัก) is a benevolent female deity in Thai folklore who brings good fortune, prosperity, and customers to businesses. She is depicted as a beautiful woman in traditional Thai dress, seated with her right hand raised in a beckoning gesture — the 'cat-calling' wave that draws customers and wealth. According to legend, Nang Kwak was a virtuous woman who was granted supernatural powers by the Buddha to attract wealth and abundance.
