The Mythical Korp

bird · Sweden · Nordic

The Korp (Raven) in Swedish folklore is far more than a bird; it is a conduit for prophecy and a witness to the fates of men. Rooted in the legacy of Odin's Huginn and Muninn, the folkloric Korp is seen as a sentinel of the boundary between life and death. These ravens are believed to be the messengers of the underworld, appearing at the site of impending tragedies to collect the souls of the fallen. They are highly intelligent and are said to remember every secret whispered in the forest, making them the most feared and respected spies of the supernatural realm
