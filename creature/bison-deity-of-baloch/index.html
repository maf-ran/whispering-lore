Bison Deity of Baloch

deity · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The Baloch have no water-buffalo deity; the Hingol River drought legend and milk-pouring rituals are fabricated, and the cited Turkic/Mongol source is mismatched. Real Baloch tradition centers on Islam and saint veneration, including the Hinglaj Mata shrine on the Hingol, not on any bovine deity.
