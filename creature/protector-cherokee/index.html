Protector (Cherokee)

guardian · United States · North America

Not attested as a specific Cherokee being. Cherokee tradition does record protective spiritual figures, including the Yunwi Tsunsdi (Little People), the spirit helpers of medicine people, and the Red Warrior of the war ceremony, but no single spirit called 'the Protector' exists. The record is a generic guardian archetype with no named Cherokee figure behind it.
