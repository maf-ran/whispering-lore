Owl Nature-Spirit (San)

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

No owl nature-spirit exists in San (Bushmen) tradition, and the San are an indigenous people of southern Africa, not of Canada. San mythology centers on |Kaggen, the praying-mantis trickster and creator. The entry pairs an African people with a Canadian country tag and invents an owl lake-spirit that no tradition records.
