Cougar Ancestor-Spirit of Baloch

ancestor-spirit · Pakistan · South Asia

The Cougar Ancestor-Spirit from Baloch tradition represents the powerful guardian spirits of the Baloch people of Balochistan, a region spanning parts of Iran, Pakistan, and Afghanistan. In Baloch folklore, big cats such as leopards and cougars are regarded as powerful spirit animals that carry the souls of revered ancestors. The harsh, arid mountains and deserts of Balochistan are believed to be patrolled by these guardian spirits, who watch over nomadic herders and their livestock.
