Saint-Chart (or Saint Chartres)

dragon · France · Mediterranean

Not independently attested in documented French folklore or academic sources: no legend records a dragon named Saint-Chart or a dragon-saint victory at Chartres. The record appears to conflate the Saint George pattern with Chartres, whose documented legends concern the martyrs' well and the Virgin's veil; its south portal merely shows Christ's feet on a lion and dragon.
