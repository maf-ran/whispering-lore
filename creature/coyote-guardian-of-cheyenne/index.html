Coyote Guardian of Cheyenne

guardian · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Cheyenne tradition features the trickster Wihio, the Thunder Spirit Nonoma, and the sacred arrows (Maahotse) brought by Sweet Medicine, but no coyote guardian who discovered the arrows is recorded. The guardian role is invented.
