Badger Spirit of Passamaquoddy

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not native to Passamaquoddy territory and is not a documented spirit being. Passamaquoddy tradition centres on Glooskap, Pamola, and wilderness spirits such as Kiwahq; no badger totem or guardian spirit appears in the ethnographic record.
