Kulfre

spirit · Albania · Balkan

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Albanian source documents a mountain spirit called 'Kulfre' or a headless dancing phantom of the Albanian Alps; the description appears to be invented. Genuine Albanian tradition includes figures such as the zana (mountain fairies) and orë, but no 'Kulfre' appears in the recorded folklore.
