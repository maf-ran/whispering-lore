Møllergut

household-spirit · Norway · Nordic

Møllergut (mill boy) is a milder, more helpful mill spirit in Norwegian folklore, counterpart to the malicious kvernknurr. The møllergut is a young apprentice spirit who helps the miller with his work — cleaning the millstones, carrying sacks of grain, and keeping the mill race clear of debris. Unlike the kvernknurr who deliberately breaks the mill, the møllergut assists if treated kindly. He expects small offerings — a bowl of porridge left in the mill at night, or a piece of flatbread.
