Wildcat Trickster of Quechua

trickster · Peru · South America

The 'Wildcat Trickster of Quechua' is not independently attested in Quechua tradition. Andean mythology features the fox (atoc) as primary trickster and the puma as a powerful being, but no specific 'wildcat trickster.' The closest analogue is the Andean fox trickster cycle documented by Arguedas and Flores Ochoa.
