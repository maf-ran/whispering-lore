Kalga Jaad Ice Woman Haida

human · Canada · North America

Kalga Jaad, 'Ice Woman,' is a primordial ancestress of Haida oral tradition, counted among the ancient beings who existed when the glaciers covered the land. She is remembered as one of the very old figures whose presence links the Haida people to the deep past of the world's making.
