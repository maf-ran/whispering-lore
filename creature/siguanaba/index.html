Siguanaba

spirit · El Salvador · Mesoamerica

La Siguanaba is one of the most famous figures in Salvadoran folklore — a shapeshifting spirit that appears as a beautiful long-haired woman from behind but reveals the face of a horse or skull when she turns around. According to Salvadoran legend, she was originally Sihuehuet (Beautiful Woman), a peasant girl who used her charms and witchcraft to marry Yeisun, son of Tláloc. After her husband went to war, she had affairs and gave birth to Cipitio.
