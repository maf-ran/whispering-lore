Melatha

spirit · United States · North America

Melatha is the male lightning spirit of Choctaw tradition, mate of Heloha the thunder bird. Dressed in lightning and rainbows, he races across the sky above the vault of heaven at incredible speed, leaving trails of sparks that flash as lightning, while Heloha's eggs rumble to produce thunder.
