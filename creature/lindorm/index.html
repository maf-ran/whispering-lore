Lindorm

serpent · Sweden · Nordic

The Lindorm is a colossal, wingless serpent of the earth, often confused with dragons but possessing a distinct, more terrestrial nature. In Swedish folklore, it is a symbol of primal power and hidden danger, often dwelling in deep caves or beneath ancient mounds. Unlike the fire-breathing drakes, the Lindorm's power lies in its immense strength and its ability to influence the land around it, sometimes bringing blight or prosperity depending on its mood.
