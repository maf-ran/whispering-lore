Ngabia Bate

monster · Chad · Central Africa

Ngabia Bate ('man with horns') is a Chadian cryptid legend from the Mayokebi mountains — a wild man of supernatural wisdom, resembling Bigfoot traditions worldwide. Described as a horned humanoid living in mountainous terrain, it symbolizes stamina, wisdom, and grounding. The legend resonates with global 'wild man' motifs but is specific to Chad's Mayokebi region.
