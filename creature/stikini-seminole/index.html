Stikini

spirit · United States · North America

The Stikini are dreaded owl-witches of Seminole folklore. By day they look like ordinary people, but at night they vomit up their souls and internal organs and become undead owl-monsters with bloodshot eyes that feed on human hearts. In some communities, saying the name aloud is itself thought to risk transformation.
