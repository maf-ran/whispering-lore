Hawk Water-Spirit of Yoruba

water-spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Not independently attested. No hawk water-spirit appears in documented Yoruba tradition; the Yoruba water deities are Mami Wata, Oshun and Olokun, and no source records a hawk intermediary between water and sky. The entry's generic 'messenger spirit' framing is unsupported and appears fabricated.
