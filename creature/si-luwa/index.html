Si'luwa

demon · Iraq · Middle East and North Africa

The Si'luwa is a female water demon from Iraqi folklore that haunts rivers, streams, and caves near running water. She is a variant of the Si'lah (Sila), a type of female jinn known for shapeshifting. The Si'luwa appears as a woman covered in long hair with pendulous breasts so long she throws them over her shoulders when nursing her children, whom she carries on her back. She dwells in rivers and caves near running water, and is known to lure humans, particularly men, as lovers.
