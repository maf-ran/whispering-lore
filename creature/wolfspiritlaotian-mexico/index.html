Wolf Spirit (Laotian)

spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested as a Laotian forest spirit, and wolves are not native to Laos, where folklore features phi nature spirits, nagas and other beings rather than wolf deities. The same mislabeled record appeared in an earlier batch and is a modern fabrication; the Mexico placement compounds the error.
