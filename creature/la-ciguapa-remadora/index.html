La Ciguapa Remadora

spirit · Dominican Republic · Caribbean

La Ciguapa Remadora is a regional variant of the Ciguapa legend from the Dominican Republic, specifically associated with the Samaná Peninsula and the northeastern coastal regions. The name 'Remadora' derives from the Spanish word 'remar' (to row), referring to this particular type of Ciguapa that is strongly associated with rivers, lakes, and coastal waters rather than deep forests. While sharing many characteristics with the standard Ciguapa, the Remadora prefers aquatic environments and is more actively predatory toward fishermen, boatmen, and those who travel waterways at night.
