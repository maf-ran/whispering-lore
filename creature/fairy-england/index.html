Fairy

nature-spirit · England · Northern Europe

English fairy tradition encompasses diverse beings: the trooping fairies of hills and mounds, solitary spirits like the brownie (household helper), boggart (malevolent house spirit), pixie (Southwest), and the Lincolnshire/Fenland Tiddy Mun. Not a single entity but a class.
