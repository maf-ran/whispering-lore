Cougar Ancestor-Spirit of Aymara

ancestor-spirit · Bolivia · South America

The Cougar Ancestor-Spirit of the Aymara people is a powerful guardian being from the Andean highlands of Bolivia, Peru, and Chile. In Aymara cosmology, the puma or cougar was one of the most sacred animals, representing strength, courage, and the earthly realm known as Kay Pacha. The Aymara believed that powerful ancestral spirits known as Achachilas, who dwell in the highest mountain peaks, could manifest as cougars to protect their descendants and the natural world.
