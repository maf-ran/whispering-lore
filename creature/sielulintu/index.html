Sielulintu

spirit · Finland · Nordic

The Sielulintu is not a bird of flesh and bone, but a luminous projection of the human psyche. It is the ethereal vessel that carries the spark of consciousness from the void into the womb at birth, and guides the exhausted spirit back to Tuonela upon the final breath. To see one's own Sielulintu is an omen of profound transition, often appearing as a shimmering, translucent raptor that weaves through the aurora borealis, singing a melody that resonates with the frequency of the soul's deepest longing
