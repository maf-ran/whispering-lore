Kayeri

forest-spirit · Colombia · South America

Kayeri is a seasonal forest spirit from Colombian folklore, particularly associated with the indigenous cultures of the Amazon and Orinoco regions. These mysterious beings are believed to appear during the rainy season when the forest is at its most lush and vibrant. According to tradition, all forest mushrooms are considered to be manifestations or aspects of Kayeri, making these spirits intimately connected with the fungal life of the tropical rainforest.
