Nyaminyami (Zambezi River God)

deity · Zambia · Southern Africa

Nyaminyami is the most famous river deity in Zambian mythology, the serpent-like god of the mighty Zambezi River. According to Tonga legend, Nyaminyami has the body of a snake and the head of a fish, symbolizing his dominion over both water and land. He is believed to control the flow of the Zambezi River and has the power to either bless or curse the people living along its banks. The legend of Nyaminyami gained international attention during the construction of the Kariba Dam (1955-1959), when the Tonga people warned that damming the river would anger the river god.
