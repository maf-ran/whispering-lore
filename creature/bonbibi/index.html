spirit · India · Asia

Bonbibi (Unknown). is a spirit. Bonbibi is the guardian spirit of the forests in the Sundarbans region of Bengal. She is revered by Hindu and Muslim communities alike as the protector against the dangers of the mangrove forest, part. Source: Bengali folklore; Sundarbans oral tradition
