Kerepunu

spirit · Papua New Guinea · Pacific

Kerepunu is a dangerous water spirit from Papuan mythology, particularly known among the peoples of the Gulf of Papua and the southern coast of Papua New Guinea. This malevolent entity dwells in deep rivers, swamps, and mangrove forests, where it lies in wait for unwary travelers. Kerepunu is known to drag people beneath the water, drowning them, and is especially feared by women and children. The spirit is associated with the darker aspects of the natural world, representing the dangers hidden within seemingly placid waters.
