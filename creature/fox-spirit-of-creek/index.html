Fox Spirit of Creek

spirit · United States · North America

No fox messenger spirit is attested in Creek (Muscogee) tradition. Documented Muscogee supernatural beings include Chufi, the Rabbit trickster; Kolowa, a hairy man-eating ogre; the underwater Tie-snakes; and Isti Papa, the 'man-eater' giant. The entry invents a messenger role with no support in ethnography.
