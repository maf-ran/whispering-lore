Sint-Holo

mythical-serpent · United States · North America

Sint-Holo is the horned serpent of Chickasaw and Choctaw tradition, a great water serpent who dwells in rivers, lakes and springs. Dangerous to those who disrespect it, it drags careless people to watery deaths, yet it also appears as a spirit guide during fasting, bestowing power and gifts on the worthy; it is the avowed enemy of the Thunder spirits.
