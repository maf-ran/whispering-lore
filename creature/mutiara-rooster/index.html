Mutiara (The Magic Rooster)

animal · Brunei · Southeast Asia

Mutiara was a legendary fighting rooster owned by Awang Senuai, nephew of Brunei's first Sultan, Awang Alak Betatar (Sultan Muhammad Shah). The rooster was famed as undefeated in cockfighting. News of Mutiara's prowess reached Raden Angsuka Dewa, King of the Majapahit Empire, who challenged Awang Senuai to a match between Mutiara and his own champion rooster Asmara. During the epic fight, Asmara was fatally wounded and fled into the sea, transforming into Pulau Pilong-Pilongan island. Mutiara, in pursuit, was cursed by the Majapahit king and turned into Pulau Lumut-Lunting
