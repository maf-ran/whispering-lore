Cougar Spirit (Mixtec)

spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

In the rich mythological landscape of the Mixtec people the cougar spirit embodies transformation and the liminal space between earthly existence and celestial power. Mixtec religion recorded in surviving pre-Columbian codices describes a universe created through the sacrifice of primordial beings and maintained by interactions between humans, nature spirits, and gods like Dzahui and Nine Wind. The cougar spirit belongs to this intricate web of relationships, serving as a psychopomp guiding souls of Mixtec nobles through the nine levels of the underworld into the presence of ancestors.
