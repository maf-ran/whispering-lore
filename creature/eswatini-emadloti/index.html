Emadloti

spirit · Eswatini · Southern Africa

The emadloti are the ancestral spirits of Swazi traditional religion, the most active forces in daily life. Unlike the remote creator Mvelincanti, they watch over descendants and shape fortune; neglect brings illness or misfortune until offerings restore harmony. Offerings include beer libations, prayer and animal sacrifice at life events, the king as chief intermediary to the royal ancestors.
