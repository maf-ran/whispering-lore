Slattenpatte

troll · Denmark · Nordic

Slattenpatte (Saggy-Breast) is a giant elf-woman or troll-woman from Danish folklore, distinguished by her extraordinarily long breasts that she throws over her shoulders or dips into streams to nurse fish and other water creatures. Her most famous depiction is a 1668 woodcarving by Abel Schrøder under the pulpit of Vejlø Church near Næstved, where she stands as a caryatid with her breasts hanging to her knees.
