Moose Guardian of Aymara

guardian · Bolivia · South America

Not independently attested in Aymara tradition. Moose are absent from South America. Aymara high-altitude mythology (Altiplano) features camelids (llama, vicuña), condors, and fox tricksters, not moose. Lake guardians include the serpent Amaru and mountain deities (Achachila). 'Moose Guardian' is a fabricated template entry.
