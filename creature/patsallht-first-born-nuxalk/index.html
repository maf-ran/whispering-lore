Patsallht First Born Nuxalk

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested under this name in the available Nuxalk (Bella Coola) sources. The name is associated with the Nuxalk origin account of Ista, the First Woman who descended to land, and with the creation of the first people; closer documentation is needed before the figure can be verified.
