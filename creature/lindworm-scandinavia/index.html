Lindworm

dragon · Norway · Nordic

The Lindworm is a serpentine dragon from Scandinavian mythology, a massive legless or two-legged reptile that guards treasures, haunts deep forests, and terrorizes rural communities. Unlike the winged dragons of southern Europe, the Lindworm crawls on its belly like a great snake, with iridescent scales and a venomous bite. In Swedish and Norwegian folklore, Lindworms often appear as cursed princes or princesses who can only be freed by a brave knight or a clever maiden.
