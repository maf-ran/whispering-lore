Fox Nature-Spirit of Haida

nature-spirit · Canada · North America

No genuine fox nature-spirit is documented in Haida tradition; this entry is an unverifiable composite. Haida cosmology centers on Raven as trickster and creator, the Bear Mother, the supernatural killer-whale beings Sgaana, and the culture hero Nanasimget, none of which includes a fox spirit.
