Hathor (Cow Goddess of Love and Music)

deity · Egypt · Africa

Hathor is the great Egyptian cow goddess of love, beauty, music, dance, fertility, and motherhood. One of the most beloved and widely worshipped deities, she embodies feminine grace and nurturing. Associated with the Milky Way, she was considered the celestial cow who gave birth to the sky. She welcomed the dead into the afterlife with food and drink (Lady of the West). As the Eye of Ra, she had a wrathful aspect and could transform into the lioness Sekhmet. Her temple at Dendera is one of the best-preserved in Egypt.
