Poshaiyankya

spirit · United States · North America

Poshaiyankya is a spirit of the sky associated with the winter solstice in Zuni Pueblo tradition. He figures in kachina religious practice and is connected to the creation of ceremonial life, teaching the people songs and rituals tied to the turning of the year and the renewal of the world.
