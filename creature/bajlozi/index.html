Bajlozi

giant · Albania · Balkan

Baloz, recorded in the database as 'Bajlozi,' is a monstrous giant or sea-monster of Albanian heroic poetry, an antagonist fought by the Kreshnik heroes, especially Muji and Halili, in the Songs of the Frontier Warriors. Epic heroes overcome him through courage and guile. The input's 'shadow giant fond of riddles' is inaccurate; the figure belongs to the martial oral epics.
