Eagle Guardian (Zulu)

guardian · South Africa · Southern Africa

In Zulu-influenced traditions of Myanmar, the eagle guardian reflects the syncretism of African and Southeast Asian spiritual beliefs about raptors as divine messengers. The eagle is revered as the king of birds, a symbol of royal authority, courage, and spiritual vision. Eagle feathers are reserved for leaders and warriors, conferring the bird's power of clear sight and connection to the divine. The eagle guardian watches over communities, carries prayers to the spirit realm, and protects against evil forces.
