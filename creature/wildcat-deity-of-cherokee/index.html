Wildcat Deity of Cherokee

deity · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat deity in Cherokee tradition. Cherokee animal lore includes the panther as a powerful being, but no deified wildcat figure is recorded; Cherokee spirit animals are more prominently the bear (whose people became the Yonva bear clan) and the wolf. The record likely conflates a generic feline spirit with Cherokee culture, where no such deity appears.
