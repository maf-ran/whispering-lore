demon · Nepal · Asia

Lakhey (Unknown). is a demon. The Lakhey is a fearsome demon figure in Nepalese folklore, particularly central to Newar culture in the Kathmandu Valley. Dancers costumed as the Lakhey perform during the Indra Jatra festival.. Source: Nepalese folklore; Newar tradition
