Haetae

chimera · South Korea · East Asia and Pacific

Haetae (also known as Haechi) is a mythical guardian beast from Korean mythology, depicted as a chimeric lion-like creature covered in scales with a single horn on its forehead. It is one of the most important and beloved mythical creatures in Korean culture, serving as the official symbol of Seoul. Haetae are believed to possess the ability to distinguish good from evil, making them symbols of justice and law.
