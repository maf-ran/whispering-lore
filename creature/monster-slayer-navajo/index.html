Monster Slayer (Naayééʼ Neizghání)

hero · United States · North America

Monster Slayer (Naayééʼ Neizghání) is the elder of the Hero Twins of Navajo (Diné) tradition, son of Changing Woman (Asdzą́ą́ Nádleehé) and the Sun. With his younger brother Born-for-Water he travelled the Fifth World destroying the Anaye, the alien monsters that devoured the people, using weapons of lightning; he remains the great protector of the Diné.
