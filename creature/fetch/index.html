Fetch

ghost · Ireland · Celtic

The Fetch is a spectral double or apparition of a living person in Irish and Northern English folklore, regarded as an omen of impending death. The term likely derives from Hiberno-English adaptation of Old Irish faith meaning seer, or from Old English faecce found in medieval glossaries. In 19th-century collections by John and Michael Banim, the fetch appears as an exact duplicate whose sighting carries profound significance.
