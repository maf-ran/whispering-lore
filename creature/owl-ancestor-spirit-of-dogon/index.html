Owl Ancestor-Spirit of Dogon

ancestor-spirit · Mali · West Africa

Not independently attested in Dogon tradition. Dogon ancestor spirits are the Nommo (amphibious) and Lebe (serpent). Owls appear in Dogon iconography on sanctuary doors but not as named ancestor-spirits guarding valleys. Record appears fabricated from generic 'owl guardian' templates.
