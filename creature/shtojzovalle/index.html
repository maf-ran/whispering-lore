Shtojzovalle

spirit · Albania · Balkan

Shtojzovalle, the 'moonlight dancers' of Albanian folklore, are beautiful spirit women who dance in moonlit meadows and mountain clearings, enchanting mortals with hypnotic grace. Those who join risk being led astray or bound to the otherworld, and the dancers dissolve at sunrise. They embody the allure and peril of Albanian nature spirits.
