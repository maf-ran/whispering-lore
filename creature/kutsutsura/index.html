Kutsutsura

undead · Japan · East Asia and Pacific

Kutsutsura is a malevolent undead entity from the folklore of the Xhosa and Zulu peoples of South Africa. The name Kutsutsura derives from terms meaning 'to be cut off' or 'to be separated,' referring to its nature as a soul severed from the community of ancestors. Unlike benevolent ancestral spirits (amadlozi/izinyanya), Kutsutsura are the restless, corrupted ghosts of evil people, sorcerers, or those who died with unresolved grudges.
