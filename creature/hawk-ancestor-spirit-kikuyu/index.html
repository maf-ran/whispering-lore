Hawk Ancestor-Spirit (Kikuyu)

ancestor-spirit · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested: while the Kikuyu honor ancestor spirits (ngoma) and show respect for birds of omen, no specific hawk ancestor-spirit is documented in Gikuyu tradition. The description is a generic 'hawk as messenger between worlds' template. The closest genuine figures are the ancestral ngoma spirits and the veneration of Ngai, but none is named a hawk ancestor-spirit.
