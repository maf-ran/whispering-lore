Moose Ancestor-Spirit of Kikuyu

ancestor-spirit · Kenya · East Africa

Not independently attested in Kikuyu tradition. Moose (Alces alces) are absent from East Africa. Kikuyu ancestor spirits (ngoma cia andu a kale) associate with indigenous fauna—elephant, leopard, hyena—or the sacred mugumo fig tree. This entry appears fabricated, conflating North American moose lore with Kikuyu spirituality. Closest analogue: Mugumo tree spirit.
