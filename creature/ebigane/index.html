Ebigane

hybrid · Equatorial Guinea · Central Africa

Ebigane is a predatory hybrid monster from Equatorial Guinean (various groups) folklore. It haunts dense rainforests, combining stealth and versatile attacks. Described as a composite earth-creature, it represents primal wilderness dangers. Fire is its weakness. The legend encodes wisdom about natural and spiritual worlds.
