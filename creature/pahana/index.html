Pahana

spirit · United States · North America

Pahana (also Bahana) is the 'True White Brother,' the lost elder brother of Hopi prophecy who left the Hopi people long ago and is destined to return to restore balance and reunite the world. He carries the sacred tablets of the Hopi, and his coming is bound up with the tribe's stories of the four worlds and their migrations.
