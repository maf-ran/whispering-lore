Lei

spirit · East Timor · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of East Timor folklore; searches locate no figure called 'Lei,' the Tetum word for the grass Imperata cylindrica. The recorded mountain, spring, and rock spirits align with documented Tetum beliefs in mate bein, ancestral ghosts, and lulik, sacred places guarded by land spirits, but no named being matches the entry.
