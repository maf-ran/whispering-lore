Hawk Guardian of Inuit

guardian · Canada · North America

In the vast Arctic world of the Inuit people, the Hawk Guardian (known in relation to the rough-legged hawk and gyrfalcon) is a spirit being that embodies survival, keen vision, and mastery over the frozen landscape. The Inuit, who inhabit the northern regions of Alaska, Canada, and Greenland, regard birds of prey as powerful spiritual allies that can traverse the boundary between the earth and the sky. The Hawk Guardian is particularly associated with the harsh winter months when food is scarce and survival depends on the sharpest senses. Inuit shamans (angakkuit) call upon this guardian duri
