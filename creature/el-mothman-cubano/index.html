El Mothman Cubano

monster · Cuba · Caribbean

El Mothman Cubano is a cryptid from modern Cuban folklore, described as a large winged humanoid that appears before significant disasters and tragic events. The legend emerged in the late 20th century, drawing parallels to the famous Mothman of Point Pleasant, West Virginia, while incorporating distinctively Cuban elements. Sightings have been reported in the mountainous regions of the Sierra Maestra and the Escambray Mountains, as well as near the coast. Witnesses describe it as a harbinger that appears days or weeks before hurricanes, earthquakes, or other calamities
