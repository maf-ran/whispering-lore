Olifat

trickster · Federated States of Micronesia · Pacific

Olifat is a trickster god in Micronesian mythology, particularly among the islands of Chuuk. He was the grandson of the great god Anulap, born to the god Lugeleng and the mortal woman Tarisso, daughter of the octopus goddess Hit. When Lugeleng's wife tried to prevent the union, Hit danced so lewdly that the wife fainted from arousal and had to be carried back to the sky, allowing Olifat's conception.
