Amma

deity · Mali · West Africa

Amma is the supreme creator god of the Dogon people of Mali, who created the universe from a cosmic egg called aduno tal which contained all future matter and motion. Alone at the beginning of time, Amma created the world but the first being, Ogo (Yurugu the Pale Fox), was born prematurely and without a twin, embodying incompleteness and disorder. To restore balance, Amma created the Nommo, twin amphibious fish-beings who represent water, speech, and cosmic order. Amma is a sky god who governs the cosmos from afar, accessible through ritual and divination
