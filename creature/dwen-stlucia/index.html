Dwen

spirit · Saint Lucia · Caribbean

The Dwen is a haunting figure in Saint Lucian folklore — the spirit of a child who died before baptism, condemned to wander the earth. Called Dwen in Saint Lucia (Douen in Trinidad and Dwenn in Grenada), these entities are characterized by backward-facing feet with heels to the front and knees also reversed. They have no faces — only a smooth surface where features should be, except for a small mouth to speak with. They wear large floppy straw hats to hide their facelessness.
