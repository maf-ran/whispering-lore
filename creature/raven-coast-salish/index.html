Raven Coast Salish

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Raven is the transformative trickster of Coast Salish peoples, a shapeshifting creator-being who brought light, fire, and salmon into the world. His stories are told from the Squamish to the Halkomelem and Nooksack, where he shares the stage with other animal transformers in the tales of the myth age.
