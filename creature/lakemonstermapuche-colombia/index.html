Lake Monster (Mapuche)

water-spirit · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Colombia. Mapuche lake monsters (Coi Coi-Vilu, Trentren Vilu, serpent of Lake Lanalhue) belong to Chile and Argentina. The Colombian Monster of Lake Tota is a Muisca serpent, not Mapuche. Country attribution is wrong.
