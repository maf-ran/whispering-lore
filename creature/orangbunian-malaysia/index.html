Orang Bunian

forest-spirit · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

The orang bunian are the invisible 'hidden people' of Malay folklore: beautiful beings who live in deep forests and high mountains, dressed in ancient Southeast Asian style, sometimes said to lack a philtrum. They form families, clans, even kingdoms, befriend dukun, bomoh, and pawang, and are usually benevolent; tales tell of humans intermarrying with them only to find years have passed.
