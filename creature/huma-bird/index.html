Huma Bird

bird · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

The Huma Bird (also known as Homa or Homay) is a legendary mythical bird from Persian folklore and a common motif in Sufi poetry, symbolizing fortune, kingship, and spiritual transcendence. According to tradition, the Huma never alights on the ground but lives its entire life flying invisibly high above the earth — some legends even claim it has no legs. Its shadow is said to bestow kingship upon whomever it falls, making it a powerful symbol of divine right and legitimacy in Persian and Mughal imperial iconography.
