Wildcat Animal-Spirit of Inuit

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in Inuit tradition. No wildcats or bobcats live in the Arctic, and no wildcat spirit appears in Inuit mythology, whose animal spirits include the polar bear (Nanook), the wolf (Amarok), the raven, and the whale. The record's forest-dwelling wildcat is a misattribution that ignores Inuit geography and oral tradition.
