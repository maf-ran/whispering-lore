Havmand (Merman)

mermaid · Denmark · Nordic

Havmand ('sea-man') is the Danish merman: rugged man's upper body, fish lower body with shimmering blue-green scales. Solitary, melancholic; uses enchanting voice and current manipulation to woo human women to depths for their souls. Inhabits deep ocean, coral reefs, underwater caves. Vulnerable to land confinement, bright sun, iron. Counterpart to havfrue.
