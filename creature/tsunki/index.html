Tsunki

water-spirit · Ecuador · South America

Tsunki is the primordial water spirit of the Shuar and Achuar (Jivaroan peoples, Ecuador/Peru). Appears as a beautiful woman or anaconda near remote waterfalls. Source of all shamanic power (tsentsak); snip grants crystal tsentsak to initiates. Features in myths 'The Tsunki and the Hunter' and 'Tsunki and the Chased Woman'. Documented by Perruchon (2003), Whitten (2008), Beyer (2009).
