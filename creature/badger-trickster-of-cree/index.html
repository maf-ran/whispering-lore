Badger Trickster of Cree

trickster · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Cree tradition features a badger trickster. The Cree culture-hero and trickster is Wisakedjak (Whiskey Jack), with the hare and, further west, Coyote in some regional variants; the badger has no documented place in Cree storytelling.
