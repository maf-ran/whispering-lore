Coyote Spirit (Zulu)

spirit · United States · North America

Coyote Spirit (Zulu) is a spirit from USA mythology. These sacred earths, particularly white clay called umcako used by sangomas for healing ceremonies and by brides for beautification, are believed to be petrified remains of ancestral offerings transformed by the spirit world. The coyote spirit guards these mineral resources, regulating their harvest and ensuring use only for intended sacred purposes. The spirit's role as protector of medicinal earths connects to the broader Zulu concept of ubuntu, the interconnectedness of all beings, as earth's healing minerals flow from the spiritual realm
