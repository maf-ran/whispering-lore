Raven Kwakwaka Wakw

animal-spirit · Canada · North America

Raven is the great transformer-trickster of Kwakwaka'wakw tradition, a being who reshapes the world through appetite and deception. In the tale of Great-Inventor recorded by Boas, Raven tricks the water-woman of Bull Harbor, fills a bladder with stolen water, and urinates to create the rivers. He also works to release daylight and fix the order of things.
