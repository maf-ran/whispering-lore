Jé-rouges

werewolf · Haiti · Caribbean

Jé-rouges (Lougawou) are Haitian werewolf-like shapeshifters from Vodou tradition, capable of transforming into wolves or other animals to hunt at night. They function as familiars for bokors (sorcerers), characterized by red glowing eyes. Documented in Haitian folklore collections by Simpson (1942) and later ethnographies.
