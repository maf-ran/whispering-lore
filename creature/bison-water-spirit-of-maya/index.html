Bison Water-Spirit of Maya

water-spirit · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The entry itself describes the spirit as a fusion invented around the bison. Chaac is a genuine Maya rain deity but is never depicted with or served by a bison; the oceanic steed, Venus-link, and calendar-round associations are fabricated.
