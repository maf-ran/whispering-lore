Cougar Ancestor-Spirit (Muisca)

ancestor-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

The Muisca of the Colombian highlands (not Mexico) revered animal forms and ancestral powers, with deities such as Bachue and Bochica central to their cosmology. The puma, native to the Andes, could carry such symbolic weight, but a specific Muisca 'cougar ancestor-spirit' is not directly attested in colonial sources. The entry misplaces the culture in Mexico.
