Whale Trickster of Dogon

trickster · Mali · West Africa

Not independently attested in Dogon tradition. Dogon trickster is exclusively Ogo (Pale Fox). No whale trickster exists in landlocked Mali. The 'whale trickster guarding rivers' with flight and storm-calling is fabricated, possibly conflating Ogo's chaos with Nommo's water association.
