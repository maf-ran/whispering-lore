Diser

spirit · Sweden · Nordic

The Diser (singular: Dis, from Old Norse dís meaning "lady") are a collective of powerful female spirits in Norse mythology and Germanic tradition who act as protective guardians of individuals, families, and clans. They are intimately associated with fate, fertility, and death, occupying a mysterious space between deities, ancestral spirits, and supernatural guardians. The Disir encompass a broad range of female supernatural figures including valkyries (called "Odin's Disir"), norns, and certain goddesses like Freyja.
