Raven Spirit (Laotian)

spirit · Laos · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named figure of Lao folklore; searches locate no 'Raven Spirit.' A crow appears in the Lao legend of the crow and the peacock, in which the two birds painted each other's plumage, and the raven's scavenging ties it to death in village belief, but no documented entity named 'Raven Spirit' exists in Lao tradition.
