Fée

fairy · France · Mediterranean

The Fée (French fairy) tradition represents one of Europe's most sophisticated and influential fairy lore systems, deeply interwoven with French literature, court culture, and folk magic. Unlike the diminutive winged fairies of Victorian England, the French Fée are powerful supernatural women—often of noble stature and immense magical ability—who intervene directly in human affairs.
