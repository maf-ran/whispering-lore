Sun Spirit (Olmec)

nature-spirit · Mexico · Mesoamerica

Not independently attested as a named sun spirit of Olmec folklore. The Olmec pantheon as reconstructed from iconography includes the Maize God, a rain or were-jaguar figure, and a celestial Bird Monster, but no dedicated 'sun spirit' appears in the archaeological record. The record appears to be a generic forest-spirit template rather than a documented Olmec being.
