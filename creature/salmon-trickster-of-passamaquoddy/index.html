Salmon Trickster of Passamaquoddy

trickster · United States · North America

Not attested as a salmon trickster in Passamaquoddy folklore. The Passamaquoddy trickster is Lox, and Leland's Algonquin Legends includes a tale of Lox catching salmon; but no salmon character acts as a trickster. The entry inflates a single episode into a nonexistent trickster figure.
