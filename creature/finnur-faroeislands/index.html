Finnur hin Fróði

human · Faroe Islands · Nordic

Finnur hin Fróði (Finn the Wise) is the most renowned figure in Faroese wizard folklore, a legendary wise man and magic-worker associated with the 'Black Book' (Cyprianus/Svarteboken) tradition that spread across Scandinavia. In Faroese folk belief, wizards and cunning folk possessed books of black magic that could only be passed on to a willing successor. These practitioners could control weather, heal the sick, find lost objects, ward off trolls and hidden people, and counter malevolent magic.
