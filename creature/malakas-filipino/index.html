Malakas (The First Man)

demigod · Philippines · Southeast Asia

In Tagalog creation myth, Malakas (Strong) is the first human man, who together with Maganda (Beautiful), emerged from a split bamboo stalk. According to the myth, the world began as a vast emptiness with only the sky and the sea. A huge bird (believed to be the deity Amihan) grew tired of flying and stirred up the sea, causing an island to emerge. The bird then pecked at a bamboo stalk until it split, revealing the first man Malakas and the first woman Maganda. They became the ancestors of all humanity who populated the Philippine archipelago.
