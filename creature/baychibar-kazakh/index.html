Baychibar

mythical-horse · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Baychibar is the magic horse of Alpamysh in the Turkic epic of that name. Bought as a scrawny colt, he grows into a matchless steed who carries Alpamysh to win Barchin's contests, speaks to give counsel, and warns of treachery on the long roads of the tale; no unworthy rider can mount him, and he is Alpamysh's truest companion.
