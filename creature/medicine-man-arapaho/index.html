Medicine Man

human · United States · North America

The Medicine Man is a recurring human hero figure in Arapaho mythology. In the story of How Medicine Man Resurrected Buffalo, a powerful medicine man saved his starving people by seeking a vision, receiving instructions from the Earth Spirit, and opening a passage to the underground buffalo world. He demonstrates the power of faith, ceremony, and spiritual dedication. Medicine men (also called shamans or holy men) are central figures in Arapaho spirituality who communicate with the spirit world, heal the sick, and maintain the balance between the human and spirit realms
