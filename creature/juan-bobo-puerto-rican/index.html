Juan Bobo (Puerto Rican Trickster Folk Hero)

folk-hero · Puerto Rico · Caribbean

Juan Bobo (Simple John) is the beloved trickster folk hero of Puerto Rican folklore -- a simple-minded but well-intentioned young boy whose absurd misunderstandings and literal interpretations of his mother instructions create a cascade of hilarious disasters. For over 200 years, Juan Bobo stories have been a cornerstone of Puerto Rican oral tradition and children literature. He is simultaneously a fool and a trickster: his actions are driven by genuine kindness and good intentions, but his complete lack of common sense leads to catastrophes.
