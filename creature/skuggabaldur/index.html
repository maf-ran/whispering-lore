Skuggabaldur

shapeshifter · Iceland · Nordic

Skuggabaldur (Shadow Baldur) is a hybrid creature in Icelandic folklore, the opposite cross of the skoffín — born from a tomcat and a vixen (female fox). Skuggabaldur has very dark fur, almost black, and sometimes possesses the same deadly gaze as the skoffín. It preys primarily on livestock. The most famous skuggabaldur story involves one that was killed by a farmer. When it was dying, the creature spoke and said: Tell the tomcat at Bollastaðir.
