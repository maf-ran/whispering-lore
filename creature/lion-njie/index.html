The Lion of Njie

guardian · Senegal · West Africa

In Serer tradition of Senegal, the lion is a totemic guardian associated with the Njie (Njai) clan and lineage. Clans hold specific animals as sacred ancestors and protectors, and the lion's strength and dignity mirror the clan's claims to authority. The figure connects the living community with the ancestors and the natural world.
