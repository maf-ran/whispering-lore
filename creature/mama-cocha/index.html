Mama Cocha

goddess · Peru · South America

Mama Qucha (Mama Cocha, 'Mother Sea/Lake') is the Inca goddess of the sea, lakes, and all waters. Wife of Wiraqucha, mother of Inti (Sun) and Mama Killa (Moon). One of four Elemental Mothers with Pachamama, Mama Nina, Mama Wayra. Worshipped by coastal peoples for calm seas and good fishing; snip inland peoples honored her as lake goddess. Inhabits Hanan Pacha (upper world).
