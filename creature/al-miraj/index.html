Al-Mi'raj

mythical-horse · Lebanon · Middle East and North Africa

Al-Mi'raj is a bizarre and fearsome creature from Arabian folklore — a giant yellow rabbit with a foot-long black spiraled unicorn horn growing from its forehead. Despite its rabbit-like form, Al-Mi'raj is a ferocious carnivore that uses its horn to impale much larger victims before devouring them. According to al-Qazwini's Wonders of Creation, Al-Mi'raj lived on a mythical island in the Indian Ocean called Jazira al-Tennyn (Dragon Island).
