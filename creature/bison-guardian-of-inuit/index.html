Bison Guardian of Inuit

guardian · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a guardian of Inuit folklore. Bison never reached traditional Inuit lands; the large grazer of the far north is the muskox (umingmak), not the buffalo. No Inuit bison-guardian appears in documented traditions, which instead feature beings such as Sedna and the tuurngait.
