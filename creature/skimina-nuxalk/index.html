Skimina (Granddaughter of Alhkw'ntam)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Skimina is the granddaughter of the Nuxalk (Bella Coola) creator Alhkw'ntam, who lives in the upper world. In the story of how Raven gave light to the world, she unknowingly holds the transforming Raven as he changes into a baby, and her care of the crying child lets him reach the box where the sun was hidden.
