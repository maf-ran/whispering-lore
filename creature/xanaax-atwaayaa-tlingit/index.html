Xanaax Atwaayaa Tlingit

nature-spirit · United States · North America

In the Tlingit story of the Salmon Box (Kudatan Kahidi), Xanaax.atwaayaa is a being who holds a staff tipped with a supernatural octopus tentacle. Raven, disguised, trades him a magic arrow in the form of a magpie and gains the staff, whose power he uses to draw the salmon close to shore and to control their coming.
