La Planchada

ghost · Mexico · North America

La Planchada (The Ironed One, referring to her perfectly pressed nurse's uniform) is a ghost legend from Mexico City hospitals that has spread through Mexican-American communities in the US Southwest. According to the story, a beautiful nurse named Eulalia worked at Hospital Juárez in the 1930s, deeply dedicated to her patients. She fell in love with a handsome doctor who courted her but ultimately abandoned her to marry another woman.
