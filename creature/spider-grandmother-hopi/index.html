Spider Grandmother (Spider Woman)

spirit · United States · North America

Kokyangwuti, Spider Grandmother, is the Earth Goddess and helper of Hopi tradition, partner to Tawa. She created Huzruiwuhti (Woman of Hard Substances), sealed the righteous in hollow reeds during the great flood, guided clans through their migrations, and aided heroes across many Hopi narratives.
