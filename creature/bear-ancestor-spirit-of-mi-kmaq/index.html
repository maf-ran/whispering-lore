Bear Ancestor-Spirit of Mi'kmaq

ancestor-spirit · Canada · North America

Among the Mi'kmaq people of Eastern Canada, the bear holds a significant place as a powerful ancestral figure and clan animal. The Mi'kmaq recognize the bear as a wise being that appears in their oral traditions as a teacher of survival skills and spiritual knowledge. Bear ancestor spirits are believed to guide hunters through the forests of the Maritime provinces, offering protection to those who show proper respect through songs and ceremonial offerings.
