Tsui-//Goab

deity · Namibia · Southern Africa

Tsui-//Goab is the supreme being and creator god of the Khoikhoi people, associated with the east, the moon, and good fortune. His name means 'Wounded Knee,' stemming from his eternal battle against //Gaunab, the malevolent being who causes sickness and death. The Khoikhoi traditionally prayed at dawn facing east, offering supplications for rain and prosperity. The moon was seen as his visible manifestation, and its cycles were deeply tied to Khoikhoi religious observance. Tsui-//Goab represents the forces of life, health, and cosmic order
