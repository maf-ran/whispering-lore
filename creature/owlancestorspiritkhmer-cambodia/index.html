Owl Ancestor-Spirit (Khmer)

ancestor-spirit · Cambodia · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested in documented Khmer folklore or academic sources as a named 'Owl Ancestor-Spirit.' Owls do carry omen meanings in Cambodia—their calls are often read as ill omens or messages—but no source records a specific ancestor-spirit embodied as an owl and tied to lakes. The entry appears to combine general owl-omen belief with an unattested spirit name.
