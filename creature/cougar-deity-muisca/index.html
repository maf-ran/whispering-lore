Cougar Deity (Muisca)

deity · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No cougar or puma deity appears in the documented Muisca pantheon of the Colombian Altiplano. Colonial records of the 1563 Ubaque ceremony mention a 'tiger' — perhaps a puma or jaguar — tied to the culture-hero Bochica, but no cave-guardian cougar deity is attested.
