Fox Ancestor-Spirit of Lakota

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

In Lakota tradition the fox (sungila) is a notable animal in medicine and storytelling, and the Kit Fox Society (sungila okolakiciye) was a warrior order that policed the home camp and supplied food. The fox appears in tales as a clever figure valued for sharp wits and survival in dangerous country.
