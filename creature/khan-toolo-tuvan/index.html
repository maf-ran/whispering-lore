Khan Toolo

hero · Russia · Central Asia

Not independently attested as a distinct hero of Tuvan folklore. No source confirms an epic hero named 'Khan Toolo' with superhuman strength and horse mastery; Tuvan heroic tales record figures such as the hunters of the Tuvan epic cycle, but no hero by this name appears in published sources. The traits recorded appear to derive from modern speculation.
