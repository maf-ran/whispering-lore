Yurugu (Pale Fox)

trickster · Mali · West Africa

Yurugu (also called Ogo, the Pale Fox) is the first being of Dogon creation, born prematurely from Amma's flawed creative act. He limps across the Sahel on uneven legs as the living embodiment of disorder and incompleteness. Despite his chaotic nature, Yurugu serves a vital purpose through divination: each night, Dogon diviners draw a rectangular grid in fine sand, and Yurugu walks it, leaving paw-prints that are read at dawn to interpret the future.
