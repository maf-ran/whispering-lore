Rock Crystal Boy

spirit · United States · North America

Rock Crystal Boy is a resident deity in Navajo tradition, appointed by First Man and First Woman to dwell at the sacred mountain Sisnaajiní (Blanca Peak) in the East. Along with Rock Crystal Girl, he was created when the sacred mountains were re-established in the Fourth World. Sisnaajiní was decorated with white shells, white lightning, white corn, and dark clouds representing male rain. Rock Crystal Boy and Rock Crystal Girl serve as guardians and spiritual presences at this eastern sacred pea
