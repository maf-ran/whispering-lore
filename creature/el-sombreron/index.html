El Sombrerón

goblin · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

El Sombrerón (The Big-Hatted One) is a legendary goblin-like figure from Guatemalan and southern Mexican folklore who is particularly prominent in Mexican-American storytelling. He is described as a small man — no taller than a child — wearing an enormous hat, a red tie, and riding a tiny horse or mule. El Sombrerón is best known for braiding the manes and tails of horses, donkeys, and mules into intricate patterns that cannot be undone.
