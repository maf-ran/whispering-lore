La Siguanaba

shapeshifter · Guatemala · Mesoamerica

A BEAUTIFUL WOMAN spirit from Mesoamerican folklore, particularly prevalent in El Salvador, Guatemala, and southern Mexico, with variants told in Mexican-American communities of the US Southwest. The legend has pre-Columbian origins among the Pipil and Maya peoples, where female water spirits were associated with both fertility and danger. La Siguanaba appears as a stunningly beautiful woman with long, flowing hair, seen bathing in rivers or washing clothes.
