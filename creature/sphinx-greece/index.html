Sphinx

guardian · Greece · Mediterranean

The Greek Sphinx is a winged monster with a woman's head, lion's body and serpent's tail, sent by Hera to plague Thebes; she devoured all who failed her riddle until Oedipus answered 'Man' and she destroyed herself. Where the Egyptian sphinx symbolized royal power and wisdom, the Greek sphinx embodied dangerous, riddle-guarded knowledge, haunting temple entrances and crossroads.
