Moose Guardian of Muisca

guardian · Colombia · South America

Not independently attested in Muisca tradition. Moose are absent from South America. Muisca mythology (Bacatá/ Bogotá savanna) centers on Bochica, Chía, Sué, and lake deities (Sie). No cervid guardians of cliffs exist; snip the spectacled bear (oso de anteojos) is the largest native mammal. 'Moose Guardian' is a fabricated template entry.
