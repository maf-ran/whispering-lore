Sasabonsom

giant · Ghana · West Africa

Sasabonsom is a terrifying giant monster in Akan mythology that dwells in the deepest parts of the forest. Unlike the relatively benign Mmoatia, Sasabonsom is malevolent — a huge, hairy creature with large eyes and long arms that sits on high branches and preys on humans who venture too deep. It is often described as having backward-pointing feet like the Mmoatia but is far more dangerous. Sasabonsom is the enemy of Anansi in some tales, representing brute force and evil that must be overcome through cunning.
