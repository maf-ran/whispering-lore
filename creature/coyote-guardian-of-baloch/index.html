Coyote Guardian of Baloch

guardian · Pakistan · South Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. Coyotes are not native to Balochistan. The mountain wolf, gurg-e-koh, belongs to genuine regional lore, but a water-finding coyote guardian watching over dry riverbeds is invented; no Balochi source records it.
