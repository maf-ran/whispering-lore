Crane Ghost of Ojibwe

ghost · Canada · North America

No crane ghost is attested in Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) tradition in Canada or the United States. The crane, Ajijaak, is the hereditary leadership clan, paired with the Loon Clan, and is celebrated for its loud voice at council rather than feared as a spirit. This entry appears to be an AI-generated invention with no basis in documented Anishinaabe folklore.
