Eagle Ancestor-Spirit of Ute

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

The Eagle Ancestor-Spirit is a sacred guardian figure in Ute mythology, representing the deep spiritual connection between the Ute people of the Great Basin and Rocky Mountains region and the natural world around them. As an ancestor spirit taking the form of an eagle, this powerful being is the guardian of remote springs and water sources in the mountainous Ute homeland, protecting these vital water supplies that sustain life in the arid landscape. The eagle is one of the most significant animal spirits in Ute tradition, symbolizing wisdom, courage, and connection to the creator Sinawav. Ute
