Abram Moos Kara Taaci

spirit · Russia · Central Asia

Abram Moos Kara Taaci is a powerful female shamanic figure of Altai epic tradition, linked to the underworld and called a daughter of Erlik. Capable of shapeshifting and wielding dark magic, she knows the lower world deeply and acts as both guide and challenger to epic heroes; in the Maadai-Kara epic she confronts Kögüdei Mergen. Her role reflects female shamanic power in Altai epic.
