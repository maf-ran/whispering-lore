Koyemsi

spirit · United States · North America

Koyemsi, also known as the Mudhead or Kachina Clown, is a distinctive figure in Hopi and Puebloan spiritual traditions of the Southwestern United States. These sacred clowns play a vital role in Hopi kachina ceremonies, serving as both entertainers and spiritual teachers who use humor and mockery to reinforce proper social behavior. Unlike the solemn kachinas that represent deities and ancestors, Koyemsi figures are deliberately comical and sometimes crude, using slapstick, mimicry, and parody to convey important cultural lessons.
