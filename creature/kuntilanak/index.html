Kuntilanak

vampire · Indonesia · Southeast Asia

Kuntilanak is a female vampire spirit from Indonesian and Malay folklore, one of the most feared supernatural beings in Southeast Asia. She is the ghost of a woman who died during childbirth or as a virgin, condemned to wander the earth in torment. The kuntilanak shares characteristics with the similar pontianak spirit of Malay tradition, and the names are sometimes used interchangeably across the region.
