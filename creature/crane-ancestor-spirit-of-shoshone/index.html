Crane Ancestor-Spirit of Shoshone

ancestor-spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested as an ancestor-spirit of Shoshone folklore. Cranes figure in some Shoshone narratives, but no crane ancestor-spirit is documented in Shoshone oral tradition, which centers figures such as Coyote and the Wolf brothers. The ancestor-spirit role appears to be invented with no supporting source.
