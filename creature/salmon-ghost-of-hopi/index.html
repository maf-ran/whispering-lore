Salmon Ghost of Hopi

ghost · United States · North America

Not attested in Hopi folklore. The Hopi, a Pueblo people of the arid Southwest, have no salmon in their environment or mythology; their spiritual world centers on katsinam, the Snake Dance, and corn. A Hopi 'salmon ghost' does not appear in any recorded tradition and is an invented entry.
