Owl Guardian of Tlingit

guardian · Canada · North America

No owl guardian is attested in Tlingit tradition. Tlingit lore records the woman of the Kîksa'dî clan who was turned into an owl, whose cry warns of storms and ill news, and owls carry associations with death omens. These are the extent of Tlingit owl beliefs; no guardian or tutelary owl exists.
