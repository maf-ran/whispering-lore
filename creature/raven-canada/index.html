Raven

deity · Canada · North America

Raven is the great trickster and culture hero of the Pacific Northwest, celebrated among the Haida, Tlingit, Tsimshian and neighboring peoples. A shape-shifter and thief of supernatural powers, he stole the sun, moon and stars from a stingy keeper, letting light into the world, and brought people fresh water and fire.
