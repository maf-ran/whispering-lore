Bison Spirit (Malagasy)

spirit · Madagascar · East Africa

Not independently attested: Malagasy tradition holds cattle (zebu) and the ancestors (razana) sacred, but no 'bison spirit' is recorded in Malagasy folklore, and bison are not native to Madagascar. The description duplicates the Kenya variant of the same fabricated entry verbatim. The genuine related belief is respect for zebu and the fady (taboo) system, not a bison guardian spirit.
