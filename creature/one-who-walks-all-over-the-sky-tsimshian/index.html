One Who Walks All Over The Sky Tsimshian

deity · Canada · North America

A figure of Tsimshian tradition of the Northwest Coast, associated with the origin of the sun and moon. The name describes a being who traverses the sky, and the story belongs to the Tsimshian cycle of sky-world tales that account for the celestial bodies and their movements. Details of the name and narrative vary among Tsimshian and neighbouring peoples.
