Fox Ghost of Inuit

ghost · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a ghost of Inuit folklore. Inuit tradition knows the fox-woman, a fox who takes human form and marries a hunter — a shape-shifter tale, not a ghost story. No Inuit fox-ghost is documented; the fox's roles are those of trickster and spirit-wife, not revenant.
