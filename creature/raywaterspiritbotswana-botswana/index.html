Ray Water-Spirit (Botswana)

water-spirit · Botswana · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Botswana tradition. No ethnographic source documents a "Ray Water-Spirit" protecting fields. The Herero have Zumwani (water woman), Tswana have water spirits (modimo wa metsi), but a ray-specific spirit is unverified. Appears to be a fabricated templated entry.
