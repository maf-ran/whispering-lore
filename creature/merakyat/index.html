Merakyat

water-spirit · Malaysia · Southeast Asia

Not independently attested as a named spirit of Malaysian folklore; searches locate no being called 'Merakyat.' The recorded water spirit of rivers and waterfalls, appearing as a beautiful woman or an old man, matches the attested Malay hantu air (water spirit) and penunggu sungai (river guardian), who demand respect and cleanliness from those who use the water.
