La Diablesse (The Devil Woman of the Road)

spirit · Trinidad and Tobago · Caribbean

La Diablesse (French for the she-devil) is a seductive yet deadly spirit in Trinidadian and West Indian folklore. She appears as a breathtakingly beautiful woman -- usually tall, light-skinned, with long flowing black hair and piercing eyes -- wearing a wide-brimmed hat and a flowing white, black, or red dress that conceals her horrifying secret: one leg ends in a cloven cow hoof instead of a human foot.
