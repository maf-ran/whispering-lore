Havsfrun

mermaid · Sweden · Nordic

While similar to the Sjöjungfru, the Havsfrun is a more ancient and powerful entity of the salt-water depths. She is the sovereign of the open sea, possessing a voice that can command the tides and call the whales. In Swedish coastal lore, the Havsfrun is a figure of tragic beauty and immense power, often mourning the loss of the land-world. She does not lure men to their deaths for sport, but rather out of a profound loneliness, seeking a companion who can withstand the crushing pressure and freezing cold of her abyssal kingdom
