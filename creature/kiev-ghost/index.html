Kiev Ghost

ghost · Ukraine · Slavic

The Ghost of Kyiv is a Ukrainian wartime legend that emerged in the first days of the 2022 Russian invasion: a mysterious MiG-29 pilot reportedly downing multiple Russian aircraft over the capital. It became a symbol of resistance and morale before officials clarified that the story was a composite. The entry's medieval 'catacomb haunting' is replaced with the attested modern legend.
