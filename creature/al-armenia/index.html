Al

demon · Armenia · Caucasus

Al (also called Alk in Armenian) is a dwarfish, half-human half-animal evil spirit that preys specifically on pregnant women and newborn infants in Armenian folklore. Described with iron teeth, brass or copper fingernails, fiery eyes, a clay nose, disheveled hair, tusks of a wild boar, and sagging breasts resembling a crone's form, the Al wears a pointed hat covered with small bells and can become invisible.
