Gandharva (Celestial Musician)

spirit · India · South Asia

Gandharvas are a class of celestial beings in Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions who serve as divine musicians and singers in the courts of the gods. Originating in the Rigveda as a singular celestial being who watches over Soma, they evolved into a class of minor deities numbering 6,333 according to the Atharvaveda. They are described as handsome beings who wear fragrant attire and have exceptional musical skills.
