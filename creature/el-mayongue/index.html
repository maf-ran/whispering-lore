El Mayongue

water-spirit · Dominican Republic · Caribbean

El Mayongue is a dangerous water spirit from Dominican Republic folklore, said to inhabit rivers, streams, and watering holes throughout the island's countryside. The name derives from African Taíno and African linguistic roots, reflecting the syncretic nature of Dominican folk beliefs. This river spirit is particularly feared by children and is used by parents as a cautionary tale to keep young ones away from dangerous waters. In rural areas of the Cibao Valley and the Cordillera Central, the legend of El Mayongue remains a common warning told to prevent drownings
