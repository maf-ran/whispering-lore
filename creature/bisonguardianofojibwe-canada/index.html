Bison Guardian of Ojibwe

guardian · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a guardian of Ojibwe folklore. The seven original Ojibwe doodem (totem) clans — crane, loon, bear, marten, deer, fish and bird — include no bison clan. No Ojibwe bison-guardian is documented in the standard clan accounts or spirit traditions.
