Ice Giant (Muisca)

giant · Colombia · South America

The Ice Giant is a mythical figure from Muisca mythology, the advanced pre-Columbian civilization of the Altiplano Cundiboyacense in the Colombian Andes. While the Muisca are best known for the legend of El Dorado, their mythology also includes giants and other supernatural beings. The Ice Giant likely relates to the Muisca understanding of their high-altitude environment, where freezing temperatures and paramo ecosystems shaped daily life.
