Um Hmaar

jinn · Bahrain · Middle East and North Africa

Um Hmaar (Mother of Donkeys) is a legendary half-woman, half-donkey jinn from Bahraini folklore who preys on children who venture outside at noon. She is described as having the upper body of a woman and the lower body of a donkey, with hooves that make a distinctive sound as she walks through neighborhood streets at midday. Dressed all in black, Um Hmaar knocks on doors while parents take their afternoon naps, begging for food or water. If children open the door, she devours them.
