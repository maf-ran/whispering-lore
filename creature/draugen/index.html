Draugen

ghost · Denmark · Nordic

Draugen is the sea-revenant of Danish and Norwegian coastal folklore, the ghost of a fisherman or sailor drowned at sea. He appears as a drenched figure rising half from the water, sometimes rowing a half-boat, and his appearance or cry foretells drowning and shipwreck. Belief was strongest in Norwegian fishing communities, where the draug warned of storms.
