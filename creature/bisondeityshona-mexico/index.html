Bison Deity (Shona)

deity · Mexico · Mesoamerica

This entry pairs a Shona (southern African) buffalo figure with a Mexican country tag, an evident synthesis error. In Shona tradition, ancestral spirits (mhondoro) and lineage spirits may appear through animals, and the buffalo (njati) is respected for strength. The 'Bison Deity' name is a loose rendering of that animal-ancestor complex, not a Mexican or a named Shona deity.
