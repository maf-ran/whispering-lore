Crane Trickster of Haida

trickster · Canada · North America

No crane trickster is attested in Haida tradition. The Haida trickster is Raven, the great transformer who stole the sun, moon, and stars; the crane appears only as a minor character in stories such as Kwaie'tek, the Sea Gull, where Raven tricks Crane and Sea Gull into fighting. This entry appears invented.
