Cóiste Bodhar

ghost · Ireland · Celtic

The Cóiste Bodhar (Irish 'deaf coach') is the death coach of Irish folklore: a black, windowless carriage pulled by three headless black horses, driven by the Dullahan (headless horseman carrying his rotting head). It collects souls of the wicked and those with unsettled debts, calling the dying person's name at crossroads—death follows within three days.
