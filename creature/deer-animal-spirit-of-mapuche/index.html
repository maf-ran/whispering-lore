Deer Animal-Spirit of Mapuche

animal-spirit · Chile · South America

The Deer Animal-Spirit of the Mapuche people of Chile and Argentina is a sacred being in the spiritual traditions of the most populous indigenous group in South America. The Mapuche, whose name means 'people of the land,' have a deep reverence for the natural world, and the huemul (south Andean deer) holds special significance as a symbol of the mountain forests. This deer spirit represents the newen (spiritual force) that flows through all living things, teaching the Mapuche about balance, healing, and the interconnection of all beings in the natural world
