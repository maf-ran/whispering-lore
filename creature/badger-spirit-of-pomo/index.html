Badger Spirit of Pomo

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Pomo source records a badger spirit, despite badgers being native to Pomo territory. Pomo ceremonial life centres on the Kuksu (Guksu) dance cult and figures such as Coyote and Bear; the badger plays no documented role in Pomo ritual or cosmology.
