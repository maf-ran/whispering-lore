Sun Goddess (Muisca)

deity · Colombia · South America

Partially attested but gender is incorrect. The Muisca sun deity is Sué (Xué, Zuhé), a male god, not a goddess. Sué was husband of Chía (moon goddess), worshipped at the Sun Temple of Sugamuxi (Sogamoso). The record's 'Sun Goddess' reflects a common modern error reversing the gender.
