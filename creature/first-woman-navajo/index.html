First Woman (Áłtsé Asdzą́ą́)

spirit · United States · North America

First Woman (Áłtsé Asdzą́ą́) is a primordial figure in Navajo creation mythology. She was formed from Blue Cloud and Yellow Cloud in the West, carrying yellow corn, white shell, turquoise, and yucca. She made a fire with turquoise and joined with First Man. She caused a great conflict by boasting that women could live without men, leading to the Separation of the Sexes which lasted four years and resulted in the birth of monsters.
