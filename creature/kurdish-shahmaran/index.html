Shahmaran (Kurdish Serpent Queen of Wisdom)

serpent · Turkey · Middle East and North Africa

Shahmaran is a legendary half-woman, half-serpent figure in Kurdish, Turkish, Persian, and Mesopotamian folklore. The name means 'King of the Serpents' in Persian. According to legend, a young man named Camsab accidentally discovered the underground realm of the serpents, where Shahmaran (the beautiful serpent queen) ruled. She shared her wisdom with him. When Camsab was forced to reveal her location to a tyrannical vizier, Shahmaran accepted her fate.
