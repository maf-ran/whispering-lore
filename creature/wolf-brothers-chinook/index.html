Wolf Brothers Chinook

animal-spirit · United States · North America

In Wasco (Chinookan) tradition, the Five Wolf Brothers appear as traveling companions in a tale cycle in which Coyote places the stars and orders the sky. Variants tell of rival sets of brothers whose contest produced the winds; the wolf brothers embody the strength of the pack, which Chinookan peoples respected in their storytelling.
