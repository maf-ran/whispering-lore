Yeshe Tsogyal

deity · China · Central Asia

Yeshe Tsogyal, the 'Victorious Lake of Wisdom,' was an eighth-century Tibetan princess and consort of Padmasambhava, revered as a master, consort, and dakini who attained the rainbow body. Credited with concealing the terma treasures with her master, she is invoked for healing and protection and remains a central female figure of Tibetan Buddhism.
