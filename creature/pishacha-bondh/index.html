Pishacha Bondh

demon · India · South Asia

Not independently attested in Indian tradition as 'Pishacha Bondh'. The name appears to be a modern compound of 'Pishacha' (flesh-eating demon) and 'bondh' (Bengali for 'bound'). No classical Sanskrit, Pali, or vernacular source mentions this entity. The giant temple guardian description resembles generic rakshasa or kṣetrapāla motifs, not a specific named being.
