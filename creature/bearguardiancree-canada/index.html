Bear Guardian (Cree)

guardian · Canada · North America

Bears are spiritually significant among the Cree (maskwa), respected as powerful beings and healers in the boreal forest world. The 'Bear Guardian watching over lakes' is a synthesis rather than an attested named figure: Cree tradition centers bears in hunting respect, dreams, and medicine, not specifically as lake spirits. The Cree are a Canadian people; the country metadata here is correct.
