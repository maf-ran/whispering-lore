Chichkhan

dwarf · Kazakhstan · Central Asia

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Kazakh mountain-dwarf spirit named 'Chichkhan' appears in any collection of Kazakh or Central Asian Turkic folklore. Kazakh animistic tradition does describe yer-su earth-water spirits, divs, and witch-figures such as Zheztyrnak, but none matches this name.
