Ant Spirit ( Maori)

spirit · New Zealand · Pacific

No ant spirit or ant atua is attested in Maori tradition; the entry's narrative of a forest-guardian ant is invented. Maori supernatural beings include the patupaiarehe, pale-skinned fairy folk of forest and mountain, the maero, and the taniwha, water guardians — none of which is an ant.
