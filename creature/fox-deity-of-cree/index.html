Fox Deity of Cree

deity · Canada · North America

In Cree oral tradition the fox appears as a shrewd, adaptable figure of the northern forests and plains, admired by hunters for thriving in harsh winters. Beyond general respect for the animal's cleverness, no named Cree fox deity is documented; the fox's role is that of a wise animal in tales rather than a formal spirit.
