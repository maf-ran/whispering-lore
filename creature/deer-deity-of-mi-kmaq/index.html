Deer Deity of Mi'kmaq

deity · Canada · North America

In Mikmaq tradition, the Deer Deity is a revered ocean guardian that watches over the Atlantic coastal waters and the abundant life within them. As a central figure in the Mikmaq spiritual landscape, this deity governs the seasonal runs of fish, the migrations of seals, and the safety of those who travel by canoe along the rugged coastline. It is closely associated with Glooscap teachings about the interconnectedness of all living things and the responsibility of humans to act as caretakers rather than conquerors of the natural world
