Olorun

deity · Nigeria · Africa

Olorun (Unknown). is a deity. Olórun is the supreme god in the Yoruba pantheon, the owner of the sky and the source of all life force (àṣẹ). He is considered the creator of the universe and delegates authority to the Orishas.. Source: Yoruba cosmology; Ifá tradition
