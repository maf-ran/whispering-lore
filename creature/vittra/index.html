Vittra

wight · Sweden · Nordic

Vittra are invisible underground wights of northern Sweden (Norrland), living in parallel cities with livestock. Resemble humans but unseen unless they choose. Peaceful if paths/dwellings undisturbed; cause accidents, illness, livestock loss if buildings block roads. 'Vittra-way' and 'Vitterställe' denote relocated places.
