Džambas

demon · North Macedonia · Slavic

Džambas is a fierce demon from Macedonian folklore, a terrifying creature that haunts the dark forests of North Macedonia armed with iron teeth and copper claws. This malevolent entity is one of the lesser-known but deeply feared figures in Slavic Balkan mythology, representing the dangers that lurk in the wilderness beyond the safety of human settlements. The Džambas specifically targets lone travelers who venture into its forest domain, attacking with brutal ferocity and showing no mercy to its victims.
