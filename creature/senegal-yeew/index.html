Yééw

spirit · Senegal · West Africa

Yééw is the hyena of Wolof folktales, a greedy and deceptive figure whose schemes always backfire, teaching children moral lessons through comic misfortune. In Senegalese oral tradition Yééw teaches the folly of selfishness while mirroring human weakness in village life.
