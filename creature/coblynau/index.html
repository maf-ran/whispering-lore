Coblynau

fairy · Wales · Celtic

The Coblynau are mythical gnome-like creatures from Welsh folklore that haunt mines and quarries throughout Wales and areas of Welsh settlement in America. The name is related to the English word 'goblin' and may derive from the German 'kobold' via French. First documented extensively by Wirt Sikes in his 1880 book 'British Goblins', the Coblynau are considered friendly fairies despite their grotesque appearance.
