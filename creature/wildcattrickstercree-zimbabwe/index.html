Wildcat Trickster (Cree)

trickster · Zimbabwe · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in Zimbabwe tradition. The Cree are an Indigenous people of North America (Canada/USA). The entry misattributes a Cree wildcat trickster to Zimbabwe. Fabricated cross-continental misattribution; snip wildcat tricksters appear in Cree/Algonquian tradition, not Shona.
