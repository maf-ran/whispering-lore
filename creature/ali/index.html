Ali

demon · Georgia · Caucasus

Ali is a demonic figure from Persian and Central Asian folklore, belonging to the same class of spirits as the Al and Albasty traditions found across the Caucasus and Turkic regions. These spirits are specifically associated with harming pregnant women, causing miscarriages, and interfering with childbirth. The concept of Ali is deeply rooted in pre-Islamic Persian beliefs and has parallels throughout the Middle East and Central Asia.
