Piath

monster · South Sudan · North Africa

Piath is a colossal serpent of Dinka belief, dwelling in the waters of the White Nile and its tributaries. Part of a world of river beings that shape floods and fishing fortunes, it demands care from those who use the water. As a nature spirit it is feared and respected, its moods read in the river's rise and fall.
