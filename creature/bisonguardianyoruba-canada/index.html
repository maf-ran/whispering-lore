Bison Guardian (Yoruba)

guardian · Canada · North America

Not independently attested as a guardian of Yoruba folklore. The bison is absent from Yoruba cosmology; Yoruba forest guardians and spirits are beings such as Esu, Ogun and the orisa of the wild, while divine buffalo-figures belong to Fon and Vodun traditions. This Canadian-diaspora entry appears to be a fabrication.
