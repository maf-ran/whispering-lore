Nk (or Nkanyin)

spirit · Democratic Republic of the Congo · Central Africa

Nkornkanyin (Nk or Nkanyin) is a Congolese forest guardian spirit (DRC). A tall humanoid with long hair, leaf clothing, and glowing eyes, it can appear as a gorilla or leopard. It enforces forest protocol, cursing overhunters and resource-wasters with disease. Healing and nature manipulation are its powers. It reinforces sustainable hunting and respect for nature.
