Baba Djenka

ancestor-spirit · Ghana · West Africa

Not independently attested in documented Ghanaian folklore or academic sources: no collection records a forest spirit named 'Baba Djenka'. The description's traits — a spirit that steals items, twists paths and makes travelers lose their way — resemble the documented Akan mmoatia, small forest dwarfs with backward-turned feet, but no figure bears this name.
