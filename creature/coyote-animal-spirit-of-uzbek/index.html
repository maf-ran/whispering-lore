Coyote Animal-Spirit of Uzbek

animal-spirit · Uzbekistan · Central Asia

In Uzbek folklore, the coyote animal-spirit occupies a unique position as a trickster figure adapted from the broader Central Asian tradition of cunning animals, which includes the fox, wolf, and jackal. Uzbek mythology, influenced by Zoroastrian, Tengrist, and Islamic traditions, features this spirit as a messenger between the surface world and the underground realms of spirits known as the Yer-Suv.
