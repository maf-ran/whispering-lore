Agbé

deity · Benin · West Africa

Agbé is the powerful sea deity of the Fon people of Benin, ruling the ocean and all marine life within the Vodun pantheon. As the third-born son of the creator pair Mawu and Lisa, Agbé occupies a crucial position in the divine hierarchy, governing the vast, unpredictable, and life-sustaining waters that border the Fon homeland. For the coastal communities of Benin whose survival depended on fishing and maritime trade, Agbé's presence was immediate and essential.
