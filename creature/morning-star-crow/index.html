Morning Star

deity · United States · North America

Morning Star is a deity from Crow mythology. Placed in the East, he was commanded to overcome the Evening Star in the West, who resisted the divine plan to create humankind. Morning Star fought and conquered forces in the western sky with his fireball to mate with her, producing the first human. He is associated with warfare, sacrifice, and cosmic order. The Skidi Pawnee performed the Morning Star sacrifice ceremony—a ritual sacrifice of a captive girl to ensure agricultural fertility—reenacting the cosmic battle between Morning Star and Evening Star
