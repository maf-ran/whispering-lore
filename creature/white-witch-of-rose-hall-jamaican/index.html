White Witch of Rose Hall (Annie Palmer)

ghost · Jamaica · Caribbean

The White Witch of Rose Hall is Jamaica most famous ghost story -- the restless spirit of Annie Palmer, the cruel mistress of Rose Hall Plantation in Montego Bay, who allegedly murdered three husbands and countless enslaved workers through her practice of black magic (obeah). According to legend, Annie Palmer was born in England but raised in Haiti, where she learned voodoo from her Haitian nurse.
