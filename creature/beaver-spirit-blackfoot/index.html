Beaver Spirit

animal-spirit · United States · North America

The Beaver Spirit is a powerful animal spirit in Blackfoot religion, central to the sacred Beaver Bundle—the most important medicine bundle of the Blackfoot people. According to legend, a Blackfoot man encountered beavers who taught him sacred songs, rituals, and the use of medicinal plants. The Beaver Bundle contains animal skins, pipes, and herbs, each with specific powers. The Beaver Bundle ceremony involves elaborate rituals, singing, and smoking the pipe, used for healing, good fortune, and maintaining balance between human and spirit worlds
