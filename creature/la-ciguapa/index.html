La Ciguapa

spirit · Dominican Republic · Caribbean

La Ciguapa (sometimes spelled La Ciguapa or Ciguapa Remadora) is a legendary female creature from Dominican Republic folklore, particularly from the rural Cibao Valley region. She is one of the most iconic figures in Dominican mythology, representing the wild, untamed feminine spirit of the island. The legend likely blends Taíno indigenous beliefs about forest spirits with African and Spanish folk traditions introduced during the colonial period.
