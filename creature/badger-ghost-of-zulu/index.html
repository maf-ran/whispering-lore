Badger Ghost of Zulu

ghost · South Africa · Southern Africa

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Zulu tradition records a badger ghost, although the honey badger (ratel) is respected for its ferocity in African animal lore. Genuine Zulu spiritual beings include the amadlozi (ancestral spirits), the trickster Hlakanyana, and the impundulu (lightning bird); no badger figure appears in the record.
