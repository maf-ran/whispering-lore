Djibo

shapeshifter · Mali · West Africa

A Malian hyena-shapeshifter figure: a being said to pass between hyena and human form in the service of sorcerers, aligned with the Bambara (Bamana) Korè initiation society, whose suruku hyena masks are danced in ritual and whose members 'become' hyenas by roleplay.
