Flying Troll

troll · Sweden · Nordic

Not independently attested in Swedish folklore. Trolls in Nordic tradition are earth-bound (mountain, forest, sea); snip flight is not a traditional attribute. The 'flying troll' appears in the literary fairy tale 'Dag and Daga and the Flying Troll of Sky Mountain' but this is a modern fairy-tale construction, not oral tradition.
