Rusalka

water-spirit · Russia · Slavic

Rusalka is the female water spirit of Slavic origin who occupies a central place in Ukrainian folklore distinct from the forest-dwelling Mavka. She is the soul of a young woman who drowned, either by suicide after a broken heart or by accident, or of an unbaptized child. Rusalki appear as beautiful pale maidens with long, loose green or brown hair, disproportionately long arms, and translucent skin.
