Vodnik

water-spirit · Slovenia · Europe

Vodnik (Unknown). is a water-spirit. The Vodnik is a water spirit from Slavic folklore, particularly known in Slovenian tradition. It is said to dwell in rivers, lakes, and streams, and can appear as a humanoid with green hair and webbed. Source: Slavic Folklore: A Handbook (Kononenko, 2007)
