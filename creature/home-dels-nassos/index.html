Home dels Nassos

spirit · Spain · Mediterranean

Home dels Nassos (Catalan for 'Man of the Noses') is a traditional Catalan mythological figure associated with New Year's Eve. According to legend, this peculiar being has as many noses as there are days in the year — 365 on January 1st, losing one each day as the year progresses. The trick, of course, is that on December 31st, he has only one nose remaining, meaning any ordinary person on the street could be him.
