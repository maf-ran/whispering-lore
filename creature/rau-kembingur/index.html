Rauðkembingur

sea-monster · Iceland · Nordic

Rauðkembingur (Red-Crest) is the most savage of all the illhveli (evil whales) in Icelandic folklore. It has a distinctive red crest or mane running along its back and is 10-20 meters long, streamlined, and extremely fast in the water. Rauðkembingur attacks ships with unmatched ferocity, ramming them at high speed and leaping across decks. It neighs ominously before attacking. According to one legend, a callous young man was cursed by elves into becoming a rauðkembingur as punishment for his cruelty.
