Wildcat Deity of Ute

deity · United States · North America

Not independently attested as a wildcat deity in Ute tradition. Ute mythology centers on Sinawav (Coyote) as creator and culture hero, and the wolf plays a major role in Ute origin accounts; no wildcat god or goddess appears in recorded Ute stories. The record is a misattribution, likely generated from a generic feline template rather than Ute oral tradition.
