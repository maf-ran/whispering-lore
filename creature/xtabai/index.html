Xtabai

spirit · Belize · Mesoamerica

Xtabai (also spelled Xtabay) is a demonic femme fatale from Yucatec Maya folklore who lures men to their deaths with her incredible beauty. She appears as a stunning woman with long black hair falling to her ankles, wearing a white dress, and combs her hair with the spines of a Tzacam cactus while waiting behind a ceiba tree. According to legend, she was once Utz-colel, a haughty and pure woman who, after death, prayed to evil spirits and became a demon.
