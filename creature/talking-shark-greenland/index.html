Talking Shark

spirit · Greenland · North America

Skalugsuak (Eqalussuaq) is the legendary Greenland shark of Inuit lore, said to have been born when an old woman's urine-soaked hair cloth blew into the sea and became the shark; the Igloolik Inuit say it lives in Sedna's urine pot and acts as a helping spirit to shamans. Its flesh, toxic with urea, smells of urine. A 'talking' benevolent shark, however, is not documented.
