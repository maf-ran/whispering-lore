Kakamora

spirit · Solomon Islands · Pacific

The Kakamora are mythical elf-like creatures in the folklore of Makira and Guadalcanal in the Solomon Islands. They are described as small, hairy beings with great strength who live in the deep forests and remote mountains. The Kakamora are shy but mischievous, sometimes stealing food from villages or playing tricks on travelers. In some traditions they are considered forest guardians who protect sacred groves and punish those who disrespect nature. Their small size allows them to move through the forest undetected.
