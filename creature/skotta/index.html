Skotta

ghost · Iceland · Nordic

Skotta (meaning Tail) is a specific type of female ghost in Icelandic folklore named for the distinctive tail-like appearance of her headdress hanging down her back. Skotta wears the traditional Icelandic Faldbúningur headdress but backward — the long tail of the headdress hangs down her back like a tail (skott). She wears rust-red headwear, red socks, and is often described sucking her fingers or chewing on her own hair.
