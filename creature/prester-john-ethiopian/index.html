Prester John

folk-hero · Ethiopia · East Africa

Prester John (Latin: Presbyter Ioannes) is a legendary Christian patriarch and king widely believed in medieval Europe to rule a vast Christian kingdom beyond the Islamic world, later identified with Ethiopia. The legend began in the 12th century with a purported letter to Byzantine Emperor Manuel I Komnenos describing a magnificent kingdom of riches ruled by a Nestorian Christian priest-king descended from the Three Magi.
