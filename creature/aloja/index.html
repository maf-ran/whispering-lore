Aloja

siren · Spain · Mediterranean

Aloja is a type of siren or water spirit from Slavic folklore, particularly found in Polish, Czech, and Slovak traditions. Like the Greek sirens, Alojas are beautiful female water spirits who use their enchanting voices to lure men to their doom. They are associated with rivers, lakes, and other bodies of water where they sing to passing travelers, captivating them with their ethereal voices. The men who fall under their spell are drawn into the water where they drown.
