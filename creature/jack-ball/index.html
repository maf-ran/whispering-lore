Jack Ball

object · United States · North America

The jack ball is a protective charm of African American Hoodoo and conjure: a red-flannel bundle of roots, herbs, bones, coins, nails, and personal objects bound with string, dressed with oils and 'fed' to maintain its power. Placed to guard a home or carried as a talisman, it descends from Kongo nkisi power objects and parallels the mojo bag.
