Mami Wata

water-spirit · Nigeria · West Africa

Mami Wata of Nigeria manifests as a seductive mermaid goddess with long hair and a fish tail, believed to control the sea, storms, and fortune. Her devotees enter trance states and spirit marriage, and her cult is deeply tied to coastal communities, fishing economies, and trade along the Bight of Benin.
