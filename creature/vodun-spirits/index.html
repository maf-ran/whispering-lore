Vodun Spirits

deity · Benin · West Africa

Vodun is the Fon/Ewe/Aja religion of Benin/Togo/Ghana venerating hundreds of spirits (vodún) under Nana Buluku and Mawu-Lisa. Key spirits: Legba (crossroads), Sakpata (earth/smallpox), Xêbioso (thunder), Gû (iron/war), Dàn (serpent wealth), Mami Wata (water wealth). Living priesthood tradition; Benin's national religion since 1990s.
