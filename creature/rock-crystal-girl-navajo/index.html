Rock Crystal Girl

spirit · United States · North America

Tséghádiʼnídíinii atʼééd, Rock Crystal Girl, is a Navajo holy being who dwells with her brother Rock Crystal Boy at Sisnaajiní (Blanca Peak), the sacred mountain of the East. She was created by the Holy People when the six sacred mountains were re-established after the emergence into the Fourth World, embodying the white shell and crystal essence of dawn.
