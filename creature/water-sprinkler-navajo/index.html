Water Sprinkler (Tó Neinilí)

spirit · United States · North America

Water Sprinkler (Tó Neinilí) is a Navajo Holy Person closely associated with water, rain, and all forms of moisture. He works alongside Talking God and other Diyin Dine'é in the creation narratives, helping to shape the sacred mountains and assisting in the emergence from the Third World during the great flood.
