Yara-ma-yha-who

spirit · Australia · Pacific

The Yara-ma-yha-who is a vampiric spirit from Aboriginal Australian Dreaming legends, particularly in the mythology of the Yuin people of southeastern Australia. This creature is described as a small, red-skinned being standing about four feet tall with an oversized head, a large mouth, and long, skinny limbs. Each of its hands and feet ends in suckers like those of an octopus or frog. The Yara-ma-yha-who lives in fig trees and drops down on unsuspecting travelers, using its suckers to drain their blood.
