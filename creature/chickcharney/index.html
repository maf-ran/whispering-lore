Chickcharney

cryptid · Bahamas · Caribbean

The Chickcharney is a legendary bird-like creature from the folklore of Andros Island in the Bahamas, one of the most distinctive mythological beings in Caribbean oral tradition. According to Bahamian legend, the Chickcharney inhabits the dense pine forests of Andros, the largest island in the Bahamas archipelago. The creature has been part of local folklore for centuries, with stories passed down through generations.
