Pond Spirit (Guarani)

water-spirit · United States · North America

Not attested in North American folklore. This entry is a misattributed Guarani water-spirit template placed in the United States, with no named being and a habitat field garbled as 'Paraguay, USA rivers.' Genuine Guarani tradition has water beings such as Yacy Yateré and the Ypytarã of streams, but no specific 'pond spirit' is documented here.
