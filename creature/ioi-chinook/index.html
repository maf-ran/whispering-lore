Ioi

spirit-animal · United States · North America

Ioi is the sister of Blue Jay in Chinook tradition. Taken as a wife into the land of the dead, she tried patiently to teach her brother the customs of the ghost people, but Blue Jay mocked and mishandled everything and her warnings went unheeded. Her story is a lesson in approaching the supernatural world with humility and respect.
