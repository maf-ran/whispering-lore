White Reindeer

spirit-animal · Norway · Sami

The White Reindeer is the most sacred animal in Sami belief, a spirit creature associated with the sun goddess Beaivi and the mythic reindeer Meandash. Seeing a white reindeer was considered a powerful omen — an encounter with the divine. Its appearance in dreams or visions signaled major life changes. The white reindeer was the most valuable sacrifice to the gods — white reindeer were specifically offered to Beaivi at the winter solstice to ensure the sun's return, and to Bieggolmmái to control the winds. In Sami shamanism, the reindeer was the most important power animal, used for offerings
