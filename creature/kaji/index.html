Kaji

household-spirit · Georgia · Caucasus

Kaji are a race of dwarfish master craftsmen in Georgian (Caucasian) folklore. Small and skilled, they live in hidden mountain realms, create magical items, and possess stone-manipulation and house-protection powers. Though helpful, they can be mischievous. Their hidden workshops and magical craftsmanship parallel Norse dvergar and Germanic Wichtel.
