Kuman Thong

household-spirit · Thailand · Southeast Asia

Kuman Thong is a household spirit from Thai folklore, the animated spirit of a child that has been bound to a golden statue through black magic rituals. The name Kuman Thong means "Golden Child Spirit," and these beings are created through complex and controversial ceremonies performed by traditional sorcerers known as wei monks or ajahn. Originally, the creation of a Kuman Thong involved the remains of a child who died in the womb or shortly after birth, but modern practice typically uses consecrated statues. These spirits are believed to bring good luck, protection, and wealth to their owner
