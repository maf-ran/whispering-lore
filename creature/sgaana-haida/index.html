SGaana (Supernatural Killer Whale)

spirit-animal · United States · North America

SGaana are the powerful supernatural killer whales of Haida mythology, considered the most powerful of the undersea beings. They can transform into human form and own houses, territories, and crests just like human chiefs. The SGaana live in magnificent underwater villages with houses made of copper and abalone shell. Those who drown do not truly die but are taken to live among the SGaana in the undersea kingdom, where they may acquire great supernatural power and wealth. The SGaana are the guardians of the sea and control the wealth of the ocean
