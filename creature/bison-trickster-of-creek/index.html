Bison Trickster of Creek

trickster · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. No Creek (Muscogee) bison trickster exists; bison were marginal in the Southeast, and Creek trickster stories center on figures like Rabbit (Chukfi). The swamp-guardian, shape-shifting, and 'hard lessons' moralizing are invented with no named tradition.
