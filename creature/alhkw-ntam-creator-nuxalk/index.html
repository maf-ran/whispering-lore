Alhkw Ntam Creator Nuxalk

creator-deity · Canada · North America

Alhkw'ntam is the creator of the Nuxalk (Bella Coola), who made the world and set the sun and moon in their boxes in the sky house Nusmata. With the Four Carpenters, his helpers Masmasalanicw, he shaped the land, gave the people their arts, and established the order of the seasons and ceremonial life.
