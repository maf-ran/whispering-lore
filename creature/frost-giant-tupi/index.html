Frost Giant (Tupi)

giant · Brazil · South America

Not independently attested as a figure of Tupi tradition. Tupi-Guarani cosmology of tropical Brazil records beings such as Tupã, Anhangá and the Curupira, but no source mentions a frost giant or attributes unseasonable cold to one; frost is rare in the lowlands and is not personified in Tupi mythology. The entry is a generic giant archetype misassigned to the Tupi.
