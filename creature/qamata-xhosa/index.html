Qamata

deity · South Africa · Southern Africa

Qamata (also known as uThixo) is the supreme creator god of the Xhosa people who existed before all things. From a primordial void of darkness and silence, Qamata created the world using Uhlanga (sacred reeds) as the material for all life. He divided existence into three realms: the sky for himself, the earth for humans, and the underworld for ancestors. He defeated the chaos giant Iqungqungqu to bring order to the cosmos, but remains distant from daily affairs, working through ancestors and natural signs with thunder and the sun representing his power
