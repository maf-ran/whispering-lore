Crop Eaters

spirit · United States · North America

No 'Crop Eaters' spirits appear in any documented Native American tradition, and none in Blackfoot culture, a Plains people who were bison hunters rather than farmers. The entry is internally inconsistent and appears entirely fabricated. Real analogues such as the Arikara Mother Corn or the Cherokee Corn Mother Selu do not match this description.
