Mátsailéma (Evening Star, War God)

deity · United States · North America

Mátsailéma is the Zuni god of war and the Evening Star, the younger twin of Áhaiyúta; together the brothers are the Áhayuta, the Twin War Gods of Zuni tradition. In Cushing's tale the pair fared with the Unborn-Made Men of the underworld, and warriors and priests invoke them in the great war ceremonial.
