Wee-Tent (or Wee-Tin)

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested under the name Wee-Tent. The Ojibwe and other Anishinaabe peoples do tell of little people, known as Bagwajiwininiwag, 'the wilderness little Anishinaabe,' along with the water-dwelling memengwesi and the mischievous bajiine, but no source names one of them Wee-Tent. The entry appears to be a garbled or invented label for a genuine class of little forest spirits.
