Los Ahuiillas

spirit · Peru · South America

Los Ahuiillas (or Ahuillas) are Peruvian Andean road spirits: ghosts of murdered travelers who appear at night near abandoned houses and mountain paths. They warn or terrify passersby, reflecting colonial-era dangers on highland routes. Documented in central/southern Peru oral tradition.
