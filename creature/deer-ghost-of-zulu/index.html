Deer Ghost of Zulu

ghost · South Africa · Southern Africa

Among the Zulu people of South Africa, the Deer Ghost is a spirit that inhabits the river valleys and deep ravines of KwaZulu-Natal, serving as a guardian of the ancestral burial grounds that lie hidden in these secluded places. In Zulu cosmology, the spirits of the ancestors, known as amadlozi, often take the form of animals as messengers between the living and the dead. The Deer Ghost is one such spirit, a manifestation of an ancestor who was particularly connected to the wilderness and who now watches over the boundary between human settlements and the untamed bushveld
