Kachin-mana

spirit · United States · North America

Kachin-mana is a spirit being in Hopi mythology associated with the initiation ceremonies of young women. She appears as a benevolent figure who guides maidens through the transition to adulthood, teaching them the sacred songs and dances of the Kachina tradition. Kachin-mana is often depicted alongside the other Kachina spirits during the Bean Dance (Powamuya) and the Home Dance ceremonies. She carries a corn husk basket filled with sacred meal and distributes blessings of fertility, health, and harmony to the village
