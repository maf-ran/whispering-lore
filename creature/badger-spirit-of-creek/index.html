Badger Spirit of Creek

spirit · United States · North America

Not independently attested in folklore literature. The badger is not among the documented Muscogee (Creek) animal clans or spirit figures. Muscogee tradition instead honours the Master of Breath, clan animals such as Bear and Deer, and beings like the Tie-Snake and Little People; no badger spirit is attested.
