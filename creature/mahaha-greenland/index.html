Mahaha

demon · Greenland · North America

The Mahaha is a thin, pale-blue demon of Arctic Inuit folklore that hunts solitary people on the ice. It sneaks up on victims and tickles them with long, sharp fingernails until they die of suffocation, leaving their faces frozen in a permanent smile. It captures the dread of isolation and of sudden, absurd death in the winter night.
