Passadinha

bird · Cape Verde · West Africa

Passadinha is a trickster bird in Cape Verdean folklore, known for vocal mimicry and mischief in village gardens and forests. It embodies wit and playfulness, deceiving humans through imitation. Documented in Cape Verdean oral tales alongside figures like Ti Lobo and Chibinho.
