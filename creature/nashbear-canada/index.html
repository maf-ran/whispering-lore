Nash Bear

spirit · Canada · North America

Not independently attested in any recorded Canadian First Nations tradition. No published collection or tribal account documents a spirit named the Nash Bear, and the entry appears to be invented or garbled. Across Canada, however, the bear is a deeply honored being: a clan ancestor, medicine spirit, and guardian among nations such as the Cree, Ojibwe, and Coast Salish.
