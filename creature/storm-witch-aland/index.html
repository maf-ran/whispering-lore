Åland Storm-Witch

witch · Åland · Nordic

The storm-witch of Åland is a female practitioner of weather magic who possesses the secret art of tying the wind into knots of rope. Unlike malevolent hags who conjure storms through incantations, the Åland storm-witch operates as a trader of winds: she sells sailors knotted ropes that release favorable breezes when untied. Untying one knot produces a light wind, untying two fills the sails, but untying all three awakens a devastating tempest.
